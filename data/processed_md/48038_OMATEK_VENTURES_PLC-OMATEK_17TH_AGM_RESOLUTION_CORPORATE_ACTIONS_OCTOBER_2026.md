17TH ANNUAL GENERAL MEETING: RESOLUTIONS PASSSED
THEREAT
At the 17th Annual General Meeting of Omatek Ventures Plc. (the
Company) held on the 30th day of September, 2026, at the Lagos
Airport Hotel, 111 Obafemi Awolowo Way, Ikeja, Lagos; it was
proposed to be resolved and was accordingly resolved as follows:
1. That the Audited Financial Statements of the Company as at 31
December, 2025, together with the Reports of the Directors and the
Auditors laid before the Meeting is hereby received and adopted”.
2. That Dr. Timothy Farinre who by Special Notice, is over 70 years of age
and offered himself for re- election as a Director of the Company is
hereby re-elected.
3. That Alhaji Ibrahim Dasuki Nakande who by Special Notice, is over 70
years of age and offered himself for re- election as a Director of the
Company is hereby re-elected.
4. That the following persons are hereby elected as members of the
Statutory Audit Committee of the Company:
a. Mr. Basil Ubah
b. Mr. Peter Eyanuku
c. Mr. Chuks Nwosa Osadinizu

5. That the following persons are hereby nominated to represent the Board
on the Statutory Audit Committee of the Company:
a. Alhaji Ibrahim Dasuki Nakande
b. Mr. Temidayo Seriki
6. That in the matter of the Remuneration of Directors of the Company, the
Board should proceed to fix the remuneration of the Directors.
7. That the said resolutions are with immediate effect.
Femi David Ikotun Esq.
