30th June, 2026
Lagos, Nigeria
DANGOTE SUGAR REFINERY PLC – NOTICE OF CLOSED PERIOD AND MEETING OF
THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS
In compliance with Rule 17:17 to 17:18 of the Issuers’ Rules of Nigerian Exchange Limited (As
Amended), Dangote Sugar Refinery Plc (the Company) hereby announces the commencement
of a Closed Period for trading in the Company’s securities from 1st July, 2026 until twenty-four
(24) hours after the filing of the Unaudited Financial Statements for the Half Year ended 30th
June 2026 (HY 2026 UFS) via the NGX Issuers’ Portal.
Furthermore, the Company wishes to notify the Nigerian Exchange Limited (NGX) and the
investing public that a meeting of the Board of Directors of the Company is scheduled to be
held on Thursday 30th July, 2026, to consider the HY 2026 UFS.
No insider of the Company and their connected persons may deal directly or indirectly in the
securities of the Company during the Closed Period.
All Dangote Sugar Refinery PLC Insiders have been duly informed.
Thank you.
