PRESS RELEASE Lagos, Nigeria | 26 July 2026
NGX Group Delivers Record First-Half Performance, Announces ₦1.30 Interim Dividend
Revenue rises 118% as profit before tax increases 170%, reflecting strong market activity,
operating leverage and increased contribution from investee companies.
Nigerian Exchange Group Plc (“NGX Group” or “the Group”) has announced an interim
dividend of ₦1.30 per ordinary share for the six months ended 30 June 2026, following a
record first-half financial performance.
The dividend reflects the strength of the Group’s earnings, improved cash generation and the
Board’s confidence in the sustainability of NGX Group’s growth trajectory, while preserving
capacity for continued investment in technology, market development and strategic
opportunities across the capital market value chain.
Record Financial Performance
NGX Group recorded revenue of ₦17.60 billion in the first half of 2026, representing an
increase of 118% from ₦8.08 billion in the corresponding period of 2025.
Total income increased by 96% to ₦19.34 billion, compared with ₦9.86 billion in H1 2025.
The performance was driven principally by increased market activity, with transaction fees
rising by 169% to ₦13.34 billion from ₦4.96 billion. Listing fees increased by 59% to ₦2.38
billion, while technology income rose by 19% to ₦447.86 million.
Operating profit increased by 155% to ₦10.62 billion, compared with ₦4.16 billion in the
corresponding period. This reflected strong operating leverage, as growth in income
significantly outpaced the increase in operating expenses.
The Group also recorded a 130% increase in its share of profit from equity-accounted
investees to ₦4.14 billion, driven primarily by the strong performance of Central Securities
Clearing System Plc.
Consequently, profit before tax increased by 170% to ₦14.76 billion, from ₦5.46 billion in H1
2025, while profit after tax rose by 146% to ₦10.36 billion, compared with ₦4.22 billion in the
prior-year period.
The Group’s balance sheet remained strong, with total assets increasing to ₦75.87 billion at
30 June 2026, while shareholders’ equity rose to ₦60.49 billion from ₦55.20 billion at the end
of 2025.
Chairman’s Statement
Commenting on the results and dividend, the Group Chairman of NGX Group, Alhaji (Dr.)
Umaru Kwairanga, said:
“The Board’s approval of an interim dividend of ₦1.30 per ordinary share reflects the strength
of NGX Group’s first-half performance and our confidence in the Group’s long-term prospects.

We are encouraged by the significant growth recorded across the business and by the

Group Managing Director/Chief Executive Officer’s Statement
Commenting on the performance, the Group Managing Director and Chief Executive Officer

“Our first-half results demonstrate the strength and scalability of NGX Group’s business model.

enabled us to translate this growth into substantially improved profitability.
We remain focused on sustaining this momentum by deepening market liquidity, expanding
investor participation, accelerating the development of technology-enabled products and
building a more diversified financial market infrastructure group. The ₦1.30 interim dividend
reflects both the progress made and our confidence in the Group’s capacity to deliver
sustainable long-term value.”

• Profit after tax: ₦10.36 billion, up 146%

• Interim dividend: ₦1.30 per ordinary share
Dividend Information
Shareholders whose names appear in the Register of Members on the applicable qualification
date will be entitled to the interim dividend of ₦1.30 per ordinary share.
The qualification date, closure period and payment date will be communicated in accordance
with the approved corporate action timetable and applicable regulatory requirements.
