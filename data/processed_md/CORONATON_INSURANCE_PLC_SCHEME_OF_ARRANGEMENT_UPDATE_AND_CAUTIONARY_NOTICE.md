OCTOBER 23, 2026
LAGOS, NIGERIA
UPDATE ON THE PROPOSED SCHEME OF ARRANGEMENT BETWEEN CORONATION
INSURANCE PLC AND THE HOLDERS OF ITS FULLY PAID ORDINARY SHARES
On 22 December 2023, Coronation Insurance plc (the “Company”), held a court-ordered
meeting, convened pursuant to an order of the Federal High Court, at which its
shareholders considered and approved a scheme of arrangement between the Company
and the holders of its fully paid ordinary shares (the “Scheme”). The Scheme is in
furtherance of a proposal from Coronation Capital (Mauritius) Limited and its related
parties (together, the “Core Shareholders”) to acquire the shares of the other shareholders
in the Company.
On 27 December 2023, the Company applied to the Securities and Exchange Commission
(the “SEC”) for formal approval of the Scheme. However, the SEC noted its inability to
proceed with its review due to certain pending matters. Following diligent and thorough
action, these matters have now been resolved.
Accordingly, the Company wrote to the SEC on 22 September 2026 to inform it of this
development and to request its formal approval of the Scheme. By an email sent on
Wednesday, 7 October 2026, the SEC acknowledged the conclusion of these matters and
requested that the Company submit an application for formal approval together with all
the required documents. The Company submitted its application on 16 October 2026.
The Company has communicated these developments to the Core Shareholders, who have
confirmed their continued interest in, and intention to proceed with, the Scheme, on the
basis that its implementation is undertaken in compliance with applicable laws,
regulations, market rules and precedents. Subject to the receipt of all required regulatory
approvals, the consideration price payable under the Scheme shall be the highest trading
price recorded for the Company’s shares on the Nigerian Exchange Limited (“NGX”) during
the six (6) months preceding the date of this notification.
The Company therefore intends to proceed with the outstanding steps for implementing
the Scheme, namely: obtaining receipt of SEC’s formal approval of the Scheme; the
petition to the Federal High Court for an order sanctioning the Scheme; the filing of a
certified true copy of the sanction order with the Corporate Affairs Commission; the
payment of the scheme consideration to the minority shareholders; the application to
NGX for the delisting of the Company’s shares; and the delisting of the Company's shares
from the NGX.
The Company will keep shareholders and the investing public informed of further
developments.

Completion of the Scheme remains subject to the approval of the SEC, the sanction of the
Federal High Court and the filing of a certified true copy of the sanction order with the
Corporate Affairs Commission. Shareholders and the investing public are therefore
advised to exercise caution when dealing in the Company’s shares until further
announcements are made.
By Order of the Board
