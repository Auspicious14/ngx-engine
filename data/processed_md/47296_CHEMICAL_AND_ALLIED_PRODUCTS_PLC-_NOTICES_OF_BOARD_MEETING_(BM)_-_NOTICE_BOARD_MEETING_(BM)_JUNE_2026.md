N OTIC E OF Q2 2026 BOARD OF DIRECTORS’ MEETING AND CLOSED PERIOD
Notice is hereby given that the meeting of the Board of Directors (the “Board”) of Chemical and Allied
Products PLC (the “Company”) will be held on Thursday, July 23, 2026 at 2:00pm at 2, Adeniyi Jones
Avenue, Ikeja Lagos and hosted virtually via Teams conferencing platform.
The meeting is the regular quarterly meeting of the Board to consider, among other things, the
Company’s Unaudited Financial Statements for the quarter ending June 30, 2026 (the “Q2 2026
Financials”).
In accordance with the relevant provisions of the Rule Book of the Nigerian Exchange Limited (“NGX”),
the Closed Period for trading in the shares of the Company will commence from July 1, 2026, in respect
of the Q2 2026 Financials.
Hence, no director, persons discharging managerial responsibility, employees with sensitive information,
advisers and consultants of the Company, other insiders, and their connected persons may directly or
indirectly deal in the securities of the Company until twenty (24) hours after the release of the Q2 2026
Financials via NGX’s Issuers’ Portal and by extension, the general public.
