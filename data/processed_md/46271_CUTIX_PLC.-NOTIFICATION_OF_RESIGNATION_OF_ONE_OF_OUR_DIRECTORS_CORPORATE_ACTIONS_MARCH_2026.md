NOTICE OF RESIGNATION OF ONE OF OUR DIRECTORS
Cutix Plc (“the Company”) wishes to notify Nigerian Exchange Limited (NGX),
our esteemed Shareholders and the investing public that Mrs. Ijeoma Ezeasor an
Independent Non-Executive Director has resigned from the Board of Cutix Plc.
Her resignation took effect from March 3, 2026.
The Board of Directors expresses its sincere appreciation to Mrs. Ijeoma Ezeasor
for her valuable contributions to the growth and development of Cutix Plc during
her tenure on the Board and wishes her success in her future endeavors.
For: CUTIX PLC
