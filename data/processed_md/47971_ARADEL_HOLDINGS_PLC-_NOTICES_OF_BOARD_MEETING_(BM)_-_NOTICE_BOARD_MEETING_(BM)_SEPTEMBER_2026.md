Lagos, Nigeria - September 30th, 2026.
NOTICE OF BOARD MEETING
In line with the Rulebook of the Nigerian Exchange Limited (NGX), the Board of Directors of Aradel
Holdings Plc (the “Company”) hereby notifies the NGX and the investing public that a meeting of
the Board of Directors of the Company has been scheduled to hold on Tuesday, 27th of October
2026.
Among items slated for discussion at the meeting is to consider and approve the Unaudited Financial
Statements for the period ending 30th September 2026 (“Q3 2026 UFS”).
Accordingly, the closed period for trading in the Company’s shares will remain in effect from 1st
October 2026 until twenty-four (24) hours after the Q3 2026 UFS are released on the NGX portal.
During this period, insiders and their connected persons are prohibited from dealing in the
Company’s securities.
By order of the Board.
