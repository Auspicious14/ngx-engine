UAC of Nigeria PLC Enters into an Agreement to Sell its 43% Stake in DP World Logistics Limited
(formerly MDS Logistics Limited) to 22 Plus Invest Limited, a member of Tropical General
Investments (TGI) Group
Lagos, Nigeria – 24 September 2026 – UAC of Nigeria PLC (“UAC”) has entered into an agreement
to sell its 43% shareholding in DP World Logistics Limited (formerly MDS Logistics Limited) (“DP World”
or the “Company”), to 22 Plus Invest Limited, a member of Tropical General Investments (TGI)
Group (“TGI”), subject to requisite regulatory approval.
Fola Aiyesimoju, Group Managing Director of UAC, said:
“UAC has worked closely with DP World’s management to support the significant growth and
expansion of the Company over the years. We believe TGI, following completion of the proposed
transaction, will be a strong long-term partner for DP World, its management and employees,
bringing deep market knowledge and a strong track record of building businesses in Nigeria. We
look forward to seeing the Company continue to grow and strengthen its contribution to Nigeria’s
logistics sector.”
Farouk Gumel, Vice Chairman of TGI Group, said:
"This proposed acquisition and partnership with DP World reflects our shared ambition to reshape
the future of trade and logistics in Africa. By combining DP World's global expertise with TGI Group's
deep market knowledge and extensive distribution network, we are building resilient supply chains
that will unlock growth, strengthen regional connectivity and support economic prosperity for
generations to come."

About UAC of Nigeria PLC
UAC is a house of scalable quality brands, with subsidiary and associate companies operating in
the Packaged Food and Beverages, Paints, Animal Feeds and Other Edibles, Quick Service
Restaurants, Logistics and Real Estate segments. UAC has played a prominent role in Nigeria’s
development for over a century. The company is focused on building its businesses into leaders in
their chosen segments.
About TGI Group
Tropical General Investments (TGI) Group is a diversified conglomerate with over four decades of
operating history in Nigeria and the broader West African region amongst others. The Group
operates across Agriculture, Food processing, FMCG manufacturing, Distribution, Chemicals,
Poultry Farming, Real Estate, Financial Services etc., serving millions of consumers across the region
through a portfolio of leading household brands. These brands are underpinned by the Group’s
long-standing commitment to sustainability and to the communities in which it operates. TGI
Group’s operations extend beyond West Africa into other key emerging and developing markets
including India, UAE etc., supported by large manufacturing and distribution operations and a
diverse workforce of over 25,000 people.
