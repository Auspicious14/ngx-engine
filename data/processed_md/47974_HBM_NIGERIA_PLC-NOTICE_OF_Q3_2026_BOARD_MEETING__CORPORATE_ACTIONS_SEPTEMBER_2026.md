LAGOS: 30TH SEPTEMBER 2026
NOTICE OF BOARD MEETING AND CLOSED PERIOD
We hereby notify Nigerian Exchange Limited (“NGX”) and the investing public that a meeting
of the Board of Directors of HBM Nigeria Plc (the “Company”) has been scheduled to hold on
Thursday, 29th October 2026 to consider the Company’s Unaudited Financial Statements for
the period ending 30th September 2026 (the “Unaudited Financial Statements”).
Please note that the Closed Period for trading in the Company’s shares will commence on
Thursday, 1st October 2026 and will continue until twenty-four (24) hours after the Unaudited
Financial Statements is filed with NGX and released to the public in accordance with the
Rules of NGX.
Accordingly, no Insider may directly or indirectly deal in the securities of the Company in any
manner during the Closed Period.
