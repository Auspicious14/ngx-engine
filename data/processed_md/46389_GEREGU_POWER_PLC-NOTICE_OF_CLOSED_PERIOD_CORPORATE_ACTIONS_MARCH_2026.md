30th March, 2026
Notice of Closed Period
In accordance with the provisions of 17.18(a) Closed Period Rules, Rulebook of
The Exchange, 2015 (As amended) (Issuers’ Rules), the Closed Period for
trading in the shares of Geregu Power Plc (the Company) will commence on
1st April, 2026 in respect of the First Quarter Unaudited Financial Statements &
Accounts for the period ending 31st March, 2026.
Consequently, no Insider, or consultant of the Company and their connected
persons may directly or indirectly deal in the securities of the Company until 24
hours after the release of the First Quarter Unaudited Financial Statements &
Accounts for the period ending 31st March, 2026 through the Issuers’ Portal of
Nigerian Exchange Limited and by extension, the general public.
