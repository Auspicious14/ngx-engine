PZ CUSSONS NIGERIA PLC – RESOLUTIONS PASSED AT THE FIRST QUARTER
2026/2027 FINANCIAL YEAR BOARD MEETING HELD ON 29TH SEPTEMBER
2026
This is to notify our esteemed Shareholders, the investing public and the Nigerian
Exchange Limited that the Board of Directors of PZ Cussons Nigeria PLC (“the
Company”), at its meeting held on Tuesday, 29th September 2026, considered and
approved, amongst other things, the following:
1. The First Quarter Unaudited Financial Statements for the period ended 31st
August 2026
2. The Board also noted the resignation of Mr. Kareem Moustafa as a Non-
Executive Director of the Company, with effect from 28th September 2026. The
Board appreciates Mr. Moustafa's contributions and service to the Company
during his tenure and wishes him success in his future endeavours.
Please note that the Company’s closed period, which commenced on 1st September
2026, will remain in effect until twenty-four (24) hours after the release of the
Unaudited Financial Statements for the period ended 31st August 2026 to the
market.
