Lagos, 20th October 2026
NOTICE OF CHANGE OF BOARD MEETING DATE FOR THE
CONSIDERATION AND APPROVAL OF 3RD QUARTER 2026 UNAUDITED
FINANCIAL STATEMENTS
We refer to our earlier notice dated 6th October 2026 informing Nigerian Exchange
Limited (NGX) and the investing public that a meeting of the Board of Directors of BUA
Foods Plc (the Company) was scheduled to hold on Thursday, 29th October 2026 to
consider amongst other matters, the 3rd Quarter 2026 Unaudited Financial Statements for
the period ended 30th September 2026 (2026 Q3 UFS).
This is to notify NGX and the investing public that the Board meeting has been
rescheduled and will now hold on Friday, 30th October 2026.
The Closed Period declared by the Company remains effective and will continue until 24
hours after the release of the 2026 Q3 UFS.
BY ORDER OF THE BOARD
Oluseye Alayande
Company Secretary
