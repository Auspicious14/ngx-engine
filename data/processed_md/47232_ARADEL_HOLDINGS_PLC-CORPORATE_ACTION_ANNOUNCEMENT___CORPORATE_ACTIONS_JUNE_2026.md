CORPORATE ACTION ANNOUNCEMENT
ARADEL HOLDINGS PLC hereby announce as follows:
Period Ended December 31st 2025
A Final Dividend of N23.00 for every share (bringing the total dividend
for the financial year ended December 31st, 2025, to ₦33.00), subject
to appropriate withholding tax and approval, will be paid to
shareholders whose names appear in the Register of Members as at
Proposed Dividend the close of business on Thursday July 9th, 2026.
Proposed Bonus NIL
Closure of Register The Register of Shareholders will be closed on Friday, July 10th, 2026.
Qualification Date Thursday, July 9th, 2026
On Thursday, July 30th, 2026, dividends will be paid electronically to
shareholders whose names appear on the Register of Members as at
July 9th, 2026 and who have completed the e-dividend registration and
mandated the Registrar to pay their dividends directly into their Bank
Payment Date accounts.
Shareholders who are yet to complete the e-dividend registration are
advised to download the Registrar’s E-Dividend Mandate Activation
Form, which is available at https://coronation.ng, complete and
submit to the Registrar at their address below.
E-Dividend Alternatively scanned completed forms can be sent to
Registration customercare@coronationregistrars.com
Shareholders with dividend warrants and share certificates that have
Unclaimed Dividend remained unclaimed or are yet to be presented for payment or
Warrants and Share returned for validation are advised to complete the e-dividend
Certificates registration or contact the Registrar.
Date of Annual The Company’s Annual General Meeting will be held on July 30th, 2026,
General Meeting at 10:00 am & the venue will be communicated in due course.
