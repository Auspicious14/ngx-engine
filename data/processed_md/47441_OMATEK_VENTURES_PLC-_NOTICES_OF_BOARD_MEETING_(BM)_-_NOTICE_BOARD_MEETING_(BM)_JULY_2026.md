NOTICE OF PROPOSED BOARD MEETING AND CLOSED PERIOD
OMATEK VENTURES PLC. (the Company) wishes to inform Nigerian Exchange Limited
(NGX), its shareholders and the investing public that the Board of Directors of the
Company will meet on Tuesday, July 28, 2026 to amongst other matters, consider the
Second Quarter Unaudited Financial Statements for the period ended June 30, 2026
(2026 Q2 UFS) and other issues relating to the business of the Company.
In line with NGX’s Rules on Closed Period (As Amended), the Company commenced a
closed period from Wednesday July 1, 2026 and this will continue to be in force until
twenty-four (24) hours after the filing of the 2026 Q2 UFS and other Issues relating to
the business of the Company have been released to the public via NGX’s Issuers’
Portal.

restricted from dealing in the securities of the Company.
