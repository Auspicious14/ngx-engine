Lagos | Nigeria: 30 October 2026
MTN NIGERIA COMMUNICATIONS PLC – APPROVAL OF THE UNAUDITED
CONDENSED CONSOLIDATED FINANCIAL STATEMENTS FOR THE PERIOD
ENDING 30 SEPTEMBER 2026 AND COMMENCEMENT OF CLOSED PERIOD
We refer to our announcement dated 16 September 202 6 notifying Nigerian
Exchange Limited (NGX) and the investing public of the meeting of the Board
of Directors of MTN Nigeria Communications Plc (the Company).
Please be informed that the Board, at its meeting held on Thurs day, 29
October 202 6, considered and approved the Condensed Consolidated Interim
Financial Statements for the nine months ended 30 September 202 6 amongst
other agenda items.
In line with the NGX’s post -listing requirements, the Closed Period will remain
in effect until twenty -four (24) hours after the Financial Statements have been
released to the public. Accordingly, insiders of the Company and their
connected persons should not directly or indirectly deal in the Company's
securities in any manner during the Closed Period.

About MTN Nigeria
MTN Nigeria is one of Africa’s largest providers of communications services, connecting over
94 million people in communities across the country with each other and the world. Guided by
a belief that everybody deserves the benefits of a modern connected li fe, MTN Nigeria’s
leadership position in coverage, capacity, and innovation has remained constant since its
launch in 2001. The Company's subsidiaries are MoMo Payment Service Bank Limited, Yello
Digital Financial Services Limited and XS Broadband Limited. MTN Nigeria is part of the MTN
Group - a multinational telecommunications group which operates in 19 markets in Africa and
the Middle East, serving over 300 million people.
