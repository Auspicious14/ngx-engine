Total Voting Rights and Capital
London and Lagos, 2 March 2026: In accordance with the Financial Conduct
Authority's (FCA) Disclosure Guidance and Transparency Rule 5.6.1R, the Company
notifies the market that:
1. The Company’s issued share capital as at close of business on 28 February 2026
consisted of 3,654,881,028 ordinary shares of USD 0.50 per share (“Ordinary Shares”),
each with one vote, including 9,238,490 Ordinary shares held in Treasury. Ordinary
Shares held in Treasury do not carry any voting rights.
2. However, the total number of voting rights figure that may be used by shareholders as
the denominator for the calculations by which they will determine whether they are
required to notify their interest in, or a change to their interest in, the Company under
the Financial Conduct Authority's Disclosure Guidance and Transparency Rules, is
3,645,502,538.
3. The difference between the issued share capital and the total number of voting rights
(9,378,490 shares) relates to the 9,238,490 Ordinary shares held in Treasury and the
unsettled share purchases (140,000 shares) which are yet to be cancelled in
accordance with the ongoing share buyback programme of the Company as announced
on 22 September 2025.
