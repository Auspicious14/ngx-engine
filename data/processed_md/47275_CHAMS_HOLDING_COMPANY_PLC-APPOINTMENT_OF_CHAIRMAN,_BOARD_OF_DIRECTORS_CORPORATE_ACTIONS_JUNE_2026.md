26th June 2026
Lagos, Nigeria
CHAMS HOLDCO PLC - APPOINTMENT OF CHAIRMAN, BOARD OF DIRECTORS
Chams Holding Company Plc (the “Company”) hereby notifies the Nigerian Exchange
Limited (“NGX”) and the investing public that the Company has appointed MR. SEGUN
OLOKETUYI, as the Chairman of the Board of Directors of the Company, effective 9th July
2026.
The Board of Directors approved the appointment in line with the Company’s governance
processes and applicable regulatory requirements. Mr. Oloketuyi succeeds Sir Demola
Aladekomo, Founder of the Chams Group, after a 40-year strategic leadership tenure, which
ended following his successful retirement. The Board expresses its sincere appreciation to the
outgoing Chairman for his invaluable contributions, exemplary leadership, and dedicated
service to the Company.
Before his appointment, Mr. Oloketuyi served as a Non-Executive Director on the Board of
Directors of the Company and made significant contributions to the Company’s strategic
direction and governance oversight.
PROFILE OF MR SEGUN OLOKETUYI
Mr. Segun Oloketuyi, B.Sc., MBA, FCA, is a distinguished banking and financial services
professional with extensive experience spanning banking, corporate finance, enterprise risk
management, and corporate governance.
He retired as the Managing Director/Chief Executive Officer of Wema Bank Plc, where he
provided strategic leadership and drove the Bank’s transformation and growth.
Prior to this, he served as General Manager, Business Optimization Division, and later as
Executive Director, Business Development at Skye Bank Plc. He also served as Deputy
General Manager in charge of Corporate and Commercial Markets at Polaris Bank (formerly
Prudent Bank Plc), and subsequently as Executive Director, Finance and Enterprise Risk
Management.
Mr. Oloketuyi also previously served as Managing Director of Bond Bank Limited and began
his professional career as an Auditor with Deloitte & Touche.
He is a Fellow of the Institute of Chartered Accountants of Nigeria (ICAN) and a member of
the Institute of Directors and the Ikoyi Club.

Mr. Oloketuyi was a former Chairman of Great Nigeria Insurance Plc and currently serves as
Chairman of Japaul Gold and Ventures Plc and Greenwich Merchant Bank.
The Board is confident that Mr. Oloketuyi’s wealth of experience, strategic insight, and proven
leadership will further strengthen the Company’s governance framework, enhance Board
effectiveness, and drive sustainable value creation for shareholders and stakeholders.
This announcement is issued in compliance with applicable regulatory requirements and in
the interest of transparency.
