Lagos, Nigeria | 2 October 2026
DELAY IN THE PUBLICATION OF ACCESS HOLDINGS PLC’S INTERIM AUDITED
FINANCIAL STATEMENTS FOR THE PERIOD ENDED 30 JUNE 2026
Access Holdings Plc ("the Company") hereby notifies the investing public that Nigerian
Exchange Limited ("NGX") has granted the Company a further extension to file its
Interim Audited Financial Statements for the period ended 30 June 2026 ("the
Results").
In the Company’s earlier announcement, NGX had approved an extension of the filing
deadline for the Results to 30 September 2026, to enable the Company to complete
the requisite regulatory approval process.
As the Company awaits completion of the regulatory approval process, NGX has
granted a further extension to the Company to publish the Results promptly upon
receipt of the requisite approval.
The Company will keep the market informed of any material development relating to
this matter.
BY ORDER OF THE BOARD
SUNDAY EKWOCHI
COMPANY SECRETARY
