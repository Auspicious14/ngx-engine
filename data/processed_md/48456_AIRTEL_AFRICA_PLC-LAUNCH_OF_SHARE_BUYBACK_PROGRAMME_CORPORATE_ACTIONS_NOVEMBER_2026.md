Airtel Africa plc
(‘Airtel Africa’, or the ‘Company’)
Launch of share buyback programme
London and Lagos, 27 November 2026: Airtel Africa, a leading provider of telecommunications and mobile
money services with operations in 14 countries across Africa, announces that the Board of directors has
approved the further repurchase of up to 1% of the Company’s issued share capital. Following this approval, the
company has entered into a new share buyback programme (the “Programme”) with Barclays Capital Securities
Limited (“Barclays”), following the completion of the previous share buyback programme on 26 November 2026.
The Board’s decision reflects the Group’s disciplined approach to capital allocation, prioritising investment
across its markets to support future growth, while maintaining a strong and resilient balance sheet. The Group’s
strong financial position enables it to continue investing for growth while returning surplus capital to
shareholders, with share buybacks an important component of its diversified shareholder return strategy.
The Company has entered into an agreement with Barclays to conduct the Programme and carry out on-market
purchases of its ordinary shares with the Company subsequently purchasing its ordinary shares from Barclays
(the “Agreement”). Barclays will act as riskless principal pursuant to the Agreement.
The Agreement has commenced today and comprises two elements which will operate in parallel:
• a non-discretionary element pursuant to which Barclays will purchase up to $70m of ordinary shares (and
not less than $60m) and will make trading decisions independently of the Company which is anticipated to
terminate no later than 30 March 2027; and
• a discretionary element pursuant to which the Company may, at its discretion and subject to the provisions
of the Market Abuse Regulation (EU) No 596/2014 (as it forms part of domestic law by virtue of the
European Union (Withdrawal) Act 2018, as amended), provide instructions to Barclays for the purchase of
additional ordinary shares provided that the aggregate number of ordinary shares purchased under the non-
discretionary and discretionary elements does not exceed the 1% authority, which is currently anticipated
to terminate no later than 30 June 2027.
The sole purpose of the Programme is to reduce the capital of the Company. As such, all shares purchased under
the Agreement will be cancelled.
Any purchases of ordinary shares under either element of the Agreement will be carried out in accordance with
certain pre-set parameters set out in the Agreement and in accordance with (and subject to the limits prescribed
by) the Company's general authority to repurchase ordinary shares granted by its shareholders from time to
time (at the annual general meeting on 9 July 2026, shareholders gave the Company authority to purchase a
maximum of 364,577,234 ordinary shares and following the completion of the previous buyback programme,
the remaining authority amounts to a maximum of 340,693,642 ordinary shares), Financial Conduct Authority's
UK Listing Rules 9.6 and the provisions of the Market Abuse Regulation (EU) No 596/2014 (as it forms part of
domestic law by virtue of the European Union (Withdrawal) Act 2018, as amended). The discretionary element
may include irrevocable, non-discretionary instructions to Barclays to continue to operate the buyback
programme during closed periods. Barclays will therefore operate the discretionary element of buyback
programme autonomously during those periods. For the avoidance of doubt, no repurchases will be made on
the Nigerian Exchange Limited.
