NOTICE OF MEETING OF THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF CUTIX PLC AND
CLOSED PERIOD
Cutix Plc (the Company) hereby notifies Nigerian Exchange Limited (NGX) and the investing
public that a meeting of the Board of Directors of the Company is scheduled to hold on
Thursday, June 26, 2026. The following items will be transacted during the meeting:
1. Consideration and Approval of Audited Financial Statements for the year ended April 30,
2026 (2026 AFS)
2. Any Other Business
A notification will be released to the public via the Issuers’ Portal not later than 24 hours after
the Board meeting.
Given the above, the Company hereby announces that the existing Closed Period which
commenced on 1 May 2026 still subsists until twenty-four (24) hours after the 2026 AFS is
released to the public. All those who are considered “insiders” of the Company in accordance
with the provisions of the Rules of The Exchange are under restriction not to trade or deal in
the shares of the Company within this closed period.
