Lagos, 20 February 2026.
NOTIFICATION OF THE APPOINTMENT OF AN EXCUTIVE DIRECTOR (TECHNICAL).
This is to notify Nigerian Exchange Limited (NGX) and the investing public of the
appointment and approval of National Insurance Commission (NAICOM) of Mr.
Abhinandan Singh as an Executive Director on the Board of Prestige Assurance PLC
(“the Company”) effective 18 February 2026.
Profile of Mr. Abhinandan Singh
Mr Singh is a versatile insurance professional with extensive experience across Technical
Underwriting, Claims Administration, Operations, Accounts, and Energy business, with a
strong marketing acumen gained within the New India Assurance Company Limited.
He is a Fellow of the Insurance Institute of India, an accomplished insurer and
administrator with a proven track record across diverse functional areas, supported by
over fifteen (15) years of industry experience.
The Board is confident that he will bring his wealth of experience to bear in driving the
growth and expansion of the business, and that the Company will be further
strengthened by his valuable contributions.
MRS CHIDINMA IBE-LOUIS
Company Secretary
