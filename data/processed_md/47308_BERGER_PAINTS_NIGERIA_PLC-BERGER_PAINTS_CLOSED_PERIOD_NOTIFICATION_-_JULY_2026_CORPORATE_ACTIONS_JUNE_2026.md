Lagos: 30 June 2026
NOTIFICATION OF CLOSED PERIOD/BOARD MEETING TO CONSIDER AND APPROVE THE
QUARTER 2 2026 UNAUDITED FINANCIAL STATEMENTS
This is to notify Nigerian Exchange Limited (NGX) and by extension, the public, that the Board of
Directors of Berger Paints Nigeria Plc. (the Company) will be meeting on Tuesday, 28 July 2026,
to, amongst other matters, consider and approve the Company’s Unaudited Financial Statements
for the second quarter ending 30 June 2026 (Q2 2026 UFS).
Accordingly, a ‘Closed Period’ shall commence from Wednesday, 01 July 2026, until twenty- four
(24) hours after the Q2 2026 UFS is filed with NGX, in line with the provisions of NGX’s Closed
Period Rule (as amended).
Consequently, all insiders of the Company are not allowed to deal directly or indirectly in the
securities of the Company within this Closed Period.
Thank you.
