Lagos, 23 June 2026
RESOLUTIONS PASSED AT THE ANNUAL GENERAL MEETING OF INDUSTRIAL & MEDICAL GASES
NIGERIA PLC
At the 66TH Annual General Meeting of Industrial & Medical Gases Nigeria Plc (IMG or the Company)
held on Friday, 19th June 2026, the following resolutions were duly passed:
1. That the Audited Financial Statements of the Company for the year ended 31st December 2025
together with the reports of the Directors, Auditors and Audit Committee thereon be received,
2. That payment of dividend of 50 kobo per 50 kobo ordinary share held by shareholders is
hereby approved in respect of the year ended December 31, 2025.
3. That the following Directors be and are hereby re-elected as Non-Executive Directors: Mr.
Ishaya Danjuma, Mrs. Adebola Oluwadeyi and Mr. Aminu Ado.
4. That the Directors be and are hereby authorized to fix the remuneration of the Auditors
5. The remuneration of Managers of the Company was disclosed to the members at the meeting.
6. That the following persons be and are hereby elected as members of the Statutory Audit
Committee to represent the shareholders:
● Mr. Bello Kazeem Owonikoko
● Mr. Kenneth Nnabike Nwosu
● Mr. Okafor Emmanuel Nkwachukwu
That the following Non-Executive Directors are hereby nominated to represent the Board on
the Statutory Audit Committee:
• Mr. Adeleke Adebayo
● Mrs. Olusola Oworu
Special Business
7. It was disclosed that Mr. Olawale Oyedele a Non-Executive Director has attained the age of
70 years.
8. That the remuneration of Non-Executive Directors for the year ended 31st December
2025 be and is hereby approved.
By Order of the Board
Aderonke Segun-Alabi (Mrs).
Company Secretary
