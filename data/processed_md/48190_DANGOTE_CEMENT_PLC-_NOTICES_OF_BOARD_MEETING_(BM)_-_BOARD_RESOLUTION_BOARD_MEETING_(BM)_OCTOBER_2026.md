DANGOTE CEMENT PLC – OUTCOME OF BOARD MEETING
This is to inform Nigerian Exchange Limited (“NGX”) and all other stakeholders that

or before 30 October 2026, in line with the rules of NGX.
The Closed Period, which commenced on 1st October 2026, will remain in effect until
twenty-four (24) hours after the submission of the 2026 Q3 Interim Financial
Statements to the public.
