The Company delivered a solid performance and continued to demonstrate resilience in a challenging
operating environment marked by macroeconomic volatility.
Group revenue increased by 9%, reflecting the benefit of revenue management actions and strategic
management initiatives. These included sustained investment behind strategic brands to drive growth,
focused execution across the value chain, continued contribution from premium brands and the malt category.
Gross profit margin expanded by 2 percentage points with results from operating activities increasing by 8%.
Profit before tax went up by 18% supported by a 61% reduction in net finance expense. The impact of the
new tax rates limited the group net profit growth to 5%.
Overall, the Company further strengthened its financial position underpinned by improved liquidity and
reduced financing pressure by keeping its borrowing at a zero level. This strengthened cash position provides
greater flexibility to respond to evolving market conditions while supporting ongoing business priorities. The
Company has also now restored its retained earnings to a positive position.
Looking ahead, the Board and Management remain focused on disciplined execution, revenue optimisation,
cost efficiency and strong cash generation. The Company will continue to monitor key exposures, take
decisive mitigation actions and preserve financial flexibility to sustain performance in a dynamic operating
environment.
