ABU JA: September 16, 2026
NOTIFICATION OF BOARD MEETING OF VERITAS KAPITAL ASSURANCE
PLC
The Board and Management of Veritas Kapital Assurance Plc (the
Company) wish to notify Nigerian Exchange Limited (NGX), esteemed
Shar eholders, Stakeholders, and the general public that the Board of
Directors of the Company will be approving the Unaudited Financial
Statements of the Company for the period ending September 30, 2026
(2026 Q3 UFS) by circularization.
However, notice is hereby given that a meeting of the Board of Directors
of the Company will be held on Thursday, October 15, 2026, at 10:00 a.m.
to ratify the 2026 Q3 UFS and review the operational strategies of the
Com pany.
Per the post-listing requirements of NGX, the Closed Period for trading in
the shares of the Company by Insiders will commence with effect from
October 1, 2026. Accordingly, no insider of the Company and their
connected persons may directly or indirectly deal in the securities of the
Company until twenty-four (24) hours after the release of the 2026 Q3
UFS to NGX and by extension, the general public.
