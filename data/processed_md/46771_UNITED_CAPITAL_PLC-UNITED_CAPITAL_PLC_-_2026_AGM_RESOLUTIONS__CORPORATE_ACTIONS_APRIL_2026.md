LAGOS, NIGERIA | APRIL 27, 2026
UNITED CAPITAL PLC - RESOLUTIONS PASSED AT THE ANNUAL GENERAL MEETING
At the Annual General Meeting of United Capital Plc (the “Company”), held
on Friday April 24, 2026, at the Transcorp Hilton Hotel, Abuja, the following
resolutions were proposed and approved by the shareholders:
1. The Audited Financial Statements for the year ended December 31,
2025, and the Report of the Directors, Auditors and Audit Committee
thereon.
2. A final dividend of N12.6 billion in respect of the year-ended December
31, 2025, i.e., 70 kobo per share to be paid to all shareholders registered
in the books of the Company as of the close of business on April 7, 2026.
This is in addition to the interim dividend of 30 kobo paid at half-year of
2025, bringing the total dividend for the 2025 financial year to N1.00 per
ordinary share.
3. The approval of the re-election of Mr. Uche Ike, Mr. Chiugo Ndubisi and
Mrs. Rose Nat Eshiett as Non-Executive Directors.
4. The authorisation of the Board of Directors to fix the remuneration of the
Auditors for the financial year ending December 31, 2026.
5. The election/re-election of the following persons as members of the
Statutory Audit Committee:
5.1 Mr. Oladipupo Fatokun - Board Representative
5.2 Mr. Oluleke Ogunlewe - Board Representative
5.3 Mr. Paul Olele - Shareholder Representative
5.4 Mr. Adeshina Alayaki - Shareholder Representative
5.5 Mr. Lawrence Oguntoye - Shareholder Representative
6. The disclosure of the remuneration of the Managers of the Company on
page 184 of the annual report duly noted by the shareholders.
7. The approval of the remuneration of the Non-Executive Directors which
was fixed at N325 million cumulatively for the financial year ending
December 31, 2026.
8. The approval of transactions with related parties or interested parties,
and with a general mandate in respect of all recurrent transactions
entered into with a related party or interested person, provided such
transactions are of a revenue or trading nature or are necessary for the
Company’s day-to-day operations. The mandate shall commence on
the date on which this resolution is passed and shall continue to operate
