Lagos, 21st September 2026
NOTICE OF BOARD CHANGES
SUNU Assurances Nigeria Plc (“the Company”) hereby notifies the public, Nigerian Exchange Limited (NGX) and its
esteemed shareholders of the appointment of Mr. Michael Uchenna Chukwu as an Independent Non-Executive Director
on the Board of the Company effective September 2026.
Mr. Michael Uchenna Chukwu is a seasoned financial executive, Chartered Accountant and a private equity leader with
over four (4) decades of experience spanning banking, taxation, corporate governance and investment management.
Mr. Michael Uchenna Chukwu is the founder & Chief Executive Officer of Assetswise Capital Ltd, where he has steered
private equity investments since 2007, building a reputation for strategic insight and value creation. His public service
record includes serving as Executive Chairman of the Ebonyi State Internal Revenue Board between 2019–2020, where
he restructured operations, automated processes, and doubled the state’s internally generated revenue within a year.
He was the founding Managing Director/CEO of Spring Bank Plc between 2005–2007, leading the historic merger of six
(6) banks and pioneering the new institution. Prior to that, he served as MD/CEO of Guardian Express Bank between
2001–2005 and has held senior leadership roles at ACB International Bank, First Atlantic Bank, UBA, FCMB, Citizens
International Bank, Fidelity Bank, and Price Waterhouse Cooper (PWC), respectively.
He holds a B.Sc. in Political Science from the University of Nigeria, Nsukka and is a Fellow of the Institute of Chartered
Accountants of Nigeria (ICAN), Chartered Institute of Taxation of Nigeria (CITN), Chartered Institute of Bankers of Nigeria
(CIBN), Chartered Institute of Management of Nigeria, and Institute of Directors Nigeria (IoD). His academic excellence
was recognized early with a Distinction in WAEC in 1980 and the first prize in ICAN Foundation Exam in 1986.
Mr. Chukwu has sharpened his leadership skills through global executive programs, including the Chief Executive
Programme at Lagos Business School in 2003 and the High Potential Leadership Program at Harvard Business School in
2006.
He brings extensive board-level experience, having served as Chairman and/or Director on the Board of the financial
services, research, and technology sectors, including Eartholeum Networks Ltd, ENL-NOVA Ltd, Cowry Asset
Management Ltd, and the National Root Crops Research Institute.
Beyond his corporate achievements, Mr. Chukwu is deeply committed to philanthropy, lecturing, and community service,
with personal interests in reading, travelling, chess and football.
The Board and Management wish the newly appointed Director all the best as he takes on his appointment.
