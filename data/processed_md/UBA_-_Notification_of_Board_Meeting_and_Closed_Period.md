UNITED BANK FOR AFRICA PLC
NOTIFICATION OF BOARD MEETING AND CLOSED PERIOD
Lagos, Wednesday, September 30, 2026
In line with the post-listing requirements of Nigerian Exchange Limited
(NGX) for listed companies, United Bank for Africa Plc (the Bank) hereby
informs its shareholders, NGX, and the investing public that the Board of
Directors of the Bank is scheduled to meet on Thursday, October 29,
2026, to consider, amongst other matters, Unaudited Financial
Statements for the third quarter ending September 30, 2026.
In compliance with the post-listing requirements of NGX for listed
companies, United Bank for Africa Plc hereby declares the
commencement of the closed period for trading in the Bank’s shares
from October 1, 2026, in respect of the Unaudited Financial Statements
for the third quarter ending September 30, 2026, until the expiration of
twenty-four (24) hours after the release of the results.
Accordingly, no insider of the Bank and their connected persons may
directly or indirectly deal in the Securities (Shares and Bonds) of the Bank
during the closed period.
BY THE ORDER OF THE BOARD
