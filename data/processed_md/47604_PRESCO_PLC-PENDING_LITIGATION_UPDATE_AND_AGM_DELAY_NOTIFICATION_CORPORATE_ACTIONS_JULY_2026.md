PRESCO PLC
July 29, 2026
Benin City, Nigeria
PENDING LITIGATION — UPDATE
Presco Plc (the "Company") wishes to update its Shareholders, the Investing Public and other
Stakeholders on the status of the previously reported pending litigation.
By a Ruling delivered on 11 December 2025 in Suit No. FHC/B/CS/37/2024, the Federal High Court
(Benin Judicial Division) made certain interlocutory Orders in respect of the Company's Annual
General Meeting held on 19 August 2025. The Company has, on the advice of counsel, appealed the
said Ruling to the Court of Appeal. Judgment in the appeal has been reserved by the Court of Appeal,
for a date to be communicated.
In view of the reserved judgment, and out of deference to the appellate process, the Board
considers it prudent to await the delivery of the judgment before communicating the date of the
Company's next Annual General Meeting.
The Company confirms that the pendency of the appeal does not affect the ordinary discharge of the
Board's and Management's statutory and operational responsibilities, and the Company remains
fully committed to good corporate governance and to acting in the best interest of all its
stakeholders.
Further updates will be provided as material developments occur.
Thank You:
For : PRESCO PLC
FREDERICK ICHEKWAI
COMPANY SECRETARY
