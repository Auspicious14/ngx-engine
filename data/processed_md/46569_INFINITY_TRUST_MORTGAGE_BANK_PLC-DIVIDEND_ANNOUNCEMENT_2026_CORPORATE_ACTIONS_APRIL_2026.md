Corporate Actions Announcement
INFINITY TRUST MORTGAGE BANK PLC hereby announce as follows:
Period Ended 31st December 2025
A Final Dividend of 35kobo for every share of 50k Ordinary share subject to appropriate withholding tax and
approval will be paid to shareholders, whose names appear in the Register of Members as at the close of
Proposed Dividend business on 17th day of April, 2026.
Bonus shares of 1 (One) new ordinary share for every 1 (one) ordinary shares held at the Qualification Date,
Proposed Bonus subject to shareholders’ approval and regulatory approvals.
Closure of Register The Register of Shareholders will be closed from 20st to 24th April 2026.
Qualification Date 17th April , 2026
On 26st May 2025, dividends will be paid electronically to shareholders whose names appear on the Register of
Members as at 17th April, 2026 and who have completed the e-dividend registration and mandated the
Payment Date Registrar to pay their dividends directly into their Bank accounts.
Bonus issue allotment To be communicated upon completion of necessary regulatory approvals and shareholder approvals at the
date AGM.
Shareholders who are yet to complete the e-dividend registration are advised to download the Registrar’s E-
Dividend Mandate Activation Form, which is also available on the website of the Registrars:
E-Dividend Registration http://africaprudential.com /downloads/. , complete and submit to the Registrar or their respective Banks.

Warrants and Share presented for payment or returned for validation are advised to complete the e-dividend registration or contact
