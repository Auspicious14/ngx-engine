Lagos, Nigeria
10th March 2026
NOTICE OF BOARD OF DIRECTOR’S MEETING AND NOTICE OF CLOSED PERIOD
This is to notify Nigeria Exchange Limited (The Exchange), our Stakeholders and the investing public
that a meeting of the Board of Directors of NEM Insurance Plc (“the Company”) is scheduled to be held
on Wednesday, 11th March 2026 to consider NEM Insurance Plc’s Audited Financial Statements for the
year ended 31 December 2025 (2025 AFS) amongst other matters.
Consequently, and in compliance with the Rule Book of the Exchange, the closed period which
commenced on 1st January 2026 will continue until twenty-four (24) hours after the 2025 AFS have been
filed via the Exchange’s Issuers’ Portal.
The Closed period is applicable to all Directors, Senior Managers and other interested parties that may
at any time possess sensitive information that materially affect the price of securities of the Company.
Accordingly, no insider of the Company and/or their connected persons may directly or indirectly deal
with/in the shares of the Company during the Closed Period.
For further enquiries, please contact nem@nem-insurance.com.
