ARADEL HOLDINGS PLC PROPOSES ₦7.00 INTERIM DIVIDEND PER SHARE TO
SHAREHOLDERS
Lagos, Nigeria – 30th October 2026
Aradel Holdings Plc (“Aradel”, “Aradel Holdings”, or “the Group”), Nigeria’s leading
integrated indigenous energy Company, listed on the Nigerian Exchange Limited (NGX),
announces an interim dividend of ₦7.00 per ordinary share (subject to appropriate WHT)
to be paid to shareholders whose names appear in the Register of members as at the close
of business on 27th of November 2026.
Period Ended 30 September 2026
An Interim Dividend of ₦7 per ordinary share each,
(subject to appropriate withholding tax) will be paid to
shareholders whose names appear in the Register of
Shareholders as at the close of business on 27 November
Dividend 2026.
To enable Aradel’s Registrars, Coronation Registrars Limited,
Closure of Register prepare for the payment of the dividend, the Register of
Shareholders will be closed on 30 November 2026.
Qualification Date 27 November 2026.
On 7 December 2026, the interim dividend will be paid
electronically to shareholders whose names appear on the
Register of Members as of 27 November 2026, and who
have completed the e-dividend registration and mandated the
Registrar to pay their dividends directly into their Bank
Payment Date accounts.
Shareholders who are yet to complete the e-dividend
registration are advised to download the E-Dividend Mandate
Activation Form, complete and submit to the Registrar or their
respective Banks.

Notes to Editors
Aradel Holdings Plc (“Aradel Holdings” or “the Company”) is Nigeria’s foremost integrated
independent energy company, delivering critical energy solutions in a sustainable and responsible
way. Aradel Holdings was incorporated on 25 March 1992 (as the Midas Drilling Fund), changed its
name to Niger Delta Exploration and Production Plc in November 1996, assumed its current name
in May 2023, and was listed on the main board of the NGX on 14 October 2024.
The Company operates through its subsidiaries:
▪ Aradel Energy Limited (100%): operator of the Ogbele (PML 14), Omerelu (PPL 247), Olo and
Olo West marginal fields, and of the OPL 227 joint venture (subject to NUPRC approval).
▪ Aradel Gas Limited (100%): the only Nigerian independent non-JV gas supplier to Bonny LNG,
with a 100 mmscf/d gas processing facility.
▪ Aradel Investments Limited (100%): holds and manages the Group's non-oil and gas
investments.
▪ Aradel Refineries Limited (95%), a 3-train 11kbbl/d independent operating midstream refinery.
Produces AGO, DPK, MDO, HFO and Naphtha.
▪ ND Western Limited (81.67%), an independent Nigerian oil and gas exploration and production
company comprising three limited liability companies (being Aradel Energy, First Exploration &
Petroleum Development Company, and Waltersmith Petroman Oil) as shareholders.
▪ Renaissance Africa Energy Company, a 53.3% total equity holding made up of
- A direct holding of 12.5% and
- Through ND Western, an indirect holding of 40.7%
For further information please refer to our website: https://www.aradel.com
