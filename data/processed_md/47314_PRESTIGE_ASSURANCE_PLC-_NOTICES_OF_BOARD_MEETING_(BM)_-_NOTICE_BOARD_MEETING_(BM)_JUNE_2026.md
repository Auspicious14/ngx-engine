Lagos, 30 June 2026.
NOTICE OF CLOSED PERIOD AND BOARD MEETING.
Prestige Assurance Plc (“the Company”) hereby wishes to inform Nigerian Exchange
Limited (NGX), its esteemed shareholders and the investing public that a meeting of the
Board of Directors of the Company will be held hybrid on Wednesday, 29th July 2026 at
No. 19, Ligali Ayorinde Street, Victoria Island, Lagos, to consider and approve the Q2 2026
Unaudited Financial Statements for the period ending 30 June 2026 (Q2 2026 UFS),
amongst other agenda items.
In accordance with the relevant provisions of the Rule Book of NGX, the Closed Period
for trading in the Company’s shares by insiders will commence on 1st July, 2026 and will
continue until twenty-four (24) hours after the Q2 2026 UFS has been released to the
public.
Pursuant to the foregoing, no insider of the Company and their connected persons may
directly or indirectly deal in the securities of the Company in any manner until twenty (24)
hours after the release of the Q2 2026 UFS via NGX’s Issuers’ Portal and by extension, the
general public.
MRS CHIDINMA IBE-LOUIS
Company Secretary
