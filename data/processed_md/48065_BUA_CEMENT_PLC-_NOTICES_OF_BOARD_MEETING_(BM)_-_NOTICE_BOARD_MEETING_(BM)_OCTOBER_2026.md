Lagos: Monday, 12th October 2026.
NOTICE OF BOARD MEETING OF BUA CEMENT PLC
BUA Cement Plc (the “Company”) hereby notifies the Nigerian Exchange Limited
(NGX) and the investing public that a meeting of its Board of Directors is
scheduled to be held on Tuesday, 20 October 2026, to consider, amongst other
matters, the Company’s Unaudited Financial Statements for the period ended 30
September 2026.
In compliance with the post-listing requirements of NGX applicable to listed
companies, the closed period for trading in the shares of the Company has
commenced on 1st October 2026 and shall continue until twenty-four (24) hours
after the release of the Company’s Unaudited Financial Statements for the period
ended 30 September 2026 to NGX and the investing public.
BY ORDER OF THE BOARD
Satomi Hauwa
Company Secretary/CLO
