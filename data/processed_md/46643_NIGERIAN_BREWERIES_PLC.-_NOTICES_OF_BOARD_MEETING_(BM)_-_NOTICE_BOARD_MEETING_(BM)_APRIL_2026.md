LAGOS, 13th APRIL 2026
NOTIFICATION OF BOARD MEETING AND CLOSED PERIOD
We hereby notify the Nigerian Exchange Limited (“NGX”) and the investing public that
a meeting of the Board of Directors of Nigerian Breweries Plc (“the Company”) has
been scheduled for Thursday, 23rd April 2026.
An item on the agenda for the meeting is the consideration and approval of the 2026
Quarter 1, Unaudited Financial Statements of the Company for the period ended 31st
March 2026.
Furthermore, please be informed that the Company’s Closed Period commenced on
23rd March 2026 and will continue until twenty-four (24) hours after the said Unaudited
Financial Statements are uploaded on the NGX portal.
