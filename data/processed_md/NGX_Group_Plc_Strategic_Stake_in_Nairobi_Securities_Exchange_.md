NGX Group Acquires 10% Strategic Stake in Nairobi Securities Exchange
Lagos, Nigeria – 16 November 2026: Nigerian Exchange Group Plc (NGX Group) has acquired a 10%
equity stake in the Nairobi Securities Exchange (NSE), a strategic investment that extends the Group’s
participation in East Africa and advances its pan-African growth strategy. This strategic investment aligns with
NGX Group’s priority of building regional leadership through partnerships and African market integration,
strengthening its investment presence across West and East Africa.
This latest development builds on NGX Group’s strategic investment in the Ethiopian Securities Exchange
(ESX), further demonstrating the Group’s long-term commitment to participating in the development and growth
of African capital market infrastructure.
Strategic Rationale
The acquisition provides NGX Group with exposure to one of East Africa’s most established capital markets,
broadening its investment portfolio beyond Nigeria and positioning the Group to benefit from the long-term
growth of the region’s capital markets and increasing financial integration across the continent. It also reflects
NGX Group’s disciplined approach to capital allocation, balancing strategic growth opportunities with its
commitment to sustainable shareholder returns.
The investment supports three key strategic priorities:
1. Regional Integration: Strengthening connections between African capital markets and supporting
cross-border investment opportunities.
2. Portfolio Diversification: Expanding NGX Group’s investment exposure beyond Nigeria,
complementing its existing investments
3. Shareholder Value: Pursuing long-term growth opportunities through disciplined capital allocation,
while maintaining a commitment to sustainable shareholder returns.
Chairman’s Statement
Commenting on the investment, Alhaji Umaru Kwairanga, Group Chairman, NGX Group, said: “African capital
markets are at an inflection point, where integration is moving from aspiration to architecture. Exchange groups
that commit patient capital to market infrastructure will help shape how capital flows across the continent. Our
investment in the Nairobi Securities Exchange is a disciplined, long-term step in that direction, strengthening
the financial linkages between West and East Africa while creating opportunities for sustainable shareholder
value.”
Group Managing Director/Chief Executive Officer’s Statement
Temi Popoola, Group Managing Director/Chief Executive Officer, NGX Group, said: “This investment reflects
our strategy to build a more diversified and resilient exchange group with a stronger presence across African
markets. Kenya is a cornerstone of East Africa’s financial ecosystem, and our investment in NSE provides
exposure to its long-term growth potential while broadening our sources of investment returns beyond Nigeria.
