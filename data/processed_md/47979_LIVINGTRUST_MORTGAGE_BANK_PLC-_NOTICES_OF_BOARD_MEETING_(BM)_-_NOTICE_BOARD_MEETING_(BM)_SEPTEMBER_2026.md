Osogbo: September 30, 2026.
Commencement of Closed Period and Notice of Board of Directors Meeting
This is to notify the Nigerian Exchange Limited (The Exchange) and members of the
public, particularly the shareholders of LivingTrust Mortgage Bank Plc (the Bank) that a
Board of Directors meeting has been scheduled to hold on 30th October, 2026. The
meeting will discuss amongst other items, the company’s unaudited financial statement
for the quarter ending 30th September 2026.
In line with The Exchange’s Rules on Closed Period, shareholders and insiders are hereby
notified that the Bank will commence a closed period from the 30th September 2026.,
being the end of the quarterly financial period in review, to end 24 hours after the
Quarter 3, 2026 unaudited financial statement has been released to the public, after
which the trading period will be resumed.
During this period, insiders and /or person(s) connected to them and such other
interested parties should observe this earnings-related closed trading period and not
buy or sell shares of LivingTrust Mortgage Bank Plc.
This is in compliance with the rules of The Exchange.
Should you have any questions regarding this disclosure, please contact the Company
Secretary.
For: LivingTrust Mortgage Bank Plc.
Dorcas Ajayi
Ag. Company Secretary
