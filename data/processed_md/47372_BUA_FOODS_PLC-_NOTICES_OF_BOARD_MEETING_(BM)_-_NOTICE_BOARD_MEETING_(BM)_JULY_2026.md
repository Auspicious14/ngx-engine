Lagos, 2nd July 2026
ANNOUNCEMENT OF BOARD MEETING FOR THE CONSIDERATION AND
APPROVAL OF 2ND QUARTER 2026 UNAUDITED FINANCIAL STATEMENTS
This notice serves to inform Nigerian Exchange Limited (NGX) and the investing public
that a meeting of the Board of Directors of BUA Foods Plc (the Company) is scheduled
for Wednesday 29th July 2026 to consider the 2nd Quarter 2026 Unaudited Financial
Statements for the period ended 30th June 2026 (2026 Q2 UFS) after which the Financial
Statements will be simultaneously filed with the relevant regulatory bodies and released to
the public.
In compliance with the post listing requirements of NGX for listed companies, the closed
period in respect of the 2026 Q2 UFS commenced on 1st of July, 2026 and will continue
until twenty-four (24) hours after the release of the 2026 Q2 UFS.
Accordingly, no insider of the Company and persons connected to them may directly or
indirectly deal in the shares of the Company, until twenty-four (24) hours after the release
of the 2026 Q2 UFS to NGX and the public.
BY ORDER OF THE BOARD:
Oluseye Alayande
Company Secretary
