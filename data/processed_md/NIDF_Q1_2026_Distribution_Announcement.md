Lagos | April 15, 2026
CHAPEL HILL DENHAM NIGERIA INFRASTRUCTURE DEBT FUND – Q1 2026 DISTRIBUTION ANNOUNCEMENT
Distribution Period March 31, 2026
Distribution N4.53 per unit (paid to unitholders whose names appeared in the Register of
Unitholders as at the close of business on April 28, 2026, subject to the deduction
of appropriate withholding tax)
Bonus Nil
Qualification Date April 28, 2026
Closure of Register April 29, 2026
Payment Date May 6, 2026
The distribution will be paid - electronically - to Unitholders whose names appear
on the Register of Unitholders as of April 28, 2026 (and have completed the E-
Mandate Form and authorised the Registrar to pay their distributions directly into
their Bank accounts)
E-Mandate Unitholders who have not yet completed the E-Mandate are advised to download
the Registrar’s E-Mandate Activation Form, which is available on the website of the
Registrars: https://coronationregistrars.com/e-mandate/
Please complete and submit the E-Mandate Activation Form to the Registrar.
