Lagos, 26th February, 2026
DELAY IN SUBMISSION OF AUDITED FINANCIAL STATEMENTS FOR THE
YEAR ENDED 31ST DECEMBER, 2025
Lasaco Assurance Plc (the Company) wishes to notify Nigerian Exchange Limited
(NGX), its shareholders and the investing public that the Company will not be able
to file its Audited Financial Statements for the year ended 31st December, 2025
(2025 AFS) within the regulatory due date.
This is due to the delay encountered in the valuation of our liabilities, which we rely
on third parties to determine and the exigencies of the recapitalization process
presently ongoing.
We are optimistic that the 2025 AFS will be submitted to NGX on or before 30th
April, 2026 together with the 2026 Q1 UFS, and regret the inconveniences caused
by the late submission of the 2025 AFS.

Gertrude Olutekunbi
Company Secretary/ Legal Adviser
