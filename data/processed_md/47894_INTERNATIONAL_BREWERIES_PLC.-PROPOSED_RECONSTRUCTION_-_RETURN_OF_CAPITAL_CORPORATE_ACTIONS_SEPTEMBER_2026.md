INTERNATIONAL BREWERIES PLC – NOTICE TO SHAREHOLDERS ON RETURN OF CAPITAL
International Breweries Plc (“the Company”) is pleased to notify its esteemed shareholders, stakeholders,
Nigerian Exchange Limited (“the Exchange”) and the investing public of the date of Qualification Date, Closure of
Register, and Settlement Date for the Company's Return of Capital.
The Qualification Date for the Return of Capital shall be September 16, 2026. Only shareholders whose names
appear in the Register of Members of the Company as at the close of business on that date shall be entitled to
the Return of Capital ("Qualifying Shareholders").
Following the Qualification Date, the Register of Members shall be closed on September 17 for the purpose of
determining and finalising the shareholders entitled to participate in the Return of Capital.
The Settlement Date, being the date on which the Return of Capital will be paid to Qualifying Shareholders, shall
be September 23, 2026.
This announcement is made further to the approval of the Securities and Exchange Commission, the approval
obtained from shareholders at the Annual General Meeting held on July 30, 2026 and subsequent sanction of the
Federal High Court that the sum of ₦84,000,000,000 from the Company’s share premium account be distributed
to shareholders as a Return of Capital on a pro rata cash basis. Each qualifying shareholder will be entitled to
₦0.50k (Fifty Kobo) per share.
The Company will continue to keep shareholders, the Exchange and investing public informed of any material
developments relating to the Return of Capital.
