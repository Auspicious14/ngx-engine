RC. 2321
Lagos, 18 June 2026
NOTIFICATION OF 5% SHAREHOLDING OF NGX GROUP PLC SHARES BY A SINGLE
SHAREHOLDER – UNITED CAPITAL PLC
Further to the provisions of Rule 17.13 of the Rulebook of the Exchange, 2015 (Issuers’ Rules), we
have been formally notified by United Capital Plc that its total shareholding in Nigerian Exchange
Group Plc (NGX Group) stands at 5% of the issued share capital of NGX Group as of 15 June 2026.
We write to formally notify the Market of this development.
Izuchukwu Akpa
Group Company Secretary
