Lagos, 22nd June 2026
PREMIER PAINTS PLC - NOTIFICATION OF DELAY IN COMMENCEMENT DATE FOR THE
MANDATORY TENDER OFFER (MTO) BY XENERGI PLC.
Premier Paints Plc (“The Company”), will like to notify its esteemed Shareholders, Stakeholders, The Nigerian
Exchange Limited ("The Exchange") and the general public that the Mandatory Tender Offer (MTO) of Premier
Paints Plc by Xenergi Plc. (“The Transaction”) which was planned to commence on Monday June 22nd 2026 has
been postponed due to the need to register the Bid Documents with the Securities and Exchange Commission
(SEC).
Upon our obtaining the required no-objection from the SEC, we will announce the new date for the
commencement of the MTO.
The Company will continue to inform the public of the progress of the Transaction.
For: Premier Paints Plc
Alozie Nwokoro – AMN PRACTICE
(Company Secretary)
