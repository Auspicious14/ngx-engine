20th August 2026
Lagos, Nigeria
GEREGU POWER PLC - CONFIRMATION OF PAYMENT OF THE 8TH COUPON UNDER
THE SERIES 1 FIXED RATE BOND
The Board of Geregu Power Plc (“GPP” or the “Company”) hereby discloses to
Nigerian Exchange Limited (“NGX”) and the investing public that the 8th
coupon and part principal repayment due under the Company’s
₦40,085,000,000 (Forty Billion, Eighty-five Million Naira) Series 1 Fixed Rate Bond
(the “Bond”), issued under the Company’s Bond Issuance Programme, have
been paid to the Trustees.
The Board of Directors reaffirms its commitment to all stakeholders as part of its
ongoing dedication to preserving shareholder value and upholding the
highest standards of corporate governance. The Company will continue to
engage constructively with relevant parties to resolve legacy matters pre-
dating the current Board and confirms that this process will not impact the
Company's ability to meet its obligations.
The Board thanks the Bondholders, the Securities and Exchange Commission,
the Nigerian Exchange Limited, and the Nigerian Electricity Regulatory
Commission for their continued engagement and understanding while this
matter was being resolved and reaffirms its commitment to full compliance
with its obligations.
This disclosure is made in compliance with the Rules of Nigerian Exchange
Limited.
