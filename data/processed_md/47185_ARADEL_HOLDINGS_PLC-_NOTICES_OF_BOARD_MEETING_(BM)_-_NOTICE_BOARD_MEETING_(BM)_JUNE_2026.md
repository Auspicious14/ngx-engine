Lagos, Nigeria June 16th, 2026
NOTICE OF BOARD MEETING
In line with the Rulebook of the Nigerian Exchange Limited (NGX), the Board of Directors of Aradel
Holdings Plc (the “Company”) hereby notifies the NGX and the investing public that a meeting
of the Board of Directors of the Company has been scheduled to hold on Friday, 19th of June
2026.
Among items slated for discussion at the meeting is to receive, consider, and approve the
Company’s Audited Financial Statements for the year ended 31st December 2025 (“2025 AFS”),
as well as its Unaudited Financial Statements & Accounts for the period ended 31st March 2026
(“Q1 2026 UFS”).
Consequently, the Closed Period for trading in the Company’s shares will continue until twenty-
four (24) hours after the 2025 AFS, as well as its Q1 2026 UFS, have been released on the floor
of NGX. Therefore, no insider or their connected persons are permitted to deal in the securities
of the Company during the Closed Period.
By order of the Board
