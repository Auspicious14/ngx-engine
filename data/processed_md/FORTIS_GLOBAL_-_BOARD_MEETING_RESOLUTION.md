LAGOS, 23rd October 2026
BOARD MEETING RESOLUTION
At the meeting of the Board of Directors of FORTIS GLOBAL INSURANCE PLC (the
Company) held on Thursday, 22nd October 2026 at the Board Room, Fortis Global
Insurance PLC Head Office, Plot 20C, Akin Ogunlewe Street, Victoria Island, Lagos, at
12:00 p.m, the following resolutions were proposed and duly passed:
1. “That the Unaudited Financial Statements of the Company for the third quarter
(Q3) ended 30 September 2026 (2026 Q3 UFS) be and are hereby approved.
2. That Management be and are hereby authorized to take all necessary steps to
give effect to this resolution, including submission to relevant regulatory
authorities.
The 2026 Q3 UFS will be filed on or before 30 October 2026.
