Lagos Nigeria, March 30, 2026
Transnational Corporation Plc – Notice of Closed Period
This is to notify Nigerian Exchange Limited (NGX), shareholders of Transnational
Corporation Plc (Transcorp Plc or the Company) and the public that in
accordance with the provisions of 17.18 (a) Closed Period Rules. Rulebook of
the Exchange, 2015 (As amended) (Issuers’ Rules), the Closed Period for
trading in the shares of Transcorp Plc will commence on April 1st, 2026, in
respect of the submission of the Unaudited Financial Statements for the quarter
ending 31st March 2026 (2026, Q1 UFS) of the Company.
Consequently, no insider and their connected persons may directly or
indirectly deal in the securities of the Company until twenty-four (24) hours after
the release of the 2026 Q1, UFS through the Issuers’ Portal of NGX and by
extension, the general public.
Thank you.
