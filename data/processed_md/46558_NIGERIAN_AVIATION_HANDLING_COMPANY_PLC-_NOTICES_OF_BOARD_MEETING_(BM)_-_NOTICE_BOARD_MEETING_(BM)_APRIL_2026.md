Lagos, March 31, 2026
ANNOUNCEMENT OF BOARD MEETING DATE AND CLOSED PERIOD OF NIGERIAN AVIATION
HANDLING COMPANY PLC
This is to inform Nigerian Exchange Limited (NGX or The Exchange) and our esteemed
shareholders that a meeting of the Board of Directors of Nigerian Aviation Handling Company
Plc (the Company) has been scheduled to hold on Wednesday 29th April 2026, to consider and
approve the Company’s 2026 First Quarter Unaudited Financial Statements (2026 Q1 UFS)
along with other agenda items.
Our esteemed shareholders and Insiders are also informed that in compliance with the
Amended Closed Period Rules of The Exchange, Nigerian Aviation Handling Company Plc has
declared a closed period from 1st April 2026 until twenty-four (24) hours after the Company’s
2026 Q1 UFS is released to the Market.
Accordingly, no insider of the Company and their connected persons should deal in the shares
of the Company directly or indirectly or in any manner during the closed period.
