CORPORATE ACTIONS ANNOUNCEMENT
MUTUAL BENEFITS ASSURANCE PLC hereby announces as follows:
Period Ended 31st December 2025
Proposed Dividend A Final Dividend of 4 Kobo for every ordinary share of 50 kobo
each, subject to appropriate withholding tax and approval, will
be paid to shareholders whose names appear in the Register
of Members as at the close of business on the 21st day of July,
2026.
Proposed Bonus NIL
Closure of Register The Register of Shareholders will be closed from 22nd July
2026 to 28th July 2026
Qualification Date 21st July 2026
Payment Date On the 30th day of July, 2026, dividends will be paid
electronically to shareholders whose names appear on the
Register of Members as at 21st July, 2026 and who have
completed the e-dividend registration and mandated the
Registrar to pay their dividends directly into their Bank
accounts.
E-Dividend Registration Shareholders who are yet to complete the e-dividend
registration are advised to download the Registrar's E-
Dividend Mandate Activation Form, which is also available on
the website of the Registrars:
file:///C:/Users/HP/Downloads/E-Dividend-copy-2.pdf
Unclaimed Dividend Shareholders with dividend warrants and share certificates
Warrants and Share that have remained unclaimed or are yet to be presented for
Certificates payment or returned for validation are advised to complete the
e-dividend registration or contact the Registrar.
Date of General Meeting The Company’s Annual General Meeting will be held on 30th
July, 2026.
