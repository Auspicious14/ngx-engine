Lagos, Nigeria,
PZ Cussons Nigeria Plc hereby announces as follows:
Year Ended 31st May 2026
Proposed Dividend A Final Dividend of N2.50 per ordinary share of 50 kobo each, subject to
the deduction of the appropriate withholding tax and approval at the
Annual General Meeting (“AGM”) of the Company slated for 28th October
2026, will be paid to shareholders whose names appear in the Register of
Members as at the close of business on the 9th of October 2026.
Proposed Bonus Not Applicable.
Closure of Register The Register of Members will be closed from Monday, 12th of October to
Friday, 16th October 2026 (both dates inclusive).
Qualification Date 9th October 2026
Payment Date On 30th October 2026, dividends will be paid electronically to Shareholders
whose names appear in the Register of Members as at close of business on
the 9th October 2026 (Qualifying Shareholders) and who have completed
the e-dividend registration, mandating the Registrar to pay their dividends
directly into their bank accounts.
Scrip Dividend The Directors are also recommending to Shareholders for their approval at
the forthcoming AGM a right of election for Qualifying Shareholders to
receive new ordinary shares in the Company instead of the final dividend in
cash. The election is required to be made on or before the 23rd October
2026.
The Reference Share Price for the purpose of determining the number of
shares due to Qualifying Shareholders who elect for the share option will
be a ten-day trading average of the Company’s share price on the floor of
the Nigerian Exchange Limited, starting on the 12th October 2026.
E-Dividend Registration Shareholders who are yet to complete the e-dividend registration are
advised to download the Registrar's E-Dividend Mandate Activation Form,
which is available at E-Dividend Form | First Registrars, complete the same,
and submit it to the Registrar.
Unclaimed Dividend Shareholders with dividend warrants and share certificates that have
Warrants and Share remained unclaimed, or are yet to be presented for payment or returned
Certificates for validation, are advised to contact the Registrar.
Date of General The Company’s Annual General Meeting will be held at the Ballroom, Fraser
Meeting Suites, Abuja, on 28th October 2026 at 11:00 a.m.
Registrar First Registrars and Investor Services Limited, No 2, Abebe Village Road,
Iganmu, Lagos; 01-27011079 or 01-2799880 (telephone);
DIRECTORS: Ifueko M. Omoigui Okauru MFR (Chair), Oghale J. Elueni (MD/CEO), Ebenezer O. Elusakin
(CFO), Ballama Manu, Oluwatoyin Odutayo, Suleman A. Ndanusa, OON, Kareem Moustafa (Australian),
Richard Walker (British), Anthony Idigbe, SAN, PhD
