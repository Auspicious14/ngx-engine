29th OCTOBER 2026
LAGOS, NIGERIA
NOTICE OF DEADLINE FOR ELECTION TO PARTICIPATE IN SCRIP DIVIDEND ARRANGEMENT
NOTICE IS HEREBY given to the shareholders of PZ Cussons Nigeria Plc (“the Company”) of the
following resolutions proposed and passed by the Company’s shareholders at the 78th Annual General
Meeting (“AGM”) of PZ Cussons Nigeria Plc duly convened and held on October 28, 2026:
1. A final dividend of ₦2.50 (Two Naira, Fifty Kobo) per ordinary share for the financial year ended
May 31, 2026 (“FY26 Final Dividend”).
2. Shareholders shall have the option to receive all or part of their net cash dividend in new ordinary
shares of the Company (‘’the Scrip Dividend Arrangement’’).
3. The election period for the Scrip Dividend Arrangement is Wednesday, November 4, 2026, and
not Friday, October 23, 2026, as earlier communicated.
4. Payment of the final dividend of ₦2.50 (Two Naira, Fifty Kobo) per ordinary share has been
rescheduled from Friday, October 30, 2026, to Friday, November 6, 2026, after the conclusion of
the Scrip Dividend election process.
Shareholders are to note that a Qualifying Shareholder who does not make an election will receive
the FY26 Final Dividend in cash, subject to applicable withholding tax. For purposes of this Scrip
Dividend Arrangement, “Qualifying Shareholders” are shareholders whose names appeared on the
Company’s Register of Members as at close of business on October 9, 2026.
Election to Receive Scrip Shares
Qualifying Shareholders who wish to receive Scrip Shares must complete and submit the Election Form
to the Registrar - First Registrars and Investor Services Limited at 2 Abebe Village Road, Iganmu, Lagos,
or by email to info@firstregistrarsnigeria.com - on or before Wednesday, November 4, 2026. The
Election Form will be available at the Registrar’s offices, on the Registrar’s website
info@firstregistrarsnigeria.com, and on the Company’s website at
www.pzcussons.com/investors/nigeria-investors.
Shareholders are implored to update their email address, mobile number, and other contact details
with the Registrar by submitting to info@firstregistrarsnigeria.com; Subject of Email – PZCN Data

Additional Explanatory Note on the Scrip Dividend Arrangement
i. The Reference Share Price for the Scrip Dividend Arrangement has been determined as ₦65.29
per ordinary share, based on the average closing share price over the 10-trading-day reference
period from October 12, 2026 to October 23, 2026.
ii. The number of new ordinary shares to be allotted to each participating shareholder will be
calculated as follows:
Number of Scrip Shares = Net Cash Dividend Elected for Conversion ÷ ₦65.29
iii. “Net Cash Dividend” means the relevant gross dividend entitlement less applicable Withholding
Tax. Where a shareholder makes a partial election, only the portion of the Net Cash Dividend
specified in the valid Election Form will be applied towards the allotment of Scrip Shares.
iv. The balance will be paid in cash.
