Commenting on the Group’s performance, the Group Chief Executive Officer, Mr. Peter
Ashade, had this to say:
“I am pleased to inform all stakeholders that United Capital Plc ended the third quarter
of the year on a remarkable note as demonstrated by the company’s strong earnings
growth and impressive performance across key financial metrics.
During the period under review, United Capital gross earnings rose by 50% year-on-
year to N62.47 billion, profit before tax grew by 66% year-on-year to N41.58 billion, profit
after tax increased by 65% year-on-year to N34.83 billion while Shareholders’ Funds rose
by 58% year-to-date to N237.08billion, reflecting the Company's solid financial strength
and enhanced capacity to drive sustainable growth.
Looking ahead to the final quarter of the year, we are well-positioned to build on our
momentum, driven by a continued focus on strong execution, sustainable growth, and
long-term value creation for our shareholders.”

Comparing 9M 2026 with 9M 2025, the following are worthy of note:
 Gross Earnings: United Capital’s gross earnings rose by 50% year-on-year to N62.47
billion in 9M 2026 compared to N41.54 billion in 9M 2025. This robust earnings growth
was driven by growth in fee and commission income (+27% year-on-year), net
investment income (+95% year-on-year), net trading income (+29% year-on-year)
and Net gain on financial assets FVTPL (+22% year-on-year).
 PBT and PAT: The company’s profit before tax increased by 66% year-on-year to
N41.58 billion while profit after tax rose by 65% year-on-year to N34.83 billion. This
impressive performance was driven by 50% growth in gross earnings.
 Total Assets: United Capital’s total assets grew by 3% year-to-date to N1.81 trillion
as of September 2026, compared to N1.76 trillion in December 2025. The increase
was primarily driven by a 115% year-to-date growth in cash and cash equivalents.
 Managed Funds: The company’s managed funds increased by 4% year-to-date to
N1.04 trillion in September 2026 compared to N993.64 billion in December 2025.
 Shareholders’ Fund: United Capital Shareholders’ funds increased by 58% year-to-
date to N237.08 billion as of September 2026, compared to N149.99 billion in
December 2025. This strong growth was primarily driven by a 76% increase in the
fair value reserve and a 38% rise in retained earnings, underscoring the Company’s
robust capital position and sustained value creation for shareholders.

EDITOR’S COMMENT
United Capital Plc is a leading Pan-African financial and investment services group,
with a mission to provide bespoke and innovative value-added services to its client.
The group aims to transform the African continent by providing innovative and
creative investment banking solutions to governments, companies, and individuals.
The company which was listed on the Nigerian Stock Exchange on the 17th of January
2013 is setting the pace to becoming the financial and investment role model across
Africa, by deploying innovation, technology, and specialist skills to exceed client
expectations, while creating sustained value for all stakeholders.
As a pacesetter, United Capital is the first Investment Bank to be listed on the Nigerian
Exchange. It operates as a group with subsidiaries in the Investment Banking,
Consumer Lending, Trusteeship, Securities Trading and Asset Management, Wealth
Management and Microfinance Banking.
