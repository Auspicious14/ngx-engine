Lagos, Nigeria – 23 October 2026
OUTCOME OF FCMB GROUP PLC BOARD OF DIRECTORS’ MEETING
At the Board of Directors’ meeting of FCMB Group Plc (the Company) held on Friday,
October 23, 2026, the Board approved the Group’s Unaudited Financial Statements for
the Quarter ended September 30, 2026.
The Company had previously obtained an extension of time for the filing of the
Unaudited Financial Statements to allow for the completion of the ongoing interim audit
of its largest subsidiary, First City Monument Bank Limited, for the period ended 30
September 2026, given the significance of the subsidiary to the Group’s consolidated
financial results.
Accordingly, the Unaudited Financial Statements will be filed upon completion of the
ongoing interim audit process and, in any event, on or before 29 December 2026, in line
with the extension obtained from the Nigerian Exchange Limited (NGX) by the
Company.
The closed period which commenced October 1, 2026 will continue until twenty-four (24)
hours after the Unaudited Financial Statements for the Quarter ended September 30,
2026 has been released to the public.
