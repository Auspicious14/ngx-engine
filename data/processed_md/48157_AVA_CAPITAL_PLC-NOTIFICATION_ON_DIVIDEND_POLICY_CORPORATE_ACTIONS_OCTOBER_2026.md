AVA CAPITAL PLC ADOPTS DIVIDEND POLICY TO PROMOTE
SUSTAINABLE SHAREHOLDER VALUE
AVA Capital Plc (“the Company”) wishes to inform its shareholders, investors and the
general public that its Board of Directors , at its meeting held on October 21, 2026,
approve d a Dividend Policy establishing a framework for dividend recommendations,
declarations and payments , subject to applicable regulatory approvals .
The Policy reflects the Company’s commitment to delivering sustainable shareholder
returns while maintaining financial resilience, preserving adequate capital for future growth
and ensuring compliance with applicable legal and regulatory requirements.
Under the Policy, the Company targets a dividend payout ratio within the range of 35% to
70% of Profit After Tax (PAT) , subject to the Company’s financial performance, capital
position, liquidity requirements, regulatory obligations and other relevant internal and
external factors.
The Policy establishes a disciplined approach to the allocation of profits, with priority given
to shareholder returns, while recognising the importance of reinvestment in the business
to strengthen the Company’s capital base, finance strategic initiatives , support business
expansion and enhance long -term shareholder value. The Policy also provides for the
possible allocation of a portion of PAT to an employee profit -sharing scheme, subject to
applicable laws and approved frameworks.
Dividend recommendations will be guided by the Company’s financial performance,
earnings, cash flows, capital adequacy, planned investments, strategic priorities and
prevailing economic conditions. The Board may recommend a payout ratio above or below
the target range where justified by prevailing circumstances.
The Policy further provides for the consideration of interim and final dividends, subject to
the satisfaction of applicable conditions. Final dividends shall be recommended by the
Board, supported by audited financial statements and submitted to shareholde rs for
approval at the Annual General Meeting, in accordance with applicable requirements.
The Company emphasises that the Policy does not constitute a guarantee that dividends
will be declared in any financial year. The Board retains the discretion to determine
whether a dividend should be recommended, having regard to the Company’s financial
stability, capital adequacy, liquidity, regulatory compliance and long -term shareholder
interests.

corporate governance, responsible capital allocation and the creation of sustainable value
for its shareholders. Dividend announcements and related information will be
