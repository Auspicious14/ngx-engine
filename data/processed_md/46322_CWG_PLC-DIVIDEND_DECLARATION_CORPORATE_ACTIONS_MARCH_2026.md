Corporate Actions Announcement
CWG PLC hereby announce as follows:
Period Ended 31st December 2025
A Final Dividend of 70 kobo per share, subject to appropriate withholding tax
and approval will be paid to shareholders whose names appear in the Register
Proposed Dividend of Members as at the close of business on Tuesday, 7th April 2026.
Proposed Bonus NIL
Closure of Register The Register of Shareholders will be closed on Wednesday, 8th April 2026.
Qualification Date 7th April 2026
On 17th April 2026, dividends will be paid electronically to shareholders whose
names appear on the Register of Members as at 7th April 2026 and who have
completed the e-dividend registration and mandated the Registrar to pay their
Payment Date dividends directly into their Bank accounts.
Shareholders who are yet to complete the e-dividend registration are advised
to download the Registrar’s E-Dividend Mandate Activation Form, which is also
available on the website of the Registrars: www.cordros.com, complete and
E-Dividend Registration submit to the Registrar or their respective Banks.
Unclaimed Dividend Shareholders with dividend warrants and share certificates that have remained
Warrants and Share unclaimed or are yet to be presented for payment or returned for validation are
Certificates advised to complete the e-dividend registration or contact the Registrar.
The Company’s Annual General Meeting will be held at The Jewel Aeida, Plot
Date of Annual General 105B Hakeem Dickson Link Road Lekki Phase 1, Lagos on 16th April 2026 at
Meeting 10a.m.
