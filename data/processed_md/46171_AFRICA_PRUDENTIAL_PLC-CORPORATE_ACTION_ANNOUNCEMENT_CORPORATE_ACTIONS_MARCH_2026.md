Africa Prudential Plc hereby announces as follows:
PERIOD ENDED: 31st of December 2025
PROPOSED DIVIDEND: A final dividend of 40 Kobo per 50 Kobo ordinary share, subject to
appropriate Withholding Tax will be paid to shareholders whose
names appear in the register of members as of close of business
on the 12th of March 2026. This, when combined with the Interim
dividend of 10Kobo paid at half year, will bring the total dividend
for the 2025 financial year to 50Kobo per ordinary share.
CLOSURE OF REGISTER: The Register of Shareholders will be closed from 13th of March 2026
to 18th of March 2026 (both dates inclusive).
QUALIFICATION DATE: 12th of March 2026.
PAYMENT DATE: On 2nd of April 2026, dividend will be paid electronically to
shareholders whose names appear on the Register of Members
as of 12th of March 2026 and have completed the e-dividend
registration and mandated the Registrar to pay their dividends
directly into their bank accounts.
E- DIVIDEND REGISTRATION: Shareholders who are yet to complete the e-dividend registration
are advised to complete our digitized e-mandate form on
https://africaprudential.com/claimyour-dividend-here/, which is
also available on our website: www.africaprudential.com
UNCLAIMED DIVIDEND Shareholders whose dividends have remained unclaimed are
advised to complete the e-dividend registration form.
DATE OF ANNUAL GENERAL The Annual General Meeting of the Company will be held virtually
MEETING on Thursday 2nd April 2026 via Zoom link -
https://africaprudential.zoom.us/webinar/register/WN_DExqWjM
