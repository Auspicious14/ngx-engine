Lagos, 26th October 2026
NOTIFICATION OF CHAMS HOLDING COMPANY PLC’S 83RD BOARD OF
DIRECTORS’ MEETING SCHEDULED TO HOLD ON 26TH OCTOBER 2026
Chams Holding Company Plc (Chams or the Company) wishes to inform Nigerian Exchange
Limited (NGX) and the investing public that the 83RD Board of Directors’ Meeting is scheduled
to hold on Monday, 26th October 2026, to consider Q3 2026 Unaudited Financial Statements
for the period ended 30 September 2026 and other related matters within the period under
review.
In compliance with NGX’s Listing requirements, Chams declared the commencement of a
closed period for trading in its shares by insiders and their connected persons from Thursday,
1 October 2026 until twenty-four (24) hours after the Unaudited Financial Statements for the
period ended 30th September 2026 have been published or released to the investing public.
