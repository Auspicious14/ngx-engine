Beta Glass Plc Announces Changes to its Board of Directors
Lagos, Nigeria – 5th August 2026 - Beta Glass Plc (Beta Glass or the Company), the leading
glass container manufacturer in West and Central Africa, announces the departure of the Board
Chairman, Dr. Vitus Ezinwa, from its Board of Directors. The Board expresses its sincere
gratitude for his dedicated service and strategic guidance, which have been instrumental to
the Company's growth. The Board wishes him continued success in his future pursuits.
Following Dr. Ezinwa's departure, the Board is pleased to announce the appointment of Mr.
‘Boye Olusanya as the new Chairman of the Board, effective 30th July 2026.
Mr. ‘Boye Olusanya (Non-Executive Director) is a transformational business leader with over
25 years of executive leadership experience across FMCG, agriculture, telecommunications,
technology, energy, infrastructure, and financial services. He serves as Group Chief Executive
Officer of FMN Holdings Limited, leading the transformation of one of Africa's largest food and
agro-industrial businesses into a vertically integrated, consumer-centric platform. Previously,
he held senior leadership roles at VMobile (formerly ECONET Wireless), Dancom
Technologies, Dangote Industries, 9mobile, and Helios Investment Partners, driving enterprise
transformation and operational excellence. He previously served as Chairman of Axxela
Limited, is the Vice Chairman of the Nigerian Economic Summit Group, and a member of the
Presidential Food Systems Coordination Unit.
The Board expresses full confidence in Mr. Boye Olusanya, his leadership, vision, and
expertise, believing that under his guidance the Company will be directed toward new
opportunities, strengthened governance, and sustainable growth. His appointment represents
a significant milestone in positioning the Company to achieve greater heights and deliver
enhanced value to its shareholders and stakeholders.

About Beta Glass Plc
Beta Glass Plc is a leading manufacturer of quality glass packaging solutions for beverage,
pharmaceutical and food industries.
Headquartered in Lagos, Nigeria, the company also exports to Central and West African
markets such as Ghana, Burkina Faso, Ivory Coast, etc., serving both regional and international
clients with a strong commitment to quality, innovation, and sustainability.
