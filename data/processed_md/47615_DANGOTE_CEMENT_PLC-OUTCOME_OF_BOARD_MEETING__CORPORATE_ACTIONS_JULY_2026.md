29 July 2026
Lagos, Nigeria
DANGOTE CEMENT PLC – OUTCOME OF BOARD MEETING
This is to inform Nigerian Exchange Limited (“NGX”) and all other stakeholders that
the Board of Directors of Dangote Cement Plc, at its meeting held on 29 July 2026,
among other matters, considered and approved the Unaudited Financial Statements for
the quarter ended 30 June 2026 (the “2026 Q2 UFS”).
The approved 2026 Q2 UFS will be released to the public on or before 30 July 2026, in
line with the rules of NGX.
The Closed Period, which commenced on 1 July 2026, will remain in effect until twenty-
four (24) hours after the submission of the 2026 Q2 UFS to the public.
Thank you.
