LAGOS, NIGERIA, 21 JULY 2026
ETERNA PLC - NOTICE OF BOARD MEETING
Notice is hereby given that a meeting of the Board of Directors of Eterna Plc (the
"Company") will take place on Monday, 27 July 2026, to consider the Company's Second
Quarter Unaudited Financial Statements for the period ended 30 June 2026 (Q2 2026 UFS)
and other items slated for the meeting.
As earlier announced and in compliance with the post listing requirements of Nigerian
Exchange Limited (NGX) for listed companies, the Company’s closed period which
commenced on 1 July 2026 will continue until twenty-four (24) hours after the Q2 2026 UFS
and other price-sensitive information is disclosed to the public.
Consequently, no insider and their connected persons may directly or indirectly deal in the
securities of the Company during the closed period.
BY ORDER OF THE BOARD
David Edet
Company Secretary/Legal Adviser
