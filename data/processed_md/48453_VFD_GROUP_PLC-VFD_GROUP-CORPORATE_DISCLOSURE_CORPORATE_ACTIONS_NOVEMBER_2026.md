Lagos, Nigeria
November 26, 2026
VFD Group Plc (the Group) wishes to announce a change in the composition of its Board
of Directors, arising from the Group’s succession planning process. The change forms
part of the Group’s continued commitment to strong governance, effective leadership
continuity and sustainable growth across its subsidiaries.
Consistent with this commitment, Mrs. Morenike Ominike will be redeployed to Anchoria
Capital Group (ACG), to serve as Group Executive Director, Operations, effective
December 1, 2026. In this role, she will oversee ACG’s operational activities across our
capital market subsidiaries, ensuring close alignment with the Group.
This move reflects the Group’s commitment to leadership continuity and the deliberate
development of its executive talent. Succession planning ensures that the right leaders
are in the right roles at the right time, and that critical positions across the Group are
filled by seasoned executives with deep institutional knowledge. Placing such leadership
within key subsidiaries strengthens execution, deepens accountability and safeguards
the Group’s long-term stability.
We are confident that her experience and institutional knowledge will serve ACG and, by
extension, the Group.
On behalf of the Board, we thank Mrs. Ominike for her continued dedicated service,
valuable contributions and commitment to the Group.
