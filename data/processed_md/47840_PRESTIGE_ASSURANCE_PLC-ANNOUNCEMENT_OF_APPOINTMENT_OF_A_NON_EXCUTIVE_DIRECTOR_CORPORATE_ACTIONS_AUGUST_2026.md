Lagos, 21 August, 2026.
ANNOUNCEMENT OF APPOINTMENT OF A NON EXCUTIVE DIRECTOR
This is to notify Nigerian Exchange Limited (NGX) and the investing public of the
appointment of Mr. Seetharaman Sivasankar as a Non-Executive Director on the Board
of Prestige Assurance PLC (“the Company”) effective 20th August, 2026 subject to
regulatory approval.
Profile of Mr. Seetharaman Sivasankar
Mr. S. Sivasankar joined National Insurance Company Ltd. (NICL), India as an Officer in
1991 and has since built a career spanning over 36 years within the Company. A
graduate of Commerce and a Fellow of the Insurance Institute of India [FIII], he has
acquired extensive experience across the insurance value chain through progressive
leadership roles at the Branch, Divisional, Regional, and Head Offices.
During his distinguished career, Mr. Sivasankar served as Head of the Accounts
Department at the Divisional Office for approximately eight years, where he was
responsible for overseeing financial operations. He subsequently headed several Branch
and Divisional Offices over a period of 16 years, driving business growth, and enhancing
operational efficiency across the Company's operations.
He also managed Corporate and Large Claims for three years, before his promotion to
the position of Chief Manager. In that capacity at the Head Office, he was responsible
for the Fire and Engineering Claims portfolio. Following his elevation to Deputy General
Manager, he assumed responsibility for the Fire and Engineering businesses, overseeing
both Underwriting and Claims functions, as well as the Techno-Marketing portfolio.
In recognition of his extensive industry experience, and significant contributions to the
Company, Mr. Sivasankar was appointed to the Board of New India Assurance as an
Executive Director on 20th February, 2026.
The Board is confident that he will bring his wealth of experience to bear in driving the
growth and expansion of the business, and that the Company will be further
strengthened by his valuable contributions.
For: PRESTIGE ASSURANCE PLC.
