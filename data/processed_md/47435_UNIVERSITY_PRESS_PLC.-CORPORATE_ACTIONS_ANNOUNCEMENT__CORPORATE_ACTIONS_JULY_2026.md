Corporate Actions Announcement
UNIVERSITY PRESS PLC hereby announces as follows:
Period Ended 31st March 2026
A Final Dividend of 18k for every ordinary share of 50k, subject to appropriate
withholding tax and approval will be paid to shareholders whose names appear
in the Register of Members as at the close of business on the 28th day of August
Proposed Dividend 2026.
Proposed Bonus NIL
The Register of Shareholders will be closed from 31st August to 4th September
Closure of Register 2026.
Qualification Date 28th August, 2026
On Thursday 24th September 2026, dividends will be paid electronically to
shareholders whose names appear on the Register of Members as at 28th
August 2026 and who have completed the e-dividend registration and
Payment Date mandated the Registrar to pay their dividends directly into their Bank accounts.
Shareholders who are yet to complete the e-dividend registration are advised
to download the Registrar’s E-Dividend Mandate Activation Form, which is also
available on the website of the Registrars:www.greenwichregistrars.com,
E-Dividend Registration complete and submit to the Registrar or their respective Banks.
Unclaimed Dividend Shareholders with dividend warrants and share certificates that have remained
Warrants and Share unclaimed, or are yet to be presented for payment or returned for validation
Certificates are advised to complete the e-dividend registration or contact the Registrar.
The Company’s Annual General Meeting will be held at Kakanfo Inn &
Date of Annual General Conference Centre, 1 Nihinola Street, Joyce B Road, Off Ring Road, Ibadan on
Meeting Thursday 24th September 2026 at 11 am.
