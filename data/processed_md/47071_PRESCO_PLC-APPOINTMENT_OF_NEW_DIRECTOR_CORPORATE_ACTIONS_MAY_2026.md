NOTIFICATION OF CHANGE ON THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS
Benin City, Nigeria
29 May 2026
Presco Plc (“the Company”) hereby notifies the Nigerian Exchange Limited (“NGX”), its shareholders and
other stakeholders of the following change on its Board of Directors (“the Board”).
Appointment of Non-Executive Director
The Company is pleased to announce the appointment of Mr. François Van Hoydonck as a Non-Executive
Director to fill a casual Board vacancy. Mr. François Van Hoydonck’s appointment will be presented to
shareholders for ratification at the Company’s next Annual General Meeting.
He is a seasoned business executive and finance professional with over four decades of experience in the
agribusiness and plantation sector. A Belgian national born on 29 August 1959, he holds a Bachelor’s
degree in Accountancy and Tax Law from St. Eligius Business School, Antwerp, Belgium, and has been a
member of the Institute for Accountants and Bookkeepers (IAB) since 1983.
Mr. François Van Hoydonck commenced his professional career with SIPEF in July 1979 and has since
played significant leadership roles within the Group. He served as Chief Financial Officer of SIPEF from
February 1995 to August 2007, during which he was responsible for the Group’s financial management
and strategic financial planning.
In September 2007, he was appointed Managing Director of SIPEF and also became a Director of the
company. During his tenure as Managing Director, which lasted until September 2024, he provided
strategic leadership and contributed significantly to the growth and development of the Group’s
international agribusiness operations.
Mr. François Van Hoydonck also serves as a Director of Jabelmalux SA and, following his retirement as
Managing Director of SIPEF on 1 September 2024, continues to serve on the SIPEF Board as a Non-
Executive Director.
In addition, he serves as a Non-Executive Director of Verdant Bioscience, Singapore, and was appointed
an Independent Non-Executive Director of the SIAT Group in June 2025.
Mr. François Van Hoydonck brings to the boards on which he serves extensive expertise in corporate
governance, finance, international business management, and agribusiness operations.
The Board warmly welcomes Mr. François Van Hoydonck and looks forward to his valuable contributions
to the Company.
By the Order of the Board
Frederick Ichekwai
Company Secretary
