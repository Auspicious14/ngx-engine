1. REPORTING ENTITY
Nigerian Breweries Plc, a public Company quoted on the Nigerian Stock Exchange, was incorporated in Nigeria on
16th November 1946, under the name Nigerian Brewery Limited. The name was changed on 7th January 1957, to
Nigerian Breweries Limited and thereafter to Nigerian Breweries Plc in 1990 when the Companies and Allied
Matters Act of that year came into effect. The Company is a subsidiary of Heineken N.V., a company domiciled in
the Netherlands, the latter having approximately 72.90% interest in the equity of Nigerian Breweries Plc. The
Company’s registered office address is 1, Abebe Village Road, Iganmu, Lagos. The condensed interim financial
statements of the Company as at and for the six-month period ended 30th June 2026 comprise the Company and
its subsidiaries' financial position.
The financial statements of Nigerian Breweries Plc as at and for the year ended 31st December 2025 are available
on the Company’s registered website www.nbplc.com/investor-relations.html .

the Board, employees, and other persons classified as insiders when effecting transactions in the Company’s shares

period when transactions are not allowed to be effected on the Company’s shares and securities (“Closed Period”)
