OUTCOME OF THE MEETING OF THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF VFD GROUP PLC
This is to notify Nigerian Exchange Limited (“The Exchange”) and the Investing Public that
the Q3 meeting of the Board of Directors of VFD Group Plc (“VFD Group” or “the Company”)
was held on Friday, 30th October 2026 at the Company’s offices located at 8 MacGregor
Road, Ikoyi, and virtually via Microsoft Teams.
The Board reviewed the Company’s Q3 2026 Management Report, Performance Updates
and ratified the Company’s Q3 Unaudited Financial Statements.
The Company has since published the Q3 Unaudited Financial Statements.
