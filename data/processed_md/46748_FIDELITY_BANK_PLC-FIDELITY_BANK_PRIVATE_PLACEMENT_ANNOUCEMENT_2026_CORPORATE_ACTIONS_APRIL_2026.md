Private Placement of 14,800,000,000 Ordinary Shares of
50 Kobo each at ₦17.50 per share
Payable in full on Application
Announces the result of the Private Placement of 14,800,000,000 ordinary shares of 50 kobo each at ₦17.50
per share (the “Offer”), which opened on Wednesday, December 31, 2025 and closed on Wednesday,
December 31, 2025.
• Twenty (20) applications for 16,776,200,000 ordinary shares werereceived, deemed valid, accepted and
presented to the Central Bank of Nigeria (CBN) for capital verification.
• Fourteen (14) applicationsfor 12,238,058,000 ordinaryshareswere fully verified and allotted in full.
• One (1) applicationfor 1,609,550,000 ordinaryshareswas partially verified, with 735,942,000 ordinary shares
verified and allotted.
• Five (5) applications for 2,928,592,000 were not verified.
• In summary, the Offer recorded 113.4% in valid applications and achieved a subscription level of
87.7% upon conclusion of the CBN capital verification process.
