Lagos, 22nd July, 2026
CORNERSTONE INSURANCE PLC – NOTICE OF BOARD MEETING AND CLOSED
PERIOD
We hereby notify Nigerian Exchange Limited (NGX), our shareholders and the investing public, that a
meeting of the Board of Directors of Cornerstone Insurance PLC (“the Company”) will hold on
Wednesday, 29th July, 2026 at Cornerstone Complex, 21, Water Corporation Drive, Off Ligali Ayorinde
Street, Victoria Island, Lagos at 12:00 pm to consider the Unaudited Financial Statements for the Second
Quarter Ended 30th June, 2026 (Q2 UFS 2026).
Accordingly, in line with the provisions of Rule 17.18(a): Closed Period, Rulebook of the Exchange,
2015 (Issuers’ Rule) as amended, and which has been incorporated into Sections 5 and 6 of the
Company’s Securities Trading Policy, the Company commenced a Closed Period from Wednesday, 1st
July 2026.
During this Closed Period, all insiders shall not trade in the Company's securities until twenty-four (24)
hours after the Q2 UFS, 2026 have been released to the public.
