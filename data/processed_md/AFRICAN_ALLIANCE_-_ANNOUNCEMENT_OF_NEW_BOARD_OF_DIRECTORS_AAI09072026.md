AFRICAN ALLIANCE INSURANCE PLC ANNOUNCES THE APPOINTMENT OF A NEW BOARD
African Alliance Insurance Plc. (“the Company”) announces the appointment of a new board of directors
following the handover from the Interim Management Board appointed on October 30, 2024, by its primary
regulator, the National Insurance Commission (NAICOM).
The reconstituted board comprises the following members:
1. Rear Admiral Anthony Odogba Musa Isa Chairman/Independent Non-Executive Director
2. Mr Ayobami Olakunle Ogunkeye Managing Director/CEO
3. Ubochi Andrew Chukwuka Executive Director Technical
4. Harrison F. Ogalagu Non-Executive Director
5. High Chief Olabode Akeem Mustafa Non-Executive Director
6. Chief Boniface Chinedu Moore SAN Independent Non-Executive Director
7. Mr Ataraire Gideon Independent Non-Executive Director
By Order of the Board
Oluchi Nwaizim
Company Secretary

Profiles of Directors
1. Rear Admiral Anthony Odogba Musa Isa - Rear Admiral Anthony Isa joins the Board of African Alliance
Insurance Plc as an Independent Non-Executive Director and has been appointed as Chairman of the Board.
He has served in the Nigerian Navy and is an alumnus of the Nigerian Defence Academy. He holds a degree
in Mechanical Engineering and an MSc in Advanced Marine Engineering from the Royal Naval Engineering
College, United Kingdom, as well as an MSc in National Security and Resource Strategy from the National
Defence University, Washington, D.C., where he graduated with the University's highest award. During his
distinguished military career, he held several strategic appointments both in Nigeria and internationally,
including serving as the Federal Government of Nigeria's Chief Delegate on the warships project in Italy,
Marine Engineer Officer of NNS Aradu, the Nigerian Navy's flagship, and Commandant of the National
Defence College, Abuja. He brings extensive experience in engineering, defence, security strategy, and
leadership at the highest levels of national service.

Diploma in Insurance from the University of Lagos and an Associate of the Chartered Insurance Institute of Nigeria
(AIIN). A well-grounded Industry veteran with specifics in Business Development, Retail Strategic Sales, and
Partnerships.

assignment for CIIN, NIA, NCRIB and other trade bodies within the Insurance Industry. He is extremely
passionate about creating non-conventional channels for insurance sales through strategic partnerships.
With over 30 years working experience in the Insurance industry, he pioneered the Strategic Alliance Partnership
Initiative between Conventional Banks and Insurance companies in Nigeria which later evolved as what is now known
today as the Banc Assurance model that many companies have adopted in the last 18 years.
His wealth of experience about sales and strategic partnership has consistently provided different training
platforms within and outside the Insurance Industry both locally and internationally where he shares his

4. Harrison F. Ogalagu - Appointed as a Non-Executive Director at African Alliance Insurance PLC, Harrison
Ogalagu Friday brings over 15 years of high-stakes legal expertise, corporate governance insight, and risk
management experience to the board. As a Partner at Tope Adebayo LP and a registered SEC Capital Market
Solicitor, Harrison has spent his career advising on complex corporate investment, financial restructuring,
insolvency, and commercial dispute resolution. His extensive background in guiding multinational financial
institutions and managing multi-billion-naira asset portfolios ensures robust fiduciary oversight and
regulatory compliance. Harrison holds an LLM in Comparative and International Dispute Resolution from
Queen Mary University of London and a Ph.D. in Investment Arbitration.
5. High Chief Olabode Akeem Mustapha – High Chief Olabode Akeem Mustapha joins the board of African
Alliance Insurance PLC as a Non-Executive Director, bringing an extraordinary blend of high-level corporate
management, legislative expertise, and strategic risk oversight. Chief Mustapha is a seasoned business leader
and politician who served as a distinguished member of the Nigerian House of Representatives, where he
pioneered crucial national legislative frameworks including the Police Security Trust Fund bill. His extensive
executive background includes serving as a German-trained Department Store Manager and leading major
commercial operations as the Manager of UTC. A deeply committed lifelong learner and prominent
statesman, he holds a first-class degree in Business, Management & Marketing from the Goethe Institute in
Germany, alongside an MBA and a Master’s in Peace Studies and Conflict Resolution from the National Open
University of Nigeria.
6. Chief Boniface Chinedu Moore – Chief Chinedu Moore (SAN) is a versatile personality and a consummate
legal practitioner. He holds master’s degrees in law (Corporate Management and Finance Law) and in Public
Administration (MPA), a diploma in Banking and Finance and a Certificate in Insurance Broking. He is a
chartered Arbitrator (MCArb) and a Barrister and Solicitor of the Supreme Court of Nigeria.
He had a stint of corporate law practice in the Legal and Company Secretary Division of First Bank of Nigeria
Plc and thereafter went into forensic law Practice at the chambers of Chief Mike Ozekhome and the law firm
of Ilonzeh, Ezeoke & Co, before setting up his own private law practice.
