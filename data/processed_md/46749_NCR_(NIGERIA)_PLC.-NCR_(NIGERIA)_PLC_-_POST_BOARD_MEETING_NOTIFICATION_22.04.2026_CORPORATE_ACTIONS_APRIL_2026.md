NCR (NIGERIA) PLC – APPROVAL OF THE UNAUDITED FINANCIAL
STATEMENTS FOR THE FIRST QUARTER ENDED 31 MARCH 2026
This is to notify the investing public and Nigerian Exchange Limited that the Board of
Directors of NCR (Nigeria) PLC (the Company) held a meeting on Wednesday, 22
April 2026, to consider and approve, amongst other things, the Company’s Unaudited
Financial Statements for the first quarter ended 31 March 2026 (the “Unaudited
Financial Statements”) and the Company’s Audited Financial Statements for the full
year ended 31 December 2025.
While the Board approved the Unaudited Financial Statements, it was unable to
approve the 2025 Audited Financial Statements at its meeting due to ongoing internal
reviews and alignments with the External Auditors.
Accordingly, please note that the closed period prohibiting the Company’s Insiders
from transacting in the shares of the Company, which commenced on 31 December
2025, is still in place until twenty-four (24) hours after the Audited Financial Statements
for the year ended 31 December 2025 and the Unaudited Financial Statements for the
first quarter ended 31 March 2026 have been released to the Market.
