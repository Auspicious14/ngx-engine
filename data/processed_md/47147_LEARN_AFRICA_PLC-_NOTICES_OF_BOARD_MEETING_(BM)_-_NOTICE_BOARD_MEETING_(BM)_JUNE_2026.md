9 June 2026
Notification of Closed Period for Learn Africa Plc
Pursuant to the provisions of the Nigerian Exchange Limited (“NGX”) Rulebook and the
Company’s Insider Trading Policy, we hereby notify NGX, our shareholders and the investing
public that the Board of Directors of Learn Africa Plc (“the Company”) is scheduled to meet
on Thursday, 25th June 2026 to consider the Audited Financial Statements for the year
ended 31st March 2026, amongst other matters.
The closed period commenced on Wednesday 1st April 2026 and will remain in effect until
twenty-four (24) hours after the Audited Financial Statements for the year ended 31st March
2026 is released to NGX and the public.
During this period, all insiders and their connected persons are advised not to trade in the
shares of the Company.
