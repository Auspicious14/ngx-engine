UNILEVER NIGERIA PLC
ANNOUNCEMENT REGARDING THE PROPOSED TRANSFER OF THE FOODS BUSINESS
Lagos, Nigeria, November 23, 2026
Unilever Nigeria PLC (“Unilever Nigeria” or the “Company”) hereby provides the following
update to its shareholders, the Nigerian Exchange Limited (“NGX”) and the investing public
in accordance with the applicable disclosure requirements of NGX.
Proposed transfer of the Unilever Nigeria Foods Business
Further to the Company’s announcement dated 8 April 2026 regarding its proposed
strategic business realignment, the Board of Directors of Unilever Nigeria (the “Board”)
wishes to provide an update on the proposed transfer of the Company’s Foods business (the
“Foods Business”).
On 31 March 2026, Unilever PLC, the parent company of Unilever Nigeria, entered into an
agreement with McCormick & Company, Inc. (“McCormick”) to combine Unilever PLC's Foods
business with McCormick (the "Global Transaction"). As part of the Global Transaction, the
Company received an offer for the proposed transfer of the Foods Business assets falling
within the transaction perimeter, for cash consideration and on a cash-free and debt-free
basis (the “Offer”). Under the terms of the Offer, the Company would transfer the Foods
Business to MCK Nigeria Ltd, a subsidiary of McCormick (the “Proposed Transaction”).
Following its evaluation of the Offer and its terms, including the implications of the
Proposed Transaction for the Company and its shareholders, and having considered advice
from its Fairness Opinion Adviser, Financial Adviser and Legal Adviser, the Board has agreed
to recommend the Proposed Transaction to its shareholders for approval, subject to the
conditions set out below.
The Proposed Transaction remains subject to completion of the Global Transaction, the
approval of the Company’s shareholders, the receipt of applicable regulatory approvals,
including the approval of the Federal Competition and Consumer Protection Commission,
and the satisfaction or waiver of other applicable legal, regulatory and contractual
requirements and conditions to completion.
For clarity, the Proposed Transaction relates to the transfer of the Foods Business and does
not involve a transfer of shares in Unilever Nigeria or any change to the existing
shareholding structure of the Company.

Following completion of the Proposed Transaction, Unilever Nigeria will become a Beauty,
Personal Care and Home Care focused business, with a portfolio of established brands
including Vaseline, Rexona, Closeup and Pepsodent.
The Company intends to continue developing these categories through innovation,
portfolio expansion and strengthened in-market execution. Any future introduction of
additional brands or products, or further development of local manufacturing capabilities,
will be subject to the Company’s applicable investment requirements, corporate approvals
and regulatory requirements.
The Company will, in due course, provide shareholders with further information regarding
the Proposed Transaction, including the relevant transaction terms and details of the
general meeting at which shareholders will be invited to consider and approve the
Proposed Transaction.
Unilever Nigeria remains committed to keeping its shareholders and the investing public
informed of material developments in accordance with applicable regulatory requirements.
