JULY 6, 2026
LAGOS, NIGERIA
ANNOUNCEMENT OF BOARD OF DIRECTORS MEETING OF CORONATION
INSURANCE PLC
This is to inform the Nigerian Exchange Limited (“The Exchange”) and the Investing Public
that a meeting of the Board of Directors of Coronation Insurance Plc (“the Company”) is
scheduled to hold on Tuesday, the 28th day of July 2026 to amongst other things review
the Company’s performance for the second quarter of the 2026 Financial Year as well as
to consider and approve the Company’s Q2, Unaudited Financial Statements for the period
ended 30th June 2026 (2026 Q2 UFS).
The Company had in compliance with the Closed Period Rules of The Exchange entered a
Closed Period on 1st July 2026, in respect of transactions on its securities which continues
to be in force until twenty-four (24) hours after the release of the Company’s 2026 Q2
UFS to the Exchange and by extension, to the general public.
Accordingly, all insiders are prohibited from dealing in the Company’s securities during the
Closed Period.
FOR: CORONATION INSURANCE PLC
MARY AGHA
COMPANY SECRETARY
