LAGOS: 26th March 2026
LAFARGE AFRICA PLC - NOTIFICATION OF BOARD MEETING AND CLOSED PERIOD
We hereby notify Nigerian Exchange Limited (“The Exchange” or “NGX”) and the
investing public that a meeting of the Board of Directors of Lafarge Africa Plc (the
“Company”) has been scheduled to hold on Wednesday, 29th April 2026 to consider the
Company’s Unaudited Financial Statements for the quarter ending 31st March 2026 (the
“Unaudited Financial Statements”).
Please note that the Closed Period for trading in the Company’s shares will commence
on Wednesday, 1st April 2026 and will continue until twenty-four (24) hours after the
Unaudited Financial Statement is released on the floor of NGX in line with the
Company’s Insider Trading Policy and the Rules of The Exchange.
Accordingly, no Insider may directly or indirectly deal in the securities of the Company
in any manner during the Closed Period.
