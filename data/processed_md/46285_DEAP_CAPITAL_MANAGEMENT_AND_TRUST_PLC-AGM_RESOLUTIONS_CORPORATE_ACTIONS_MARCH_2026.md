Lagos, 16 March 2026
Resolutions Passed at the 12th Annual General Meeting of Deap Capital Management and Trust
Plc
At the 12th Annual General Meeting of Deap Capital Management &Trust Plc (the Company)
held at the Event Hall, Providence Hotel, Ikeja GRA, Ikeja, Lagos, on March 12, 2026, the
following Resolutions were presented to the Members and duly passed.
A. ORDINARY BUSINESS
1. Adoption of the Financial Statements for the years ended 30 September 2024 and 2025
The audited accounts for the years ended September 30, 2024, and 2025 were presented to
the members and were duly approved.
2. Elections of Directors
The appointments of Mr. Lamon Rutten, Dr Israel Ovirih, Mr. Temitope Oduseso and Mr. Francis
Ekeng were ratified by the shareholders.
3. Renumeration of the External Auditors
The Directors were authorized to fix the remuneration of the External Auditors for the year
ending September 30, 2026 in accordance with section 408(1)b of CAMA 2020.
4. Election of Members to the Statutory Audit Committee
The following shareholders were re-elected as members of the Statutory Audit Committee:
(a) Mr. Samuel Adedoyin.
(b) Mr. Banji Olugbosun.
(c) Mr. Abioye Tomori.
The following Directors were nominated by the Board to represent it as members on the
statutory Audit Committee
(a) Mr Edmond Ani.
(b) Mr Tope Oduseso.
Directors: Dr. Kenneth Olise, (Chairman), Mr. Edmund Ani, (Vice Chairman) Mr. Tony Adigwe (Managing Director),
Mr. Joe Idudu, Mr. Joel Omole, Dr. Joseph Asaolu

SPECIAL BUSINESS
5. Increase of Share Capital
That the share capital of the Company be and is hereby increased from ₦1,500,000,000
divided into 3,000,000,000 ordinary shares of ₦0.50K each to ₦5,029,411,764 divided into
10,058,823,528 (Ten Billion, Fifty Eight Million, Eight Hundred and Twenty Three Thousand, Five
Hundred and Twenty Eight) units of ordinary shares of ₦0.50K each by the creation of
7,058,823,528 units of ordinary shares of N0.50K each ranking pari passu in all respects with the
existing shares of the Company to be issued and fully allotted to BANKLINK Africa Private
Equities Limited and RGM Materials Solutions Limited at N0.85 each.
6.Authority to Undertake Capital Raising
That consequent upon the increase of share capital, the Directors be and are hereby
authorised to undertake a capital raising exercise by way of Private Placement, public
subscription, or such other means inclusive of Global Private Equity Fund origination or creation
or co-creation as they deem fit, subject to the approval of the relevant regulatory authorities;
both local and global.
7. Authority to Make Regulatory Filings
That the Directors be and are hereby authorised to make all necessary filings with the Securities
and Exchange Commission, Nigerian Exchange Limited, Corporate Affairs Commission and
other relevant authorities to give effect to these resolutions.
SPECIAL RESOLUTIONS
8. Change of Name of the Company
That the name of the Company be and is hereby changed from Deap Capital Management
& Trust Plc to Critical Minerals Financing Corporation Plc (CMFC Plc) or such other name as
may be approved by the Corporate Affairs Commission (CAC).
9.Amendment of the Memorandum and Articles of Association
That the Memorandum and Articles of Association of the Company be amended to reflect,
Object Clauses the revised share capital, governance structure and strategic direction of the
Company to becoming Africa's premier private-sector led minerals finance Institution.
10. General Authority
That the Directors and the Company Secretary be and are hereby authorised to do all such
acts, deeds, matters and things as may be necessary to give effect to the above resolutions.
