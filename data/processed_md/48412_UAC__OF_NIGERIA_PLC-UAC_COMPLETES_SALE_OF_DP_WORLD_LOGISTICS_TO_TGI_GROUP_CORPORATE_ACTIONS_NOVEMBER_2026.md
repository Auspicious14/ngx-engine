UAC Completes Sale of DP World Logistics Limited to 22 Plus Invest Limited
Lagos, 18 November 2026 – UAC of Nigeria PLC (“UAC”) today announces the completion of the
sale of its 43% equity interest in DP World Logistics Limited (formerly MDS Logistics Limited) (“DP
World”) to 22 Plus Invest Limited (“22 Plus Invest”), a member of Tropical General Investments (TGI)
Group (“TGI”), following approval of the transaction by the Federal Competition and Consumer
Protection Commission (“FCCPC”).
The transaction follows UAC’s announcement on 24 September 2026 of an agreement for the sale
of its shareholding in DP World.
Fola Aiyesimoju, Group Managing Director of UAC, commented: “We are pleased to have
completed this divestment and thank the DP World team for their contributions to the UAC Group
over the years. We wish DP World and 22 Plus Invest a successful partnership and every success in
the future.”
Farouk Gumel, Vice Chairman of TGI Group, said: “We are pleased to have completed this
acquisition and look forward to working with DP World to advance our shared vision for Africa’s
trade and logistics sector. Together, we will strengthen supply chains, enhance regional
connectivity, and create long-term value for customers and economies across continent.”
