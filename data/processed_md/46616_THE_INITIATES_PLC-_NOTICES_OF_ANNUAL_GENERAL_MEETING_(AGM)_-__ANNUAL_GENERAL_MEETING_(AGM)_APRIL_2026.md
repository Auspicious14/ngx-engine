NOTICE OF THE 27TH ANNUAL GENERAL MEETING OF THE INITIATES PLC
NOTICE IS HEREBY GIVEN that the 27th Annual General Meeting (2025) of Shareholders
of The Initiates Plc. (TIP) shall hold on Thursday, 30th April,2026 at Golden Tulip Garden
City Port Harcourt Hotel, No.63 Ken Saro wiwa road, formerly Stadium Road by Mummy
B Junction, Port Harcourt, Rivers State by 11:00am to discuss the following:
Ordinary Business:
1. To receive and consider the Audited Financial Statement as at December 31, 2025,
the report of the Directors, the Audit Committee, the Auditors Report therein;
2. To declare dividend to Shareholders
3. Having attained the statutory age of 70years; to retire the following Directors of the
Company:
3a. To retire Prof.Edward Alikor as a Director of the Company
3b. To retire Mr. Joseph Ebinum as a Director of the Company
4a. To elect Mr. Chijioke Mbagwu as an Executive Director of the Company
4b. To ratify the appointment of Mr. Christian Ogidi as Executive Director of the
Company
5. To appoint the Audit firm of BBC Professionals as the Company’s External Auditors
6. To authorize the Directors to fix the remuneration of Auditors.
7. To appoint members of the Audit Committee.
8. To disclose remuneration of Managers
Special Business:
9. To consider and fix the remuneration of the Non-Executive Directors of the Company
10. To increase the Issued Share Capital of the Company from N1,000,000,000 (One
Billion Naira) divided into 2,000,000,000 (Two billion) ordinary units of N0.50kobo each TO
N1,052,500,000(One Billion, Fifty- Two Million, Five Hundred Thousand Naira) by the
creation of 105million (One hundred and Five million) new ordinary shares of N0.50 kobo
each ranking parri pasu with the existing ordinary shares of the Company and that the Board
of Directors of the Company be and is hereby authorized , if deemed necessary to cancel
any unallotted shares of the Company, increase the share capital of the Company to an
amount sufficient to accommodate any transaction undertaken by the Company pursuant
to the resolutions presented for adoption herein
11. That the Board be and is hereby authorized to raise additional capital up to the new
issued share capital of the company by way of Private Placement or other mode or
combination of modes, in such tranches, series, amounts, pricing or proportions and on
such terms and conditions and at such times as may be determined by the Board, subject

and vote instead of a member shall also have the same right as the member to
speak at the meeting. Executed proxy forms should be deposited with the Company
Secretary at the registered office of the Company not later than 48hours before the
meeting.
