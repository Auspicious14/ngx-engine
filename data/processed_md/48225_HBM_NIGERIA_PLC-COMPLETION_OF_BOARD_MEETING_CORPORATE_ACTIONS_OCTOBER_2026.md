LAGOS: 29th October 2026
HBM NIGERIA PLC - OUTCOME OF BOARD MEETING
We hereby notify Nigerian Exchange Limited (“NGX”) and the investing public that at the
meeting of the Board of Directors of HBM Nigeria Plc (the “Company”) held today, Thursday
29th October 2026, the Board considered and approved the Company’s Unaudited Financial
Statements for the quarter ended 30th September 2026 (the “Unaudited Financial
Statements”).
Please note that the Closed Period for trading in the Company’s shares, which commenced
from 1st October 2026, will continue until twenty-four (24) hours after the Unaudited
Financial Statements is released to the public in line with the Company’s Insider Trading
Policy and the Rules of NGX.
