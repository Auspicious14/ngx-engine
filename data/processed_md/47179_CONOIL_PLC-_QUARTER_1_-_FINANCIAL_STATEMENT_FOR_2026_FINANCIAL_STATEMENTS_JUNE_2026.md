Conoil Plc (“The Company”) was incorporated in 1960. The Company's authorised share capital is
700,000,000 ordinary shares of 50k each.
The Company was established to engage in the marketing of refined petroleum products and the
manufacturing and marketing of lubricants, household and industrial chemicals.
