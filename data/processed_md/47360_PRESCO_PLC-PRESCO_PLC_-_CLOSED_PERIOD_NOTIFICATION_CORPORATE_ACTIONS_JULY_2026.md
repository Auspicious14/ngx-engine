PRESCO PLC
Notice of Closed Period
2 July 2026
Benin City, Nigeria
NOTICE OF CLOSED PERIOD
In accordance with Rule 17.18(a) (Closed Period Rules) of the Nigerian Exchange Limited
Rulebook, 2015 (as amended) (Issuers' Rules), NOTICE IS HEREBY GIVEN that the Closed
Period for trading in the shares of Presco Plc (the "Company") commenced on 1 July 2026 in
respect of the Company's Unaudited Financial Statements for the period ended 30 June
2026 ("Q2 2026 Unaudited Financial Statements").
Accordingly, all insiders of the Company, and their connected persons, as defined under the
Company's Securities Trading Policy and applicable laws and regulations, are prohibited
from dealing, directly or indirectly, in the securities of the Company until twenty-four (24)
hours after the Q2 2026 Unaudited Financial Statements have been released to the market
through the Nigerian Exchange Limited (NGX) Issuers’ Portal.
All the Company’s insiders and the investing public are hereby notified of the
commencement of this Closed Period.
The Company's Securities Trading Policy is available on the Company's website at
https://www.presco-plc.com. Should you have any questions regarding the Policy or its
applicability to you or your activities, please contact the Company Secretary by email at
presco.investors@siat-group.com.
By order of the Board
Frederick Ichekwai
Company Secretary
