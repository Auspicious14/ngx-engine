Lagos, Nigeria, 12th July 2026
UPDC Plc – Notice of Board Changes
The Board of UPDC Plc (the Company) announces the resignation of Mr Folasope Aiyesimoju as
a Director of the Company with effect from 12th July 2026.
The Board of the Company expresses its profound appreciation to Mr Aiyesimoju who served on
the Board of the Company for eight (8) years, initially as the Managing Director of the Company
and later as a Non-Executive Director. The Board notes that Mr Aiyesimoju brought his wealth of
experience to the Board room and contributed significantly to the repositioning of the Company.
The Board wishes Mr Aiyesimoju well in his future endeavours.
New Appointment
The Board is pleased to announce the appointment of Mr Adebolanle Badejo to the Board of the
Company with effect from 13th July 2026.
Mr Adebolanle Badejo is an investment executive with more than eighteen (18) years of
experience spanning investment banking, private equity, and corporate leadership across Africa.
He currently serves as Group Investment Director at UAC of Nigeria Plc, a publicly listed
investment holding company, where he leads group-wide investment strategy and capital
allocation. He also sits on the boards of several of the group's major subsidiaries.
Prior to this role, Mr. Adebolanle Badejo served as Chief Executive Officer of UAC Restaurants
Limited. Earlier, as a Principal at Themis Capital Management, he supported the execution of
strategic equity investments in high-growth African businesses, including the investment in UAC
of Nigeria Plc.
Mr. Adebolanle Badejo began his career in investment banking, holding roles at Standard Bank
in Johannesburg, where he executed more than $2 billion in M&A and capital markets
transactions across sub-Saharan Africa, and at Morgan Stanley in New York within its Natural
Resources Group.
He holds a MBA from Columbia Business School and a B.Sc. in Systems Engineering from the
University of Virginia.
