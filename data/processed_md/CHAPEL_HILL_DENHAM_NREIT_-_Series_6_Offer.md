Lagos | Nigeria July 15, 2026
NOW OPEN – NIGERIA REAL ESTATE INVESTMENT TRUST SERIES 6 OFFER OF UP TO N30.4 BILLION OF
289,630,000 UNITS ISSUED AT N105 UNDER THE N400 BILLION PROGRAMME
Nigeria Real Estate Investment Trust ("NREIT" or the "Fund"), Nigeria's largest REIT, announces that the
Series 6 Offer ("the Offer") under the N400 billion Issuance Programme opened on July 9, 2026.
NREIT is a SEC authorised and registered Shariah compliant real estate investment trust that acquires high
quality A-grade, income producing commercial real estate assets in Nigeria across the office, industrial,
corporate residential, and other specialty asset sub-sectors to deliver attractive risk-adjusted real estate
returns to investors, including a quarterly cash distribution.
With a current capitalisation of over N180 billion, NREIT is the strongest capitalised REIT in Nigeria. On
completion of the Series 6 Offer, the Fund's total capitalisation will exceed N210 billion, further extending
its position as the market leader and deepening the REIT market in Nigeria.
The issuance represents the Fund's sixth capital raise since inception in 2020 and the second since its
Listing on the Nigerian Exchange Limited. The proceeds from the Series 6 Offer will be used to acquire
target assets in its pipeline.

About NREIT
The Chapel Hill Denham NREIT is a Shariah compliant Real Estate Investment Trust (“REIT”) denominated in Naira
and focused exclusively on the Nigerian real estate market. The Fund is registered as a REIT under the relevant rules
and regulations of the Securities & Exchange Commission (“SEC”) Nigeria. The Fund’s Units are listed on the Nigerian
Exchange Group (“NGX”). NREIT is sponsored by Chapel Hill Denham and Chapel Hill Denham Management is the
Fund Manager of NREIT.
The Fund’s strategy is to acquire multiple assets to establish a portfolio of high quality, A-grade, income producing
real estate assets, diversified by sector, geography, tenants and lease expiry profile. NREIT targets Shariah compliant
investments across target sectors, including the office, industrial and corporate residential sectors, with long-term
corporate tenants.
