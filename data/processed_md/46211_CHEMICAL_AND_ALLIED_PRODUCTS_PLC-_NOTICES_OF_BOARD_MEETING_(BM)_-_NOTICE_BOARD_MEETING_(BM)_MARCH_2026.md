NOTICE OF Q4 2025 BOARD OF DIRECTORS’ MEETING
Notice is hereby given that a meeting of the Board of Directors (the “Board”) of Chemical
and Allied Products PLC (the “Company”) will be held on Wednesday, March 25, 2026 at
10:00am at 2, Adeniyi Jones Avenue, Ikeja Lagos and hosted virtually via Teams
conferencing platform.
The meeting is the regular quarterly meeting of the Board to consider and approve (among
other things) the Company's Audited Financial Statements for the financial year ended
December 31, 2025 (“Audited FYE 2025 Financial Statements”).
The previously announced closed period prohibiting trading in the shares of the Company
by insiders remains unchanged until twenty-four (24) hours after the release of Audited FYE
2025 Financial Statements via NGX’s Issuers’ Portal and by extension, to the general public.
