APPOINTMENT OF MANAGING DIRECTOR/CEO
Benin City, Nigeria
24 November 2026
Presco Plc (the “Company”) hereby notifies the Nigerian Exchange Limited (“NGX”), its shareholders and
other stakeholders that its Board of Directors has approved the appointment of Mr. Gangadhar Shetty as
the Managing Director of the Company, with effect from 1st January 2027.
Profile of Mr. Shetty
Mr. Shetty brings over 29 years’ experience in agribusiness transformation, profit centre management,
global trade and operations leadership across West Africa and Asia. Until his current appointment, he
served as Managing Director of Ghana Oil Palm Development Company (GOPDC), a wholly owned
subsidiary of Presco Plc, having joined GOPDC as Chief Commercial Officer before progressing to Chief
Executive Officer.
Under his leadership, GOPDC developed into an integrated agribusiness operation having a workforce of
more than 4,000 employees. His experience spans commercial and operational excellence, supply chain
management, international trade, cost efficiency, governance and organisational transformation.
Mr. Shetty is a National Executive Committee Member of the Oil Palm Development Association of Ghana
and a Board Member of the Tree Crop Development Authority of Ghana and the Association of Ghana
Industries (Eastern and Volta Regions).
He holds a MBA with a specialization in Marketing/Marketing Management, and has attended functional
and executive leadership programmes. He is an alumnus of the Lagos Business School, where he
completed the Chief Executive Programme.
Board Statement
The Board warmly welcomes Mr. Shetty to his new role and is confident that his extensive industry
experience and leadership will support the Company’s next phase of growth, operational excellence and
long-term shareholder value creation.
Leadership Transition
The Company also notifies NGX, its shareholders and other stakeholders that Mr. Reji George has
resigned as Managing Director/Chief Executive Officer of the Company and as a member of the Board, with
effect from 31 December 2026.
The Board expresses its sincere appreciation to Mr. George for his significant contributions and dedicated
service during his tenure and wishes him continued success in his future endeavours.
By Order of the Board
