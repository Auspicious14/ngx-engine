Corporate Actions Announcement
UNITED CAPITAL PLC hereby announces as follows:
Period Ended June 30, 2026
An interim dividend of Thirty Kobo (N0.30) per 50 kobo ordinary share,
subject to appropriate withholding tax and approval, will be paid to
Proposed shareholders whose names appear in the Register of Members as at the
Dividend close of business on Monday, July 27, 2026.
Closure of The Register of Shareholders will be closed from July 28, 2026, to July 29,
Register 2026 (both dates inclusive).
Qualification Monday, July 27, 2026
Date (Interim
Dividend)
On Thursday, July 30, 2026, dividends will be paid electronically to
shareholders whose names appear on the Register of Members as at
July 27, 2026, and who have completed the e-dividend registration and
mandated the Registrar to pay their dividends directly into their Bank
Payment Date accounts.
Unclaimed
Dividend Shareholders with dividend warrants and share certificates that have
Warrants and remained unclaimed or are yet to be presented for payment or
Share returned for validation are advised to complete the e-dividend
Certificates registration or contact the Registrar.
