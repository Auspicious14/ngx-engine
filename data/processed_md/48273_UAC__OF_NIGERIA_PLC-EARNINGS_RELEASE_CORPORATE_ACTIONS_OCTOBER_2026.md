Financial Results for the third quarter and nine months ended 30 September 2026
30 October 2026 – UAC of Nigeria PLC (“UAC”) announced its results for the period ended 30 September 2026.
9M 2026 Highlights
• Revenue grew 228% to ₦523bn, gross profit increased 262% to ₦143bn, and operating profit was 344% higher at
₦60bn. Profit before tax of ₦38bn was 263% higher than prior year PBT of ₦10bn. 2026 is the first full year of
consolidating C.H.I. Limited.
Q3 2026 Highlights
• ₦158bn revenue, 221% higher than Q3 2025, driven by the contribution of the Packaged Food and Beverages
segment (+655%), and topline growth in the Paints segment (+10.8%).
• ₦38bn gross profit, 3.4x Q3 2025 (₦11bn). Gross margin expanded 143bps to 24.0%, reflecting earnings mix (greater
contribution from the higher-margin Packaged Food and Beverages segment) and moderating input prices.
• ₦10.6bn operating profit, 12.8x Q3 2025 (₦824mn). EBIT margin expanded 501bps to 6.7%, reflecting gross margin
expansion.
• Profit before tax of ₦3.2bn compared to a loss before tax of ₦703mn in Q3 2025.
Commenting on the results, Group Managing Director, Fola Aiyesimoju, stated: “Our results for the first nine months of
2026 reflect continued progress against our objectives. Group revenue grew 3.3x to ₦523bn, operating profit 4.4x to
₦60bn and profit before tax 3.6x to ₦38bn, driven by our Packaged Food and Beverages and Paints businesses. Strong
cash generation, supported by working capital release, delivered free cash flow of ₦98bn. During the quarter, we entered
into agreements to sell our shareholdings in Livestock Feeds PLC and DP World Logistics Limited, subject to regulatory
approvals. We remain focused on implementing our plan to drive value creation, including the completion of the
announced divestments and continued deleveraging.”

Group Performance and Financial Review
Revenue in Q3 2026 increased by 221% YoY to ₦158bn, driven by topline growth in the Paints, Packaged Food and
Beverages and Quick Service Restaurants segments, which more than offset revenue decline recorded in the Edibles and
Feed segment.
Gross profit increased by 242% to ₦38bn. Gross profit margin improved 143bps to 24%, reflecting earnings mix (greater
contribution from the higher-margin Packaged Food and Beverages segment) and moderating input costs. Operating
profit grew 12.8x to ₦10.6bn in Q3 2026 (Q3 2025: ₦824mn). The increase in profitability reflects gross margin expansion.
The Group’s opex/sales ratio improved by 368bps to 18.6% in Q3 2026 from 22.3% in Q3 2025.
Net finance cost of ₦8.1bn in Q3 2026 compared to ₦2.1bn in Q3 2025, reflecting higher borrowings following the C.H.I.
acquisition. Share of profit from associate companies of ₦760mn compared to ₦598mn in Q3 2025 mainly driven by
topline growth at DP World Logistics (formerly called MDS Logistics Limited).
Profit before tax was ₦3.2bn compared to loss before tax of ₦703mn in Q3 2025. Total profit for the period was ₦1.9bn
compared to a net loss of ₦2.0bn in Q3 2025.
Earnings per share of 63 Kobo in Q3 2026 compared to loss per share of 59 Kobo recorded in Q3 2025. Annualised Return
on Invested Capital (ROIC) was 18.4% as at 9M 2026 (9M 2025: 20.7%).
Cash flow, debt and liquidity
Free cash flow was ₦98.4bn in 9M 2026 (9M 2025: ₦18.5bn). The increase reflects higher operating profit and a ₦33.4bn
working capital release, as reductions of ₦51.8bn in inventories and ₦4.7bn in receivables more than offset a ₦23.3bn
reduction in payables, reflecting the Group’s focus on optimising working capital. Cash conversion cycle improved by 15
days to 96 days, driven by a 26-day reduction in inventory days to 110 days partly offset by a reduction in payable days.
Net debt reduced by ₦62.8bn to ₦231bn and gross borrowings by ₦82.2bn to ₦263bn over the nine-month period. The
reduction in gross borrowings includes exchange gains (₦11bn) and the reclassification of Livestock Feeds’ borrowings to
held for sale (₦7.9bn). Long-term debt-to-LTM EBITDA ratio improved to 1.5x as at 30 September 2026.

Packaged Food and Beverages
₦129bn revenue, 655% higher YoY compared to ₦17bn in Q3 2025. Performance was supported by the consolidation of
C.H.I. and volume growth at UAC Foods Limited. Operating profit of ₦10bn in Q3 2026 grew 5.6x compared to ₦1.8bn in
Q3 2025. Profit before tax of ₦4.1bn was 114% higher than ₦1.9bn recorded in Q3 2025.
Paints
Segment revenue was ₦11.3bn, 10.8% higher YoY compared to ₦10.2bn recorded in Q3 2025, driven by 21% volume
growth, supported by the decorative range and the relaunch of Sandtex Select in August 2026. Operating profit of ₦2.0bn
was 23.0% higher compared to ₦1.6bn in Q3 2025, as higher gross profit and lower marketing expenses more than offset
higher selling expenses. Profit before tax was ₦2.2bn in Q3 2026, 27.2% higher compared to ₦1.7bn recorded in Q3 2025.
Edibles and Feed
Revenue declined 19.1% YoY to ₦17.4bn in Q3 2026 (Q3 2025: ₦21.4bn) reflecting lower finished-goods prices across the
agricultural commodities value chain relative to the prior year. The segment recorded an operating profit of ₦65mn in
Q3 2026, against an operating loss of ₦570mn in Q3 2025. Improved performance compared to prior year was driven by
lower input prices and gross margin expansion. Loss before tax narrowed to ₦1.3bn in Q3 2026 from ₦1.9bn in Q3 2025,
reflecting improvement in operating performance.
Portfolio update: On 30 September 2026, UAC entered into an agreement to sell its 73.29% shareholding in Livestock
Feeds PLC (LSF), subject to regulatory approvals. Accordingly, LSF’s assets and liabilities have been reclassified as a
disposal group held for sale in UAC’s Statement of Financial Position (SOFP). LSF’s results continue to be consolidated in
the Group’s Statement of Profit or Loss until completion. The Group’s inventory, gross borrowings and net debt as at 30
September 2026 exclude LSF balances of ₦8bn, ₦7.9bn and ₦6.9bn respectively, which are presented within the disposal
group held for sale.
Quick Service Restaurants (QSR)
Revenue grew 3.6% YoY to ₦667mn (Q3 2025: ₦644mn) driven by improved sales performance from stores. The
segment’s operating loss narrowed to ₦53mn (Q3 2025: ₦217mn), reflecting gross margin improvement and
cost-efficiency initiatives. Loss before tax reduced to ₦213mn, from ₦371mn in Q3 2025.
Associate company update: DP World Logistics Limited
On 24 September 2026, UAC announced that it had entered into an agreement to sell its 43% shareholding in DP World
Logistics Limited (formerly MDS Logistics Limited), subject to regulatory approval. Consequently, UAC’s investment in
DP World Logistics has been reclassified to non-current assets held for sale in the SOFP.

About UAC
UAC of Nigeria PLC (UAC) is a holding company with subsidiary and associate companies operating in the Packaged Food
and Beverages, Paints, Edibles and Feed, Quick Service Restaurants, Logistics and Real Estate segments. UAC has played
a prominent role in Nigeria’s development for over a century. The company is focused on building its businesses into
leaders in their chosen segments.
1. UAC has four operating platforms
Packaged Food and Beverages
• C.H.I. Limited (100% ownership) – a leading FMCG company with three distinct business segments – drinking
yoghurt, juice, nectar and still drinks, and snacks. The company operates brands such as Chivita, Hollandia, Capri-
Sun, Beefie and SuperBite. C.H.I. was consolidated into the UAC Group in Q4 2025 following completion of the
acquisition in October 2025.
• UAC Foods Limited (99.96% ownership) – a leading player in the packaged food and beverages industry with
three distinct business segments – snacks, ice-cream, and spring water. It owns iconic brands such as Gala, SWAN
Spring Water, Supreme and Funtime.
Paints
• Chemical and Allied Products PLC (57.9% ownership) – the leading paints and coatings company in Nigeria with
a diversified product range spanning decorative and marine segments. CAP PLC is the sole technology licensee
for AkzoNobel’s decorative range in Nigeria and is also a distributor for Hempel’s industrial products. The
company’s brand portfolio comprises Dulux, Sandtex, Caplux, and Hempel. The company benefits from a unique
distribution model - franchised retail outlets, which it pioneered in Nigeria’s paint industry. CAP PLC is listed on
NGX.
Animal Feeds and Other Edibles
• Grand Cereals Limited (71.4% ownership) – a leading producer of cereals, edible oils, poultry feed, fish feed,
ruminant feed and dog food. The company has production and distribution facilities in Northern and South
Eastern Nigeria. It owns a portfolio of strong brands including Grand, Vital, and BestMate.
• Livestock Feeds PLC (73.29% ownership) – produces and distributes poultry feed, feed concentrates and full fat
soya. The company recently expanded its offering to include veterinary drugs. Livestock Feeds’ geographic
strength is in South West Nigeria. The company is listed on The Nigerian Exchange (“NGX”).
Quick Service Restaurants
• UAC Restaurants Limited (51% ownership) – a joint venture with Famous Brands, manages the network of Quick
Service Restaurants across Nigeria under the Mr Bigg’s and Debonairs Pizza brands.
2. UAC owns minority stakes in Logistics and Real Estate businesses
Logistics: DP World Logistics Limited (43% ownership) – a leading logistics provider in Nigeria, offers the complete suite
of outbound logistics and supply chain services including Warehousing, Haulage and Distribution.
Real Estate: UPDC PLC (41.3% ownership) – a leading property development and management company quoted on NGX.
