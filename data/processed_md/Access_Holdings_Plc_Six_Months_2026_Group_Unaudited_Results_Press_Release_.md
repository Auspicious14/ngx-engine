Lagos, Nigeria – November 2, 2026
Group Unaudited Results for the Six Months Ended 30 June 2026
Access Holdings Plc (“the Group”) today announced its unaudited results for the six
months ended 30 June 2026, marking a further step in its evolution from a platform
defined by scale to one increasingly focused on value creation, efficiency and earnings
quality.
The Group delivered resilient performance in a period shaped by transition, reflecting
the strength of its franchise.
Commenting on the results, the Group Managing Director/Chief Executive Officer,
Innocent C. Ike, stated:
“Our half-year results reflect the progress we are making in translating the strength and scale
of Access Holdings into sustainable value for shareholders.
Our expansion over the years has been deliberate, creating a diversified financial services
group with a strong balance sheet, an extensive African and international network and
capabilities to serve customers across the markets in which we operate.
With this foundation firmly established, our focus is increasingly on unlocking the full potential
of our franchise by delivering stronger business performance, enhanced customer value and
sustainable long-term returns for our shareholders.”
Financial Highlights
During the period, the Group reported Profit Before Tax of ₦414.1 billion, compared to
₦320.6 billion in H1 2025. This double-digit growth of 29.2% was driven by a 70.0%
reduction in the impairment charge and a 51.1% increase in net fee and commissions.

Balance Sheet Strength and Growth
The Group’s balance sheet has continued to demonstrate resilience, supported by
customer confidence across the markets in which it operates.
1. Total assets grew by 6.8% to ₦55.2 trillion as at 30 June 2026, from ₦51.7
trillion as at 31 December 2025.
2. Customer deposits increased by 7.9% to ₦37.3 trillion as at 30 June 2026, from
₦34.6 trillion as at 31 December 2025.
3. Loans and advances to customers grew by 5.9% to ₦14.1 trillion as at 30 June
2026, from ₦13.3 trillion as at 31 December 2025.
4. Shareholders’ Funds grew by 2.0% to ₦4.57 trillion as at 30 June 2026, from
₦4.48 trillion as at 31 December 2025.
Group Performance Metrics
The Group’s Cost-to-Income Ratio (CIR) increased to 65.2% in H1 2026 from 51.7%
in FY 2025, due to higher personnel expenses as the Group continued to invest in
talent and growth initiatives across its markets. Other operating expense lines
recorded more moderate growth during the period.
The Group remains focused on cost optimisation leveraging technology, automation
and process improvements to reduce the cost to serve, improve efficiency and support
long-term profitability.
The Group’s annualised Return on Average Equity (ROAE) closed at 18.3% pre-tax
and 11.8% post-tax in H1 2026, down from 24.4% and 18.4% in FY 2025.
To improve ROAE, the Group is focused on growing interest income, expanding its
low-cost deposit base, and increasing fee and commission income and digital
revenues. It is also working to contain the cost-to-income ratio and optimise capital
through disciplined deployment.
Business Portfolio Performance Highlights
Access Banking Group: Unlocking the Value of Our Pan-African and
International Banking Franchise
Access Bank, the Group’s flagship subsidiary, remained the principal earnings
contributor, accounting for 96.8% of Group Profit Before Tax (PBT) in H1 2026.
The Group’s deliberate and sustained investments across rest of Africa and
international markets continue to demonstrate their strategic and economic value,
contributing 79.8% of Banking Group PBT in H1 2026. This underscores the increasing
significance of our international franchise as a driver of earnings diversification,
resilience and long-term value creation.

Beyond their earnings contribution, our subsidiaries across Africa and international
markets form an interconnected banking network that positions the Group to capture
opportunities arising from growing intra-African trade, regional economic integration
and expanding commercial relationships between Africa and global markets.
Through this network, we are strengthening our ability to facilitate cross-border trade,
support multinational and regional businesses, mobilise deposits and liquidity, and
provide integrated trade finance, payments, treasury and cash management solutions
across key trade corridors.
The continued development of the African Continental Free Trade Area (AfCFTA),
alongside payment infrastructure such as the Pan-African Payment and Settlement
System (PAPSS), presents further opportunities to deepen financial connectivity,
improve cross-border payment efficiency and support the participation of African
businesses in regional and global value chains. Our subsidiary network provides a
platform to translate these opportunities into increased transaction flows, deeper
customer relationships and recurring revenue streams, while supporting broader
economic activity, enterprise growth and financial inclusion.
In line with our Scale-to-Value strategy, our focus is increasingly on translating
geographic reach into measurable economic returns. This includes deepening
integration across our banking network, expanding cross-border business
opportunities, strengthening operating efficiency and directing capital towards markets
and businesses offering attractive risk-adjusted returns.
Our investment proposition is therefore anchored not simply on the scale of our
international presence, but on our ability to convert that scale into sustainable
earnings, greater capital productivity, enhanced economic connectivity and recurring
long-term shareholder value.
Non-Banking Businesses
The Group’s non-banking businesses continued to demonstrate the value of portfolio
diversification, combining established, capital-light businesses with growth
opportunities in asset management, lending, insurance brokerage and payments.
Access Pensions has assets under management (AUM) of over ₦4.6 trillion on behalf
of more than two million Retirement Savings Account (RSA) holders, providing a
platform for long-term savings mobilisation, investment management and stable fee-
based income.
Access Pensions and Access Insurance Brokers delivered strong annualised returns
on equity of 57.8% and 114.2%, respectively, supported by cost-to-income ratios of
36.9% and 23.1%, respectively. These results highlight the earnings potential and
operating efficiency of businesses that generate attractive returns with relatively
modest capital requirements.

Hydrogen Payments and Oxygen X Finance are advancing Access Holdings' strategy
to democratise access to financial services through technology, digital innovation and
scalable financial solutions.
In H1 2026, Hydrogen processed transactions worth ₦86 trillion, with total assets of
₦84 billion, demonstrating the scale of its digital payments infrastructure. Oxygen X
originated approximately 12,000 loans valued at ₦20 billion, with total assets of ₦35.2
billion, expanding access to credit through technology-driven lending.
Together, the two businesses strengthen the Group's digital financial ecosystem,
deepen financial inclusion and create opportunities for recurring income, business
diversification and sustainable shareholder value.
The focus remains on strengthening the contribution of established businesses while
supporting the development of scalable platforms, with the broader objective of
improving earnings quality and sustainable returns for shareholders.
Progress on BOFIA Section 19(8)(c) Compliance
The Group has made significant progress towards achieving compliance with BOFIA
Section 19(8)(c), which limits the Bank's investment in subsidiaries to 10% of
shareholders' funds.
The Group has identified capital recalls from six banking subsidiaries which, when
completed, are expected to bring the Bank's investment-in-subsidiaries ratio within the
regulatory threshold. The recall process has already been completed in one
subsidiary, while shareholder approvals have been obtained in four of the remaining
five subsidiaries. Regulatory approvals are progressing across the five remaining
subsidiaries.
The Group expects to complete the programme within the CBN's prescribed
compliance timeline. Its completion is expected to remove the regulatory constraint on
dividend distributions.
Outlook for Full Year 2026 and Beyond
As we progress through the remainder of 2026, our focus remains on sharpening
execution of our Scale-to-Value agenda, with clear priorities around capital allocation,
regulatory compliance, cost and operational efficiency and higher returns across our
businesses, translating the scale of our franchise into stronger financial performance
and sustainable long-term shareholder value.
Across the Group, we see meaningful opportunities to deepen customer relationships,
support trade across Africa and improve capital productivity.
While the operating environment continues to evolve, we continue to strengthen
capital productivity, improve earnings quality, deepen collaboration across the Group
and maintain a prudent approach to risk and balance sheet management.

About Access Holdings Plc
Africa’s leading banking-led financial services group by total assets, with Access Bank
at the heart of its franchise and the principal driver of its scale, earnings and
international presence.
The Group combines its extensive banking operations across Africa and key global
financial centres with complementary businesses in payments, pensions, consumer
finance and insurance brokerage.
