CORNERSTONE PLC AND AIICO INSURANCE PLC ANNOUNCE PROPOSED
MERGER
LAGOS, NIGERIA – 23 November 2026 – Cornerstone Plc (“Cornerstone”) (NGX: CORNERST) and AIICO
Insurance Plc (NGX: AIICO) wishes to notify the Nigerian Exchange Limited (“NGX”), its shareholders
and the investing public of a proposed merger of Cornerstone, AIICO and its two insurance
subsidiaries. The Boards of Directors of both companies have approved the proposal in principle and
will recommend it to their respective shareholders for approval.
The merger would bring together two complementary insurance businesses, with combined
shareholders’ funds of approximately ₦194 billion and total assets of approximately ₦858 billion. On
completion, the merged company will remain listed on Nigerian Exchange Limited.
Transaction highlights
• Scale: The combined group is expected to be Nigeria’s leading listed insurance company by
gross written premium, insurance revenue, and capital.
• Capital and underwriting capacity: The larger capital base would support the underwriting of
larger risks and the retention of more business.
• Complementary capabilities: AIICO’s businesses in life insurance, annuities, and retail
distribution would complement Cornerstone’s strength in general insurance and its growing
takaful business.
• Broader product range and reach: The merger would bring together life, general, health, and
Takaful products, supported by a larger service network.
• Capacity to invest: The combined group would have greater scale to invest in digitalisation
and product development.
Strategic rationale
The proposed merger brings together complementary businesses and capabilities. AIICO contributes
established positions in general and life insurance, annuities, and retail distribution. Cornerstone adds
strength in general insurance and a growing Takaful business. Together, they would create a more
diversified insurer with a broader product range, wider distribution, and greater scale to serve
customers across the market.
That scale matters in an industry facing higher minimum capital requirements and a risk-based capital
framework under NIIRA. With combined shareholders’ funds of approximately ₦194 billion and total
assets of approximately ₦858 billion, the enlarged group would have a stronger capital base to support
larger underwriting commitments, retain more business, and invest in digitalisation and product
development.
Insurance premiums remain a small share of Nigeria’s economy, indicating significant scope to extend
insurance to more individuals and businesses. By combining their capital, distribution, and expertise,
AIICO and Cornerstone aim to build an insurer better equipped to serve a wider range of needs and
participate in the market’s long-term growth.

Structure of the merger
The merger is expected to be implemented through a Scheme of Merger. The Scheme document will
set out the full terms of the transaction, including the share exchange ratio, and will be sent to
shareholders.
Approvals and next steps
The merger is subject to the approval of the National Insurance Commission (“NAICOM”), the Federal
Competition and Consumer Protection Commission (“FCCPC”), the Securities and Exchange
Commission (“SEC”) and Nigerian Exchange Limited (“NGX”); approval by the shareholders of each
company at their respective Court-Ordered Meetings; and sanction by the Federal High Court.
Customers, partners, and employees
All Cornerstone and AIICO policies and claims remain valid. Reinsurance companies, brokers, agents,
bancassurance partners, and other distribution partners have contributed to the success of both
companies and will remain important to the enlarged group. The group will build on these
relationships, supported by a broader product range and greater underwriting capacity.
The enlarged group will build on the heritage, relationships, and expertise of both companies. Its
greater scale and broader capabilities are expected to create opportunities for employees to develop
their careers.
About Cornerstone Plc
Cornerstone Plc was incorporated in 1991 and became a public limited liability company in 1997. It
has recently completed its restructuring into a holding company, with subsidiaries providing general,
life and takaful insurance to individual and corporate customers across Nigeria.
