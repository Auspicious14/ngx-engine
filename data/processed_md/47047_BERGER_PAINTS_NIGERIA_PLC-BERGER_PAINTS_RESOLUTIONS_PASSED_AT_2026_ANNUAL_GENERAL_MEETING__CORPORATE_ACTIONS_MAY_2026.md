Lagos, 22 May 2026.
RESOLUTIONS PASSED AT THE 2026 AGM OF BERGER PAINTS NIGERIA PLC
At the 66th Annual General Meeting of Berger Paints Nigeria Plc (the Company) held virtually on
Thursday, 21 May, 2026 at 11.00 a.m., the following resolutions were duly passed:
Ordinary Business:
1. Resolution on Report and Accounts: The Reports of the Directors, Auditors, Board
Appraisers, and Audit Committee together with the Audited Accounts for the financial year
ended December 31 2025, were laid at the meeting and received by the shareholders.
2. Resolution on Declaration of Dividend: A final dividend payment of ₦1.25kobo per each 50
kobo ordinary share held by all members whose names appeared in the Company’s
Register of Members as of the close of business on 23 April 2026, subject to deduction of
the appropriate withholding tax at the time of payment, as recommended by the Board
was unanimously approved.
3. Resolution on Re-Election of Directors Retiring by Rotation: Mrs. Erejuwa Gbadebo
(Independent Non-Executive Director) and Dr. Aisha Umar (Independent Non-Executive
Director) retired by rotation and were re-elected as Directors.
4. Resolution on Authorisation of Directors to Fix the Remuneration of Auditors: It was
resolved that the Directors are authorised to fix the remuneration and expenses of the
Company’s External Auditors.
5. Disclosure on the Remuneration of Managers of the Company: The remuneration of the
Managers of the business for the 2025 financial year was disclosed on page 31 of the
Annual Reports and Accounts as being ₦280,432,513.33. No resolution was required to be
taken on this item.
6. Resolution on the Election of the Members of the Audit Committee: There were three (3)
shareholder nominees for election to the Statutory Audit Committee. Subsequently, the
three shareholders’ representatives were re-elected and they are:
i. Mr. Chibuzor Eke.
ii. Mrs. Mary Joke Shofolahan.
iii. Mr. Taiwo Afinju.
The following two (2) Directors were appointed to represent the Board on the Committee:
iv. Dr. Ogechi Iheanacho.
v. Mr. Kunle Olowokande.

Special Business
7. Resolution on Director’s Retirement benefits – It was resolved that the sum of
₦37,030,000.00 (Thirty-Seven Million, Thirty Thousand Naira) be paid to Mr. Raj Mangtani,
as retirement benefits having retired from the Board.
8. Renewal of General Mandate on Recurring Related Party Transactions – The Shareholders
approved the renewal of the General Mandate granted to the Company in respect of all
recurrent related party transactions of a revenue or trading nature or those necessary for
the Company’s day-to-day operations, in compliance with the Rules of the Nigerian
Exchange Limited (NGX) governing transactions with related parties or interested persons.
The renewed Mandate shall take effect from the date this resolution is passed and shall
remain in force until the conclusion of the Company’s next Annual General Meeting.
Thank you.
