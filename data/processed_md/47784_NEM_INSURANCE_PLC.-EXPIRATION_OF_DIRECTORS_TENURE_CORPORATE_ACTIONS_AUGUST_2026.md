Lagos Nigeria,
6th August 2026
NOTIFICATION OF EXPIRATION OF DIRECTOR’S TENURE
NEM Insurance Plc (the Company) wishes to notify Nigerian Exchange Limited (“NGX”) and the
investing public that the tenure of Mrs. Joy Teluwo, a Non-Executive Director of the Company, expired
on the 29th of July 2026.
Accordingly, Mrs. Joy Teluwo has ceased to serve on the Board of the Company with effect from the
29th of July 2026.
The Board of Directors expresses its appreciation to her for her valuable contributions to the Company
during her tenure and wishes her success in her future endeavors.
This notification is made in compliance with the applicable post-listing disclosure requirements of NGX.
