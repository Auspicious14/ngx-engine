August 28, 2026
Lagos, Nigeria
APPROVAL OF THE GROUP’s AUDITED INTERIM CONSOLIDATED AND
SEPARATE FINANCIAL STATEMENTS FOR THE HALF-YEAR ENDED JUNE 30,
2026
This is to inform the Nigerian Exchange Limited (‘The Exchange’) and the investing
public that the Board of Directors of Access Holdings Plc (‘the Company’) met on
Thursday, August 27, 2026, and considered and approved the Group’s Audited Interim
Financial Statements for the Half-Year Ended June 30, 2026 (‘the Results’), subject to
the approval of the Central Bank of Nigeria (‘CBN’).
The announcement of the Results shall be made upon the Company’s receipt of the
CBN’s approval.
In line with The Exchange’s Issuers’ Rules, the Company will remain in a Closed
Period until 24 hours after the Results are released to the public. Accordingly, no
insider and their connected persons may, directly or indirectly, deal in the securities of
the Company in any manner during the Closed Period.
BY ORDER OF THE BOARD
SUNDAY EKWOCHI
COMPANY SECRETARY
