Lagos | Nigeria: 30 April 2026
MTN NIGERIA RELEASES 2025 SUSTAINABILITY REPORT, CHARTING PROGRESS
ON RESILIENT GROWTH AND RESPONSIBLE IMPACT
…Sustained ESG Performance Across Core Metrics
MTN Nigeria Communications Plc ("MTN Nigeria" or the "Company"), Nigeria's leading
telecommunications provider, announces the release of its 2025 Sustainability
Report. Now in its seventh consecutive year, the Report underscores MTN Nigeria's
continued commitment to sustainability, transparency, and shared value creation
through strategic ESG integration and responsible business practices.
This edition marks MTN Nigeria's third consecutive year as a voluntary adopter of the
IFRS Sustainability Disclosure Standards (IFRS S1 and S2), well ahead of the
mandatory compliance deadline and aligns with key global and local sustainability
reporting frameworks.
"This report is a record of decisive action and measurable progress as we deepen our
commitment to sustainable, long-term value creation for all our stakeholders," said
Dr. Karl Toriola, Chief Executive Officer. "Sustainability shapes how we grow, manage
risk, and unlock opportunities. We are making tangible progress across every
dimension of our business."

"Together, we are Doing for Tomorrow, Today," said Tobechukwu Okigbo, Chief
Corporate Services and Sustainability Officer. "During the year, we conducted a True
Value Assessment of our social, economic, and environmental impacts from 2021 to

integration, empowering our people, and delivering measurable, net-positive

About MTN Nigeria
MTN Nigeria is one of Africa’s largest providers of communications services,
connecting over 87 million people in communities across the country with each other
and the world. Guided by a belief that everybody deserves the benefits of a modern
connected life, MTN Nigeria’s leadership position in coverage, capacity, and
innovation has remained constant since its launch in 2001. MTN Nigeria is part of the
MTN Group - a multinational telecommunications group which operates in 16
countries in Africa and the Middle East, serving over 300 million people.
Visit www.mtn.ng for more information.
