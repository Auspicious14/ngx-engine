Cornerstone Insurance Plc Reports Insurance Revenue of ₦43.31 Billion for Q3 2026, Up
16.7% Year-on-Year
LAGOS, NIGERIA – 29 October 2026 – Cornerstone Insurance Plc (NGX: CORNERST) ("Cornerstone" or
the "Group") today announced its Unaudited Financial Results for the nine months ended 30
September 2026, reporting continued growth in insurance revenue and a strengthened capital base,
supported by resilient underwriting performance across its life and non-life businesses.
Performance Summary
The Group delivered a solid operating performance in the third quarter of 2026, underpinned by
continued growth across its core insurance businesses. Insurance revenue increased by 16.7% to
₦43.31 billion, while insurance service result rose by 8.09% to ₦11.75 billion, reflecting higher business
volumes and continued underwriting discipline. The Group maintained a strong financial position, with
total assets increasing to ₦149.14 billion and shareholders' funds rising by 10.3% from year-end 2025.
Financial Performance Highlights
Earnings Analysis
 Insurance revenue increased 16.7% to ₦43.31 billion in the third quarter of 2026, from ₦24.47
billion in the corresponding period of 2025. The growth was driven by increased contributions
across the Group's life and non-life insurance businesses, reflecting sustained demand from
policyholders and business partners.
 Insurance service result increased by 8.09% to ₦11.75 billion, compared with ₦10.87 billion in
Q3 2025, reflecting improved underwriting performance supported by higher insurance
revenue.
 Net insurance and investment result, which combines underwriting performance with returns
on the Group's investment portfolio, declined by 4.4% to ₦13.50 billion from ₦14.12 billion in
the prior-year period. This reflected higher net foreign exchange losses and lower fair value
gains during the period against a strong comparative base in Q3 2025.
 Profit before tax was ₦7.91 billion, compared with ₦8.32 billion in Q3 2025, while profit after
tax declined to ₦7.41 billion from ₦8.10 billion in the corresponding period.
 Earnings per share was 41 kobo, compared with 45 kobo in Q3 2025.
Financial Position
The Group maintained a strong financial position during the period, supported by continued earnings
retention and growth in shareholders' equity.
 Total assets increased by 5.75% to ₦149.14 billion as at 30 September 2026, from ₦141.03
billion at 31 December 2025.
 Shareholders' funds increased by 10.3% to ₦80.36 billion from ₦72.86 billion at year-end
2025, reflecting retained earnings during the period and favourable fair value movements on
the Group's investment portfolio.
Outlook
Building on its solid operating performance in the third quarter of 2026, Cornerstone remains focused
on deepening its underwriting franchise, strengthening its capital position and implementing its new
group structure, separating the Group's life and general insurance businesses in line with regulatory
requirements. The Board and Management remain committed to delivering excellent service and
prompt claims settlement for policyholders and sustainable long-term value for shareholders.
