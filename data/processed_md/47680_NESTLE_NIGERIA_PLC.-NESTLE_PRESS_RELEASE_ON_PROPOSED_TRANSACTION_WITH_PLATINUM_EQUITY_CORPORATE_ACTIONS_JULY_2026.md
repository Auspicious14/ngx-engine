Press Release
Lagos, Nigeria, July 30, 2026
This announcement is made by Nestlé Nigeria PLC (Nestlé Nigeria) to its shareholders, The
Nigerian Exchange Limited and the investing public pursuant to Rule 17.6 of the Rulebook of The
Exchange, 2015, (Issuers’ Rules). The board of Nestle Nigeria hereby issues this notification for
the benefit of the relevant stakeholder and the general public.
Nestlé and Platinum Equity to create Peranel, a leading player in water and premium
beverages
Nestlé Nigeria’s majority shareholder, Nestlé S.A. (Nestlé), and Platinum Equity, have entered
into an exclusive arrangement to create Peranel, a 50/50 joint venture for Nestlé’s waters and
premium beverages business (The Transaction).
The new company is expected to bring together a portfolio of more than 30 brands sold in 120
countries, creating a dedicated business with the scale, focus and expertise to support growth
in this dynamic category. The portfolio includes iconic natural mineral water brands such as San
Pellegrino, Source Perrier and Acqua Panna, alongside premium and functional hydration
beverages, the global Nestlé Pure Life brand and other leading local water brands. Expected to
be headquartered in Paris, Peranel is expected to be led by an experienced management team
under Muriel Lienau, the current CEO of the business.
In Nigeria, subject to any required local approvals, the transaction will involve the transfer of
Nestlé Nigeria's existing waters business to become part of the new joint venture for adequate
consideration.
The Transaction will be subject to employee consultation processes, applicable regulatory
approvals and ratification by the shareholders, and is expected to close in H1 2027.
The proposed Transaction does not affect or alter the shareholding of Nestlé in Nestlé Nigeria.
Nestlé Nigeria will make further announcements in relation to the transaction, where required.
