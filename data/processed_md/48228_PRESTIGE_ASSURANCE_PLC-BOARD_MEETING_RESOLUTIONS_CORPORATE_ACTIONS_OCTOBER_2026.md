Lagos, 29 October 2026.
RESOLUTIONS PASSED AT THE BOARD MEETING OF PRESTIGE ASSURANCE PLC
At the Board of Directors’ meeting of Prestige Assurance Plc (‘the Company’), which held

Statements for the Third Quarter ended September 30, 2026.

on October 1, 2026, will subsist until twenty-four (24) hours after the release of the
Unaudited Financial Statements for the quarter ended September 30, 2026.
