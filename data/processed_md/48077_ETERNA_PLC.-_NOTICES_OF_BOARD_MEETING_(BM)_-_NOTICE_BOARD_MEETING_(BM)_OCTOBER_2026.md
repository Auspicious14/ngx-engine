Lagos, Nigeria, 13th October 2026
ETERNA PLC - NOTICE OF BOARD MEETING
Notice is hereby given that a meeting of the Board of Directors of Eterna Plc (the
Company) will be held on Monday, 26th October 2026 to consider the Company’s
Third Quarter Unaudited Financial Statements for the period ended 30th
September 2026 (Q3 2026 UFS) and other items slated for the meeting.
As earlier announced and in compliance with the post listing requirements of
Nigerian Exchange Limited (NGX) for listed companies, the Company’s closed
period which commenced on 1 October 2026 will continue until twenty-four (24)
hours after the Q3 2026 UFS and other price-sensitive information is disclosed to
the public.
Consequently, no insider and their connected persons may directly or indirectly
deal in the securities of the Company during the closed period.
BY ORDER OF THE BOARD
David Edet
Company Secretary/Legal Adviser
