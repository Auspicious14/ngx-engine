Lagos, July 31, 2026
OUTCOME OF THE EMERGENCY MEETING OF THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF VFD GROUP
PLC
This is to notify Nigerian Exchange Limited ("NGX" or "The Exchange") and the investing public that
at the Emergency Meeting of the Board of Directors of VFD Group Plc ("VFD Group" or "the
Company"), held on July 30, 2026, the Board of Directors of VFD Group reviewed and approved the
Unaudited Consolidated and Separate Financial Statements for the six months ended 30 June 2026
(H1 2026). The Board noted the strong profitability performance recorded during the period,
including; consolidated Profit After Tax (PAT) of ₦10.06 billion, representing a 100.8% increase
compared to the corresponding period of 2025; company Profit After Tax (PAT) of ₦5.03 billion,
representing a 328% increase year-on-year.
In view of the Group’s strong earnings performance and commitment to delivering sustainable
shareholder value, the Board approved an interim dividend of 24 kobo (₦0.24) per ordinary share,
amounting to an estimated total dividend payout of approximately ₦3.04 billion.
There being no further business, the meeting was duly concluded.
