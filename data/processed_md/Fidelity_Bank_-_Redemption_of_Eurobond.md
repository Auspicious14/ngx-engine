Lagos, Nigeria
October 29, 2026
Notice of Full Redemption at Maturity of the US$400 Million 7.625% Senior Unsecured Eurobond
Fidelity Bank Plc (“the Bank”) wishes to inform its esteemed shareholders, Nigerian Exchange
Limited (NGX), and other stakeholders of the full redemption of its US$400 million 7.625% Senior
Unsecured Eurobond at maturity on October 28, 2026.
Issued on October 28, 2021, the five-year Eurobond carried a coupon rate of 7.625% per
annum, payable semi-annually. The Bank met all scheduled coupon payments and repaid the
outstanding principal and final coupon in full at maturity, fulfilling its payment obligations under
the terms and conditions of the notes.
The timely redemption reflects the Bank’s disciplined liquidity planning, prudent asset and
liability management, and commitment to meeting its financial obligations.
