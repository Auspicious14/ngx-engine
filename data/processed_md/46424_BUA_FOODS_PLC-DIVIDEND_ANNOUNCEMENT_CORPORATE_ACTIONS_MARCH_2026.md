Corporate Actions Announcement
BUA FOODS PLC hereby announce as follows:
Period Ended 31st December 2025
A Final Dividend of ₦28.00 per ordinary share of 50 Kobo each, subject to appropriate
withholding tax and approval, will be paid to shareholders whose names appear in the Register
of Members as at the close of business on 4th June 2026.
Proposed Dividend
Proposed Bonus NIL
Closure of Register The Register of Shareholders will be closed from 5th June 2026 to 11th June 2026
Qualification Date 4th June 2026
On 15h July 2026, dividends will be paid electronically to shareholders whose names appear
on the Register of Members as of 4th June 2026 and who have completed the e-dividend
registration and mandated the Registrar to pay their dividends directly into their Bank
accounts.
Payment Date
Shareholders who are yet to complete the e-dividend Mandate registration are advised to fill
the Registrar’s E-Dividend Mandate form via https://africaprudential.com/claim-your-
dividend-here, which is also available on the website of the Registrars:
E-Dividend Registration www.africaprudential.com, complete and submit to the Registrar or their respective Banks
Unclaimed Dividend Shareholders with dividend warrants and share certificates that have remained unclaimed, or
Warrants and Sharea re yet to be presented for payment or returned for validation are advised to complete the e-
Certificates dividend registration or contact the Registrar
Date of Annual General The Company’s Annual General Meeting will be held on 15th July 2026 at Transcorp Hilton
Meeting Hotel, Abuja.
