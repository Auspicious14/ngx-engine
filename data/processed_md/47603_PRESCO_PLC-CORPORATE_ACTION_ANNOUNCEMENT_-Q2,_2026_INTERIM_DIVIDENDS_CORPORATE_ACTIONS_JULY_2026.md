PRESCO PLC
CORPORATE ACTION ANNOUNCEMENT
July 29, 2026
Benin City, Nigeria
PRESCO PLC hereby announce as follows:
Period Ended 30 June 2026
An Interim Dividend of N10.00 for every share of 50k held, subject to appropriate
withholding tax and approval will be paid to shareholders whose names appear in the
Proposed Dividend Register of Members as at the close of business on Friday, 14th August 2026.
Proposed Bonus NIL
The Register of Shareholders will be closed from Monday 17th August 2026 to 21st
Closure of Register August 2026 (both dates inclusive).
Qualification Date Friday 14th August 2026
On Thursday, 27th August 2026, Interim dividends will be paid electronically to
shareholders whose names appear on the Register of Members as at Friday 14th
August 2026 and who have completed the e-dividend registration and mandated the
Payment Date Registrar to pay their dividends directly into their Bank accounts.
Shareholders who are yet to complete the e-dividend registration are advised to
download the Registrar’s E-Dividend Mandate Activation Form, which is also
available on the website of the Registrars: https://docuhub3.nibss-
plc.com.ng/edmms/self-service complete and submit to the Registrar or their
E-Dividend Registration respective Banks.
Unclaimed Dividend Shareholders with dividend warrants and share certificates that have remained
Warrants and Share unclaimed, or are yet to be presented for payment or returned for validation are
Certificates advised to complete the e-dividend registration or contact the Registrar.
