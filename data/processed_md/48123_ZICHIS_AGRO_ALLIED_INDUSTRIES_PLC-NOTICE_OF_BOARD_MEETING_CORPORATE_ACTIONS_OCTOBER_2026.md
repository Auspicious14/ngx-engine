October 22, 2026
LAGOS NIGERIA
BOARD MEETING AND PROPOSED ACQUISITION
We wish to inform the general public and Nigerian Exchange Limited (NGX) that Zichis
Agro-Allied Industries Plc (the Company) shall be holding a Board Meeting on Friday, October
23rd 2026, to consider and approve the Company’s Unaudited Interim Financial Statements for
the nine-month period ended 30th September, 2026 (2026 Q3 UFS).
The Board will also consider, among other matters, the commencement of the acquisition
process for a 221-hectare palm plantation belonging to The Amandel Palm Company Limited,
located at Ifon, Ondo State.
The closed period for trading in the Company’s shares which commenced on Thursday, 1
October 2026, will continue until twenty-four (24) hours after the 2026 Q3 UFS is filed with NGX
and the general public.
This is for public notice.
Thank You.
For: Zichis Agro Allied Industries Plc
Company Secretary
Solomon Ogie Itsede
