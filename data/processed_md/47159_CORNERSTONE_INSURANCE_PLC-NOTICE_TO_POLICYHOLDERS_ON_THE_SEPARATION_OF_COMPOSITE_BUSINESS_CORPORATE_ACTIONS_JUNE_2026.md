NOTICE OF INTENTION TO UNBUNDLE/SEPARATE COMPOSITE BUSINESS
OF
CORNERSTONE INSURANCE PLC
Unbundling/Separation of Composite Business
Cornerstone Insurance Plc (“Cornerstone Insurance”) hereby informs policyholders, regulators, stakeholders
and the general public of the intention to unbundle/separate its general insurance and life insurance businesses
pursuant to the provisions of the Nigeria Insurance Industry Reform Act 2025, which has abolished composite
insurance businesses in Nigeria and requires all existing composite insurers to separate their life and non-life
businesses.
Under the terms of the unbundling/separation, the eligible assets and existing policies of the general insurance
business will be transferred to FIN Insurance Company Limited (“FIN Insurance”), an existing subsidiary of
Cornerstone Insurance. The eligible assets and policies of Cornerstone Insurance’s life business will be
transferred to Cornerstone Life Assurance Limited (“Cornerstone Life Assurance”)
The unbundling/separation of Cornerstone Insurance’s composite business will be executed by way of a Scheme
of Arrangement under the provisions of the Companies and Allied Matters Act 2020 (‘’the Scheme"). Following
the implementation of the Scheme, FIN Insurance and Cornerstone Life Assurance will be subsidiaries of
Cornerstone Insurance.
The Scheme is subject to regulatory and shareholder approval. The unbundling/separation process is expected
to be completed in the third quarter of 2026. All Cornerstone Insurance policyholders are also notified that the
terms and conditions of existing policies will remain valid and in force. Further details of the Scheme will be
communicated to shareholders of Cornerstone Insurance, FIN Insurance, and Cornerstone Life Assurance as
regulatory and judicial approvals are obtained.
Policyholders' Inspection of Corporate Information and Scheme Document
Cornerstone Insurance policyholders are invited to inspect the agreement under which the Scheme is proposed
to be implemented, which shall be available for inspection by the policyholders without payment of any fees,
from Friday June 12, 2026 to Friday July 3, 2026 at the principal and branch offices of Cornerstone Insurance on
business days between the hours of 9:00 am to 5:00 pm (this excludes weekends and public holidays).
This notice is published in fulfilment of the requirements of the Nigerian Insurance Industry Reform Act 2025
and is made particularly to the policyholders of Cornerstone Insurance and all applicable regulatory
authorities.
