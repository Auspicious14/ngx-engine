Directorate Change
London and Lagos, 22 July 2026: Airtel Africa plc, a leading provider of
telecommunications and mobile money services, with a presence in 14 countries across
Africa, announces the appointment of Mr. V.K. Viswanathan as an independent non-
executive director with immediate e(cid:431)ect.
Mr. V.K. Viswanathan will also join the Audit and Risk Committee with e(cid:431)ect from the
same date.
Vish Viswanathan brings over four decades of international business and finance
leadership experience, having served among many assignments, first as CFO and Joint
Managing Director of Bosch Limited and later as Managing Director, President and
Country Head of Bosch Group in India. Prior to that, Vish worked as Group Treasurer,
Financial Controller, Head of Mergers and Acquisitions and Audit Head for Asia Pacific at
Hindustan Unilever Ltd and Unilever plc. In both the multinational companies, he led
significant business transformation, growth and complex business restructuring
programmes across multiple jurisdictions.
Vish brings extensive Board and Audit Committee experience, having chaired the Boards
of United Spirits Limited (a Diageo plc subsidiary) and Bosch Limited. He has also chaired
the Audit and Risk Committees of several listed and unlisted Indian and multinational
companies underpinned by deep expertise in business, financial and risk management,
governance and stakeholder oversight gained through varied senior executive and board
leadership roles in global organisations, including the telecoms sector.
Gopal Vittal, Chair, commented: " “I am delighted to welcome Vish to the Airtel Africa
Board. He brings a wealth of global financial, audit and governance experience, and his
extensive board and committee expertise will be a significant asset to the Board and the
Audit and Risk Committee. We look forward to working closely with him.”
