Amended - Corporate Actions Announcement
Transcorp Power Plc hereby announces as follows:
Period Ended December 31, 2025
Proposed Dividend A Final Dividend of Four Naira (N4.00) per ordinary share, subject to
shareholders’ approval and appropriate withholding tax deduction,
will be paid to shareholders whose names appear in the Register of
Members as at the close of business on Monday, April 13, 2026. This,
when combined with the Interim Dividend of One Naira, fifty kobo
(N1.50k) per 50 kobo paid on August 18, 2025, will bring the Total
Dividend for the 2025 financial year to Five Naira, fifty kobo (N5.50k)
per ordinary share.
Closure of Register The Register of Shareholders will be closed from Tuesday April 14,
2026 to Friday, April 17, 2026 (both days inclusive).
Qualification Date Monday, April 13, 2026
Payment Date On Tuesday, April 28, 2026 dividends will be paid electronically to
shareholders whose names appear on the Register of Members as of
Monday, April 13, 2026, and who have completed the e-dividend
registration and mandated the Registrar to pay their dividends
directly into their Bank accounts.
E-Dividend Shareholders who are yet to complete the e-dividend registration are
Registration advised to fill the e-Dividend Mandate Activation Form on the Self-
service | NIBSS Document Management Hub Portal, or download the
form from the website of the Registrars at www.africaprudential.com,
complete and submit same to the Registrar or your respective banks.
Unclaimed Dividend Shareholders with dividend warrants and share certificates that have
Warrants and remained unclaimed or are yet to be presented for payment or
Share Certificates returned for validation are advised to complete the e-dividend
registration or contact the Registrar.
Date of General The Company’s Annual General Meeting will hold at Transcorp Hilton
Meeting Hotel, 1 Aguiyi Ironsi Street, Maitama, Abuja, on Monday, April 27,
2026, at 10.00 a.m.
