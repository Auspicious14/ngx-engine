Lagos, Nigeria, 24th July 2026
NEIMETH INTERNATIONAL PHARMACEUTICALS PLC - NOTICE OF OUTCOME OF BOARD
MEETING
Neimeth International Pharmaceuticals Plc. (the Company) wishes to inform the Nigerian
Exchange Limited (NGX) and the investing public that at the Board of Directors’ meeting held
on Friday, 24th July 2026, the Board approved the Company’s Unaudited Financial Statements
for Q2 ended 30th June 2026 (2026, Q2 UFS) and same will be filed on or before 30th July 2026.
Therefore, the closed period earlier announced on trading in the Company’s shares by Insiders
and connected persons shall remain until twenty-four (24) hours after the 2026 Q2 UFS is
released to the public.
CHINENYE S. ADEKANMBI
Company Secretary
