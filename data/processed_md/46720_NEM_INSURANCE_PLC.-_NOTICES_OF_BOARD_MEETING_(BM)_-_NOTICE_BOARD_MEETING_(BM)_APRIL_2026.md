Lagos, Nigeria
23rd April 2026
NOTICE OF BOARD OF DIRECTORS’ MEETING AND CLOSED TRADING PERIOD FOR
NEM INSURANCE PLC
This is to notify Nigerian Exchange Limited (The Exchange), our stakeholders and the
investing public that a meeting of the Board of Directors’ of NEM Insurance Plc (the Company)
is scheduled to be held on Wednesday 29th April 2026, to consider amongst others, the
Unaudited Accounts and Financial Statements for the period ended March 31st, 2026 (2026
Q1 UFS).
Consequently, in compliance with the Rule Book of The Exchange, the closed period
commenced on January 1, 2026, and will continue until twenty-four (24) hours after the 2026
Q1 UFS have been filed via The Exchange’s Issuers’ Portal.
The closed period is applicable to all Directors, Senior Managers and other interested parties
that may at any time possess sensitive information that materially affect the price of securities
of the company.
Accordingly, no insider of the Company and/or their connected persons may directly or
indirectly deal in the shares of the company during the Closed Period.
For further enquiries, please contact nem@nem-insurance.com.
