Lomé, June 29, 2026
NOTICE OF CLOSED PERIOD
We write to advise the Nigerian Exchange Limited (“NGX”), shareholders of Ecobank
Transnational Incorporated (“ETI”) and all stakeholders that the Board of Directors of ETI
shall hold a board meeting on July 24, 2026 to consider the 2nd Quarter Unaudited Financial
Statements for the period ending June 30, 2026 (Q2 2026 UFS).
In compliance with Rule 17.18(a) of the NGX Listing Rules 2015 as amended, a closed
period shall commence from July 1, 2026, and will continue until twenty-four (24) hours
after the outcome of the board meeting and publication of the Q2 2026 UFS on NGX.
During the closed period, no insider of ETI shares or its affiliates may deal directly or
indirectly with ETI shares.
