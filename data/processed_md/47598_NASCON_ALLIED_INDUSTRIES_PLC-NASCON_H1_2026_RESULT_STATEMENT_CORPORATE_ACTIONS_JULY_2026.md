H1 2026 UNAUDITED RESULTS
Nascon Allied Industries Plc
28th July 2026

UNAUDITED RESULTS FOR SIX MONTHS ENDED 30 JUNE 2026
Gross profit up 9% at ₦40.8B
Revenue up 4% at ₦81.2B
EBITDA up 6% at ₦24.1B
PAT up 26% at ₦19.6B
Lagos, 28 July 2026: Nascon Allied Industries Plc announces unaudited results for the
period ended 30 June 2026.
Financial highlights
• Revenue up 4% to ₦81.2B
• Gross profit up 9% to ₦40.8B
• EBITDA up 6% to ₦24.1B; 30% margin
• Profit before tax up 28% to ₦29.7B
• Profit after tax up 26% to ₦19.6B
• Earnings per share up 26% at ₦14.51
Aderemi Saka, Managing Director, said:
I am pleased to present NASCON Allied Industries Plc's unaudited financial results for the six
months ended 30 June 2026. Our first half performance demonstrates the resilience of our
business model, disciplined execution of strategic priorities, and ability to deliver sustainable
growth despite a dynamic operating environment.
Revenue increased by 4% to ₦81.2 billion, supported by sustained demand across our core
product categories and continued market penetration initiatives. More importantly, our focus
on operational efficiency, cost optimization, and margin management enabled us to deliver
stronger profitability growth ahead of revenue growth.
Gross profit rose by 9% to ₦40.8 billion, while gross profit margin improved to 50% from
48% in the corresponding period of 2025. EBITDA increased by 6% to ₦24.10 billion, with
EBITDA margin strengthening to 30%, reflecting our commitment to operational excellence
and disciplined cost management across the value chain.
Profit before tax increased by 28% to ₦29.7 billion, while profit after tax grew by 26% to
₦19.60 billion. Earnings per share also improved by 26% to ₦14.51, reinforcing the quality of
our earnings and our ability to translate operational performance into value for shareholders.
Our balance sheet remains strong, with total assets increasing by 45% to ₦161.6 billion and
cash and cash equivalents rising by 26% to ₦46.1 billion. This healthy liquidity position
provides the flexibility to support growth initiatives, maintain operational resilience, and
capitalize on emerging opportunities.
Beyond our financial performance, we are delighted to announce another significant capital
market milestone. During the period, NASCON Allied Industries Plc was admitted into the
prestigious Nigerian Exchange (NGX) 30 Index following the Exchange's half-year review of
its market indices. This inclusion places NASCON among the thirty most capitalized and liquid
companies on the Nigerian Exchange and reflects the sustained confidence of investors in our
business fundamentals, corporate governance standards, and long-term growth prospects.
As we look ahead to the second half of the year, we remain focused on driving operational
efficiency, strengthening our market leadership position, optimizing our product portfolio,
and creating sustainable value for all stakeholders. We are confident that our strategic
initiatives, strong financial position, and dedicated workforce will continue to support
profitable growth and long-term shareholder returns.

About Nascon Allied Industries Plc
NASCON Allied Industries Plc is Nigeria’s leading refiner and distributor of household, food processing and
industrial use salt. The company has since evolved with additional business lines to include Seasoning with
production. It has facilities in Lagos State (Apapa), Ogun State (Ota), and Rivers State (Port Harcourt).
The Apapa refinery, located in the Apapa Port of Lagos, was commissioned in 2001. The Port Harcourt
refinery located in the seaport in Rivers State was commissioned in 2003. The Oregun plant was
commissioned in 2004 to produce refined salt. Oregun plant was converted to a fleet workshop in 2021 with
the commissioning of the new state-of-the-art refinery at Salt Village, Apapa.
We made a strategic decision in 2011 to grow the company through new product lines. We took advantage of
our existing site in Ota and we commissioned a state-of-the-art Seasoning plant in 2014. Our plants are
primarily powered through the National Grid with generators fueled by gas or diesel, with combined capacity
to generate 6.1MW of power.
