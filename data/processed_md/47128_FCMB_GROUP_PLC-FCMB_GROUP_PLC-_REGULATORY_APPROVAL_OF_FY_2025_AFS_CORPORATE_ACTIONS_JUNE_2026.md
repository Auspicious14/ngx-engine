NOTIFICATION OF REGULATORY APPROVAL OF THE AUDITED FINANCIAL STATEMENTS FOR
FCMB GROUP PLC FOR THE YEAR ENDED 31 DECEMBER 2025.
FCMB Group Plc hereby notifies the Nigerian Exchange Limited ("NGX"), our esteemed shareholders,
and the investing public that FCMB Group Plc (“The Group”) has obtained the requisite regulatory
approval today, Friday, 5 June 2026, for its Audited Financial Statements for the financial year ended 31
December 2025.
Accordingly, the Group's FY 2025 Audited Financial Statements and Q1 2026 Unaudited Financial
Statements will be published on Monday, 8 June 2026.
This notification is made in compliance with the post-listing requirements of The Nigerian Exchange
Limited and the applicable rules of the relevant regulatory authorities.
