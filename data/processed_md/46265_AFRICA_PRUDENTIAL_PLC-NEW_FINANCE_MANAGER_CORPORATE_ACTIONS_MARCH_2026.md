NOTICE OF RESIGNATION - FINANCE MANAGER
Africa Prudential Plc (“the Company”) hereby notifies Nigerian Exchange Limited and its
stakeholders that Mr. Chidi Micheal, who served as the Finance Manager of the Company,
has resigned his position with effect from February 28, 2026.
The Company has accepted Mr. Micheal’s resignation and has appointed Mrs. Omolayo
Akindemowo, a Chartered Accountant, as his replacement pending the appointment of
a substantive Chief Finance Officer. The Company will notify the market once the
appointment process for the Chief Finance Officer has been concluded.
