SUSTAINABLE SOURCING
Cadbury Nigeria, through its parent company, Mondelēz International,
launched Cocoa Life, the company's global cocoa sustainability
programme, in July 2022. Cocoa Life now covers six states in Nigeria,
namely Ekiti, Ogun, Osun, Ondo, Oyo, and Cross River. The programme
has so far registered 8,500 farmers, providing them with training on good
agricultural practices that will improve yield and quality of the cocoa
beans grown by these farmers.
PERFORMANCE OF OUR BRANDS
Our brands continued to remain relevant and improved top of mind
awareness. Bournvita strengthened its consumer presence in the year
under review through successful television, digital, and out of home
campaigns across major cities. Our Ramadan 50g extra value offer and
the installation of over 50 gondolas nationwide, boosted visibility and
supported strong sales performance.
