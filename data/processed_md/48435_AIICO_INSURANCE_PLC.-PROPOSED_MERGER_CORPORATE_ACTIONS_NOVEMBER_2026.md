AIICO Insurance Plc and Cornerstone Insurance Plc Announce Proposed Merger
LAGOS, NIGERIA – 23 November 2026 – AIICO Insurance Plc (NGX: AIICO) and Cornerstone
Insurance Plc (Cornerstone) (NGX: CORNERST) wishes to notify Nigerian Exchange Limited (NGX),
its shareholders and the investing public of a proposed merger of the two companies. The Boards of
Directors of both companies have approved the proposal and will recommend it to their respective
shareholders for approval.
The merger would bring together two complementary insurance businesses, with combined
shareholders’ funds of approximately ₦194 billion and total assets of approximately ₦858 billion. On
completion, the merged company will remain listed on Nigerian Exchange Limited.
Transaction highlights
• Scale: The combined group is expected to be Nigeria’s leading listed insurance company by
gross written premium, insurance revenue, and shareholders’ funds.
• Capital and underwriting capacity: The larger capital base would support the underwriting
of larger risks and the retention of more business.
• Complementary capabilities: AIICO’s businesses in life insurance, annuities, and retail
distribution would complement Cornerstone’s strength in general insurance and its growing
takaful business.
• Broader product range and reach: The merger would bring together life, general, health,
and Takaful products, supported by a larger service network.
• Capacity to invest: The combined group would have greater scale to invest in digitalisation
and product development.
Strategic rationale
The proposed merger brings together complementary businesses and capabilities. AIICO contributes
established positions in life insurance, annuities, and retail distribution. Cornerstone adds strength
in general insurance and a growing Takaful business. Together, they would create a more diversified
insurer with a broader product range, wider distribution, and greater scale to serve customers across
the market.
That scale matters in an industry facing higher minimum capital requirements and a risk-based
capital framework under NIIRA. With combined shareholders’ funds of approximately ₦194 billion
and total assets of approximately ₦858 billion, the enlarged group would have a stronger capital
base to support larger underwriting commitments, retain more business, and invest in digitalisation
and product development.

Structure of the merger
The merger is expected to be implemented through a Scheme of Merger. The Scheme document
will set out the full terms of the transaction, including the share exchange ratio, and will be sent to
shareholders.
Approvals and next steps
The merger is subject to approval by the National Insurance Commission (“NAICOM”), the Federal
Competition and Consumer Protection Commission (“FCCPC”), the Securities and Exchange
Commission (“SEC”) and NGX; approval by the shareholders of each company at their respective
Court-Ordered Meetings; and sanction by the Federal High Court.
A Scheme document setting out the transaction details, the Boards’ recommendations, and notices
of the Court-Ordered Meetings will be sent to shareholders in due course. Shareholders do not need
to take any action at this time.
Customers, partners, and employees
All existing AIICO and Cornerstone policies and claims will continue to be honoured in full. Customers
do not need to take any action at this time.
Reinsurance companies, brokers, agents, bancassurance partners, and other distribution partners
have contributed to the success of both companies and will remain important to the enlarged group.
The group will build on these relationships, supported by a broader product range and greater
underwriting capacity.
The enlarged group will build on the heritage, relationships, and expertise of both companies. Its
greater scale and broader capabilities are expected to create opportunities for employees to develop
their careers.
About AIICO Insurance Plc
AIICO Insurance Plc traces its roots to 1963. It was incorporated in 1970 and listed on the Nigerian
Stock Exchange (now the NGX) in 1990. AIICO provides life, general and health insurance, as well as
deposit administration and asset management services, to individual and corporate customers across
Nigeria.
