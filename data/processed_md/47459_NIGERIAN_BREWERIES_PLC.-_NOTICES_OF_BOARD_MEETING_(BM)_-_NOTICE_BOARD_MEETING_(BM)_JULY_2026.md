LAGOS, 14TH JULY 2026
NOTIFICATION OF BOARD MEETING AND CLOSED PERIOD
We hereby notify The Nigerian Exchange Limited (“The NGX”) and the investing
public that a meeting of the Board of Directors of Nigerian Breweries Plc (“the
Company”) has been scheduled for Thursday, 30th July 2026.
An item on the agenda for the meeting is the consideration and approval of the
Unaudited Financial Statements of the Company for the half-year period (six
months) ended 30th June 2026.
Furthermore, please be informed that the Company’s Closed Period commenced
on 30th June 2026 and will continue until twenty-four (24) hours after the said
Unaudited Financial Statements are uploaded on the NGX portal.
BY ORDER OF THE BOARD:
Uaboi G. Agbebaku FCIS
Company Secretary
