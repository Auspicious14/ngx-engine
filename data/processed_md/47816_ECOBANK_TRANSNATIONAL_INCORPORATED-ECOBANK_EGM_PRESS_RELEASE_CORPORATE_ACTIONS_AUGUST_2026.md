Key Governance Enhancements approved at the Extraordinary General
Meeting of Ecobank Transnational Incorporated.
Lomé, Togo, 14 August 2026— Ecobank Transnational Incorporated (ETI) held an Extraordinary
General Meeting (EGM) on 13 August 2026, where shareholders approved key amendments to
the Company’s Articles of Association. These amendments followed a periodic review of ETI’s
governance framework.
Following the General Meeting resolutions, the following changes were made:
1. Enhancing shareholder representation: The General Meeting quorum requirement has
been fixed to shareholders representing at least 25% of the paid-up share capital
(replacing the 20-shareholder threshold). This change directly connects meeting quorum
rules to economic ownership interest, strengthening the representativity of General
Meeting outcomes.
2. Optimising Board effectiveness: The Board’s maximum size will be reduced from 15 to
12 directors to foster greater agility, focused deliberation, and enhanced individual
accountability, while maintaining strong independence and regulatory compliance.
3. Preserving experience and shareholder choice: The non-executive director tenure cap
will be removed, while retaining the mandatory retirement age of 70. The amendment does
not extend any director’s mandate automatically, and directors will continue to stand for
election or re-election by shareholders, who will retain full discretion over Board
composition. This amendment protects institutional expertise and governance continuity
in a complex regulatory landscape while ensuring shareholders retain full authority over
director elections.
4. Strengthening Board decision-making: The Board meeting quorum was increased from
a minimum of three directors to more than half of all serving directors. Raising this
threshold guarantees broader board participation, reinforcing collective accountability and
the integrity of Board decisions.
These proposals reflect ETI’s ongoing commitment to high standards of corporate governance
and will be implemented as and when appropriate and subject to all applicable laws and orders.
