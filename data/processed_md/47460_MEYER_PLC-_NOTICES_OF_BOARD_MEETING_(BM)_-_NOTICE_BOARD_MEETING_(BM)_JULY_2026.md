Lagos, 13th July 2026
NOTICE OF BOARD MEETING CLOSED PERIOD
Meyer Plc (the Company) wishes to notify Nigerian Exchange Limited (NGX or the Exchange),
its shareholders and the investing public that the Company’s Board of Directors will consider
and approve the Company’s Unaudited Financial Accounts for the second quarter ended 30th
June 2026 (2026 Q2 UFS) at its meeting scheduled to hold on Monday, the 27th of July 2026.
In line with the Amended Closed Period Rules of NGX, all Insiders and their connected persons
are prohibited from trading (i.e., buying, selling, transferring or otherwise dealing) in the
Company’s shares in view of the Closed Period which commenced from Wednesday the 1st
of July 2026 until twenty-four (24) hours after the Company’s 2026 Q2 UFS are released to
NGX and the general public.
