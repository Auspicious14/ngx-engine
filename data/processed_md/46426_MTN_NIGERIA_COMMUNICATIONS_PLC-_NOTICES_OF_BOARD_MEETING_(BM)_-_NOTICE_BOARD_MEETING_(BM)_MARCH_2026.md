Lagos | Nigeria: 30 March 2026
MTN NIGERIA COMMUNICATIONS PLC – NOTICE OF BOARD MEETING TO
CONSIDER AND APPROVE THE UNAUDITED CONDENSED CONSOLIDATED
FINANCIAL STATEMENTS FOR THE PERIOD ENDING 31 MARCH 2026 AND
COMMENCEMENT OF CLOSED PERIOD.
MTN Nigeria Communications PLC (MTN Nigeria) hereby notifies the Nigerian
Exchange Limited and the investing public that a meeting of the Board of Directors
of MTN Nigeria Communications PLC (MTN Nigeria or the Company) has been
scheduled for Wednesday, 29 April 2026, to consider and approve the Unaudited
Condensed Consolidated Financial Statements for the period ending 31 March 2026
along with other agenda items.
In line with the NGX’s post-listing requirements, MTN Nigeria will commence a Closed
Period for transactions on its securities from Wednesday, 1 April 2026. The Closed
Period will remain in effect until twenty-four (24) hours after the Financial Statements
have been released to the public.
Accordingly, insiders of the Company and their connected persons should not directly
or indirectly deal in the Company's securities in any manner during the Closed Period.

About MTN Nigeria
MTN Nigeria is one of Africa’s largest providers of communications services, connecting over
87 million people in communities across the country with each other and the world. Guided by
a belief that everybody deserves the benefits of a modern connected life, MTN Nigeria’s
leadership position in coverage, capacity, and innovation has remained constant since its
launch in 2001. MTN Nigeria is part of the MTN Group - a multinational telecommunications
group which operates in 16 countries in Africa and the Middle East, serving over 300 million
people.
