Osogbo May 07, 2026.
FURTHER EXTENSION OF TIME IN RESPECT OF FILING AND PUBLICATION OF THE 2025 AUDITED
FINANCIAL STATEMENTS AND Q1 - 2026 UNAUDITED FINANCIAL STATEMENTS - LIVINGTRUST
MORTGAGE BANK PLC
This is to notify the Nigerian Exchange Limited (The Exchange of NGX) and members of the
public, particularly the shareholders of LivingTrust Mortgage Bank Plc (the Bank) that the
Bank missed the deadline for the publication of the 2025 Audited Financial Statements for
the year ended 31 December 2025 (2025 AFS). In addition, the 1st quarter Unaudited
Financial Statement for the period 31 March 2026 (Q1-2026 UFS) has also not been filed
because NGX rule requires, that the full year audited account of the previous financial year
must be filed prior to the filing of the 1st quarter account of the following year.
Sequel to the earlier approval of the Securities and Exchange Commission extending the
publication of the 2025 AFS till 30th of April, 2026; the Commission has graciously granted
further extension to allow the publication of the 2025 AFS and the Q1-2026 UFS upon receipt
of the requisite regulatory approval.
The Bank looks forward to filing and publishing the 2025 AFS and the Q1 - 2026 UFS by 31st of
May, 2026.
We thank our esteemed shareholders and the public for their sustained interest in our Bank.
For: LivingTrust Mortgage Bank Plc.
Dorcas Ajayi
Ag. Company Secretary
