Abuja: 25 May 2026
RESOLUTIONS PASSED AT THE 20TH ANNUAL GENERAL MEETING OF INFINITY
TRUST MORTGAGE BANK PLC HELD ON MAY 21ST, 2026
At the 20th Annual General Meeting of INFINITY TRUST MORTGAGE BANK PLC duly
convened and held virtually on Thursday, May 21, 2026, the following Resolutions were
proposed and duly passed:
1) “That the Audited Financial Statements for the year ended December 31, 2025,
together with the Reports of the Directors, Auditors and Audit Committee thereon laid
before the meeting be and are hereby received”.
2) “That a Dividend of 35 Kobo per Ordinary Share amounting to N1,459,656,002 (One
Billion, Four Hundred and Fifty-Nine Million, Six Hundred and Fifty-Six Thousand, Two
Naira) with respect to the 2025 Financial Year be and is hereby approved”.
3) “That the Directors be and are hereby authorized to fix the remuneration of the
External Auditors for the 2026 Financial Year”.
4) “That the following Shareholders and Non-Executive Directors were re-elected and
nominated respectively to the Statutory Audit Committee from May 21st , 2025 until
the next Annual General Meeting”:
o (i) Dr. Franklyn Akinyosoye - Shareholders’ Representative
o (ii) Mr. Segun Owoeye - Shareholders’ Representative
o (iii) Mr. Lawrence Welle - Shareholders’ Representative
o (iv) Mrs. Abisola Obaleye (FCA) - Board Representative
o (v) Mr. James Ahmed - Board Representative
5) “That the appointment of Mr. Obasegun Adetokunbo Oni as a Non-Executive Director
of the Company is hereby ratified”.
6) “That the re-election of the following persons as Directors of the Company be and is
hereby approved”
• Dr. Olabanjo Johnson Obaleye, FCA - Non-Executive Director
• Engr. Eniola Chukwuebuka Bibilari - Non-Executive Director
• Alh. Abubakar Muhammad - Non-Executive Directo

7) That the remuneration of the Managers of the Company as disclosed in Note 34.1,
Page 132 of the Audited Accounts for 2025 Financial Year be and is hereby noted by
the meeting”.
SPECIAL BUSINESS:
I. “That the remuneration for the Non-Executive Directors for the Financial Year ending
December 31, 2026, is hereby fixed as reported in the Audited Accounts for 2025
Financial Year. Such payment to be effective January 1st, 2026”.
II. “That subject to the approval of the relevant regulatory authorities, the Board be and
is hereby authorized to allot a bonus issue of one (1) new ordinary share for every
one (1) existing ordinary share of 50 kobo each to members whose names appear in
the Company’s Register of Members at the close of business 17th April, 2026”.
III. “The Bonus Shares shall be credited as fully paid and shall rank pari passu in all
respects with the existing ordinary shares of the Company”.
IV. “That the share capital of the Company be increased by the creation of such sufficient
number of ordinary shares of 50 Kobo each representing the total number of Bonus
Shares, such shares ranking pari passu in all respects with the existing shares in the
capital”.
V. “That the Company be and is hereby authorized to increase its share capital from
N2,085,222,860 to N4,170.445.720 (or to otherwise increase its share capital to an
amount sufficient to accommodate the Bonus Issue) ”.
