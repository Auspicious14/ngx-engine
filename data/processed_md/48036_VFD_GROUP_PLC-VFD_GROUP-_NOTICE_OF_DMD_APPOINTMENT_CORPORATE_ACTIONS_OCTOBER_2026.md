NOTICE OF APPOINTMENT OF DEPUTY MANAGING DIRECTOR OF VFD GROUP PLC
The Board of Directors of VFD Group Plc (the “Company”) hereby notifies the Nigerian
Exchange Limited (“NGX”) and the investing public of the appointment of Mrs. Ijeoma
Emezie-Ezigbo as the Deputy Managing Director (Executive Director) of the Company,
effective 5 October 2026, subject to approval of shareholders at a General Meeting and
requisite regulators.
Mrs. Emezie-Ezigbo brings over twenty years of extensive experience in corporate finance,
transaction advisory, mergers and acquisitions, business valuation, and strategic financial
management gained across Nigeria, Canada, and the broader African market. She holds a
Bachelor of Science degree in Business Administration (Marketing) with First Class Honours
from the University of Nigeria, a Master of Finance degree from Cass Business School,
London, and has completed the Advanced Management Programme at Lagos Business
School. She is also a Fellow of the Institute of Chartered Accountants of Nigeria (FCA), an
Associate Chartered Certified Accountant (ACCA UK), a Chartered Professional Accountant
(CPA Ontario), and a Certified Valuation Analyst (NACVA).
Her professional career includes significant leadership roles with Stanbic IBTC Group, IBM
Canada, Canadian Tire Corporation, and KPMG in Nigeria and Canada. Most recently, she
served as Partner and Head of Transaction Services, West Africa at KPMG Nigeria.
The Board is satisfied that Mrs. Emezie-Ezigbo possesses the requisite competence,
experience, integrity, and leadership capacity required for the position and is confident that
her appointment will further enhance the Company's governance framework, strategic
oversight, and long-term value creation objectives.
