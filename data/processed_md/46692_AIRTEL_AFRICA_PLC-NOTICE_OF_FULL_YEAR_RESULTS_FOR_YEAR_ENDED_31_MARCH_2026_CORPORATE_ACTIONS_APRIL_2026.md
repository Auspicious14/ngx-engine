About Airtel Africa
Airtel Africa is a leading provider of telecommunications and mobile money services, with operations in 14
countries in sub-Saharan Africa. Airtel Africa provides an integrated offer to its subscribers, including mobile
voice and data services as well as mobile money services both nationally and internationally. The company’s
strategy is focused on providing a great customer experience across the entire footprint, enabling our corporate
purpose of transforming lives across Africa.
