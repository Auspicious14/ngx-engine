Lagos, Nigeria, 24 March 2026
ETERNA PLC - NOTICE OF BOARD MEETING
Notice is hereby given that a meeting of the Board of Directors of Eterna Plc (the
Company) will be held on Friday, 27 March 2026, to consider the Company’s
Audited Financial Statements for the year ended 31 December 2025 and other
matters scheduled for the meeting.
Pursuant to the Nigerian Exchange Limited (NGX) Rulebook, the Company
commenced a Closed Period from 1 January 2026, which will remain in effect until
twenty-four (24) hours after the release of the 2025 Audited Financial Statements for
the year ended 31 December 2025 to NGX and, by extension, the general public.
During this period, no insider or their connected persons may, directly or indirectly,
deal in the securities of the Company.
BY ORDER OF THE BOARD
David Edet
Company Secretary/Legal Adviser
