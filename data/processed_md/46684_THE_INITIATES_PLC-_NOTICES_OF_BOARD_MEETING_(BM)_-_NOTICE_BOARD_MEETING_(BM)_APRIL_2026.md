THE INITIATES PLC
WASTE MANAGERS & INDUSTRIAL CLEANERS RC266755
Port Harcourt, Rivers State; 16th April, 2026
NOTICE OF THE 59TH MEETING OF THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF THE INITIATES
PLC
NOTICE IS HEREBY GIVEN that the 59th Meeting of the Board of Directors of The Initiates
Plc. (the Company) shall hold physically on Wednesday, the 29th day of April, 2026, at
Golden Tulip Garden City Port Harcourt Hotel, No.63 Ken Saro Wiwa Road, formerly Stadium
Road by Mummy B Junction, Port Harcourt, Rivers State, by 10:00am to consider among
other things:
• The Company’s Growth Strategy;
• Audit Committee Report & Adoption/approval of the Unaudited Financial Statement for
the period ended 31st March, 2026 (2026 Q1 UFS); and
• The Management’s Report.
Accordingly, in compliance with Nigerian Exchange Limited’s post listings requirements, The
Initiates Plc. declared the commencement of a closed period for trading in its shares from 1st
of April, 2026 and this shall remain until twenty-four (24) hours after the Company’s 2026 Q1
UFS is announced to the Market.
Consequently, no insider with information or their connected persons shall deal directly or
indirectly in the securities of The Initiates Plc. during this closed period.
For: The Initiates Plc.
