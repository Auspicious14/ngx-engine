Corporate Actions Announcement
MTN Nigeria Communications Plc hereby announces as follows:
Period Ended 30 June 2026
An Interim Dividend of N26 per 2 kobo ordinary share has been approved by the
Board of Directors of MTN Nigeria Communications Plc, subject to appropriate
deduction of withholding tax. It will be paid to shareholders whose names appear
Proposed Dividend in the Register of Members as at the close of business on 20 August 2026.
Closure of Register The Register of Shareholders will be closed from 21 August 2026.
Qualification Date 20 August 2026
On 7 September 2026, dividends will be paid electronically to shareholders
whose names appear on the Register of Members as at 20 August 2026, and who
have completed the e-dividend registration and mandated the Registrar to pay
Payment Date their dividends directly into their Bank accounts.
Shareholders who are yet to complete the e-dividend mandate Form should click
here for the link to complete and submit to the Registrar and their respective
E-Dividend Registration Banks.
Shareholders are advised to click here for the link to check the list of unclaimed
dividends. Shareholders with unclaimed dividends are further advised to
Unclaimed Dividend complete the e-dividend mandate form or contact the Registrar.
