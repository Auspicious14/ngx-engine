UNITED BANK FOR AFRICA PLC
PUBLICATION OF AUDITED FINANCIAL STATEMENTS FOR THE HALF-YEAR
ENDED JUNE 30, 2026
Lagos, Nigeria – Friday, August 21, 2026
United Bank for Africa Plc (“UBA” or the “Bank”) hereby informs its
shareholders and the investing public that the Board of Directors
approved the Bank’s Financial Statements for the half-year ended June
30, 2026 (“the Results”) at its meeting held on August 13, 2026, however,
the approval of the primary regulator is required before the release of
the Results.
In this regard, the Bank has requested and obtained approval of the
Nigerian Exchange Limited (“NGX”) for an extension of time to file the
Results on or before September 30, 2026, subject to obtaining the
requisite regulatory approvals for the publication of the Results.
The Bank’s insiders are hereby reminded that the previously declared
Closed Period in respect of transactions in the Bank’s securities remains
in effect until twenty-four (24) hours after the publication of the Results.
Thank you.
BY THE ORDER OF THE BOARD
