Lagos, Nigeria.
22 July 2026
Ikeja Hotel Plc: Resolutions Passed at the 48th and 49th Annual General
Meeting
At the Annual General Meeting of Ikeja Hotel Plc (the Company) duly convened and
held on the 20th day of July 2026 at 10:00 am at Sheraton Lagos Hotel, 30, Mobolaji
Bank Anthony Way, Ikeja, Lagos, the following resolutions were proposed and duly
passed by members of the Company:
Ordinary Business:
1. That the Reports of the Directors, External Auditors, and Audit Committee
together with the Audited Accounts for the financial years ended December 31,
2024 and December 31, 2025 submitted to the meeting be and are hereby
received.
2. That dividend payment of 15 kobo per ordinary share of the Company for the
2024 financial year and 30 kobo per ordinary share of the Company for the
2025 financial year subject to withholding tax be and is hereby approved.
3. That the firms of Messrs Ugochukwu, Ike & Co and Ahmed Zakari & Co, Joint
External auditors be and are hereby re-appointed as external auditors of the
Company.
4. That the Directors be and are hereby authorized to fix the remuneration of the
auditors for 2026 financial year.
5. That Messrs Ajani Wahab Abidoye, Lukmon Adesola and Peter Eyanuku are
hereby appointed as shareholder members of the statutory audit committee of
the company, while the Board is represented by Alhaji Abatcha Bulama and Mr.
Toke Alex Ibru.
6. That the remuneration of the managers of the company as disclosed on page
68 of the annual report is hereby noted.
Special Business:
7. That the total remuneration of Directors be and is hereby fixed as follows:
i. Directors fee: ₦18,500,000 (Eighteen Million, Five Hundred Thousand
Naira); and

ii. Other expenses (including sitting allowances, seasonal gifts, health
insurance, travel ticket) ₦168,940,976 (One Hundred and Sixty-Eight
Million, Nine Hundred and Forty Thousand, Nine Hundred and Seventy-
Six Naira) such payment to be effective from January 1, 2025.
By the order of the Board
