Corporate Actions Announcement
IKEJA HOTEL PLC hereby announces as follows:
Period Ended 30th September 2026
An Interim Dividend of 3 kobo gross for every share of 50 kobo each,
subject to appropriate withholding tax and approval will be paid to
shareholders whose names appear in the Register of Members as at the
Proposed Dividend close of business on Friday, 6th day of November, 2026.
Proposed Bonus N/A
The Register of Shareholders will be closed from Monday, 9th November
Closure of Register 2026 to Friday, 13th November 2026.
Qualification Date Friday, 6th November, 2026
On Friday, 4th December 2026, dividends will be paid electronically to
shareholders whose names appear on the Register of Members as at
Friday, 6th November 2026 and who have completed the e-dividend
registration and mandated the Registrar to pay their dividends directly
Payment Date into their Bank accounts.
Shareholders who are yet to complete the e-dividend registration are
advised to download the Registrar’s E-Dividend Mandate Activation
Form, which is also available on the website of the Registrars:
E-Dividend https://greenwichregistrars.com complete and submit to the Registrar
Registration or their respective Banks.
Shareholders with dividend warrants and share certificates that have
Unclaimed Dividend remained unclaimed or are yet to be presented for payment or returned
Warrants and Share for validation are advised to complete the e-dividend registration or
Certificates contact the Registrar.
