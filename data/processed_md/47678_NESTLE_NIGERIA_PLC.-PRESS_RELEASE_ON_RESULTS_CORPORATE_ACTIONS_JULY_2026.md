Lagos, Nigeria, July 30, 2026
Nestlé Nigeria PLC Reports Solid Revenue Growth, Increased Profitability and Strengthened Equity
Position for H1 2026
Nestlé Nigeria PLC has announced its financial results for the six months ended 30 June 2026, reporting
double-digit revenue growth, increased profitability and a strengthened equity position. Profit before tax
increased by 43% to ₦126.8 billion in H1 2026, compared with ₦88.4 billion in the corresponding period
of 2025, reflecting continued revenue growth, disciplined cost management and operational resilience.
Financial Highlights: Jan – Jun 2026
• Revenue grew by 12% to ₦650.8 billion in H1 2026, from ₦581.1 billion in H1 2025.
• Operating profit increased by 8% to ₦141.4 billion in H1 2026, from ₦130.4 billion in H1 2025.
• Profit before tax rose to ₦126.8 billion in H1 2026, compared to ₦88.4 billion in H1 2025.
• Profit after tax rose by 28% to ₦64.8 billion in H1 2026, from ₦50.6 billion in H1 2025.
• Total equity increased to ₦77.6 billion at the end of June 2026, from ₦12.9 billion at the end of
December 2025.

Commenting on the results, Mr. Wassim Elhusseini, CEO/Managing Director of Nestlé Nigeria PLC, said:
"Our performance in the first half of 2026 demonstrates continued progress in strengthening the
fundamentals of our business. The delivery of a seventh consecutive quarter of profitability since our
return to profit in Q4 2024, alongside 12% revenue growth and Profit After Tax of ₦64.8 billion, reflects
the resilience of our brands, the discipline of our execution and the commitment of our people. We remain
optimistic about the outlook and will continue to pursue sustainable, profitable growth with focus and
prudence."
The company will maintain its focus on deepening consumer relevance through innovation and
renovation, strengthening margin management, enhancing efficiency across its value chain and making
targeted investments that support the long-term health of its brands and business. Nestlé Nigeria also
reiterated its commitment to investing in its people, supporting the communities where it operates and
remaining agile in responding to evolving macroeconomic and geopolitical conditions.

About Nestlé Nigeria:
Nestlé Nigeria is one of Africa’s largest food and beverage companies. For over 65 years, the company has
delivered high‑quality, nutritious food and beverages under brands including MAGGI, MILO, GOLDEN
MORN, NESTLÉ PURE LIFE, CERELAC, NESCAFÉ, and NIDO.
With three manufacturing sites, seven branch offices, and its head office in Lagos, the company continues
to help improve livelihoods, build thriving communities, and protect the planet.
