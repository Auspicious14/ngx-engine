Lagos, September 28, 2026
NOTICE OF BOARD MEETING AND CLOSED PERIOD FOR AIICO INSURANCE PLC
NOTICE IS HEREBY GIVEN that the Board of AIICO Insurance Plc (the Company) will meet on
October 29, 2026, to review and approve the draft Unaudited Financial Statements for the period
ending September 30, 2026 (Q3, 2026).
Any decision reached that affects the approval of the accounts will immediately be communicated
to the Exchange.
In compliance with the provisions of Rule 17.18(a): Closed Period, Rulebook of the Exchange, 2015
(Issuers’ Rule) as amended, the Company shall commence a Closed Period from Thursday, 1
October 2026 until twenty-four (24) hours after the release of the Company’s Unaudited Financial
Statements for the period ending September 30, 2026, and other decisions taken at the Board
Meeting.
During the closed period, no director, person discharging managerial
responsibilities, and advisers of the Company shall deal in the securities of the Company.
