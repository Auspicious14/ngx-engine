DIRECTORS’ PROFILE
Dr. Adeyinka Adedeji
Dr. Adedeji is the Chairman of the Board of AVA Capital Plc. He is a finance professional
who previously served as Group Divisional Head of Digital Banking at UBA Group,
overseeing retail and corporate digital banking strategy and product rollout across 20
African countries.
He holds a BSc (First Class) in Chemical Engineering from the University of Lagos, an MSc
and PhD in Polymer Physics from Case Western Reserve University, USA, an MBA and
Master’s in Finance from the State University of New York, Albany, and completed a
certificate in Disruptive Strategy with Clayton Christensen at Harvard Business School. He
was also a Postdoctoral Fellow at the University of Minnesota.
Dr Adedeji has extensive experience in strategy, product innovation, technology, and
operational transformation, with a proven record in multi-country digital banking rollout,
mobile money licensing, and business growth. He holds 50 patents globally (75
applications) and has 20 publications in polymer technology. He also serves as Advisory
Board Member of Verve International, Chairman and Board Member of Maximus Academy,
and Non-Executive Director of Virtual Nigeria.
Mr. Kayode Fadahunsi
Mr. Fadahunsi is the Managing Director of AVA Capital Plc. He is a seasoned investment
banking and corporate finance professional with over 26 years of experience spanning
corporate finance and banking, investor relations, and securities trading. He is a seasoned
expert in investor relations strategy with a strong track record across capital markets and
financial services.
Prior to becoming the Managing Director of AVA Capital Plc, he held senior leadership
roles, including Managing Director/Chief Executive Officer at Prosperis Holdings Company
Limited, Director of Investor Relations at UBA PLC, Managing Director, Capital Markets at
United Capital PLC, and Managing Director at United Capital Securities Ltd.
Mr. Fadahunsi is an alumnus of the Owners/Presidents Management Programme (OPM) at
Harvard Business School, Boston, and the Oxford Advanced Management and Leadership
Programme (OAMLP) at Saïd Business School, University of Oxford. He is an Associate
Member of the Institute of Directors (IoD), an Honorary Senior Member of the Chartered
Institute of Bankers of Nigeria (HCIB), a Fellow of the Chartered Institute of Stockbrokers
(FCS), and an Associate Member of the Chartered Institute of Securities and Investment
(ACISI).

Mrs. Ekanim is an Executive Director of AVA Capital Plc. Her leadership is characterised
by a relentless focus on institutional sustainability and on pursuing operational excellence.

Mr. Samson Adekunle
Mr. Adekunle is a Non-Executive Director of AVA Capital Plc. He brings over 2 decades of
distinguished experience across banking and capital markets, with core expertise in
enterprise-wide risk management, business development, strategy, financial advisory,
internal control, treasury management, and securities trading.
He began his career in the Risk Management Division of Guaranty Trust Bank, where he
spent almost a decade building a strong foundation in credit and enterprise risk. He
subsequently joined United Capital Plc as Group Head of Risk Management and Internal
Control, where he led enterprise-wide risk oversight and strengthened internal control
frameworks across the group.
He holds a B.Sc. in Microbiology and an MBA from Obafemi Awolowo University, and has
further deepened his executive capabilities through studies in Disruptive Innovation at
Harvard Business School, an Executive Certificate in Innovation and Commercialization
from the Massachusetts Institute of Technology (MIT), the Advanced Management
Programme (AMP) completed at Lagos Business School and IESE Business School,
Barcelona, Spain, and the Oxford Advanced Management and Leadership Programme
(OAMLP) at Saïd Business School, University of Oxford
Mr. Adekunle is a Certified Risk Analyst (CRA®), an SEC-Sponsored Individual, an Associate
Chartered Stockbroker, an Authorised Dealing Clerk of the Nigerian Stock Exchange, an
Associate of the Chartered Institute for Securities & Investment (UK), and a Fellow of the
Global Academy of Finance and Management (GAFM®). He is also a member of the
Professional Risk Managers' International Association, the Chartered Institute of Loan and
Risk Management of Nigeria, and holds Fellowship of the National Institute of Credit
Administration (FICA).

Dr. Hauwa Babakobi
Dr. Babakobi is an Independent Non-Executive Director of AVA Capital Plc. She is a

Administration and the Africa Institute of Public Administration. Her academic and
professional trajectory reflects deep competence in governance, leadership, and

Mr. Adebayo Odeyale
Mr. Odeyale is an Independent Non-Executive Director of AVA Capital Plc. He is a Senior
HR Professional and Fellow of CIPM and the Nigerian Institute of Management, with cross-
sector experience spanning banking, healthcare, public sector, and oil & gas.
He has worked with leading organizations, including Accenture, UBA, UBA Capital Europe,
and NHS Trust UK. He previously served as Deputy Head of HR at UBA Group and acted
as Group HR Director on multiple occasions.
His expertise is supported by international training at Euromoney, Pan African University,
and Oxford University, and hands-on experience in major HR transformation projects. He
is currently the Managing Partner of SPremier HR Ltd, providing onsite and offsite HR
consulting services.
