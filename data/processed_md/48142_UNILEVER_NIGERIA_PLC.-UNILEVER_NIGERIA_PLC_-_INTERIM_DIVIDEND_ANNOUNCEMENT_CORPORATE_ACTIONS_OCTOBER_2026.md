UNILEVER NIGERIA PLC. hereby announces as follows:
Period Ended 30th September 2026
An Interim Dividend of N2.00 kobo for every ordinary share of 50k each, subject to deduction
of appropriate withholding tax, will be paid to shareholders whose names appear in the
Proposed Interim Dividend Register of Members as at the close of business on Friday, 30 October 2026.
Proposed Bonus NIL
The Register of Shareholders will be closed from Monday, 2 November 2026 to Friday, 6
Closure of Register November 2026 (both dates inclusive).
Qualification Date Friday, 30 October 2026.
On Wednesday 18 November 2026, dividends will be paid electronically to shareholders
whose names appear on the Register of Members as at Friday, 30 October 2026 and who
have completed the e-dividend registration and mandated the Registrar to pay their
Payment Date dividends directly into their Bank accounts.
Shareholders who are yet to complete the e-dividend registration are advised to download
the Registrar’s E-Dividend Mandate Activation Form, which is also available on the website
E-Dividend of the Registrars: www.greenwichregistrars.com, complete and submit to the Registrar or
Registration their respective Banks.
Unclaimed Dividend Warrants Shareholders with dividend warrants and share certificates that have remained unclaimed
and Share or are yet to be presented for payment or returned for validation are advised to complete the
Certificates e-dividend registration or contact the Registrar.
