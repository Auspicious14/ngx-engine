CORPORATE ACTIONS ANNOUNCEMENT
NEM INSURANCE PLC hereby announce as follows:
Period Ended 31st December 2025
A Final Dividend of N1.50k per N1.00 ordinary share, subject to appropriate
withholding tax and approval will be paid to shareholders whose names appear in
the Register of Members as at the close of business on Tuesday, 14th day of April
Proposed Dividend 2026.
Proposed Bonus NIL
The Register of Shareholders will be closed from Wednesday 15th April 2026 to
Closure of Register Friday 17th April 2026
Qualification Date 14th April 2026
On 14th May 2026, dividends will be paid electronically to shareholders whose
names appear on the Register of Members as of 14th April, 2026 and who have
completed the e-dividend registration and mandated the Registrar to pay their
Payment Date dividends directly into their Bank accounts.
Shareholders who are yet to complete the e-dividend registration are advised to
download the Registrar’s E-Dividend Mandate Activation Form, which is also
available on the Registrars website: www.apel.com.ng complete and submit to the
E-Dividend Registration Registrar or their respective Banks.
Unclaimed Dividend Shareholders with dividend warrants and share certificates that have remained
Warrants and Share unclaimed or are yet to be presented for payment or returned for validation are
Certificates advised to complete the e-dividend registration or contact the Registrar.
Date of Annual General The Company’s Annual General Meeting will be held on the 14th of May 2026 at a
Meeting venue and time which shall be later advised.
