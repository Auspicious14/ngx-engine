LAGOS, 30th APRIL 2026
NOTICE OF DELAY IN FILING AND PUBLICATION OF AUDITED FINANCIAL STATEMENTS
FOR THE FINANCIAL YEAR ENDED 31ST DECEMBER 2025 AND UNAUDITED FINANCIAL
STATEMENTS FOR THE 1ST QUARTER ENDED 31ST MARCH 2026
This is to notify the Nigerian Exchange Limited (The Exchange) and the investing public that Fortis
Global Insurance Plc (“the Company”) has applied for, and obtained, the approval of NGX
Regulation Limited for further extension of time within which to file and publish its Audited
Financial Statements for the year ended 31 December 2025 (2025 Audited Financial Statements)
and Unaudited Financial Statements for the 1st Quarter ended 31st March 2026 (Q1 UFS 2026).
The Company has been granted approval to file and publish the said Financial Statements on or
before 31 May 2026.
The delay in the filing of the 2025 Audited Financial Statements arises from the pending approval
of the accounts by the Company’s primary regulator, the National Insurance Commission
(NAICOM), which remains a mandatory prerequisite for the finalisation and filing of the 2025
Audited Financial Statements.
Although the Q1 UFS 2026 have been duly prepared, the Company is unable to file same at this
time, as the 2025 Audited Financial Statements have not yet been finalised and submitted, in
accordance with the prescribed filing sequence.
The Company remains firmly committed to upholding the highest standards of financial reporting
and transparency and is working diligently to ensure that the Financial Statements are finalised
and filed within the extended timeline.
The Board and Management appreciate the continued understanding and sustained interest of
our esteemed shareholders and the investing public.
