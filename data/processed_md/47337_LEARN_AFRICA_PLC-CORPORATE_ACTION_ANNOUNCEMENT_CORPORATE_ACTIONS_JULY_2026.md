Corporate Actions Announcement
Learn Africa PLC hereby announce as follows:
Period Ended 31st March 2026
A Final Dividend 35 kobo per ordinary share of 50 kobo each, subject to
appropriate withholding tax and approval will be paid to shareholders
whose names appear in the Register of Members as at the close of
Proposed Dividend business on the Friday, 11th September 2026
