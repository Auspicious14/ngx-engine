Lagos, September 28, 2026
ANNOUNCEMENT OF BOARD MEETING DATE AND CLOSED PERIOD OF NIGERIAN AVIATION
HANDLING COMPANY PLC
This is to inform Nigerian Exchange Limited (NGX or The Exchange) and our esteemed
shareholders that a meeting of the Board of Directors of Nigerian Aviation Handling Company Plc
(the Company) has been scheduled to hold on 29th October 2026, to consider and approve the
Company’s 2026 Third Quarter Unaudited Financial Statements (2026 Q3 UFS) for the period
ending 30 September 2026, along with other agenda items.
Our esteemed shareholders and Insiders are also informed that in compliance with the Amended
Closed Period Rules of The Exchange, Nigerian Aviation Handling Company Plc has declared a
closed period from 1st October 2026 until twenty-four (24) hours after the Company’s 2026 Q3
UFS is released to the Market at the end of October 2026.
Accordingly, no insider of the Company and their connected persons should deal with the shares
of the Company directly or indirectly or in any manner during the closed period.
Bello A. Abdullahi
Dikko & Mahmoud Solicitors
Company Secretary
