FCMB Group Plc delivers a 99% growth in Profit Before Tax to ₦157.3 billion for 1H 2026,
extending the strong earnings momentum from FY 2025.
LAGOS, NIGERIA – 27 July 2026 FCMB Group Plc (Bloomberg: FCMB) (“FCMB Group”) announces its
Unaudited Group Results for the half-year ended 30 June 2026.
FINANCIAL PERFORMANCE HIGHLIGHTS
Earnings Analysis
• Gross earnings grew by 27.8% to ₦676.2 billion for 1H 2026 (1H 2025: ₦529.2 billion), driven by a
31.0% growth in interest income and a 22% growth in earning assets from ₦4.90 trillion to ₦5.98
trillion.
• Net Interest Income grew by 71.8% to ₦356.3 billion (1H 2025: ₦207.4 billion), as higher interest
income was reinforced by a 2.7% Year-on-Year decline in interest expense, reflecting an improved
low-cost deposit mix and lower cost of funds. This lifted Net Interest Margin to 11.2% for 1H 2026
(1H 2025: 9.1%).
• The Group’s digital business — comprising Payments, Lending and Wealth — sustained its growth
trajectory, with digital revenue rising to ₦89.1 billion for 1H 2026 (1H 2025: ₦73.6 billion) and
contributing 13.2% of gross earnings, as volumes continued to grow across lending, payments and
wealth.
• Operating expenses grew by just 12.3% to ₦172.0 billion (1H 2025: ₦153.2 billion), reflecting
disciplined cost management, easing inflation and the efficiency benefits of digital transformation.
Cost-to-Income Ratio improved sharply to 41.4% for 1H 2026 (1H 2025: 57.0%), extending the
improvement from 53.8% in FY 2025.
• Net impairment losses on financial instruments rose to ₦85.9 billion (1H 2025: ₦36.2 billion) as the
Group front-loaded a decisive clean-up of stage 2 loans, including approximately ₦63.4 billion of
write-offs. This reduced the Nigerian Banking subsidiary’s NGAAP non-performing loan ratio to 5.2%
as at June 2026, restoring it closer to regulatory limits.
• Profit Before Tax grew by 99% to ₦157.3 billion for 1H 2026 (1H 2025: ₦79.1 billion), extending the
growth momentum recorded in FY 2025. Profit After Tax increased by 90% to ₦139.9 billion (1H 2025:
₦73.4 billion), while annualised Return on Average Equity and Return on Average Assets
strengthened to 27.9% and 3.5% respectively (1H 2025: 20.6% and 2.0%).
• Annualised Earnings Per Share (EPS) increased to ₦4.23 in 1H 2026 from ₦3.96 for FY 2025, despite
a larger post-recapitalisation share base, demonstrating the Group’s enhanced earnings-generating
capacity.
Balance Sheet Position
• Total assets increased by 9.5% to ₦8.36 trillion at June 2026 (December 2025: ₦7.63 trillion), as the
Group continued to prioritise balance-sheet efficiency and optimisation.

DIVISIONAL PERFORMANCE
The divisions of the Group reported Year-on-Year PBT growth and contribution as follows:
• Banking Group: PBT grew Year-on-Year by 80% to ₦116.6 billion in 1H 2026 (74% PBT contribution),
benefiting from higher net-interest margins, improved low-cost deposit mix and operating cost
efficiency.
• Non-Banking Businesses: Collectively, the Group’s non-banking businesses contributed 26% of
Group PBT, with profits increasing by 185% Year-on-Year to ₦40.7 billion, reinforcing the
diversification of Groups earnings beyond banking.
o Consumer Finance (Credit Direct Limited): PBT grew Year-on-Year by 92% to ₦17.7 billion
in 1H 2026 (11% PBT contribution), sustaining its strong trajectory through technology-
enabled origination, underwriting and distribution.
o Investment Management (FCMB Pensions / FCMB Asset Management / FCMB Trustees):
PBT grew Year-on-Year by 50% to ₦5.7 billion in 1H 2026 (4% PBT contribution), on continued
growth in Assets Under Management and fee income.
o Investment Banking (FCMB Capital Markets / CSL Stockbrokers): PBT grew Year-on-Year
by 76% to ₦1.9 billion in 1H 2026 (1% PBT contribution), supported by renewed deal activity
across debt and equity capital markets.
Speaking on the 1H 2026 results, Ladi Balogun, the Group Chief Executive Officer commented that:
“Our first-half performance demonstrates the strength of our recapitalised and diversified business model.
We delivered record profitability despite accelerating the normalisation of asset quality towards regulatory
thresholds, reflecting our commitment to building a stronger balance sheet for long-term growth. Expanding
net interest margins, an improved low-cost deposit mix, disciplined cost management, and growing
contributions from our non-banking businesses continue to enhance the quality and sustainability of our
earnings. We remain firmly on track to deliver a Return on Equity (RoE) of over 25% for the 2026 financial
year”.

About FCMB Group Plc
FCMB Group Plc is a financial services group, headquartered in Lagos, Nigeria, with operating companies divided along
four business groups – The Banking Group (First City Monument Bank Limited (‘The Bank’), FCMB (UK) Limited and
FCMB Microfinance Bank Limited); Consumer Finance (Credit Direct Limited); Investment Banking (FCMB Capital
Markets Limited and CSL Stockbrokers Limited) and Investment Management (FCMB Pensions Limited, FCMB Asset
Management Limited and FCMB Trustees Limited). Listed on the Nigerian Stock Exchange (NSE) with the ticker
symbol (FCMB), FCMB Group Plc has 65,954,593,274 ordinary shares held by over 620,000 shareholders.
First City Monument Bank Limited, the flagship company, has about 15 million customers and 205 branches in Nigeria
and a banking subsidiary in the United Kingdom through FCMB Bank (UK) Limited (which is authorized by the Prudential
Regulation Authority (PRA) and regulated by the Financial Conduct Authority (FCA) and the PRA in the United Kingdom).
