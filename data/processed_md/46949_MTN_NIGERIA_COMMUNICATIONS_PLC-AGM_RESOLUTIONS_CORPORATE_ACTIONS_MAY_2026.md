Lagos | Nigeria: 4 May 2026
MTN NIGERIA COMMUNICATIONS PLC – ANNUAL GENERAL MEETING
RESOLUTIONS
At the Annual General Meeting of MTN Nigeria Communications PLC (the Company
or MTN Nigeria) held virtually on Thursday, 30 April 2026, Members of the Company
resolved that:
1. The Audited Financial Statements for the year ended 31 December 2025 together
with the Reports of the Directors, Auditors, Independent Consultants on Board
Performance Evaluation and Audit Committee as laid before Members at the
Annual General meeting be and are hereby received.
2. A final dividend totalling N314.93bn (Three Hundred and Fourteen Billion, Nine
Hundred and Thirty Million Naira), which translates to N15 per every 2 Kobo
ordinary share, be declared for the year ended 31st December 2025. Such
dividend will be paid to shareholders whose names appeared in the Company’s
Register of Members at the close of business on Wednesday, 8 April, 2026
(bringing the total dividend for the year ended December 31, 2025 to N20), subject
to withholding tax, be and is hereby approved. The dividend will be paid to
shareholders on 5 May 2026.
3. The following were re-elected as Directors of the Company:
a. Dr. Ernest Ndukwe OFR (77)
b. Mrs. Ifueko M. Omoigui Okauru MFR
c. Mr. Jens Schulte-Bockum
d. Ms. Tsholofelo Molefe
e. Mr. Muhammad K. Ahmad (72)
4. Having confirmed their willingness to continue in office, the Directors are
authorised to fix the remuneration of Ernst & Young (EY), the Auditors, for the
year ending 31 December 2026.

About MTN Nigeria
MTN Nigeria is one of Africa’s largest providers of communications services, connecting over
87 million people in communities across the country with each other and the world. Guided by
a belief that everybody deserves the benefits of a modern connected life, MTN Nigeria’s
leadership position in coverage, capacity, and innovation has remained constant since its
launch in 2001. MTN Nigeria is part of the MTN Group - a multinational telecommunications
group which operates in 16 countries in Africa and the Middle East, serving over 300 million
people.
