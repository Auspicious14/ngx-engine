Lagos, 5th May 2026
LAFARGE AFRICA PLC – 2026 AGM RESOLUTIONS
At the 67th Annual General Meeting of Lafarge Africa Plc (the “Company”) held at the Grand Banquet
Hall, The Civic Centre, Ozumba Mbadiwe Road, Victoria Island, Lagos, on Thursday, 30th April 2025 at
10:00 a.m., the following Resolutions were proposed and duly passed:
ORDINARY BUSINESS
1. Declaration of Final Dividend
“That a final dividend of N6 per unit of ordinary share, amounting to ₦96,646,774,326 (Ninety-Six
Billion, Six Hundred and Forty-Six Million, Seven Hundred and Seventy-Four Thousand, Three
Hundred and Twenty-Six Naira) only, be and is hereby payable in respect of the year ended 31st
December 2025, subject to deduction of applicable taxes”.
2. Election and Re-election of Directors:
Election of Directors
i. “That the appointment of Mr. Xuanqian Wang as an Executive Director of the Company, be and is
hereby ratified.”
ii. “That the appointment of Mr. Gang Xu as a Non-Executive Director of the Company, be and is
hereby ratified.”
iii. “That the appointment of Mr. Qian Chen as a Non-Executive Director of the Company, be and is
hereby ratified.”
iv. “That the appointment of Mr. Jiajun Wang as a Non-Executive Director of the Company, be and is
hereby ratified.”
v. “That the appointment of Mr. Lin Zhang as a Non-Executive Director of the Company, be and is
hereby ratified.”
Re-election of Directors
vi. “That Mr. Gbenga Oyebode be and is hereby re-elected a Non-Executive Director of the Company.”

Statutory Audit Committee, as well as the following Directors Representatives: Mr. Zhang Lin; and Mrs.
Oyinkan Adewale, be and are hereby elected members of the Statutory Audit Committee for the 2026
audit year.’’
SPECIAL BUSINESS
5. To fix the remuneration of Non-Executive Directors
“That the remuneration of the Non-Executive Directors in respect of the year ending 31st December,
2026, be and is hereby fixed at N146,403,431 (One Hundred and Forty-Six Million, Four Hundred Three
Thousand, Four Hundred and Thirty-One Naira).”
6. General Mandate for Related Party Transactions
“That pursuant to Rule 20.8 of the Nigerian Exchange Group Issuers Rule, general mandate be given to
the Company to procure goods and services and enter into incidental transactions necessary for its day-
to-day operations from its related parties or interested persons on normal commercial terms.’’
