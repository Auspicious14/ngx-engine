CORPORATE ACTION ANNOUNCEMENT
LAGOS | ABUJA | PORT HARCOURT
Africa Prudential hereby announces as follows:
Period Ended: 30th June 2026
Proposed Dividend: A dividend of 10 Kobo per 50 Kobo ordinary share, subject to
appropriate Withholding Tax will be paid to shareholders whose
names appear in the Register of Members as of the close of business
on the 20th July 2026.
Proposed Bonus: Nil.
Closure of Register The Register of Shareholders will be closed from 21st – 22nd July 2026.
Qualification Date: 20th July 2026.
Payment Date: On 23rdJuly 2026, dividends will be paid electronically to
shareholders whose names appear on the Register of Members as
of 20th July 2026, and who have completed the e-dividend
registration and mandated the Registrar to pay their dividends
directly into their Bank accounts.
E – Dividend Registration: Shareholders who are yet to complete the e-dividend registration
are advised to complete our digitized e-mandate form on
https://africaprudential.com/claimyour-dividend-here/, which is
also available on our website: www.africaprudential.com
Unclaimed Dividend: Shareholders whose dividends have remained unclaimed or are yet
to be presented for payment or returned for validation are advised
to complete the e-dividend registration form.
Registrar: Africa Prudential Plc - 220B, Ikorodu Road, Palmgrove, Lagos. Call:
0700 AFRIPRUD (0700 23747783); Email: cxc@africaprudential.com
Website: www.africaprudential.com
Investor Relations: Address: 220B, Ikorodu Road, Palmgrove, Lagos. E-mail:
investorrelation@africaprudential.com; Tel:0700AFRIPRUD (0700
23747783) Website: www.africaprudential.com.
