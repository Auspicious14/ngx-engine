FCMB Group Plc records 103% growth in 9M 2026 Profit Before Tax to ₦272.8 billion, driven by
stronger margins and improved efficiency, delivering a 30.1% annualised Return on Average
Equity.
LAGOS, NIGERIA – 20 November 2026. FCMB Group Plc (Bloomberg: FCMB NL) (“FCMB Group”)
announces its Unaudited Group Results (including audited Nigerian bank results) for the nine months
ended 30 September 2026.
FINANCIAL PERFORMANCE HIGHLIGHTS
Earnings Analysis
• Gross earnings grew by 21% to ₦1 trillion for 9M 2026 (9M 2025: ₦828.1 billion), driven by a 23.4%
growth in interest income and a 10.7% growth in earning assets from ₦4.90 trillion to ₦5.43 trillion.
• Net Interest Income grew by 52.3% to ₦534.2 billion (9M 2025: ₦350.8 billion), as higher interest
income was reinforced by a 3% year-on-year decline in interest expense, reflecting an improved low-
cost deposit mix and lower cost of funds. This lifted Net Interest Margin to 12.3% for 9M 2026 (9M
2025: 10.1%).
• The Group’s digital business — comprising Payments, Lending and Wealth — sustained its growth
trajectory, with digital revenue rising to ₦142.5 billion for 9M 2026 (9M 2025: ₦113.6 billion) and
contributing 14.2% of gross earnings, as volumes continued to grow across lending, payments, and
wealth.
• Cost growth remained moderated as operating expenses grew by just 12.5% to ₦268.9 billion (9M
2025: ₦239 billion), supported by sustained cost discipline, a moderating inflationary environment
and continued efficiency gains from digital transformation. Cost-to-Income Ratio improved to 43.5%
for 9M 2026 (9M 2025: 55.5%), extending the improvement from 53.8% in FY 2025.
• Net impairment losses rose to ₦76.8 billion (9M 2025: ₦57.1 billion), reflecting the Stage 2 clean-up
undertaken in Q2; Q3 benefited from a ₦9 billion net write-back. The Nigerian Bank's IFRS NPL ratio
rose to 6.4% (June 2026: 4.7%), reflecting the 13% reduction in the loan book during Q3 and new
Stage 3 classifications.
• Profit Before Tax grew by 102.8% to ₦272.8 billion for 9M 2026 (9M 2025: ₦134.5 billion), with the
Group sustaining the pace of growth recorded in the first half. Profit After Tax increased by 85.7% to
₦232.9 billion (9M 2025: ₦125.5 billion), while annualised Return on Average Equity and Return on
Average Assets strengthened to 30.1% and 3.9% respectively (9M 2025: 22.4% and 2.3%).
• Annualised Earnings Per Share (EPS) grew by 15% to ₦4.59 in 9M 2026 from ₦3.99 for FY 2025, despite
the expanded share base post-recapitalisation.

Balance Sheet Position
• Total assets increased by 9.7% to ₦8.37 trillion at September 2026 (December 2025: ₦7.63 trillion),
as the Group continued to prioritise balance-sheet efficiency and optimisation.
• Loans and advances to customers declined by 8.3% to ₦2.17 trillion as at September 2026
(December 2025: ₦2.37 trillion). Growth of over 20% in the Group's higher yielding digitally originated
Retail and SME portfolio, to ₦315 billion, was more than offset by significant repayments from
corporate clients, reflecting their stronger cash flows and access to cheaper funding through
commercial paper and bond issuance.
• Current and Savings accounts, grew by 24.4% to ₦3.60 trillion as at September 2026 (December
2025: ₦2.89 trillion), as low-cost deposit mix improved further to 76% (December 2025: 65.4%). This
led to a decline in cost of funds year-on-year with interest expense declining by 3% year-on-year in
spite of a 7% growth in total customer deposits.
• Total equity grew by 47.8% to ₦1.24 trillion as at September 2026 (December 2025: ₦836.4 billion),
supported by a growth in retained earnings and a ~₦227 billion capital injection during the first
quarter of 2026, leading to a Capital Adequacy Ratio of 24.6% as at September 2026, providing
significant resources to support our growth.
• Assets Under Management grew by 23% to ₦2.1 trillion as at September 2026 (December 2025: ₦1.7
trillion), supported by continued market-share gains at FCMB Pensions and FCMB Asset
Management.
DIVISIONAL PERFORMANCE
The divisions of the Group reported year-on-year PBT growth and contribution as follows:
• Banking Group: PBT grew year-on-year by 94% to ₦216.9 billion in 9M 2026 (80% PBT contribution),
benefiting from higher net-interest margins, improved low-cost deposit mix and operating cost
efficiency.
• Non-Banking Businesses: Collectively, the Group’s non-banking businesses and the holding
company contributed 20% of Group PBT, with profits increasing by 147% year-on-year to ₦55.9
billion, reinforcing the diversification of Group’s earnings beyond banking.
Speaking on the 9M 2026 results, Ladi Balogun, the Group Chief Executive Officer, commented:
“Our nine-month results reflect the dedication of our people to building a more efficient engine that can
scale with stronger unit economics. Through our ongoing digital transformation and the rebalancing of our
business portfolio, we are strengthening both our earnings capacity and the quality of our growth. We expect
continued earnings momentum in the fourth quarter, supported by lower operating expenses following the
full recognition of the 2026 AMCON levy within the nine-month period, alongside a seasonal uplift in digital
lending and payments activity. With nine-month RoE at 30%, we expect full-year RoE to be materially above
an earlier guidance of 25%, providing a strong foundation for further profitable growth and sustained
shareholder value creation.”

About FCMB Group Plc
FCMB Group Plc is a financial services group, headquartered in Lagos, Nigeria, with operating companies divided along
four business groups – The Banking Group (First City Monument Bank Limited (‘The Bank’), FCMB (UK) Limited and
FCMB Microfinance Bank Limited); Consumer Finance (Credit Direct Limited); Investment Banking (FCMB Capital
Markets Limited and CSL Stockbrokers Limited) and Investment Management (FCMB Pensions Limited, FCMB Asset
Management Limited and FCMB Trustees Limited). Listed on the Nigerian Exchange Limited (NGX) with the ticker
symbol (FCMB:NL), FCMB Group Plc has 65,954,593,274 ordinary shares held by over 620,000 shareholders.
First City Monument Bank Limited, the flagship company, has about 15 million customers and 205 branches in Nigeria
and a banking subsidiary in the United Kingdom through FCMB Bank (UK) Limited (which is authorised by the Prudential
Regulation Authority (PRA) and regulated by the Financial Conduct Authority (FCA) and the PRA in the United Kingdom).
