The Company has adopted a code of conduct with regard to securities
transactions and the Directors and other key personnel of the
Company are aware of the restrictions imposed on them with regards
to trading in the shares of the Company during closed periods. The
policy in place is to guide against situation where personnel in
possession of confidential and price sensitive information deal with
the Company’s securities in a manner that amounts to insider trading.
Enquiries have been made and it is hereby stated that in respect of
the interim accounts submitted in the course of the quarter under
review none of the Directors or personnel of the Company violated
the rules relating to Securities Trading.
