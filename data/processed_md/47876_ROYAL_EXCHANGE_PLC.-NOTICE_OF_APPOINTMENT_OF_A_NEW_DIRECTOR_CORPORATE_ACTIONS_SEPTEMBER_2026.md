Lagos, Nigeria, Friday, July 31, 2026
NOTIFICATION OF APPOINTMENT OF DR. GEOFFREY C. OHEN AS A NON-EXECUTIVE
DIRECTOR OF ROYAL EXCHANGE PLC
Royal Exchange Plc (Royal Exchange or the Company) wishes to inform Nigerian Exchange
Limited, its shareholders and the investing public that the Board of Royal Exchange Plc has
appointed Dr. Geoffrey C. Ohen as a Non-Executive Director of the Company effective Thursday,
July 23, 2026.
The Company is confident that Dr. Geoffrey C. Ohen has the requisite skills and experience to
make a meaningful contribution to the Company's Board and to support the Company’s continued
growth and success. Royal Exchange further confirms that the appointee has successfully
undergone the necessary process before his appointment.
PROFILE OF DR. GEOFFREY C. OHEN
Dr. Ohen serves as the Managing Director and CEO of the Geoff Ohen Group and holds several
board-level leadership roles, including Chairman of Consolidated Resources Ltd, Ekocorp Plc,
Owa Oil & Gas Ltd, and Networth Group (comprising Networth Securities & Finance Ltd and
Networth Portfolio & Asset Management Ltd). He previously served as Chairman of Piccadilly
Insurance Ltd and New Patriot Building Society Ltd, and as a Director of Daily Times of Nigeria
Plc. As a widely traveled executive and philanthropist, Dr. Ohen brings strategic vision, business
acumen and strong operational expertise to the Board, where he is expected to support the
Company's strategic leadership and expansion initiatives.
