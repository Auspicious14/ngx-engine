Lome, 23rd October 2026
PRESS RELEASE
Ecobank Transnational Incorporated: Cancellation of the Ordinary General Meeting scheduled for
26 October 2026
Ecobank Transnational Incorporated (“ETI”) hereby informs its shareholders that the Ordinary
General Meeting (“OGM”), scheduled to be held by videoconference on Monday, 26 October 2026, will
no longer take place.
The decision is intended to allow time for broader stakeholder consultations on matters pertaining
to the OGM, as directed by ETI’s primary regulator, the Banking Commission of the West African
Monetary Union (WAMU).
ETI apologises for any inconvenience the cancellation may cause and thanks its shareholders for
their understanding and continued trust.
Lome, 23rd October 2026
By Order of the Board of Directors
Madibinet Cisse
Company Secretary
