NOTICE OF ANNUAL GENERAL MEETING
NOTICE IS HEREBY GIVEN THAT the 78th Annual General Meeting of PZ Cussons Nigeria Plc (“the Company”)
will be held at The Ballroom, Fraser Suites, Federal Capital Territory, Abuja on 28th October 2026 at 11:00
am prompt to transact the following business:
ORDINARY BUSINESS:
1. To lay before the Meeting the Audited Financial Statements for the year ended May 31, 2026, together
with the reports of the Directors, Independent Auditors and the Statutory Audit Committee thereon.
2. To declare a dividend
3. To elect/re-elect Directors:
a. To approve the appointment of the following persons as directors following their appointments
after the last Annual General Meeting:
i. Oghale Elueni
ii. Oludare Ebenezer Elusakin
b. To re-elect the following Directors who are eligible for retirement by rotation:
i. Oluwatoyin Odutayo
ii. Mallam Ballama Manu, who is over 70 years old, special notice to that effect having been
received by the Company in accordance with Section 282 of the Companies and Allied Matters
Act, 2020.
4. To authorize the Directors to fix the Remuneration of the Independent External Auditors.
5. To elect members of the Statutory Audit Committee.
6. To disclose the Remuneration of the Managers of the Company.

To consider and, if thought fit, pass resolutions 7, 8 and 9, 1(vi) as ordinary resolutions and others in
resolution 9 as a special resolution of the Company.:

Annual General Meeting, provided all such transactions are conducted on normal commercial terms. .

of this meeting, be and are hereby ratified.

“Scrip Dividend Arrangement or Arrangement: means any arrangement established by the
Company, under which a shareholder may elect, in respect of all or any part of a dividend
or other distribution declared payable in cash on shares, to receive an allotment of new
fully paid shares in the Company in lieu of all or part of a cash dividend or distribution
otherwise payable to that shareholder, on such terms and subject to such conditions,
election procedures, record dates, pricing methodology, scaling arrangements and other
requirements as the Directors may determine, subject to the Act and these Articles.”
b) New Articles 122, 123, 124, 125, and 126 are inserted in the Articles to permit the
establishment and operation of a scrip dividend arrangement as follows:
Article 122- Application of Scrip Dividend Arrangement
“Subject to the Act and these Articles, where the Company declares a dividend payable in
cash, the directors may determine that members entitled to such dividends may elect to
receive, in lieu of all or part of the cash dividend payable to them, an allotment of new
fully paid shares in the Company pursuant to a Scrip Dividend Arrangement, and shall give
such members an opportunity to make such election upon such terms and conditions of
the Arrangement.”
Article 123- Terms and Conditions of Scrip Dividend Arrangement
“The directors shall determine the terms and conditions of any Scrip Dividend
Arrangement, including the basis on which the number of shares to be allotted to an
electing Member shall be calculated, the reference price or other pricing methodology, the
applicable record date, the period and procedure for making an election, the eligible
minimum number of shares, the manner in which fractional entitlements are rounded, or
aggregated subject in each case to the Act and any law or regulatory requirements.”
Article 124- Notice and Election
“The directors shall give notice of the Scrip Dividend Arrangement to the members,
specifying the procedure and deadline for making an election. An election made by a
Member in accordance with the terms of the Arrangement shall, subject to the terms of
the Arrangement, be irrevocable after the applicable election deadline.”
Article 125- Ranking of Scrip Shares
“Shares allotted pursuant to a Scrip Dividend Arrangement shall rank pari passu in all
respects with the existing issued ordinary shares of the Company.”
Article 126- Default to Cash Dividend
“Where a member does not make a valid election to participate in the Scrip Dividend

New Ordinary Shares be credited as fully paid; and, when issued, shall rank pari passu in all respects

iii. That the election to receive the New Ordinary Shares in lieu of cash dividends shall have been
exercised by shareholders on or before 23rd October 2026;
iv. That the New Ordinary Shares to be allotted to each electing shareholder shall be determined by
their cash dividend entitlements divided by a Reference Share Price, which Reference Share Price
shall be the Simple Moving Average Closing Price of the Company’s ordinary shares as traded on
the NGX over the ten (10) trading days commencing October 12, 2026; and computed by adding
the closing share prices over that ten (10) day period and dividing the same by 10;
v. That any fractional entitlement arising from the calculation of the number of New Ordinary Shares
shall be rounded down to the nearest whole number of shares, and any amount attributable to such
fractional entitlement shall be paid as cash dividend to the shareholder;
vi. The Board of Directors (“Board”) of the Company be and is hereby authorised to (i) increase the
Company’s share capital by the exact number of shares which will be required to accommodate the
allotment of the New Ordinary Shares pursuant to the Scrip Dividend Arrangement, (ii) allot the
New Ordinary Shares to the relevant shareholders as fully paid up, and (iii) amend Clause 4 of the
Memorandum of Association of the Company to reflect the new Issued Share Capital of the
Company;
vii. That the Board, Company Secretary and the Company Registrar be and are hereby authorised to
take all such lawful steps, pass all requisite resolutions and do all such other lawful acts and/or
things as may be necessary for, incidental, supplemental and/or consequential to giving effect to
the foregoing resolutions, including without limitation, making all the necessary filings and
obtaining all requisite regulatory approvals.”
