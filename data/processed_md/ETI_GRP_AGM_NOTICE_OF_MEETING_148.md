Ecobank Transnational Incorporated
Annual General Meeting
NOTICE OF MEETING
Notice is hereby given that the thirty-eighth Annual General Meeting of Ecobank Transnational Incorporated (ETI) will be held at the Conference Centre,
Ecobank Pan African Centre, 2365 Boulevard du Mono, Lomé, Togo, on Wednesday, June 3, 2026, at 10:30 am GMT, to transact the following business:
1. Approval of the Accounts
2. Appropriation of the Profits
3. Renewal of mandates of Directors
4. Ratification of the co-option of a Director
5. Election of a Director
6. Appointment of external Auditors
Any shareholder may attend and vote at the meeting or be represented by a proxy.
Shareholders are encouraged to submit in advance their proxy forms duly completed indicating their voting intentions and deposit same at Ecobank
Transnational Incorporated, 2365 Boulevard du Mono, B.P. 3261 Lomé, Togo, or send by email to AGM@ecobank.com by close of business on Tuesday, June 2,
2026.
The proxy form and other relevant documents in connection with the meeting are available to all shareholders from the date of this notice at the Registered
Office of the Company at the address above as well as on the Company’s website (www.ecobank.com). Shareholders may submit questions in advance of the
General Meeting by sending an email to AGM@ecobank.com.
Dated this 13th day of May 2026
By Order of the Board of Directors
Madibinet Cisse
Company Secretary
