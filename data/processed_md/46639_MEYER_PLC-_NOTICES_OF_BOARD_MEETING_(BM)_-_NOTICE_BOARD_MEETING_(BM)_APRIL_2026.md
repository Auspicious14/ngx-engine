Lagos, 10th April 2026
NOTICE OF BOARD MEETING CLOSED PERIOD
Meyer Plc (the Company) wishes to notify the Nigerian Exchange Limited (NGX or The
Exchange), its shareholders and the investing public that the Company’s Board of Directors
will consider and approve the Company’s Unaudited Financial accounts for the first quarter
ended 31st March 2026 (2026 Q1 UFS) at its meeting scheduled to hold on Monday the 27th
of April 2026.
In line with the Amended Closed Period Rules of NGX, all insiders and their connected persons
are prohibited from trading (i.e. Buying, selling, transferring or otherwise dealing) in the
Company’s shares in view of the Closed Period which commenced from Wednesday, the 1st
of April 2026, until the 24 hours after the Company’s 2026 Q1 UFS are released to NGX and
the general public
Thank you
