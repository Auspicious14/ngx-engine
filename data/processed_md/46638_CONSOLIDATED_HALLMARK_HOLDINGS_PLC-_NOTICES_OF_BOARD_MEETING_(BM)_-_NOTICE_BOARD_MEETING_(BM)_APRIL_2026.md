Lagos, 10th April 2026
NOTICE OF BOARD OF DIRECTORS’ MEETING
NOTICE IS HEREBY GIVEN that the 17th Meeting of the Board of Directors of Consolidated
Hallmark Holdings Plc (the Company) will hold as follows:
DATE: Wednesday, April 29, 2026.
TIME: 10:00 am.
VENUE: Virtual.
The Meeting is to consider the Company’s Unaudited Financial Statements for the period ended
31st March 2026 (2026 Q1 UFS) amongst other items.
In view of the above, the Company hereby announces that its Closed Period has commenced on
Wednesday, 1st April 2026, and will remain until twenty-four (24) hours after the 2026 Q1 UFS is
released to the public.
Within this period, all those who are considered as “insiders” of the Company in accordance
with the provisions of the Rules and Regulations of Nigerian Exchange Limited (The Exchange),
are under restriction not to trade or deal in the shares of the Company.
BY ORDER OF THE BOARD
Rukevwe Falana
Company Secretary
