RC: 611238
Lagos Nigeria, June 30, 2026
Notification of Board Meeting and Closed Period of Transnational Corporation
Plc
This is to notify Nigerian Exchange Limited (NGX), shareholders and the public
that the Board of Transnational Corporation Plc (“the Company”) will meet on
Wednesday, July 22, 2026 , to consider amongst other things, the Company’s
Quarter 2, 2026 Unaudited Financial Statements for the quarter ending June
30, 2026 (Q2 2026 Financials) and a proposal for an interim dividend.
In compliance with the provisions of 17.18 (a) Closed Period Rules. Rulebook of
the Exchange, 2015 (As amended) (Issuers’ Rules), the Closed Period in the
Company’s shares will commence on Wednesday, July 1, 2026, until 24 hours
after Q2, 2026 Financials are filed with the NGX and released to the public.
Consequently, no insiders and their connected persons may directly or
indirectly deal in the securities of the Company until twenty-four (24) hours after
the release of the Q2, 2026 Financials through the Issuers’ Portal of NGX and by
extension, the general public.
Thank you.
