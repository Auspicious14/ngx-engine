February 27, 2026, Lagos, Nigeria
NOTIFICATION ON THE OUTCOME OF BOARD MEETING
This is to inform the Nigerian Exchange Limited (“NGX”) and all other
stakeholders that the meeting of the Board of Dangote Cement Plc (“DCP
or the Company”) held on 26 February 2026, amongst other matters,
considered and approved the Company’s Audited Financial Statements for
the full Year ended 31 December 2025 (AFS 2025). The approved AFS 2025
will be released to the public on or before 1 March 2026 in line with the
rules of the NGX.
The closed period, which commenced 1 January 2026, will continue until
twenty-four (24) hours after the AFS 2025 is released to the public.
Edward Imoedemhe
Company Secretary
