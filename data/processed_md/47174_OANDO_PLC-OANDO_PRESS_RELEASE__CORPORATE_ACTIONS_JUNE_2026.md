OANDO PLC ANNOUNCES AN UPDATE ON THE PUBLICATION OF ITS 2025 AUDITED FINANCIAL STATEMENTS
Lagos, 15 June 2026
Oando PLC ("Oando" or the "Company"), Nigeria's leading indigenous energy group listed on both the Nigerian Exchange
Limited (NGX) and the Johannesburg Stock Exchange (JSE Limited), wishes to inform its shareholders and the investing
public that, further to its previous announcement regarding the approval of the Company's 2025 Audited Financial Statements
("AFS") by the Board of Directors and the subsequent submission of the AFS to the Financial Reporting Council of Nigeria
(FRCN) for regulatory review, the review process remains ongoing.
The Company continues to engage actively with the FRCN and other relevant regulatory authorities in connection with the
review process and is awaiting the completion of all requisite regulatory clearances.
Upon the conclusion of the regulatory review process and receipt of the necessary approvals, the Company will proceed
with the publication of its 2025 Audited Financial Statements. Thereafter, the Company will publish its Q1 2026 Unaudited
Financial Statements.
Oando remains fully committed to maintaining the highest standards of financial reporting integrity, transparency, and
regulatory compliance. The Company will continue to keep shareholders, regulators, and the investing public informed of
material developments and provide updates as appropriate throughout the process.
The Company appreciates the continued patience, understanding, and support of its shareholders, regulators, and other
stakeholders.
