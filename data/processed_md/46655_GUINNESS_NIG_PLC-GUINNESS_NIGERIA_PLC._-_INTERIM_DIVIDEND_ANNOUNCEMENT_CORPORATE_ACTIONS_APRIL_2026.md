Corporate Actions Announcement
Guinness Nigeria Plc hereby announce as follows:
Period Ended 31 March 2026
An Interim Dividend of ₦2.00 per ordinary share, subject to appropriate withholding
tax and approval will be paid to shareholders whose names appear in the Register of
Proposed Dividend Members as at the close of business on Monday, 20 April 2026.
Proposed Bonus NIL
Closure of Register The Register of Shareholders will be closed from 21 April 2026.
Qualification Date 20 April 2026
On 24 April 2026, dividends will be paid electronically to shareholders whose names
appear on the Register of Members as at 20 April 2026, and who have completed the e-
dividend registration and mandated the Registrar to pay their dividends directly into their
Payment Date Bank accounts.
Shareholders who are yet to complete the e-dividend registration are advised to download
the Registrar’s E-Dividend Mandate Activation Form, which is also available on our website:
Microsoft PowerPoint - Mandate Form, complete and submit to the Registrar or their
E-Dividend Registration respective Banks.
Shareholders with dividend warrants and share certificates that have remained unclaimed,
Unclaimed Dividend or are yet to be presented for payment or returned for validation are advised to complete
Warrants and Share the e-dividend registration or contact the Registrar. Please click here for the list of
Certificates unclaimed dividends.
