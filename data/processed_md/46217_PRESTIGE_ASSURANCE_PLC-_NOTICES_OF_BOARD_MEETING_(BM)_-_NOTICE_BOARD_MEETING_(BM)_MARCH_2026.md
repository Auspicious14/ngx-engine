Lagos, 4th March 2026.
NOTICE OF BOARD MEETING AND CLOSED PERIOD
Prestige Assurance Plc (“the Company”) hereby wishes to inform the Nigerian Exchange
Limited (“NGX”), its shareholders, and the investing Public that a meeting of the Board
of Directors of the Company will be held on Tuesday, 17th March 2026. The Board will
consider the Company’s Audited Financial Statements for the year ended 31st
December 2025 (2025 AFS), among other agenda items.
Consequently, the closed period earlier announced which commenced from Thursday,
1 January 2026 is still subsisting until twenty-four (24) hours after the 2025 AFS have been
released to the public.
Accordingly, no insider and their connected persons as defined by NGX may directly or
indirectly deal in the securities of the Company in any manner during the Closed Period.
MRS CHIDINMA IBE-LOUIS
Company Secretary
