Ellah Lakes Reports H1 2026 Revenue Growth, Strengthens Balance Sheet and Advances Execution
Across Processing and Livestock Operations
Lagos, Nigeria – 3 August 2026 – Ellah Lakes Plc. (“Ellah Lakes”, or “the Company”) Nigeria’s integrated agro-
industrial company, today publishes its unaudited financial results for the six months ended 30 June 2026.
The first half of 2026 marked an important period in Ellah Lakes’ development, as the Company combined
revenue growth with a significant strengthening of its capital structure and continued progress across key
operating initiatives.
During H1 2026, Ellah Lakes recorded revenue of ₦533.86 million, reflecting increased commercial activity
across the Group’s operating platform and continued progress in scaling its commercial operations. The
Company also completed the conversion of outstanding directors’ loans into equity, materially reducing
leverage and providing a stronger financial platform for the next phase of operational execution. With the
balance sheet significantly strengthened, management’s focus is now on improving asset utilisation,
increasing production throughput, expanding processing capacity and strengthening cash generation
across the Company’s integrated agribusiness platform.
Business Update
• Commercial Activity and Revenue Diversification
Ellah Lakes continued to build commercial activity across its operating platform during H1 2026, reflecting
the gradual transition of its asset base into revenue-generating operations. This progress supports the
Company’s broader objective of developing a more diversified and cash-generative agribusiness model.
• Processing Expansion and Production Efficiency
Ellah Lakes continued to advance its processing strategy during the period through investments aimed at
improving production reliability and deepening value capture across the palm value chain. The Company
procured a new press for its CPO Mill to enhance milling reliability and production efficiency, with the
equipment expected to arrive in September 2026. Once installed, the new press is expected to support
improved throughput and stronger CPO production performance. Ellah Lakes also acquired the first set of
expellers and presses for its Palm Kernel Oil (“PKO”) Mill, marking a key step in the expansion of its palm
kernel processing operations. Installation is expected to be completed by the end of the third quarter of
2026, after which the Company plans to commence production of Palm Kernel Oil and Palm Kernel Cake
(“PKC”). The ability to produce PKO and PKC is expected to broaden the Company’s product base, create

• Scaling Livestock Operations
Ellah Lakes has surpassed 1,000 pigs on its farm and commenced the sale of gilts, expanding the commercial
contribution of its livestock operations through the supply of young female breeding pigs. This reflects
continued progress in developing the piggery business as an important operating vertical within the
Company’s integrated agribusiness model.
• Strengthening the Capital Structure
During the period, Ellah Lakes completed the conversion of outstanding directors’ loans into equity,
significantly improving the Company’s balance sheet. Share capital increased to ₦3.20 billion from ₦1.93
billion, while share premium increased to ₦14.03 billion from ₦8.20 billion. The conversion eliminated ₦7.08
billion in directors’ loans from the liability base, reducing total liabilities by 93.6% to ₦504.66 million. This
has substantially reduced the Company’s leverage profile and improved its financial flexibility as it continues
to execute the next phase of its operating plan.
• Land Asset Protection Update
During the period, Ellah Lakes commenced legal action against Olumide Akinsanya and Median Agro Limited
in relation to alleged encroachment on part of the Company’s cultivated land in Iguelaba, Edo State. The
Company is pursuing appropriate legal remedies while continuing to safeguard its land assets and maintain
focus on its operating priorities
Commenting on the results, Chuka Mordi, Chief Executive Officer of Ellah Lakes Plc said: "The first half
of 2026 was an important period in strengthening the foundations of our business. We recorded continued
revenue growth, completed a major balance sheet restructuring and advanced key operating initiatives across
processing and livestock. These developments provide a stronger platform for Ellah Lakes as we continue to move
deeper into commercial execution.
Our immediate focus is to improve production reliability, restore stronger CPO Mill performance and complete the
next stage of our processing roadmap. The planned replacement of the CPO press is an important step in
improving milling efficiency, while the acquisition of PKO Mill equipment supports our broader objective of
capturing more value across the palm value chain.
We are also pleased with the continued scale-up of our livestock operations, which provides an important shorter-
cycle revenue vertical within our integrated agribusiness model. As our plantation assets continue to mature, we
remain focused on broadening our revenue base, improving operating efficiency, strengthening cash generation
and building a more resilient platform for long-term shareholder value”

the people, systems and operating infrastructure required to support the Company’s scale-up phase.

The movement was driven mainly by higher administrative and personnel expenses, alongside
depreciation and other operating costs linked to the Company’s expanding asset and operating base.
Administrative expenses stood at ₦253.46 million in Q2 2026, while personnel expenses stood at
₦259.51 million.
Management’s priority remains to improve operating efficiency and ensure that the Company’s cost
base is increasingly supported by higher production volumes, improved asset utilisation and expanded
processing activity.
• Total Assets: Total Assets stood at ₦25.25 billion as at 30 June 2026 (31 December 2025: ₦28.26 billion).
The movement was mainly driven by a reduction in current assets, particularly cash and cash
equivalents, while non-current assets remained broadly stable at ₦24.98 billion. The Company’s core
asset base continues to be anchored by property, plant and equipment of ₦24.72 billion, reflecting the
long-term productive assets supporting its integrated agro-industrial strategy. The reduction in cash

improving operating cash flow and converting the asset base into stronger commercial performance.

capital and share premium.

Outlook
Ellah Lakes enters the second half of 2026 with clear priorities across production efficiency, processing
expansion and livestock scale-up. Management’s near-term focus is to complete the CPO Mill equipment
upgrade, progress the installation of the PKO Mill equipment and continue strengthening operating
execution across the business.
Following installation, the replacement CPO press is expected to support improved milling reliability, while
the PKO Mill is expected to broaden the Company’s processing base through the production of PKO and
PKC. Livestock operations remain an important part of Ellah Lakes’ integrated agribusiness model. The
Company will continue to scale the piggery business and deepen the sale of gilts.
As part of the next phase of growth, Ellah Lakes plans to install its abattoir and cold chain facility to support
the continued expansion of its livestock operations. The facility is expected to enhance processing capacity,
improve product handling and storage, and strengthen market access for livestock products.
Management also continues to prioritise disciplined execution, stronger cash generation, asset protection,
risk management and clearer shareholder communication as the Company moves further into its execution
phase. Ellah Lakes remains committed to building a scalable agro-industrial platform anchored on
operational integration, disciplined execution and long-term value creation.

About Ellah Lakes Plc
Ellah Lakes Plc is a rapidly growing indigenous agribusiness company leading Nigeria’s agricultural
transformation. Established in 1980 as a fish farming enterprise, the Company underwent a strategic shift
in 2019 following the acquisition of Telluria Limited. It repositioned itself as a vertically integrated agro-
industrial player focused on crop cultivation, processing and sustainable value creation. Headquartered in
Benin City, Edo State, Ellah Lakes is dedicated to the production and processing of high-demand staple crops
such as oil palm, cassava, maize and soybean. The Company has successfully transitioned from traditional
farming methods to a multi-vertical operation that integrates crop production, edible oils, animal feed,
biofuels and agro-processing. Its’ long-term vision is to become the leading indigenous agribusiness in West
Africa.
