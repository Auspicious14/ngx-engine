LAGOS, NIGERIA
17 March 2026
SECURE ELECTRONIC TECHNOLOGY PLC - NOTIFICATION OF BOARD
MEETING
This is to notify Nigerian Exchange Limited (NGX) and the investing public that a
meeting of the Board of Secure Electronic Technology Plc (“SET Plc” or “the
Company”) will be held on Thursday, 26 March 2026 to consider and approve the
Audited Financial Statements for the Year ended 31 December 2025 (2025 AFS),
among other matters for deliberation.
As previously communicated to insiders in compliance with Rule 17 of the Issuers
Rules 2015 (as amended), a Closed Period commenced on 1 January 2026 and
shall remain in force until 24 (twenty-four) hours after the filing of the 2025 AFS
with the NGX and the Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC).
Consequently, no insider of Secure Electronic Technology Plc may deal in the
Company’s shares either directly or indirectly until twenty-four (24) hours after
2025 AFS are filed.
For: Secure Electronic Technology Plc.
Leticia Otomewo
Acting Company Secretary
