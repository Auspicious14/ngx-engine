LAGOS, 13TH OCTOBER 2026
NOTIFICATION OF BOARD MEETING AND CLOSED PERIOD
We hereby notify the Nigerian Exchange Limited (“NGX”) and the investing public
that a meeting of the Board of Directors of Nigerian Breweries Plc (the Company)
has been scheduled for Wednesday, 28th October 2026.
An item on the agenda for the meeting is the consideration and approval of the 2026
Quarter 3, Unaudited Financial Statements of the Company for the period ended
30th September 2026.
Furthermore, please be informed that the Company’s Closed Period commenced on
28th September 2026 and will continue until twenty-four (24) hours after the
aforesaid Unaudited Financial Statements are uploaded on the NGX portal.
Uaboi G. Agbebaku FCIS
Company Secretary
