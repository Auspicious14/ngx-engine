3. Professor Franklin Nnaemeka Ngwu, PhD
Franklin Nnaemeka Ngwu is an Economist, Chartered Risk Manager

Bankers of Nigeria and is a member of various institutions including the
Institute of Directors of Nigeria.
4. Mrs. Mary Mulili
Mrs. Mary Mulili is a seasoned banking executive with twenty-five (25)
years of experience across leading financial institutions in Africa,
including UBA, Guaranty Trust Bank, Bank of Africa, African Banking
Corporation, Kenya Commercial Bank and I&M Bank. She is currently
the Managing Director/Chief Executive Officer of UBA Kenya, having
previously served as its Executive Director, Business Development. She
has extensive experience in corporate and commercial banking,
business development, financial services and corporate governance.
She has also served on the boards of several organisations, including
the Kenya Meat Commission, where she chaired the Audit Committee.
She holds an MBA and a Bachelor's degree in Education - Economics
and Accounting from the University of Nairobi.
5. Mr. Farrukh Siddiqui
Mr. Farrukh Siddiqui is a global banking executive and strategic advisor
with over two (2) decades of experience spanning the Middle East,
Sub-Saharan Africa and South Asia. He is a Founding Partner of
Emerging Capital Management Partners, a Dubai-based corporate
finance advisory firm, and a Group Advisor at Sunbeth Global
Concepts. Previously, he served as a Managing Director at JPMorgan
Chase, where he played a key role in building and expanding the firm's
Global Trade and Loan business across the MENA and Sub-Saharan
Africa regions. His expertise covers global trade finance, corporate
banking, financial institutions, financial structuring and cross-border
transactions.
