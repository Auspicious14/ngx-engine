LAGOS: 6TH JULY 2026
HBM NIGERIA PLC – NOTICE OF BOARD CHANGES
HBM Nigeria Plc (the “Company”) hereby notifies The Nigerian Exchange Limited and the investing
public of the resignations of Mrs. Adenike Ogunlesi (Independent Non-Executive Director) and Ms.
Elenda Osima-Dokubo (Non-Executive Director) from the Board of the Company, effective 30th June
2026.
The Board and Management express their sincere gratitude to Mrs. Ogunlesi and Ms. Osima-Dokubo
for their invaluable contributions and commitment to the Company. As accomplished entrepreneurs
and business owners, they brought their wealth of commercial insight, strategic vision, and practical
business acumen to the boardroom. Their deep understanding of market dynamics and leadership
experience were instrumental in navigating challenges and advancing the Company’s growth and
governance. The Board appreciates their dedication during their tenure and wish them continued
success and fulfillment in all their future endeavors.
BOARD APPOINTMENTS
HBM Nigeria Plc (the “Company”) is pleased to announce the appointments of Dr. Fatima Akilu and
Mrs. Nella Andem-Ewa as Independent Non-Executive Directors, effective 1st July 2026. These
appointments are subject to formal ratification by the members of the Company at the next Annual
General Meeting.
The Board and Management warmly welcome Dr. Fatima Akilu and Mrs. Nella Andem-Ewa and look
forward to the valuable expertise and strategic oversight they will bring to the Company as we
continue to drive sustainable growth and excellence.
Below are the profiles of Dr. Fatima Akilu and Mrs. Nella Andem-Ewa, SAN:
Dr. Fatima Akilu – Independent Non-Executive Director
Dr. Fatima Akilu is a seasoned psychologist, peacebuilding expert, and strategic communications
leader with over 30 years of experience across public policy, security, academia, and clinical practice.
She is the Founder and Executive Director of the Neem Foundation, a leading organization focused
on preventing violent extremism, promoting mental health, and strengthening community resilience.
She previously served as Director of Behavioural Analysis and Strategic Communication at the Office
of the National Security Adviser, where she led the development of Nigeria’s countering violent
extremism programme, and has also held leadership roles at the office of Senior Special Assistant to
the President on MDG’s and the Leadership Newspapers.

Dr. Akilu holds a PhD and MSc in Psychology from the University of Reading, UK and currently serves
as Vice-Chair of the United Nations-mandated University for Peace (UPEACE), an Honorary Professor
at King's College London, and Board Member of Overseas Development Institute.
Mrs. Nella Andem-Ewa, SAN – Independent Non-Executive Director
Mrs. Nella Andem-Ewa, SAN, FCIArb, FNIPR is a distinguished legal practitioner and public policy
expert with over 30 years of experience in legal practice, governance, arbitration, and public service.
She is the Principal Partner of Lexglobal Partners, LP, a leading legal and consultancy firm in Nigeria.
She served as Attorney-General of Cross River State from 1999 to 2003 and was conferred with the
rank of Senior Advocate of Nigeria (SAN) in 2009. Mrs. Andem-Ewa has held several prominent
leadership positions, including Chairperson of the Board of Trustees of the Leprosy Mission Nigeria,
Vice Chairperson of the Nigerian Wing of the United Nations Committee on the Elimination of
Discrimination Against Women (UN CEDAW), and member of the Council of Legal Education. She has
also served on the National Executive Council of the Nigerian Bar Association, the Governing Council
of the National Human Rights Commission, and the Body of Benchers Disciplinary Committee
Mrs. Andem-Ewa is a Life Bencher, a Chartered Fellow of the Institute of Arbitration, a Fellow of the
Nigerian Institute of Public Relations, and a Fellow of the Royal Society of Arts, Manufacturing and
Commerce (UK).
