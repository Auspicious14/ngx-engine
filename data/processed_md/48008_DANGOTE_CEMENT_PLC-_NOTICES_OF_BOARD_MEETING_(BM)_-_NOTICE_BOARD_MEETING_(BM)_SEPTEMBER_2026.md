September 30, 2026
Lagos, Nigeria
NOTICE OF BOARD MEETING AND CLOSED PERIOD FOR DANGOTE CEMENT PLC.
This is to inform Nigerian Exchange Limited (NGX) and all stakeholders that a meeting
of the Board of Directors of Dangote Cement Plc (‘the Company’) will be held on
October 29, 2026, to discuss various matters including the Unaudited Financial
Statements for the quarter ending September 30, 2026 (2026 Q3 UFS).
In view of the above, Dangote Cement Plc., will enter a Closed Trading Period effective
October 1, 2026, until twenty-four (24) hours after the 2026 Q3 UFS and relevant
resolutions passed at the Board meeting are released to the public.
The Closed Trading Period applies to all parties that may at any time, possess sensitive
information that may materially affect the price of the securities of the Company.
Accordingly, no insider may deal with the Company’s securities during the Closed
Trading Period.
