TRANSCORP POWER PLC POSTS RESILIENT Q3 2026 PERFORMANCE
UGHELLI, NIGERIA October 23, 2026
Transcorp Power Plc (“Transcorp Power” or the “Company”) (NGX: TRANSPOWER),
one of the power subsidiaries of Nigeria’s leading listed conglomerate,
Transnational Corporation Plc (“Transcorp Group”), has announced its unaudited
financial results for the nine months ended September 30, 2026, reporting ₦83.1
billion in Profit Before Tax (PBT) and an improved PBT margin of 31.2%, compared
with 29.6% in the corresponding period of 2025.
KEY HIGHLIGHTS
• Revenue: ₦266.1 billion (Q3 2025: ₦308.5 billion)
• Profit Before Tax: ₦83.1 billion (Q3 2025: ₦91.18 billion)
• PBT Margin: 31.2% (Q3 2025: 29.6%)
• Total Assets: ₦629.5 billion (up 11.7%)
• Shareholders' Funds: ₦199.8 billion (up 8.9%)
• Retained Earnings: ₦147.7 billion (up 11.5%)
• Cash and Cash Equivalents: ₦10.3 billion (up 365%)
Revenue reduced by 13.7% to ₦266.1 billion (Q3 2025: ₦308.5 billion), primarily due
to endemic structural factors affecting the Nigerian electricity supply industry,
including vandalisation of critical transmission infrastructure, which constrained
power evacuation, gas supply constraints and delays in the delivery of critical
turbine parts and components due to global supply chain disruption arising from
the Middle East crisis.
Despite these challenges, the Company maintained strong operational readiness
and plant availability, delivering resilient performance with a Profit Before Tax of
₦83.1 billion and an improved PBT margin of 31.2% (Q3 2025: 29.6%).
The Company also further strengthened its financial position, through improved
collections, partial settlement of legacy market debts, and the reversal of expected
credit loss provisions, reflecting improving liquidity across the electricity market. Total
assets increased by 11.7% to ₦629.5 billion, while cash and cash equivalents grew
by 365% to ₦10.3 billion.

MD/CEO, Transcorp Power Plc, Peter Ikenga, comments:
"Our Q3 2026 results reflect the embedded resilience of our business regardless of
external challenges, including transmission constraints, vandalisation of critical
transmission infrastructure, and gas supply limitations. We have relentelessly worked
with stakeholders to address broader industry issues. Notwithstanding headwinds,
we improved our profit margin, strengthened our balance sheet, increased cash
reserves, and remained focused on operational excellence, efficient generation,
and long-term value creation for our shareholders "
Chief Finance Officer, Transcorp Power Plc, Dr. Evans Okpogoro, comments:
"Our nine-month results demonstrate strong financial discipline and resilience
despite external sector challenges that impacted revenue. We improved our PBT
margin to 31.2%, increased cash and cash equivalents by 365% to ₦10.3 billion, and
grew shareholders' funds by 8.9% to ₦199.8 billion. These results highlight the strength
of our business, prudent capital management, and our ability to deliver sustainable
value despite operational challenges largely beyond our control."
“Looking ahead, Transcorp Power will continue to pursue growth opportunities,
strengthen operational reliability and fuel security, and work across the power value
chain to support Nigeria's increasing energy needs.”
