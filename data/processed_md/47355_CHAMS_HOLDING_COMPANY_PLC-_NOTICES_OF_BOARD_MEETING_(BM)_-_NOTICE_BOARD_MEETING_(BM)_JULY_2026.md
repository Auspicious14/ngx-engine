Lagos, 1st July 2026
NOTIFICATION OF CHAMS HOLDING COMPANY PLC 82ND BOARD OF
DIRECTORS’ MEETING SCHEDULED TO HOLD ON 8TH JULY 2026
Chams Holding Company Plc (Chams or the Company) wishes to inform Nigerian Exchange
Limited (NGX) and the investing public that the 82ND Board of Directors Meeting is scheduled
to hold on Wednesday, 8th July 2026, to consider matters regarding the change of leadership
on the Board and other related matters within the period under review.
In compliance with NGX’s Listing requirements, Chams Holding Company has declared the
commencement of a closed period for trading in its shares by insiders and their connected
persons until twenty-four (24) hours after the Unaudited Financial Statements for the period
ended 30th June 2026 have been published or released to the investing public.
