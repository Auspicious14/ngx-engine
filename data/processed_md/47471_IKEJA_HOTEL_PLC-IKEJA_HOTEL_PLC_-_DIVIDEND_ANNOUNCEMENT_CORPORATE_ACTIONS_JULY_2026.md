Updated Corporate Actions Announcement
IKEJA HOTEL PLC – Updates on the AGM and Payment Dates
Period Ended 31st December 2025
A Final Dividend of 30 kobo for every share of 50 kobo, subject to
appropriate withholding tax and approval will be paid to shareholders
whose names appear in the Register of Members as at the close of business
Proposed Dividend on Friday, 3rd day of July, 2026.
Proposed Bonus NIL
The Register of Shareholders will be closed from Monday July 6, 2026, to
Closure of Register Friday, July 10, 2026.
Qualification Date Friday, July 3, 2026
On Friday, July 24, 2026, dividends will be paid electronically to
shareholders whose names appear on the Register of Members as at Friday,
July 3, 2026, and who have completed the e-dividend registration and
mandated the Registrar to pay their dividends directly into their Bank
Payment Date accounts.
Shareholders who are yet to complete the e-dividend registration are
advised to download the Registrar’s E-Dividend Mandate Activation Form,
which is also available on the website of the Registrars:
E-Dividend https://greenwichregistrars.com complete and submit to the Registrar or
Registration their respective Banks.
Shareholders with dividend warrants and share certificates that have
Unclaimed Dividend remained unclaimed, or are yet to be presented for payment or returned for
Warrants and Share validation are advised to complete the e-dividend registration or contact the
Certificates Registrar.
The Company’s Annual General Meeting will be held at Sheraton Lagos
Date of Annual General Hotel, 30 Mobolaji Bank Anthony Way, Ikeja Lagos on Monday, July 20, 2026
Meeting at 10:00 am.
