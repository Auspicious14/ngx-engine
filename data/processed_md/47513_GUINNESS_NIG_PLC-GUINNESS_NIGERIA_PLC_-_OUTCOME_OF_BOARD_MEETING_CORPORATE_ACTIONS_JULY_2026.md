GUINNESS NIGERIA PLC - OUTCOME OF BOARD MEETING
This is to inform the Nigerian Exchange Limited (NGX) and the investing public that the Board
of Directors of Guinness Nigeria Plc (the Company) at its meeting held on Wednesday
22nd July 2026, considered and approved the Unaudited Financial Statements for the quarter
ended 30th June 2026 (Unaudited Financial Statements), among other matters.
The approved Unaudited Financial Statements will therefore be filed with the regulators and
released to the investing public through the issuers' portal on or before 23rd July 2026.
Consequently, the closed period earlier communicated for trading in the shares of the
Company by insiders and their connected persons, directly or indirectly, shall continue until
24 hours after the Unaudited Financial Statements have been filed.
