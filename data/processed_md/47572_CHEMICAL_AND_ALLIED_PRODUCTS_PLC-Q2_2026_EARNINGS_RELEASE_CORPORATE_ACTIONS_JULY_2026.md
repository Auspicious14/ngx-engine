Regulated Information
Unaudited Financial Results for the Period Ended 30 June 2026
Lagos, 28 July 2026 – Chemical and Allied Products Plc (“CAP” or the “Company”), Nigeria’s leading
paints and coatings company, today announced its unaudited results for the period ended 30 June 2026.
Commenting on the results, Managing Director, Olalekan Aluko, stated:
‘We are pleased to announce our H1 2026 results, with revenue increasing by 12% and operating profit
growing by 20% year-on-year. These strong results reflect the effective execution of our strategic growth
initiatives, disciplined cost management, and continued investments in retail expansion and operational
efficiency. As we look ahead to the second half of the year, our focus remains on sustaining profitable growth
and continuous investment in our people while enhancing customer experience. We will continue to drive
innovation, strengthen operational excellence, and deliver customer-centric solutions that create long-term
value for all stakeholders’
H1 2026 Highlights
• N22.4billion revenue, 12% higher than H1 2025
• Gross profit at N9.7billion, 11% higher than H1 2025
• Operating profit at N3.8billion, 20% higher than H1 2025
• Profit before tax of N4.4billion, up 18% from N3.8billion in H1 2025

About CAP Plc
Chemical and Allied Products Plc (CAP) is a leading paints and coatings company in Nigeria with globally
recognised brands such as Dulux, Sandtex, Caplux and Hempel. CAP manufactures and sells decorative
paints and industrial coatings. CAP is the sole technological licensee of Akzo Nobel Coatings International
B.V. and is the Nigerian representative of Hempel Paints South Africa (Pty) Limited. CAP pioneered the colour
centre concept in Nigeria in 2005, which resulted in the evolution of the Nigerian paint industry. CAP is a
public company listed on The Nigerian Exchange (“NGX”). It is a subsidiary of UAC of Nigeria PLC which holds
57.85% of the company's shares. For more information visit https://www.capplc.com/
