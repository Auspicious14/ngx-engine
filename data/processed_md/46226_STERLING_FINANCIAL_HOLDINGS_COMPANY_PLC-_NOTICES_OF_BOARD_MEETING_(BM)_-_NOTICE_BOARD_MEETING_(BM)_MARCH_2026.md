Lagos, March 5, 2026
STERLING FINANCIAL HOLDINGS COMPANY PLC - NOTICE OF BOARD MEETING
TO APPROVE GROUP AUDITED FINANCIAL STATEMENTS FOR THE YEAR ENDED
31ST DECEMBER 2025
Sterling Financial Holdings Company Plc (the Company) wishes to notify the
Nigerian Exchange Limited (NGX), its shareholders, and the investing public
that a meeting of the Board of Directors of the Company will be held in Lagos
on Thursday, 12th March 2026 at 2.00 p.m. to consider the Group Audited
Financial Statements for the year ended 31st December 2025. The meeting will
also discuss issues relating to dividend, date and venue of the Annual General
Meeting.
The closed period for trading in the Company’s shares which commenced on
1st January 2026, will continue until twenty-four (24) hours after the Group
Audited Financial Statements for the year ended 31st December 2025 are
released on the floor of NGX.
Consequently, the Directors and other insiders of the Company are restricted
from dealing in the securities of the Company during the closed period.
Thank you.
For: STERLING FINANCIAL HOLDINGS COMPANY PLC
SUNNY KANABE
Company Secretary
