INSUR INVESTMENTS LIMITED AGREES A TRANSACTION FOR 30.53%
STAKE IN AIICO INSURANCE PLC
LAGOS, NIGERIA – 23 November 2026 – Cornerstone Plc (“Cornerstone”) (NGX: CORNERST) notifies
the Nigerian Exchange Limited (“NGX”), its shareholders, and the investing public that Insur
Investments Limited, an investment vehicle sponsored by Cornerstone, has agreed a transaction with
Leapfrog III Nigeria Insurance Holdings Limited relating to the acquisition of 11,173,946,135 ordinary
shares of 50 kobo each in AIICO Insurance Plc (“AIICO”). The shares represent 30.53% of AIICO’s issued
share capital.
Completion of the proposed transaction remains subject to the satisfaction of the conditions
precedent and receipt of the required regulatory approvals.
Cornerstone will keep NGX, its shareholders, and the investing public informed of material
developments, including completion of the proposed transaction.
