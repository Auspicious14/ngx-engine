PRESS RELEASE
Lagos, Monday, October 5, 2026
DELAY IN PUBLICATION OF AUDITED FINANCIAL STATEMENTS FOR THE HALF YEAR ENDED
JUNE 30, 2026
Fidelity Bank Plc (“the Bank”) wishes to inform its esteemed shareholders, Nigerian
Exchange Limited (NGX), and other stakeholders that NGX has approved a further
extension of time for publication of its Audited Financial Statements (AFS) for the Half
Year Ended June 30, 2026.
NGX had earlier approved an extension of time to September 30, 2026, as stated in the
Bank’s press release of August 21, 2026. The AFS will be published upon receipt of
regulatory approval.
Premised on the foregoing, the Bank’s Insiders and their Connected Persons are
reminded that the trading window for dealing in its shares is currently closed and shall
remain closed until twenty-four (24) hours after publication of the AFS for H1 2026 and
the Unaudited Financial Statements for the period ended 30 September 2026.
