Lagos, 14 August 2026
PZ CUSSONS NIGERIA PLC – NOTIFICATION OF BOARD MEETING, AND CLOSED
PERIOD
NOTICE IS HEREBY GIVEN THAT a Board meeting of PZ Cussons Nigeria Plc (the
“Company”) will be held on Thursday, 27 August 2026, via the Teams Meeting
Platform from 11:00 am to consider the Audited Financial Statements for the year
ended 31st May 2026.
Accordingly, in compliance with Nigerian Exchange Limited’s post-listings
requirements, the Company has declared the commencement of a closed period for
trading in its shares from 1st June 2026 until twenty-four (24) hours after the release
of the results to the Market.
Consequently, no insider of the Company or their connected persons shall deal
directly or indirectly in the securities of PZ Cussons Nigeria Plc during this closed
period.
