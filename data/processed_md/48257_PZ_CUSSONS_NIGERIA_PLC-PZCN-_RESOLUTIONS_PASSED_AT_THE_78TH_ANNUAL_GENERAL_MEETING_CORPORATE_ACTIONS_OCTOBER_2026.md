RESOLUTIONS PASSED AT THE 78TH ANNUAL GENERAL MEETING.
At the 78th Annual General Meeting of PZ Cussons Nigeria Plc held at The Ballroom, Fraser Suites, Federal
Capital Territory, Abuja, on 28th October 2026 at 11:00 am, the following Resolutions were proposed and
duly passed:
1. DECLARATION OF DIVIDEND "THAT a final dividend of N9,926,192,613
(Nine Billion, Nine Hundred and Twenty-Six
Million, One Hundred and Ninety-Two
Thousand, Six Hundred and Thirteen Naira)
that is ₦2.50 (Two Naira, Fifty Kobo) per
ordinary share of 50 Kobo (Fifty Kobo), for the
financial year ended 31st May 2026, be and is
hereby declared.
THAT the proposed final dividend shall be
subject to deduction of withholding tax at the
appropriate rate and the dividend will be
payable on 6th November 2026, to all
shareholders whose names appeared on the
Company's Register of Members at the close of
business on 9th October 2026."

7. ARTICLE 2 (DEFINITION) Special Resolution
AMENDMENT (Insert) “Scrip Dividend Arrangement or Arrangement:
means any arrangement established by the
Company, under which a shareholder may elect, in
respect of all or any part of a dividend or other
distribution declared payable in cash, to receive an
allotment of new fully paid ordinary shares in the
Company in lieu of all or part of a cash dividend or
other cash distribution payable to that shareholder,
on such terms and subject to such conditions,
election procedures, record dates, pricing
methodology, scaling arrangements and other
requirements as the Directors may determine,
subject to the Act and these Articles.”
8. INCLUSION OF ARTICLE 122 Special Resolution
TO THE ARTICLES OF
ASSOCIATION Application of Scrip Dividend Arrangement
“Subject to the Act and these Articles, where the
Company declares a dividend payable in cash, the
directors may determine that members entitled to
such dividends may elect to receive, in lieu of all or
part of the cash dividend payable to them, an
allotment of new fully paid shares in the Company
pursuant to a Scrip Dividend Arrangement, and shall
give such members an opportunity to make such
election upon such terms and conditions of the
Arrangement as approved by the Board.”
9. INCLUSION OF ARTICLE 123 Terms and Conditions of Scrip Dividend
TO THE ARTICLES OF Arrangement
ASSOCIATION
The directors shall determine the terms and
conditions of any Scrip Dividend Arrangement,
including the basis on which the number of shares
to be allotted to an electing Member shall be
calculated, the reference price or other pricing
methodology, the applicable record date, the period
and procedure for making an election, the eligible
minimum number of shares, the manner in which
fractional entitlements are rounded, or aggregated
subject in each case to the Act and any law or
regulatory requirements.

10. INCLUSION OF ARTICLE 124 TO THE Notice and Election
ARTICLES OF ASSOCIATION
The directors shall give notice of the Scrip Dividend
Arrangement to the members, specifying the
procedure and deadline for making an election. An
election made by a Member in accordance with the
terms of the Arrangement shall, subject to the terms
of the Arrangement, be irrevocable after the
applicable election deadline.
11. INCLUSION OF ARTICLE 125 TO THE Ranking of Scrip Shares
ARTICLES OF ASSOCIATION
Shares allotted pursuant to a Scrip Dividend
Arrangement shall rank pari passu in all respects
with the existing issued ordinary shares of the
Company.
12. INCLUSION OF ARTICLE 126 TO THE Default to Cash Dividend
ARTICLES OF ASSOCIATION
Where a member does not make a valid election to
participate in the Scrip Dividend Arrangement
within the period specified in the relevant notice
(or as may be amended at the Annual General
Meeting), the Member shall be entitled to receive
the relevant dividend in cash in accordance with the
terms of the dividend declaration, or as may be
amended at the Annual General Meeting.
