Lagos, 3 July 2026
NOTICE OF BOARD OF DIRECTORS’ MEETING
NOTICE IS HEREBY GIVEN that the 18th Meeting of the Board of Directors of Consolidated
Hallmark Holdings Plc (the Company) will be held as follows:
DATE: Wednesday, July 29, 2026.
TIME: 10:00 am.
VENUE: Virtual.
The Meeting is to consider the Company’s Unaudited Financial Statements for the period ended
30th June 2026 (2026 Q2 UFS), amongst other items.
In view of the above, the Company hereby announces that its Closed Period has commenced on
Tuesday, 1st July 2026, and will remain until twenty-four (24) hours after the 2026 Q2 UFS is
released to the public.
Within this period, all Insiders of the Company in accordance with the provisions of the Rules
and Regulations of Nigerian Exchange Limited (The Exchange), are under restriction not to trade
or deal in the shares of the Company.
BY ORDER OF THE BOARD
Rukevwe Falana
Company Secretary
