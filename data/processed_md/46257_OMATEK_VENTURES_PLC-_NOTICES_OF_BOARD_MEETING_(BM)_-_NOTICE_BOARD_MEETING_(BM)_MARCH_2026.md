NOTICE OF PROPOSED BOARD MEETING AND CLOSED PERIOD
OMATEK VENTURES PLC. (the Company) wishes to inform Nigerian Exchange Limited
(NGX), its Shareholders and the investing public that the Board of Directors of the
Company will meet on Friday, March 27, 2026 to amongst other matters, consider the
Audited Financial Statements for the year ended December 31, 2025 (2025 AFS) and
the First Quarter Unaudited Financial Statements for the period ending March 31,
2026 (2026 Q1 UFS) and other issues relating to the business of the Company.
In line with NGX’s Rules on Closed Period (As Amended), the Company commenced a
Closed Period from Thursday January 1, 2026 and this will continue to be in force until
twenty-four (24) hours after the filing of the 2025 AFS and the 2026 Q1 UFS and other
Issues relating to the business of the Company have been released to the public via
NGX’s Issuers’ Portal.
During the closed period, all insiders of the Company and their connected persons are
restricted from dealing in the securities of the Company.
