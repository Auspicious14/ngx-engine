9 October 2026
Lagos, Nigeria
RESOLUTIONS PASSED AT THE 38TH ANNUAL GENERAL MEETING OF
FIDELITY BANK PLC HELD ON OCTOBER 9, 2026
At the 38th Annual General Meeting of Fidelity Bank Plc duly convened and held virtually via
https://www.fidelitybank.ng/agm/ at 10.00a.m. on Friday, October 9, 2026, the following
resolutions were proposed and duly passed:
Ordinary Business:
1) “That the Audited Financial Statements for the year ended December 31, 2025, and the
Reports of the Directors, Auditors, Audit Committee and Independent Board Appraisal
Consultants thereon laid before the meeting be and are hereby received”.
2) “That the election of Dr. Jonathan O. Ososuakpor, Non-Executive Director, of the
Company be and is hereby approved”.
3) “That the re-election of Mrs. Amaka Onwughalu, Chairman, Non-Executive Director of
the Company be and is hereby approved”.
4) “That the retirement of Chief Nelson Nweke, Non- Executive Director, with effect from
October 9, 2026, be and is hereby approved”.
5) “That the Directors be and are hereby authorized to fix the remuneration of the External
Auditors for 2026 financial year”.
6) “That the remuneration of the Managers of the Company as disclosed in Note 40 of the
Audited Accounts for 2025 be and is hereby noted.”
7) “That the Statutory Audit Committee of the Company be comprised of the following
members with effect from October 9, 2026, until the next Annual General Meeting in line
with the requirements of Section 404 of the Companies and Allied Matters Act, 2020:
(a) Chief Frank Onwu - Shareholder
(b) Mr. Innocent Mmuoh - Shareholder
(c) Alhaji Kabiru Tambari - Shareholder
(d) Alhaji Abdullahi Mohammed - Independent Non-Executive Director
(e) Ms. Obiaku Okam - Non- Executive Director”
FOR: FIDELITY BANK PLC
EZINWA UNUIGBOJE
Company Secretary
