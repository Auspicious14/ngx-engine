Lagos, Nigeria, 25th March 2026
NEIMETH INTERNATIONAL PHARMACEUTICALS PLC – NOTICE OF BOARD MEETING
Further to the Notice of Board meeting dated 11th March 2026, this is to notify Nigerian Exchange
Limited (NGX) and the investing public that the meeting of the Board of Directors of Neimeth
International Pharmaceuticals Plc. (the Company) scheduled to hold on Wednesday, 25th March 2026
to consider and approve the Company’s Audited Financial Statements for the year ended 31st December
2025 (2025 AFS) will no longer hold. The meeting has been rescheduled for Friday, 27th March 2026.
In line with NGX’s Amended Closed Period Rules, the closed period commenced on 1st January, 2026
and communicated to Directors and other Insiders will continue to run up to twenty-four (24) hours
after the outcome of the meeting and the 2025 AFS is released to the public.
Thank you.
CHINENYE S. ADEKANMBI
Company Secretary
