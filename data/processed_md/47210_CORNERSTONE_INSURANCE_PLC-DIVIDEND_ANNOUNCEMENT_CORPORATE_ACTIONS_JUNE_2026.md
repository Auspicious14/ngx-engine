Corporate Actions Announcement
CORNERSTONE INSURANCE PLC hereby announce as follows:
Period Ended 31st December 2025
A Final Dividend of N0.28 kobo for every share of N0.50 kobo, subject to
appropriate withholding tax and approval will be paid to shareholders whose names
appear in the Register of Members as at the close of business on 2nd day of July, 2026.
Proposed Dividend
Proposed Bonus NIL
The Register of Shareholders will be closed from the Friday, 3rd July, 2026 to Monday,
Closure of Register 6th July, 2026
Qualification Date 2nd day of July, 2026
On the 20th day of July, 2026, dividends will be paid electronically to shareholders
whose names appear on the Register of Members as at 2nd day of July, 2026 and who
have completed the e-dividend registration and mandated the Registrar to pay their
Payment Date dividends directly into their Bank accounts.
Shareholders who are yet to complete the e-dividend registration are advised to complete
the link EDMMS self-service portal: https://docuhub3.nibss-plc.com.ng/edmms/self-
service which is also available on the website of the Company:
www.cornerstone.com.ng or submit to the Registrar, Lighthouse Registrars Limited,
at their registered address on the 2nd Floor, 39, Adeola Odeku Street, Victoria
E-Dividend Registration Island, Lagos, or via lighthouseRegistrars@
lighthouse.com.ng.
Shareholders with dividend warrants and share certificates that have remained
Unclaimed Dividend unclaimed, or are yet to be presented for payment or returned for validation are advised
Warrants and Share to complete the link https://cornerstone.com.ng/corporate/
Certificates unclaimdividend.php or contact the Registrar.
Date of Annual General The Company’s Annual General Meeting will be held at Shell Hall, MUSON Centre,
Meeting Onikan, Lagos State on 20th day of July, 2026 at 12 pm.
Lighthouse Registrars Limited, Phone: 08037446324; email:
Registrar lighthouseRegistrars@ lighthouse.com.ng.
Investor Relations Phone No. 2348128719169 email: cekeocha@cornerstone.com.ng
Note: The payment date has been amended
Dated this 19th day of June, 2026
FOR: Cornerstone Insurance Plc
PAC Solicitors
Company Secretary
