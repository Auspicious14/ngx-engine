Lagos 4 September 2026
CHAPEL HILL DENHAM NIGERIA REAL ESTATE INVESTMENT TRUST – Q2 2026 DISTRIBUTION ANNOUNCEMENT
Distribution Period 30th June 2026
Distribution N1.72 per unit (will be paid to unitholders whose names appeared in the Register of
Unitholders as at the close of business on 29th July 2026, subject to the deduction
of appropriate withholding tax). Distributions will be payable in the currency in
which income is earned.
Bonus Nil
Qualification Date 29th July 2026
Closure of Register 30th July 2026
Payment Date 8 September 2026
The distribution will be paid - electronically - to Unitholders whose names appear
on the Register of Unitholders as of 29th July 2026 (and have completed the E-
Mandate Form and authorised the Registrar to pay their distributions directly into
their Bank accounts)
E-Mandate Unitholders who have not yet completed the E-Mandate are advised to download
the Registrar’s E-Mandate Activation Form, which is available on the website of the
Registrars: https://coronationregistrars.com/e-mandate/
Please complete and submit the E-Mandate Activation Form to the Registrar.
