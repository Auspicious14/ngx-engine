1.General Corporate Information
Presco Plc was incorporated in Nigeria on 24th September, 1991 as Presco Industries Limited, a private limited liability company under
the Companies and Allied Matters Act, and became a public limited liability Company in February, 2002. Presco Plc shares are
actively traded on The Nigerian Stock Exchange with the Siat Group holding 61.2% while the Nigeria Public holds 38.8%.
