Lagos, Nigeria, Tuesday, April 7, 2026
NOTIFICATION OF CHANGE OF BOARD MEETING DATE
Custodian Investment Plc (the “Company”) wishes to inform Nigerian Exchange Limited (NGX),
its esteemed shareholders, and the investing public that the meeting of the Board of Directors
of the Company, earlier scheduled to hold on Wednesday, April 29, 2026, to consider, amongst
other agenda items, the Company’s Unaudited Financial Statements for the first quarter
ended March 31, 2026 (Q1 2026 UFS), has been rescheduled.
The meeting will now be held on Tuesday, April 28, 2026, at 10:00 a.m.
The previously announced Closed Period prohibiting trading in the shares of the Company by
insiders remains unchanged and will continue until twenty-four (24) hours after the Q1 2026 UFS
has been released to Nigerian Exchange Limited and the investing public.
