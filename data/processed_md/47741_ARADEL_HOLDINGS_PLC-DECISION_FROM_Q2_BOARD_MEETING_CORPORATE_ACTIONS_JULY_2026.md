Lagos, July 31st, 2026
ARADEL HOLDINGS PLC: NOTICE OF DECISION BY THE BOARD OF
DIRECTORS
The Board of Directors of Aradel Holdings Plc wishes to inform the general public and
Nigerian Exchange Limited (NGX) that the Board, at its meeting which held on
Wednesday, 29th July 2026, reviewed and approved its H1 2026 Unaudited Financial
Results (“H1 2026 UFS”) for the period ended 30th June 2026.
The H1 2026 UFS will be released to the public through the Issuers’ Portal. Consequently,
the Closed Period will continue until twenty-four hours after the H1 2026 UFS is released
to the public.
