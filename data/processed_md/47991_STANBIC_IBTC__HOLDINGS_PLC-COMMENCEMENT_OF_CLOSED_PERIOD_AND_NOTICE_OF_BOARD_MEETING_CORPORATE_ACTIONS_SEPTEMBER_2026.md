Stanbic IBTC Holdings PLC
Commencement of Closed Period and Notice of a Meeting of the Board of Directors
Lagos, 30 September 2026
In accordance with the provisions of Rule 17.18(b) of the Rulebook of Nigerian Exchange
Limited (the Rules), Stanbic IBTC Holdings Plc (“the Company”) would like to notify its
shareholders and Nigerian Exchange Limited (NGX) that the Company’s closed period
will commence with effect from 01 October 2026. The closed period will continue to be in
place until 24 hours after the Third Quarter 2026 Unaudited Financial Statements of the
Company are released to NGX.
Please note that in accordance with the above Rules as well as the Company’s Internal
Personal Account Trading Policy, we have advised all the Company’s Insiders that they
are prohibited from trading in the shares of the Company during the closed period.
Pursuant to the above and in accordance with the provisions of Rule 19.2(b) of the NGX’s
Rulebook 2015, Issuers’ Rules relating to Board Meetings and General Meetings of
Issuers, a meeting of the Board of Directors of Stanbic IBTC Holdings PLC is scheduled
to hold on Friday, 23 October 2026 at 10:00am. The meeting will discuss amongst other
items, the Company’s Third Quarter Unaudited Financial Statements for the quarter
ending 30 September 2026.
