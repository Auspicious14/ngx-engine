Lagos, 16 November 2026
LASACO ASSURANCE PLC
NOTIFICATION OF APPROVED FREE FLOAT COMPLIANCE EXTENSION
Lasaco Assurance Plc (“Lasaco or the Company”) hereby notifies its shareholders
and the investing public that the Company’s free float is below the minimum
requirement of 20% of issued share capital applicable to companies listed on the
Main Board of Nigerian Exchange Limited (NGX).
NGX Regulation Limited (“NGX RegCo”) has granted the Company a one (1)year
extension, until 2 November 2027, to enable the Company to comply with NGX’s
free float requirements of 20% issued and fully paid share capital or Twenty Billion
Naira free float market capitalization for companies listed on its Main Board and to
ensure that the Company returns to its post listing obligations. This is in line with
Rule 3.1.4 of The Exchange’s Rules Governing Free Float Requirements, which
states that “The Exchange may suspend trading in the Company’s securities if the
Company does not achieve the required free float within the stipulated timeframe”.
The Board and Management of Lasaco remain committed to taking all necessary
steps to achieve the required free float within the approved period and will keep
shareholders and NGX RegCo informed of progress.
