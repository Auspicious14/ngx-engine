Airtel Africa plc
("Airtel Africa", or "Group")
Announcement of Interim Dividend Currency Exchange Rates
London and Lagos, 3 November 2026: Airtel Africa, a leading pan-African provider of
telecommunications and mobile money services, with a presence in 14 countries across Africa,
today confirms the default currency and options on currency election for the interim dividend
(declared by the Board to be 3.1 cents per ordinary share payable on 11 December 2026 to
shareholders on the register at of close of business on 13 November 2026). The arrangements for
the interim dividend, and the currency exchange rates that will be applicable in determining the
interim dividend payment to any shareholders that qualify for such dividend payment, are set out
below.
Arrangements for Shareholders that hold their shares on the London Stock Exchange (LSE):
• Shareholders that hold their shares on the London Stock Exchange may elect to receive
their dividend payment in GB pounds or U.S. dollars.
• The last date to trade shares in order to be entitled to the dividend is 11 November 2026.
• CREST participants can elect to receive their dividend payment via CREST.
• Holders electing to receive a dividend should specify a choice of currency by completing the
Currency Election Form and return to Computershare Investor Services plc.
• Currency election forms can be obtained by contacting the Company's
Registrars, Computershare Investor Services plc, on the Company's dedicated telephone
line: +44 (0)370 7030027.
• The closing date for the dividend currency election to the Company's Registrars
Computershare Investor Services plc in the UK is 23 November 2026.
• Shareholders holding their shares on the LSE who do not indicate their currency of choice
before the closing date for currency election will receive their dividends in U.S. dollars.
Arrangements for Shareholders that hold their shares on the Nigerian Exchange Limited (NGX):
• Shareholders that hold their shares on the Nigerian Exchange Limited may elect to receive
their dividend payment in Nigerian naira or U.S. dollars.
• The last date to trade shares in order to be entitled to the dividend is 12 November 2026
(Qualification date).
• Holders electing to receive a dividend in U.S. dollars should complete the Currency
Election Form and return to Coronation Registrars Limited or email to
eforms@coronationregistrars.com
• Currency election forms can be found on the website of the Company’s Registrar
Coronation.ng or this direct link: Currency Election Form
• The closing date for the dividend currency election to the Company's Registrars Coronation
Registrars Limited in Nigeria is 23 November 2026.
• Shareholders holding their shares on the NGX who do not indicate their currency of choice
before the closing date for currency election will receive their dividends in Nigerian naira.
