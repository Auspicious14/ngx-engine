Lagos, 28th April 2026
RESOLUTION OF THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF SUNU ASSURANCES NIGERIA PLC
In line with the Rules of Nigerian Exchange Limited (NGX) for listed companies relating to Board Meetings and
General Meetings of Issuers, SUNU Assurances Nigeria Plc (the Company) wishes to inform its valued
shareholders and the general public that the Board of Directors at its meeting wh8ch held on Monday 27th April
2026, considered and approved the Unaudited Financial Statements for the first quarter ended 31st March,
2026. (Q1 2026 UFS)
The approved Q1 2026 UFS would be filed on or before 30th April 2026, in line with the rules of NGX.
Pursuant to Rule 17:18 (a) of the Exchange’s Rulebook on Closed Period (as amended), the existing Closed
Period shall remain in effect until twenty-four (24) hours after the release of the Company’s Q1 2026 UFS to
the public.
