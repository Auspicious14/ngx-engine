Lagos, July 1, 2026
RESOLUTIONS PASSED AT THE 13TH ANNUAL GENERAL MEETING OF FCMB GROUP PLC
At the 13th Annual General Meeting of FCMB Group Plc (the Company) held virtually on Tuesday,
June 30, 2026, the following resolutions were duly passed:
1. That the Report of the Directors and the Audited Financial Statements of the Company for the
year ended December 31, 2025, together with the Report of the Auditors thereon and the
Statutory Audit Committee Report, be and are hereby adopted.
2. That a dividend payment of N0.35 Kobo per ordinary share be and is hereby declared payable
to members so entitled whose names appear in the Register of Members at the close of business
on June 15, 2026.
3. That the appointment of Mrs. Adepeju Adebajo as an Independent Non-Executive Director of
the Company be and is hereby ratified.
4. That Mr. Ladi Jadesimi be and is hereby re-elected as a Non-Executive Director of the Company.
5. That the Directors be and are hereby authorized to fix the remuneration of the Auditors.
6. That the remuneration of managers of the Company, as disclosed on page 241 of the Annual
Reports and Accounts, was noted by members in line with section 257 of the Companies and
Allied Matters Act 2020.
7. That the under-listed shareholders’ representatives be and are hereby elected as members of
the Statutory Audit Committee for the 2026 Financial Year:
i. Evangelist Soares Peter Akinola
ii. Mr. Hakeem Abayomi Batula
iii. Mr. Victor Olusegun Adeniran Owolabi
The following Non-Executive Directors were nominated to represent the Board on the Statutory
Audit Committee:
i. Ms. Muibat Ijaiya
ii. Mrs. Adepeju Adebajo
8. That the Non-Executive Directors’ remuneration for the year ending December 31, 2026 be
maintained at N280,000,000.00 (Two Hundred and Eighty Million Naira) only.
OLUFUNMILAYO ADEDIBU (MRS.)
COMPANY SECRETARY
