Lagos, Nigeria
11th March 2026
APPROVAL OF AUDITED CONSOLIDATED AND SEPARATE FINANCIAL STATEMENTS FOR THE
YEAR ENDED 31ST DECEMBER 2025.
This is to notify Nigeria Exchange Limited (“the Exchange”), our stakeholders and the investing public
that the Board of NEM Insurance Plc (“the Company”) met on Wednesday, 11th March 2026 and
approved the Audited Consolidated and Separate Financial Statements for the year ended 31 December
2025 (2025 AFS) and payment of a Dividend subject however, to the approval of the National Insurance
Commission (“the Commission”).
Consequently, the Exchange and the investing public would be notified upon approval of the Audited
Consolidated and Separate Financial Statements for the year ended 31 December 2025 by the
Commission.
The Closed Period which commenced on 1st January 2026 will continue until twenty-four (24) hours after
the Audited Consolidated and Separate Financial Statements (2025 AFS) have been released and filed
via the Exchange’s Issuers’ Portal.
Accordingly, no insider of the Company and/or their connected persons may, directly or indirectly deal
with/in the shares of the Company during the Closed Period.
For further enquiries, please contact nem@nem-insurance.com.
