CADBURY NIGERIA PLC – NOTIFICATION OF RESIGNATION OF DIRECTOR
This to notify Nigerian Exchange Limited and the investing public that the Board has considered and
accepted the resignation of Mrs. Ibukun Awosika as Director of Cadbury Nigeria Plc (the Company) with
effect from Friday, 1st May 2026.
The Board expresses its deep appreciation to Mrs. Awosika for her valued contribution since his
appointment in October 2009.
Mrs. Awosika’s replacement on the Board of the Company will be announced in due course.
