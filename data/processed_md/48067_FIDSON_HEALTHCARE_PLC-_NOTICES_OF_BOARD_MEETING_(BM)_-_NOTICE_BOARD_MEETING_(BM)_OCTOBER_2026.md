NOTICE OF THE 120TH BOARD MEETING
Notice is hereby given to Nigerian Exchange Limited (The Exchange) and the
investing public, pursuant to The Exchange's Rules, that a Board meeting of
Fidson Healthcare Plc (the Company) has been scheduled for Thursday, 22nd of
October 2026.
One of the items on the agenda is the consideration and approval of the draft
Unaudited Financial Statements for the third quarter ended 30th September 2026
(2026 Q3 UFS).
Accordingly, a closed period to prevent Insiders from trading in the shares of the
Company has been in force since the 1st of October 2026 in line with the listing
rules, until twenty-four (24) hours after the release of the 2026 Q3 UFS to the
public.
