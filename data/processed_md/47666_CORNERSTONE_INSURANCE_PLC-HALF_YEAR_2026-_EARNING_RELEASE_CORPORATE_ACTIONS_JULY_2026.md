Cornerstone Insurance Plc Reports Insurance Revenue of ₦29.01 Billion for HY 2026, Up 18.6% Year-on-
Year
LAGOS, NIGERIA – 30 July 2026 – Cornerstone Insurance Plc (NGX: CORNERST) ("Cornerstone" or the "Group")
today announced its Unaudited Financial Results for the six months ended 30 June 2026, reporting continued
growth in insurance revenue and a strengthened capital base, supported by resilient underwriting performance
across its life and non-life businesses.
Performance Summary
The Group delivered a solid operating performance in the first half of 2026, underpinned by continued growth across
its core insurance businesses. Insurance revenue increased by 18.6% to ₦29.01 billion, while insurance service
result rose by 8.9% to ₦8.87 billion, reflecting higher business volumes and continued underwriting discipline. The
Group also maintained a strong financial position, with total assets increasing to ₦150.12 billion and shareholders'
funds rising by 11.5% from year-end 2025.
Financial Performance Highlights
Earnings Analysis
 Insurance revenue increased 18.6% to ₦29.01 billion in the first half of 2026, from ₦24.47 billion in the
corresponding period of 2025. The growth was driven by increased contributions across the Group's life and
non-life insurance businesses, reflecting sustained demand from policyholders and business partners.
 Insurance service result increased by 8.9% to ₦8.87 billion, compared with ₦8.15 billion in H1 2025,
reflecting improved underwriting performance supported by higher insurance revenue.
 Net insurance and investment result, which combines underwriting performance with returns on the
Group's investment portfolio, declined by 4.6% to ₦10.37 billion from ₦10.87 billion in the prior-year
period. This reflected higher net foreign exchange losses and lower fair value gains during the period
against a strong comparative base in H1 2025.
 Profit before tax was ₦6.12 billion, compared with ₦7.47 billion in H1 2025, while profit after tax declined
to ₦5.26 billion from ₦6.73 billion in the corresponding period. The reduction in profitability was driven
primarily by non-operating, market-related factors—principally foreign exchange movements and lower
investment valuation gains—rather than by the performance of the Group's core insurance operations.
 Earnings per share was 30 kobo, compared with 31 kobo in H1 2025.
Financial Position
The Group maintained a strong financial position during the period, supported by continued earnings retention and
growth in shareholders' equity.
 Total assets increased by 6.4% to ₦150.12 billion as at 30 June 2026, from ₦141.03 billion at 31 December
2025.
 Shareholders' funds increased by 11.5% to ₦81.22 billion from ₦72.86 billion at year-end 2025, reflecting
retained earnings during the period and favourable fair value movements on the Group's investment
portfolio.
Outlook
Building on its strong operating performance in the first half of 2026, Cornerstone remains focused on deepening its
underwriting franchise, strengthening its capital position and advancing the implementation of its proposed holding
company structure, which is designed to separate the Group's life and general insurance businesses in line with
evolving regulatory requirements. The Board and Management remain committed to delivering sustainable long-
term value for shareholders, policyholders and other stakeholders.
