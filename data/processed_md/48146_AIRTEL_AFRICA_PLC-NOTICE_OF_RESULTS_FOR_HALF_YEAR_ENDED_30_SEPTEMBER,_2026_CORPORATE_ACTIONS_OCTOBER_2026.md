Airtel Africa is a leading provider of telecommunications and mobile money services, with operations in 14

voice and data services as well as mobile money services both nationally and internationally. The company’s
strategy is focused on providing a great customer experience across the entire footprint, enabling our
corporate purpose of transforming lives across Africa.
