GUINNESS NIGERIA PLC
Lagos, 31 March 2026
GUINNESS NIGERIA PLC - NOTIFICATION OF CLOSED PERIOD
In line with Nigerian Exchange Limited (NGX) post listing requirements, Guinness Nigeria
Plc (the “Company”), hereby notifies NGX, its shareholders and the investing public that
it is declaring a Closed Period with effect from 1st of April 2026 until twenty-four (24) hours
after the release of the Company’s Unaudited Financial Statements for the quarter
ending 31st March 2026 to the public through NGX Issuers’ Portal.
Therefore, no insider of the Company and their connected persons may directly or
indirectly deal in the securities of the Company during the closed period.
