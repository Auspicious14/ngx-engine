DEAP CAPITAL MANAGEMENT AND TRUST PLC APPOINTS DUTCH LAMON RUTTEN AS
CHAIRMAN
Lagos, Nigeria — March 18, 2026
Deap Capital Management & Trust Plc (Deap Capital or the Company) has announced the
appointment of Mr. Lamon Rutten as Chairman of the Board, following the company’s recent 12th
Annual General Meeting and subsequent inaugural Board meeting held on 12th March 2026 by the
newly constituted Board.
Mr. Rutten is a globally respected expert in commodity markets, structured commodity finance, and
exchange development, bringing more than thirty (30) years of international experience across
Europe, Asia, the Middle East, and Africa.
Lamon, a Dutch national with extensive experience in Middle East, Africa and South Asia, holds a
master’s degree with honours in International Economic Management from Tilburg University,
Netherlands. He is widely reputed as a pioneer in the development of modern commodity trading and
financing infrastructure worldwide having served at Chie of Energy at the United Nations.
Mr. Rutten previously served as pioneering Chief Executive Officer of the Multi Commodity Exchange
of India (MCX) also known as the Mumbai Commodity Exchange, where he led the exchange through
a period of significant growth and quantum leap, establishing it as the world’s second largest
commodity exchanges. Under his leadership, MCX achieved a landmark public listing valued at
approximately $1.5 billion and a revenue of over $2.5 trillion by 2012.
He has also held several high-profile leadership and advisory roles globally, including serving as
Founding CEO of the Indonesia Commodity & Derivatives Exchange (ICDX) and playing a key role in
the development of the Saudi Mining Exchange initiative. Earlier in his career, he worked with the United
Nations Conference on Trade and Development (UNCTAD) where he led international program
focused on commodity risk management and structured trade finance.
Mr. Rutten is also a prolific author and policy expert, having published numerous research papers and
policy works on commodity markets, trade finance, and risk management, and has advised
governments, multilateral institutions, and financial organization on commodity market development.
Commenting on the appointment, Dr. Israel Ovirih, President of the Company, stated:
“We are pleased to welcome Lamon Rutten as Chairman of Deap Capital Management and Trust Plc.
His extensive global experience in commodity markets and financial infrastructure development will be
instrumental in guiding the Company as we build a world-class platform to finance and support the
growth of the critical minerals sector across continental Africa.”
Directors: Dr. Kenneth Olise, (Chairman), Mr. Edmund Ani, (Vice Chairman) Mr. Tony Adigwe (Managing Director),
Mr. Joe Idudu, Mr. Joel Omole, Dr. Joseph Asaolu

He stated further that, “The new chairman and his global experience should enable the unleashing of
new possibilities in the African metals and minerals space, including bringing new thoughts on how to
modern and formalize artisanal mining specifically in Nigeria”
Commenting on his appointment, Mr. Lamon Rutten, the new Chairman, stated
“It’s a new dawn for the African metals and minerals financing space; I appreciate my colleagues
unanimous decision asking me to chair the new board, the future starts now”
Mr. Rutten’s appointment further strengthens Deap Capital Management and Trust Plc’s leadership as
the company advances its mission of mobilizing capital and building financial solutions to unlock
Africa’s critical minerals potential, positioning the organization as a strategic bridge between global
investors and Africa’s emerging resource economy.
