Lagos, July 2, 2026
AXA MANSARD ANNOUNCES CHANGES TO THE BOARD
This is to notify the Nigerian Exchange Limited (NGX) and the investing public that Ms. Melina Cotlar,
a Non-Executive Director on the Board of AXA Mansard Insurance Plc. (the Company) has resigned
from the Board with effect from 29th May 2026.
Ms. Melina Cotlar obtained regulatory approval to join the Board of the Company on 6th of April 2023
as a Non-Executive Director. She served as a member of the Board Technical and Risk Management
Committee.
The Board and Management of the Company commend Ms. Melina Cotlar for her positive
contribution and service to the growth of the Company during her tenure on the Board.
for: AXA MANSARD INSURANCE PLC
OMOWUNMI MABEL ADEWUSI
Company Secretary
