Lagos, July 20, 2026.
RESOLUTIONS PASSED AT THE 28th ANNUAL GENERAL MEETING OF TANTALIZERS PLC
The shareholders of Tantalizers PLC (“the Company”) at its 28th Annual General Meeting held
virtually on Thursday, 16th day of July 2026 at 11:00 am, proposed and passed the following
resolutions:
ORDINARY BUSINESS
1. That the Audited Financial Statements for the year ended 31st December 2025, the Reports
of the Directors and Auditors and Audit Committee thereon submitted to the meeting be
received and approved.
2. That the appointment of Tade Ogidan as a Director of the Company be hereby approved.
3. That the following Directors retiring by rotation be hereby approved
a. Bose Ayeni (Mrs)
b. Bamidele Oke
c. Dr Israel Ovirih
4. That the Directors of the Company be and are hereby authorized to fix the remuneration
of the Auditors for the 2026 financial year.
5. That pursuant to Section 404(3) of the Companies and Allied Matters Act 2020, the Statutory
Audit Committee of the Company be and is hereby appointed until the completion of the
Company’s next Annual General Meeting. The following are to constitute the membership
of the Statutory Audit Committee:
(a) Mr. Bode Akinboye (Non-Executive Director)
(b) Mrs. Bose Ayeni (Non-Executive Director)
(c) Mr. Peter Eyanuku (Shareholders’ Representative)
(d) Mr. Babatunde Elekede (Shareholders’ Representative)
(e) Mr. Abayomi Adebayo (Shareholders’ Representative)
6. That pursuant to Section 257 of the Companies and Allied Matters Act 2020, that the
remuneration of the Managers of the Company be and is hereby disclosed at the Meeting.
SPECIAL BUSINESSES / ORDINARY RESOLUTIONS

7. That the Directors of the Company be and are hereby authorized to fix the remuneration
of Non-Executive Directors for the year ending December 31, 2026.
8. That the general mandate given to the Company to enter into recurrent transactions of a
revenue or trading nature with related parties or interested persons for the Company's day-
to-day operations, be and is hereby renewed.'
Olamide Babawale-Mo
Company Secretary
