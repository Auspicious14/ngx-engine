Lagos, Nigeria, 30 March 2026
UAC of Nigeria PLC hereby announces as follows:
Period Ended Year Ended 31 December 2025.
Proposed Dividend A Dividend of N1.00 per ordinary share of 50 kobo each, subject to the
appropriate withholding tax and approval at the Annual General Meeting,
will be paid to shareholders whose names appear on the Register of
Members as at the close of business on 11 June 2026 (“Qualifying
Shareholders”).
Proposed Bonus Not Applicable.
Closure of Register The Register of Members will be closed from 12 June to 18 June 2026 (both
dates inclusive).
Qualification Date 11 June 2026.
Cash Dividend On 26 June 2026, cash dividend will be paid electronically to Qualifying
Payment Date Shareholders, and who have completed the e-dividend registration and
mandated the Registrar to pay their dividends directly into their Bank
accounts.
E-Dividend Qualifying Shareholders who are yet to complete the e-dividend registration
Registration are advised to download the Registrar’s E-Dividend Form - Africa Prudential,
which is available on our Registrars website: www.africaprudential.com,
complete and submit to the Registrar or their respective Banks.
Unclaimed Dividend Qualifying Shareholders with dividend warrants and share certificates that
Warrants and Share have remained unclaimed or are yet to be presented for payment or
Certificates returned for validation are advised to complete the e-dividend registration
or contact the Registrar.
Date of Annual The Company’s Annual General Meeting will be held at Festival Hotel,
General Meeting Diamond Estate, Amuwo Odofin, Lagos, Nigeria on Thursday 25 June 2026,
at 10.00am.
