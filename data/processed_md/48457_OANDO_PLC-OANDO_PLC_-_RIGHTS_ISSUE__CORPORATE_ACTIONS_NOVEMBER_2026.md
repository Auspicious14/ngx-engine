Oando PLC Announces Final Terms and Timetable for ₦203.8 Billion Rights Issue
Lagos, Nigeria | 27 November 2026
Oando PLC (“Oando” or the “Company”), listed on the Nigerian Exchange Limited
(NGX) and the Johannesburg Stock Exchange (JSE), announces the finalisation of
the terms of its ₦203.8 billion rights issue (the “Rights Issue”) following receipt of all
regulatory approvals.
The Rights Issue forms an important part of Oando’s broader capital programme,
aimed at strengthening its balance sheet, supporting its operating businesses, and
providing capital for further development of its upstream oil and gas assets.
The Company is offering up to 4,076,185,240 new ordinary shares of 50 kobo each
at N50.00 per share, on the basis of one (1) new ordinary share for every two (2)
existing ordinary shares held by qualifying shareholders on the applicable record
date.
The Rights Issue follows Oando's acquisition of the Nigerian Agip Oil Company in
August 2024, which increased its participating interest in OMLs 60 to 63 from 20% to
40% and transferred operatorship of the assets to the Company. The transaction
materially expanded Oando's upstream operating platform, creating opportunities for
further investment in production growth and cash generation.
The Issue forms part of Oando’s recapitalisation program and expected gross
proceeds of approximately N203.8 billion, assuming full subscription, will be applied
towards working capital requirements and capital expenditure on the Company’s
OMLs 60 to 63, debt repayment and settlement of intra-group funding obligations, in
accordance with the approved Rights Circular.
Commenting on the Rights Issue, Mr Wale Tinubu, CON, the Group Chief Executive
of Oando PLC said:
“Over the past three (3) decades, we have built Oando from a petroleum trading
business into an integrated African energy company with a significant upstream
operating platform. The acquisition of the Nigerian Agip Oil Company in 2024 was a
defining milestone in that journey, doubling our participating interest in OMLs 60 to
63 and placing us in operatorship of these strategic assets.

“Our priority now is to translate this expanded asset base into sustainable production
growth, stronger cash generation and long-term value for our shareholders.
Achieving this requires a capital structure that can support our operations, fund
further investment, and provide the financial flexibility to pursue the opportunities
ahead.
“The ₦203.8 billion Rights Issue is an important step in that process. The proceeds
will support balance-sheet strengthening, working capital requirements and the
development of new oil and gas wells across our core upstream assets. Together,
these investments are intended to improve our financial resilience and strengthen the
foundations for the next phase of Oando’s growth.
“We remain committed to building a stronger, more competitive African energy
company, with a clear focus on operational performance, financial discipline, and
sustainable shareholder value. This Rights Issue provides eligible shareholders with
the opportunity to participate in that next phase, and I encourage them to review the
Rights Circular carefully and make an informed decision regarding their entitlements.”
1. Terms and Purpose of the Rights Issue
The Rights Issue is being undertaken under the authority granted by shareholders
at the Company’s 46th Annual General Meeting held on 11 August 2025, at which
shareholders authorised the Board to raise additional capital of up to N500 billion,
or its foreign currency equivalent.
The Company has received the requisite approvals from the Securities and Exchange
Commission of Nigeria (“SEC”), NGX, JSE and the Financial Surveillance Department
of the South African Reserve Bank (“SARB”).
Oando is offering up to 4,076,185,240 new ordinary shares of 50 kobo each (“Rights
Shares”) to qualifying shareholders at ₦50.00 per Rights Share, on the basis of one
(1) Rights Share for every two (2) existing ordinary shares held on the applicable
record date. The Rights Issue will raise gross proceeds of up to ₦203.8 billion if fully
subscribed. Qualifying shareholders on the South African share register will pay
R0.632487 per Rights Share. The conversion uses the Central Bank of Nigeria’s
(CBN) central exchange rate of ₦79.0530 to R1.00 published for 26 November 2026,
in accordance with the rights circular dated 27 November 2026 (the “Rights
Circular”).
The Rights Shares will, upon issue, rank pari passu in all respects with the Company's
existing ordinary shares.
Further details on the transaction including the proposed use of proceeds can be
found in the Rights Issue circular located on the Company’s website.

2. Shareholder Entitlements and Participation
The Rights Issue is renounceable, allowing qualifying shareholders to subscribe for
their entitlements or, trade or renounce their rights during the applicable offer period.
Shareholders who do not exercise their rights may experience dilution of their
percentage ownership following the issuance of new shares.
Qualifying shareholders may also apply for additional Rights Shares beyond their
respective entitlements, subject to the allocation procedures and applicable
regulatory requirements described in the Rights Circular.
On the Nigerian register, Rights Shares not taken up will be allocated on a pro-rata
basis to qualifying shareholders who apply and pay for additional shares, in
accordance with SEC requirements and the Rights Circular.
On the South African register, excess Rights Shares will be allocated on a basis
determined by the Directors, subject to SEC clearance and the provisions of the
Rights Circular.
Fractional entitlements will be treated in accordance with the rules applicable to the
respective registers, as set out in the Rights Circular.

under the arrangements announced on 29 April 2026. Transfers will resume on 07
December 2026 at 09h00. Entitlements will be determined by reference to the
applicable share register and record date, in accordance with the Rights Circular.

6. Rounding Principle
The allocation of Rights Issue shares will be such that shareholders will not be
allocated a fraction of a Rights Issue share and as such any shareholding giving rise
to a fraction of less than one of a Rights Issue will be rounded to the nearest whole
number. Standard rounding will be applied to the Rights Issue, where fractional
entitlements less than 0.5 are rounded down to the nearest whole share and fractions
of 0.5 and greater are rounded up to the next whole share.
7. Refund Process
The proceeds received from South African shareholders or their renouncees will
remain in a designated escrow account. Should any unforeseen event result in the
Rights Issue not proceeding, the proceeds will be returned to the South African
shareholders or renouncees concerned. Money received in respect of an application
that is rejected or otherwise treated as void by Oando, or which is otherwise not
validly received in accordance with the terms stipulated, will be refunded by way of
EFT (without interest) in ZAR to the applicant concerned on or about Thursday, 11
February 2027.
8. Entitlement
The Rights that are represented by letters of allocation are valuable and may be
traded on the JSE. Investors are not permitted to trade the new Rights Issue shares
or Excess Rights Issue shares until the shares have been issued on or about
Thursday, 11 February 2027 and Monday, 15 February 2027, respectively.
