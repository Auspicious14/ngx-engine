Lagos, Nigeria
October 9, 2026
NOTIFICATION OF THE RETIREMENT OF MR. OLANREWAJU BAMISEBI
Access Holdings Plc (“the Company”) hereby notifies the Nigerian Exchange Limited
(“NGX”), and the investing public of the retirement of Mr. Olanrewaju Bamisebi as
Executive Director, Information Technology and Digitalisation of Access Holdings Plc,
effective September 30, 2026, following the expiration of his term contract.
Mr. Bamisebi’s retirement will ensure the Company’s compliance with the CBN
Corporate Governance Guidelines for Financial Holding Companies in Nigeria. The
Guidelines provide that a financial holding company shall have a maximum of two
Executive Directors: the Managing Director/Chief Executive Officer and one other
Executive Director, who shall be the Chief Financial Officer, subject to the CBN’s
approval.
The Board expresses its sincere appreciation to Mr. Bamisebi for his dedicated service
and contributions to the digital transformation and growth of the Access Group during
his tenure and wishes him every success in his future endeavours.
BY ORDER OF THE BOARD
SUNDAY EKWOCHI
GROUP COMPANY SECRETARY
