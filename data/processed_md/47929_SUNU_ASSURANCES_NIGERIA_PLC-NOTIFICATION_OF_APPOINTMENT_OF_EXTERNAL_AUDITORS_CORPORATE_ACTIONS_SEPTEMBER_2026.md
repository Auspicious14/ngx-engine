Lagos, 21st September 2026
NOTICE OF CHANGE OF EXTERNAL AUDITORS
SUNU Assurances Nigeria Plc (“the Company”) hereby notifies the public, Nigerian Exchange Limited (NGX)
and our esteemed shareholders of the change of its External Auditors.
Messrs. SIAO Partners, having served as the Company’s External Auditors for the maximum tenure of Eight
(8) years stipulated by NAICOM’s Corporate Governance Guidelines 2021, have retired following the
conclusion of the 2025 financial year audit exercise.
Consequently, the Board, upon the ratification of appointment by the shareholders at the Annual General
Meeting held on Friday, 21st August 2026 have appointed Messrs. TAC Professional Services as the
Company’s new External Auditors. The profile of the External Auditors is herewith contained.
Profile of TAC Professional Services
TAC Professional Services is a leading indigenous professional and business consulting firm in Nigeria,
established in 1999. The firm provides a broad range of professional services, including Audit & Assurance,
Tax Advisory and Compliance, Financial and Business Advisory, Forensic Investigation and Accounting, and
other professional consulting services.
TAC has extensive experience serving clients across various sectors of the Nigerian economy, including
insurance, financial services, oil and gas, power, manufacturing, real estate, FMCG, telecommunications,
aviation, media, NGOs and government institutions.
The firm is a member of Integra International, a global network of independent accounting and consulting firms
operating across multiple jurisdictions, providing access to international expertise and best practices.
TAC and its partners are registered with and in good standing with relevant professional and regulatory
bodies, including the Financial Reporting Council of Nigeria (FRC), Securities and Exchange Commission
(SEC), Institute of Chartered Accountants of Nigeria (ICAN), Chartered Institute of Taxation of Nigeria (CITN),
and Association of Certified Fraud Examiners (ACFE).
With over two (2) decades of professional practice and experience, TAC brings relevant technical expertise
and sector knowledge to its appointment as the Company's External Auditors.
The Board and Management welcome TAC Professional Services as the Company's newly appointed External
Auditors and wish the firm a successful tenure.
