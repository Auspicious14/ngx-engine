ANNOUNCEMENT TO NIGERIAN EXCHANGE LIMITED AND THE INVESTING PUBLIC
DELAY IN FILING OF THE AUDITED FINANCIAL STATEMENTS FOR THE YEAR
ENDED 30 APRIL 2026
The Board of Directors of Multi-Trex Integrated Foods Plc (“Multi-Trex” or “the Company”)
informs Nigerian Exchange Limited (“NGX” or “The Exchange”), shareholders and the
investing public that the Company has not yet filed its Audited Financial Statements for the
year ended 30 April 2026 (“2026 AFS”). The 2026 AFS were due for filing on Wednesday, 29
July 2026, and the cure period under the Rules for Filing of Accounts and Treatment of
Default Filing has also expired.
The Board regrets this delay This announcement sets out the reasons for the delay and the
expected filing date.
REASONS FOR THE DELAY
The audit of the 2026 AFS by the Company’s external auditors, Baker Tilly Nigeria, took
longer than projected because of the following legacy challenges:
1. Transition from AMCON intervention. The Company was entangled for a seven-year
period under the intervention of the Asset Management Corporation of Nigeria
(AMCON). During that period, the Company’s assets and records were under AMCON’s
control. Following the hand-back of the Company in 2022, the reconstituted
management has had to reconstruct accounting records, starting from 2014, contending
with cases of damaged, lost, vandalised documentation and absence of institutional
memory resident in the employees saddled with finance function that were laid off
among over 200 others. Consequently, meeting our timely account filing obligations has
proved quite challenging – which is why the NGX compassionately granted us a two-
year moratorium in November 2024 to re-establish compliance.
CURRENT STATUS
The Board is pleased to inform the market that the audit of the 2026 AFS has now been
concluded by the Company’s external auditors, Baker Tilly Nigeria, moving us close re-
establishing full compliance.
All governance meetings, including those of the Statutory Audit Committee, Board Finance,
Risk & Audit Committee and the full Board have been scheduled to hold over the next two
weeks to consider and approve the 2026 AFS. Once approved, the 2026 AFS will be filed
with the Exchange soon after.

EXPECTED FILING DATE
Following the approval of the 2026 AFS at the scheduled governance meetings, the
Company expects to file the 2026 AFS with The Exchange on or before Friday, 20
November 2026.
MEASURES TO PREVENT RECURRENCE
To avoid a recurrence, the Company:
(a) Envisages that the adverse effect of the legacy challenges will continue to have
reducing effect on its reporting obligation
(b) Will continue to strengthen its finance team and accounting reporting systems
(c) Anticipates that the audit process will henceforth consume relatively less time.
To conclude the Board and management of Multi-Trex Integrated Foods Plc apologises to
the Exchange and investing public in general for the delay, and would like to assure that
we remain committed to full compliance with the Rules of The Exchange.
