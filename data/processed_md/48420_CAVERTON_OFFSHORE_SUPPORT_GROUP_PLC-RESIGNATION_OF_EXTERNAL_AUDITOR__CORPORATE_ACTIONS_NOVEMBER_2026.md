Caverton Offshore Support Group Plc – Resignation of External Auditors
Caverton Offshore Support Group Plc ("the Company") wishes to notify Nigerian
Exchange Limited (NGX) and the investing public that its External Auditors,
PricewaterhouseCoopers (PwC), have resigned from their appointment as External
Auditors to the Company and its subsidiaries, effective 19th November 2026.
The Board of Directors has accepted the resignation and wishes to express its sincere
appreciation to PricewaterhouseCoopers for their professionalism, dedication, and
valuable services rendered during their tenure as the Company’s External Auditors.
In compliance with the provisions of the Companies and Allied Matters Act (CAMA)
2020 and corporate governance best practices, the Board is taking the necessary steps
to appoint new External Auditors to fill the casual vacancy, subject to the ratification of
shareholders at the next Annual General Meeting. NGX and the investing public will be
duly notified once the appointment process is concluded.
Please accept the assurances of our highest regards.
