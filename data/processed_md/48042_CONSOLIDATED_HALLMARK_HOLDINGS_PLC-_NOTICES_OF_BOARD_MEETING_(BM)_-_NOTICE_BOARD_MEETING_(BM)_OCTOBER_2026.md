Lagos, 2nd October 2026
NOTICE OF BOARD OF DIRECTORS’ MEETING
NOTICE IS HEREBY GIVEN that the 19th Meeting of the Board of Directors of Consolidated
Hallmark Holdings Plc (the Company) will hold as follows:
DATE: Wednesday, October 28, 2026.
TIME: 10:00 am.
VENUE: Virtual.
The Meeting is to consider the Company’s Unaudited Financial Statements for the period ended
30th September 2026 (2026 Q3 UFS). amongst other items.
In view of the above, the Company hereby announces that its Closed Period commenced on
Thursday, 1st October 2026, and will remain until twenty-four (24) hours after the 2026 Q3 UFS is
released to the public.
Within this period, all insiders of the Company and their connected persons are under restriction
not to trade or deal in the securities of the Company.
