Lagos, July 6, 2026
RESOLUTIONS PASSED AT THE ANNUAL GENERAL MEETING OF R.T. BRISCOE (NIGERIA) PLC
At the Annual General Meeting of R.T. Briscoe (Nigeria) PLC held on Thursday, July
2, 2026 at 18, Fatai Atere Way, Matori, Lagos at 11.00 a.m., the following Resolutions
were duly passed:
a. That the Audited Financial Statements of the Company together with the
report of the Directors and Auditors thereon as well as the Audit Committee
Report for the year ended 31st December 2025 be received;
b. That the re-election of the following Directors – HRM Sir, Sunday Nnamdi
Nwosu and Mrs. Folasade Oluwatoyin Ogunde retiring by rotation be and is
hereby approved.
c. That the following shareholders’ representatives be and are hereby
appointed as members of the Statutory Audit Committee:
i. Mr. Adeniyi Araunsi Adebisi;
ii. Mr. Kenneth Nwosu; and
iii. Mr. Anthony Katchy.
The Board’s representatives on the statutory Audit Committee are:
i. Mr. Akin Ajayi; and
ii. Alhaji Ali Madugu, mni.
d. That the compensation of the Managers of the Company be and is
hereby disclosed.
e. That the Directors be and hereby authorized to fix the remuneration of the
Auditors for the financial year ended 31st December, 2025.

f. That the remuneration of the Non-Executive Directors for the 2025 financial year
be and is hereby approved.
g. That the Company be and hereby authorized to procure goods and
services necessary for its operations from related Companies.
h. That the Directors be authorized to increase the share capital of the
Company and raise N10,000,000,000.00 (Ten Billion Naira) to be
implemented by one or more transactions through the issuance of ordinary
shares by way of public offering, private placement, rights issuance to
existing shareholders or otherwise via a combination of these upon such
terms and conditions to be determined at the discretion of the directors
and subject to requisite regulatory approvals.
i. That upon the successful completion of the process of increase of the
company’s share capital, the Memorandum and Article of Association of
the company be amended as necessary to reflect the company’s new
issued share capital.
BY ORDER OF THE BOARD
Michael Olabode
Company Secretary
