Lagos: 31 March 2026
NOTIFICATION OF CLOSED PERIOD/BOARD MEETING TO CONSIDER AND APPROVE THE
QUARTER 1 2026 UNAUDITED FINANCIAL STATEMENTS
This is to notify Nigerian Exchange Limited (NGX) and by extension, the public, that the Board of
Directors of Berger Paints Nigeria Plc. (the Company) will be meeting on Thursday, 16 April 2026,
to, amongst other matters, consider and approve the Company’s Q1 2026 Unaudited Financial
Statements for the first quarter ending 31 March 2026 (Q1 2026 UFS).
Accordingly, a ‘Closed Period’ shall commence from Wednesday, 1 April 2026, until twenty- four
(24) hours after the Q1 2026 UFS is filed with NGX, in line with the provisions of NGX’s Closed
Period Rule (as amended).
Consequently, all insiders of the Company are not allowed to deal directly or indirectly in the
securities of the Company within this Closed Period.
Thank you.
For: Berger Paints Nigeria Plc.
