UPDC REAL ESTATE INVESTMENT TRUST
NOTICE OF EXTRAORDINARY GENERAL MEETING
NOTICE IS HEREBY GIVEN that an Extraordinary General Meeting of the Unitholders of the UPDC Real
Estate Investment Trust (REIT) will be held virtually on Wednesday, 6 May 2026 at 11:00am to transact
the following business.
BUSINESS TO BE TRANSACTED
1. Ratification of the Fourth Supplemental Trust Deed
2. Ratification of the Amended Property Management Agreement
3. Ratification of the Amended Safe Custody Agreement
PURPOSE OF AMENDMENTS
The amendments reflect: (i) the SEC-approved appointment of SFS Capital Nigeria Limited as Fund
Manager, replacing Stanbic IBTC Asset Management Limited; (ii) the CAC-certified name change of
FBNQuest Trustees Limited to First Trustees Limited; and (iii) updated registered addresses for the Fund
Manager and United Capital Trustees.
