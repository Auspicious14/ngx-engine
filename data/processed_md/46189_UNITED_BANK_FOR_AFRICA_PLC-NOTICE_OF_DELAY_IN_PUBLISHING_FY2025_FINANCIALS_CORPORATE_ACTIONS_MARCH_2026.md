UNITED BANK FOR AFRICA PLC
PUBLICATION OF AUDITED FINANCIAL STATEMENTS FOR THE YEAR ENDED
DECEMBER 31, 2025
Lagos, Nigeria - Monday, March 2, 2026
United Bank for Africa Plc (UBA or the Bank) hereby informs its shareholders
and the investing public that the Board of Directors approved the Bank’s
Audited Financial Statements for the year ended December 31, 2025, on
February 19, 2026 (the results) and requisite regulatory approval is being
pursued.
The Bank has obtained the approval of the Securities and Exchange
Commission for an extension to file the results on or before March 31, 2026,
subject to the approval of the Central Bank of Nigeria.
The Bank’s insiders are kindly reminded that the earlier declared Closed
Period in respect of transactions on its securities remains in place until
twenty-four (24) hours after release of the results.
Thank you.
BY THE ORDER OF THE BOARD
BILI A. ODUM
GROUP COMPANY SECRETARY/LEGAL COUNSEL
