13thOctober 2026
eTRANZACT INTERNATIONAL PLC
NOTIFICATION OF CHANGES TO THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS
In accordance with the Rules of Nigerian Exchange Limited (“NGX”), eTranzact International Plc
(the “Company”) hereby notifies NGX and the investing public of changes to the composition of its
Board of Directors (the “Board”).
In 2025, Access Bank Plc, a major shareholder and strategic partner of the Company, decided to
exit the Company. Pursuant to a tag-along arrangement between Access Bank Plc, Meristem
Trustees Limited (as trustee for Accelerex Holdings) and eTranzact Global Limited, the other
parties also decided to exit the Company.
Accordingly, Access Bank Plc, Meristem Trustees Limited (as trustee for Accelerex Holdings) and
eTranzact Global Limited transferred an aggregate of 5,168,921,170 ordinary shares in the
Company, representing a 56.18% equity interest, to Trademarks Global Concepts Limited.
Following completion of the above transfer, the directors representing the exiting shareholders
resigned from the Board, alongside two Independent Directors. Also, Directors nominated by
Trademarks Global Concepts Limited have been appointed to the Board, and two new
Independent Directors have also been appointed.
Details of the changes are set out below:
1. Resignations:
a. Chuma Ajene – Non-Executive Director
b. Benjamin Dabra – Non-Executive Director
c. Afolabi Oladele – Non-Executive Director
d. Binta Max-Gbinije – Independent Non-Executive Director
e. Maj Gen Emeka Onwuamaegbu (rtd). – Independent Non-Executive Director
2. Appointments:
a. Akinwunmi (Akin) Akinfemiwa – Non-Executive Director
Akin Akinfemiwa has vast experience in transformational leadership, ideation and
execution of strategic and tactical initiatives, which he has demonstrated throughout his
career across different fields, including Engineering, Information Technology, Banking,
Commodity Trading, Oil and Gas, Power Generation, Corporate Restructuring as well as
high-value mergers and acquisitions, to mention a few.
He is a Non-Executive Director at First Bank of Nigeria Limited. He was the former
Managing Director/Chief Executive Officer, Geregu Power Plc and also former Group

Chief Executive Officer, Forte Oil Plc/African Petroleum Plc. He was the Chairman,
Board of Directors, Forte Upstream Services Ltd and as a seasoned Director, he has
served on the Board of major companies including Amperion Power Distribution
Company and AP Oil and Gas Ltd, Ghana. He was the Chairman, Major Oil Marketers
Association of Nigeria (MOMAN), Director and Trustee, Association of Power
Generation Companies of Nigeria. He is currently a trustee of the University of Ibadan
Alumni Association Trust Fund (UIAATF).
b. Olatunde Akande – Non-Executive Director
Olatunde Akande is a seasoned development finance and investment executive with over
two decades of experience spanning banking, private equity, financial inclusion,
agribusiness and SME development across Sub-Saharan Africa. His expertise includes
capital mobilisation, investment structuring and developing commercially sustainable
businesses.
He is currently a Non-Executive Director of Southwest Agric Company (SWAgCo), a subsidiary
of Odu’a Investment Company Limited, and previously served as Executive Director, Technical
at NIRSAL Plc. He was also Director of Portfolio Management at the Nairobi-based Africa
Enterprise Challenge Fund (AECF), where he oversaw its regional investment portfolio. His
career includes roles at the Central Bank of Nigeria, International Finance Corporation (IFC) and
First Funds Limited. He holds an MPhil in Development Finance (Cum Laude) and is an alumnus
of the Harvard Business School General Management Program (GMP).
c. Doron Grupper – Non-Executive Director
Doron Grupper is an experienced senior executive with extensive experience in banking,
energy, infrastructure and corporate management.
Throughout his career, he held several senior leadership positions, including:
• Chief Executive Officer, Israel Bank of Agriculture Ltd.
• Chief Executive Officer, Manara Pumped Storage (2014) Ltd.
• President and Head of Business Development for Africa, BSG Energy.
• Chief Executive Officer, Dorad Energy Ltd.
• Chief Executive Officer, Elran Infrastructure Ltd.
• Executive Chairman of the Board, Mekorot Water Company Ltd., Israel’s national
water company.
He has also served on the boards of several companies, including VID Desalination
Company Ltd., Mekorot Water Company Ltd., Pe’ilim Capital Markets, Geregu Power
Plc in Nigeria, Mivtach Shamir Energy Ltd. and Kesem Energy Ltd.
Doron holds a B.Sc. and an M.Sc. in Economics and Administration from the Hebrew
University of Jerusalem, awarded in 1975 and 1980, respectively.

d. Omobola Hollist – Independent Non-Executive Director
Omobola Hollist is a development finance executive and governance specialist with over
35 years of experience in international banking and multilateral development finance. She
served as Financial Controller at the African Development Bank Group. Her experience
spans financial management, treasury and asset-liability management, credit and financial
risk management, institutional governance, and business transformation.
She has served on institutional committees covering asset and liability management, audit,
credit risk, business transformation, and information systems governance, contributing
to strategic decision-making, risk oversight, and financial stewardship.
e. Mustapha Ajenifuja, SAN – Independent Non-Executive Director
Mustapha Ajenifuja, SAN, is a Senior Advocate of Nigeria and an experienced legal
practitioner and corporate governance adviser with over 13 years of post-qualification
experience spanning commercial litigation, regulatory compliance, corporate governance,
financial transactions and capital market advisory. He has extensive experience in
banking, insolvency, debt recovery, receivership, mergers and acquisitions, debenture
registration and regulatory engagements with financial institutions.
The Company has obtained the approval of the Central Bank of Nigeria for the appointment of
the new directors and is updating its records with the Corporate Affairs Commission accordingly.
This notification is issued for the information of NGX and the investing public.
