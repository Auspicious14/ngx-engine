NOTIFICATION OF EMERGENCY BOARD MEETING – CHAMPION BREWERIES PLC
This is to notify the shareholders of Champion Breweries Plc (the Company), Nigerian Exchange
Limited (NGX or The Exchange) and the investing public that a meeting of the Board of Directors
of the Company has been scheduled to hold on Friday, June 12, 2026, to, among other items,
consider and approve the resignation of a Director.
Pursuant to Rule 17:18 (a) of The Exchange’s Rulebook on Closed Period (as amended), the
Closed Period which commenced on 1 June 2026, shall remain in effect until twenty-four (24)
hours after the Board meeting.
Consequently, no insider of the Company and their connected persons shall deal in the products
of the Company or with sensitive information, directly or indirectly, during this Closed Period.
