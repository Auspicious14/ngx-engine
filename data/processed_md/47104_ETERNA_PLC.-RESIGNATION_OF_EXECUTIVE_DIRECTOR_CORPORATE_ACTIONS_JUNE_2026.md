LAGOS, 2 JUNE 2026
RESIGNATION OF DR. BUNMI AGAGU-ADU AS EXECUTIVE DIRECTOR,
ETERNA PLC
Eterna Plc ("the Company") hereby notifies Nigerian Exchange Limited ("NGX"), its
shareholders, and the investing public of the resignation of Dr. Bunmi Agagu-Adu
as Executive Director of the Company, effective 25 May 2026.
The Board of Directors expresses its sincere appreciation to Dr. Agagu-Adu for her
dedicated service, commitment, and valuable contributions to the growth and
development of the Company during her tenure. The Board wishes her continued
success in all her future endeavours.
Thank you.
David Edet
General Counsel/Company Secretary
