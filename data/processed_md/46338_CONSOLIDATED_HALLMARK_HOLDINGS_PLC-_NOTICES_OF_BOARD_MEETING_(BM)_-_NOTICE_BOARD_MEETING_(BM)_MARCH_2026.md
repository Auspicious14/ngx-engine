Lagos, 26th March 2026
NOTICE OF BOARD OF DIRECTORS’ MEETING
NOTICE IS HEREBY GIVEN that the 16th Meeting of the Board of Directors of Consolidated
Hallmark Holdings Plc (the Company) will hold as follows:
DATE: Monday, March 30, 2026.
TIME: 1:00pm.
VENUE: Virtual.
The Meeting is to consider the Audited Financial Statements of the Company for the Year ended
31st December 2025 amongst other items.
In view of the above, the Closed Period which commenced on 1st January 2026, as earlier
announced, will continue until twenty-four (24) hours after the Audited Financial Statements for
the Year ended 31st December 2025 are released to the public.
During this period, all those who are considered as “insiders” of the Company are under
restriction not to trade or deal in the shares of the Company.
BY ORDER OF THE BOARD
Rukevwe Falana
Company Secretary
