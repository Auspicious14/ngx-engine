Corporate Actions Announcement
WEMA BANK PLC hereby announce as follows:
Period Ended 31st December 2025
A Final Dividend of N1.25 per ordinary share subject to appropriate
withholding tax and approval will be paid to shareholders whose names
appear in the Register of Members as at the close of business on the 29th day
Proposed Dividend of April 2026.
Proposed Bonus NIL
The Register of Shareholders will be closed from 30th of April 2026 to 6th of
Closure of Register May 2026.
Qualification Date 29th day of April, 2026
On 20th of May 2026, dividends will be paid electronically to shareholders
whose names appear on the Register of Members as at 29th of April 2026 and
who have completed the e-dividend registration and mandated the Registrar
Payment Date to pay their dividends directly into their bank accounts.
Shareholders who are yet to complete the e-dividend registration are advised
to download the Registrar’s E-Dividend Mandate Activation Form from
https://greenwichregistrars.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/08/GRDS-e-
Mandate-Activation-Form-UPDATED-1.pdf, which is also available on the
website of the Registrars at: www.greenwichregistrars.com, complete and
E-Dividend Registration submit to the Registrar or their respective banks.
Shareholders with dividend warrants and share certificates that have
Unclaimed Dividend remained unclaimed or are yet to be presented for payment or returned for
Warrants and Share validation are advised to complete the e-dividend registration or contact the
Certificates Registrar.
Date of Annual General The Company’s Annual General Meeting will be held virtually via:
Meeting https://wemabank.com/AGM/ on the 20th of May 2026 at 11am.
