Lagos, Nigeria, Tuesday, April 1, 2026
NOTIFICATION OF BOARD MEETING AND CLOSED PERIOD - CUSTODIAN INVESTMENT PLC
In compliance with the post-listing requirements of the Nigerian Exchange Limited, Notice is
hereby given that a meeting of the Board of Directors of Custodian Investment Plc (“the
Company”) will hold on Wednesday, April 29, 2026 at 9:00am, to deliberate on and approve
the Company’s Unaudited Financial Statements for the first quarter ended March 31, 2026 (Q1,
2026 UFS).
Consequently, the Company has entered a Closed Period from April 1, 2026, until twenty-four
(24) hours after the Company’s Q1, 2026 UFS is released to Nigerian Exchange Limited and the
general public.
Accordingly, no Insider of Custodian Investment Plc may directly or indirectly deal in the
products or securities of the Company in any manner during the Closed Period.
