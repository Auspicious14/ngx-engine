Lagos, Nigeria – 15th July 2026
CORONATION INFRASTRUCTURE FUND PROPOSES ₦8.985 SEMI-ANNUAL INCOME
DISTRIBTUTION FOR H1 2026
The Coronation Infrastructure Fund (“CIF” or the “Fund”), an actively managed closed-end debt fund
that focuses on making long-term investments within specific sectors in Nigeria, announces that
pursuant to the Section 25 of the Programme Trust Deed, a semi-annual distribution of ₦8.985 per
unit (subject to appropriate WHT) to be paid to unitholders whose names appear in the Register of
members as at the close of business on 22nd of July 2026.
Distribution Period June 30, 2026
₦8.985 per unit (paid to unitholders whose names appeared in the register of
Distribution unitholders as at the close of business on July 22, 2026, subject to the deduction
of appropriate withholding tax)
Qualification Date July 22, 2026
Closure of Register July 23, 2026
July 28, 2026
The distribution will be paid – electronically – to Unitholders whose names appear
Payment Date
on the Register of Unitholders as of July 22, 2026 (and have completed the E-
mandate Form and authorised the Registrar to pay their distributions directly into
their Bank accounts.

the Registrar’s E-mandate Activation Form, which is available on the website of
