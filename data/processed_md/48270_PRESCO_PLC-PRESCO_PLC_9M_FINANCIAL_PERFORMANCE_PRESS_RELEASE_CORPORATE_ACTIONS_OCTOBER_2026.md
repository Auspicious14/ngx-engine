PRESS RELEASE
Presco Plc Delivers 8.5% Profit Growth to ₦151.5 Billion Despite Global Geopolitical Tensions and
Domestic Edible Oil Business Pressures
Benin, Nigeria (October 30 2026) - Presco Plc (Bloomberg: PRESCO:NL | Reuters: PRESCO.LG | NGX:
PRESCO), Nigeria’s leading fully integrated Edible Oil Business, today announced an improved
performance for the first nine months of 2026 ended 30 September 2026 (“9M 2026”), highlighted
by an 8.5% year-on-year increase in Profit Before Tax (PBT) to ₦151.5 billion. This PBT growth,
achieved despite prevailing inflation and declining Edible Oil Prices, underscores the company's
operational resilience and strategic financial management.
Key Investor Highlights for 9M 2026:
• Resilient Profitability: Group Profit Before Tax (PBT) grew 8.5% YoY to N151.5 billion. Presco
standalone PBT saw a substantial 12.7% increase to ₦96.9 billion, with standalone Profit After
Tax rising 11.9% to ₦68.7 billion, demonstrating strong underlying earnings capacity.
• Operational Efficiency Gains: Presco's standalone cost of sales declined by 6.2% to ₦46.2
billion, reflecting improved cost management. Group gross profit remained robust at ₦202.8
billion, with margins exceeding 73%.
• Deleveraging and Financial Strength: Total Group borrowings were reduced by
approximately 57% to ₦137.1 billion (a reduction of ₦180.2 billion). Total Group liabilities
decreased by 42.4% to ₦278.2 billion. This substantial deleveraging enhances financial
flexibility for future growth.
• Reducing Finance Costs: Group finance costs saw a significant reduction of 34.5%, decreasing
from ₦30.0 billion to ₦19.6 billion, a direct benefit of active balance sheet management.
• Strengthened Equity and Liquidity: Group shareholders' equity grew 10.1% to ₦487.3 billion,
with retained earnings increasing to ₦253.5 billion. The company maintained a substantial
cash position of ₦135.2 billion.
• Domestic Market Strength: Revenue from Nigeria increased by 12.7% to ₦222.0 billion,
highlighting the strength of the Group's core market. SNL also reported significant revenue
and PBT growth.
• Strategic Growth Initiatives: The commencement of Fresh Fruit Bunches production from
the recently acquired Saro Oil Palm Limited plantation in Q4 is set to boost future
operational and financial performance.
Presco Plc’s resilient integrated business model, strategic investments, and operational efficiencies
have strengthened its financial performance and balance sheet, positioning the company for
sustained growth and market leadership in Nigeria and West Africa’s edible oils industry. Through a

continued focus on operational excellence, sustainability, and long-term value creation, Presco
remains well-positioned to deliver sustainable returns to shareholders.
MD's Commentary
Commenting on the results, Reji George, Managing Director/ Chief Executive Officer of Presco Plc,
said:
“Our comprehensive business operations remain robust and well-positioned for continued success.
Across our plantations, processing facilities, and supply chain, we have maintained disciplined cost
management and optimized productivity, which has translated into strong financial results. Our
diversified operational approach and strategic investments have enabled us to withstand economic
headwinds while delivering growth. The commencement of Fresh Fruit bunches production from our
new Saro Oil Palm Limited plantation in Q4 is a key catalyst that will further enhance our production
capacity and profitability.
As we approach the end of 2026, our strategic focus remains on leveraging operational efficiencies,
expanding our productive capacity, and reinforcing our commitment to sustainability. We are
confident in our ability to close the year strongly and deliver long-term value, supported by our
resilient business model and proactive management approach. As an organization, We remain
committed to sustainable practices that support long-term value creation, ensuring we deliver
superior returns to our shareholders while upholding our environmental and social responsibilities."

About Presco Plc
Presco Plc is Nigeria’s foremost fully integrated edible oils company, engaged in the cultivation of oil
palm and the production, refining, and marketing of specialty fats and oils. Its operations are
reinforced by two key subsidiaries—Ghana Oil Palm Development Company Limited (GOPDC) and
Siat Nigeria Limited—extending Presco’s footprint and market leadership across West Africa.
