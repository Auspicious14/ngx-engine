Lagos, Nigeria, Wednesday, July 1, 2026
NOTICE OF BOARD MEETING
Notice is hereby given that a Board meeting of Ikeja Hotel Plc (‘IHPLC’ or ‘the Company’) will
be held via Zoom tele-conferencing on Friday, July 17, 2026 by 11:00 am to receive and
consider among other business, the Second Quarter Unaudited Financial Statements of the
Company for the period ended June 30, 2026 (2026 Q2 UFS).
Consequently, and pursuant to Rule 17.18 of the Rulebook of Nigerian Exchange Limited
(Issuers’ Rules) as amended, Ikeja Hotel Plc wishes to bring to the attention of the investing
public and the Company’s insiders that the Company commenced a closed period today, July
1, 2026, and will continue until twenty-four (24) hours after the 2026 Q2 UFS are released to
the public.
Therefore, no Insider and their connected persons shall deal in the securities of the Company
during the closed period.
The general public should please take note.
