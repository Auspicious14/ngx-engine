8th May 2026
Lagos Nigeria
NOTIFICATION OF EXPIRATION OF DIRECTOR’S TENURE
NEM Insurance Plc (“the Company”) wishes to notify Nigerian Exchange Limited (“NGX”) and the
investing public that the tenure of Alhaji Ahmed I. Yakasai an Independent Non-Executive Director of
the Company expired on the 31st of December 2025.
Accordingly, Alhaji Ahmed I. Yakasai has ceased to serve on the Board of the Company with effect
from the above date.
The Board of Directors expresses its appreciation to him for his valuable contributions to the Company
during his tenure and wishes him success in his future endeavors.
This notification is made in compliance with the applicable post-listing disclosure requirements of NGX.
