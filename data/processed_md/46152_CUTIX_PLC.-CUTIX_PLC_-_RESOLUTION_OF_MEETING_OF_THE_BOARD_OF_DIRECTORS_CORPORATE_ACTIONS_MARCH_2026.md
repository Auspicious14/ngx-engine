OUTCOME OF MEETING OF THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF CUTIX PLC
Cutix Plc (“the Company”) wishes to notify Nigerian Exchange Limited (NGX) and the
investing public that the 170th meeting of the Board of Directors of the Company was
held on Thursday, February 26, 2026, as scheduled.
The Third Quarter Unaudited Financial Statements (Q3 UFS) for the period ended
January 31, 2026, amongst other matters were considered and approved by the Board
subject to few amendments.
The Q3 UFS will be filed with NGX on or before February 28, 2026. Consequently, the
closed period earlier announced by the Company will subsist until 24 hours after the Q3
UFS have been made public.
