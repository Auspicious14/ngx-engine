Lagos, 20th February 2026
RESOLUTION OF THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF SUNU ASSURANCES NIGERIA PLC
In line with the Rules of Nigerian Exchange Limited (NGX) relating to Board Meetings and General Meetings of
Issuers, SUNU Assurances Nigeria Plc (the Company) wishes to inform its valued shareholders and the general
public that the Board of Directors at its meeting held on Friday, 20th February 2026 considered and approved
the Company’s Audited Financial Statement for the year ended 31st December, 2025.
The Audited Financial Statement for the year ended 31st December 2025 will be submitted to the Company’s
primary regulator, the National Insurance Commission for approval.
Please note that the Company’s closed period, which commenced on 1 January 2026, will remain in effect until
24 hours after the release of the Audited Financial Statement for the year ended 31st December, 2025 to the
market.
