NCR (NIGERIA) PLC - RESOLUTIONS PASSED AT THE 74TH ANNUAL GENERAL
MEETING
The shareholders of NCR (Nigeria) PLC (the “Company”) at its 74th Annual General
Meeting (AGM) held virtually on Wednesday, 5 August 2026, via the Zoom Cloud
Platform, at 11.00 am, proposed and unanimously passed the following resolutions:
ORDINARY BUSINESS
1. That pursuant to section 274 (2) of the CAMA, Chief Prosper Okpue’s
appointment as a Director of the Company with effect from 22 October 2025 be
and is hereby ratified.
2. That pursuant to section 274 (2) of the CAMA, Her Excellency Mrs Oluwatoyin
Saraki’s appointment as a Director of the Company with effect from 11 June
2026 be and is hereby ratified.
3. That pursuant to section 285 (1) of the CAMA, Chief Liaquat Bisade Biobaku
be and is hereby re-elected a Director of the Company.
4. That pursuant to section 285 (1) of the CAMA, Mr. Onyekachi Caleb
Chukwueke be and is hereby re-elected a Director of the Company.
5. That the resignation of Grant Thornton as External Auditors of the Company
with effect from the conclusion of the AGM be and is hereby noted, and BDO
Professional Services be and are hereby appointed as the External Auditors of
the Company with effect from the conclusion of the AGM.
6. That the Directors be and are hereby authorised to fix the remuneration of the
new Auditors, BDO Professional Services.
7. That the following persons be and are hereby elected as shareholders’
representatives on the Statutory Audit Committee of the Company for the
2026/2027 financial year.
• Mrs. Martina Nnenna Amadi
(FCA)
• Alhaji Mustapha Ishola Jinadu
(FcIoD)
• Mr. Taiwo Kashimawo Akanji
(FCA)
Directors: Mr. Matthew Adefila (Chairman), Mr Abdulqudus Ashimi (Country Manager), Chief Bisade Biobaku, Mr
Onyekachi Caleb Chukwueke, Chief Prosper Okpue, Her Excellency Mrs Oluwatoyin Saraki

SPECIAL BUSINESS: ORDINARY RESOLUTION
9. That the remuneration of the Non-Executive Directors of NCR (Nigeria) Plc for
the year ending 31 December 2026, and until further notice, be and is hereby
fixed at ₦4.5 million (four million and five hundred thousand Naira) only as
Directors’ fees.
10. That the sitting allowance payable to the Non-Executive Directors of NCR
(Nigeria) Plc be and is hereby fixed at₦125,000 (One Hundred and Twenty-Five
Thousand Naira) per meeting with effect from the date of this Annual General
Meeting and until otherwise reviewed by the Company in a General Meeting.
11. That the sitting allowance payable to the Chairman of NCR (Nigeria) Plc be and
is hereby fixed at ₦250,000 (Two Hundred and Fifty Thousand Naira) per
meeting with effect from the date of this Annual General Meeting and until
otherwise reviewed by the Company in a General Meeting.
12. That, pursuant to Rule 20.8 of the Rule book of The Exchange 2015 (Issuers’
Rules), a general mandate be and is hereby given, authorising the Company
during the 2026 financial year and up to the date of the next Annual General
Meeting, to procure goods, services, and financing and enter into such incidental
transactions necessary for its day-to-day operations from its related parties or
interested persons on normal commercial terms consistent with the Company’s
Transfer Pricing Policy. All transactions falling under this category, which were
entered into in 2026 prior to the date of this meeting, are hereby ratified.
SPECIAL BUSINESS: SPECIAL RESOLUTION
13. That a new Article 73 be inserted in the Articles of Association of the Company
as follows:
