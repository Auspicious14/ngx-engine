Our Performance
The Company's financial position has been fundamentally transformed by the successful recapitalisation. Cash and financial
assets increased by over ₦20 billion, reflecting the injection of new capital and the conversion of debt to equity. This has
strengthened liquidity, improved solvency, and positioned the Company to meet its regulatory obligations under NIIRA
2025. Insurance revenue grew significantly year-on-year, reflecting renewed underwriting activity. Investment income also
surged, driven by higher yields and improved portfolio management. However, underwriting remains loss-making because
of prior-period reserve strengthening. The net loss narrowed slightly, as strong investment income offset the underwriting
deficit.
Outlook
The Company is now financially stable and fully capitalised. Management's priority is to translate this stability into
sustainable profitability. With the balance sheet repaired and legacy issues resolved, the Company is well-positioned to
return to profitability in the coming periods.
