Lagos, 4th June, 2026
RESOLUTIONS PASSED AT THE 33RD ANNUAL GENERAL MEETING OF TRANS-NATIONWIDE
EXPRESS PLC
The members of Trans-Nationwide Express PLC (the Company) at its 33rd Annual General Meeting held
virtually on Wednesday, 3rd June, 2026 at 11.00 a.m., proposed and passed the following resolutions:
Ordinary Business
1. That the Audited Financial Statements for the year ended 31st December, 2025 together with the
Reports of the Directors, the Auditors and the Audit Committee thereon submitted to the meeting be
received and adopted.
2. That the following Directors retiring by rotation be and are hereby duly re-elected:
(a) Mr. Sulaiman A. Adedokun
(b) Mr. Adebayo A. Adeleke
3. That the Company’s External Auditors, Messrs. Baker Tilly Nigeria (Chartered Accountants) be and
are hereby re-appointed Auditors to the Company and that the Directors be and are hereby authorized
to fix their remuneration for the 2026 financial year.
4. That pursuant to Section 404 (4) & (5) of the Companies and Allied Matters Act 2020, the following
individuals were duly elected as members of the Company’s Statutory Audit Committee:
(a) Shareholders’ Representatives:
i. Mr. Oluwaseun Babajide Olukoya
ii. Mr. Olusegun David Oguntoye
iii. Mr. Chuks Nwosa Osadinizu
(b) The following Directors’ Representatives were nominated to the Statutory Audit Committee:
i. Dr. Oluwasegun Isaiah Adeoye
ii. Mr. Adegoke Johnson Olasoko
Special Business
5. That the sum of N430,000 be and is hereby approved as remuneration for each of the Non-Executive
Directors for 2026 financial year.
Francis Asuquo
Cautious Services Limited
Company Secretaries
Mr. Sulaiman Adedokun (Chairman), Mr. Eric C. Emecheta (MD/CEO), Ms. Daniella F.
