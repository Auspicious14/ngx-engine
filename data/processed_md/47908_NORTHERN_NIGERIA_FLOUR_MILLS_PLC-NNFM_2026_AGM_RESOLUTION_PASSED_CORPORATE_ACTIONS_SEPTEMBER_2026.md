16 September 2026
ORDINARY RESOLUTION OF THE SHAREHOLDERS OF NORTHERN NIGERIA FLOUR MILLS PLC (“THE
COMPANY”) PASSED PURSUANT TO SECTION 123 OF THE COMPANIES AND ALLIED MATTERS ACT, 2020
At the 54th Annual General Meeting of Northern Nigeria Flour Mills Plc (NNFM Plc “the Company”) held at
the Tahir Guest Palace, 4, Ibrahim Natsuguna Road, Ahmadu Bello Way, Kano on Monday, 14
September 2026, the following resolutions were passed;
1. The Audited Financial Statements for the Year ended March 31 2026, and the Reports of the
Directors, Auditors, and Audit Committee thereon were received.
2. The following directors retiring by rotation and having offered themselves for re-election were re-
elected.
a) Mr John Coumantaros
b) Alhaji Rabi’u Mohammed Gwarzo, OON
c) Mr Joseph Umolu
3. The following Directors who were appointed after the last Annual general Meeting were ratified.
i) Mr Devlin Hainsworth
ii) Mr Nassib Raffoul
iii) Bar. (Mrs.) Batulu Mohammed Koguna
4. The Directors were authorized to fix the remuneration of the external auditors.
5. The following members of the Statutory Audit Committee representing the shareholders were re-
elected until the conclusion of the next Annual General Meeting.
a) Alhaji Bello Umar Gwangwazo
b) Alhaji Lawan Sule Garo
C) Alhaji Sadiq A Othman
The following Directors were nominated to represent the board on the Statutory Audit committee:
a) Alhaji Sani Umar
b) Alhaji Auwalu Muktari
