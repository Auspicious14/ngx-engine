The Chapel Hill Denham Nigeria Infrastructure Debt Fund (“NIDF”) delivers ₦17.36 billion PBT for
the nine-month (9M) period ended 30 September 2026 and declares ₦4.53 Q3 2026 Distribution
LAGOS, NIGERIA – 8th October 2026 - Chapel Hill Denham Nigeria Infrastructure Debt Fund (NGX: NIDF) (“NIDF” or
the “Fund”) has announced its unaudited results for the period ended 30 September 2026, reporting continued
strong earnings performance, with Profit Before Tax (PBT) increasing by 2.9% to ₦17.36 billion compared with the
corresponding period despite the reduction in interest rates.
Reflecting the Fund’s consistent performance and strong income generation, the Investment Committee has
approved a Q3 2026 distribution of ₦4.53 per unit. This will make it the 38th Distribution (37 quarterly and 1 special),
with cumulative ₦110 billion distributed to investors since inception.
The Q3 2026 results come at an exciting juncture for the Fund, as NIDF continues to deepen its role as a leading
provider of long-term infrastructure financing in Nigeria. The Fund commenced its Series 12 capital raise on 23
September 2026, seeking to raise ₦45 billion to unlock new opportunities and support the current pipeline.
With over US$625 million in infrastructure projects financed since inception, NIDF remains focused on mobilising
long-term capital to help bridge Nigeria’s infrastructure financing gap. The Series 12 Offer represents the Fund’s
twelfth (12th) capital raise since inception in 2017 and its fourth (4th) capital raise since listing on the NGX. The
Offer is priced at ₦113.54 per unit, compared with the NGX closing price of ₦147.70 per unit as at 5 October 2026,
representing a 23.13% discount to the market price.
Performance Summary
Since its first listing in June 2017 - the Fund has achieved a 472.22% return on every naira invested and currently
offers a dividend yield of 19.64% (last 12 months, based on NAV per unit).
We believe that long-term, naira-denominated, project finance debt is an essential ingredient to Nigeria's
development and NIDF has seen greater demand for its services with every passing year. We continue to position
ourselves as the leading Infrastructure Fund in Nigeria and Sub-Saharan Africa aimed at impacting lives through
investing in sectors accretive to Nigeria’s economic development.

Notes to editors:
About NIDF
The Nigeria Infrastructure Debt Fund (NIDF) is Nigeria’s largest and Africa’s first-ever listed infrastructure debt fund, providing
long-term, Naira-denominated financing for infrastructure projects in Nigeria. It is a closed-ended debt fund which operates
in the form of a company, registered and regulated by the Nigerian Securities and Exchange Commission and listed on FMDQ
and the Nigerian Exchange (NGX).
NIDF provides investors with regular, sustained, long-term distributions and preserves capital over the long-term by generating
exposure to Naira-denominated infrastructure debt in Nigeria, which generates regular and predictable long-term cash flows.
It offers the most attractive yield on the NGX and has continuously paid quarterly distributions.
With its current capital base of over N131.9 billion, NIDF has a diversified and growing portfolio of infrastructure loans in a
broad range of sectors including power generation, energy infrastructure, transportation, telecom, and social infrastructure
(education and healthcare). By mobilising domestic capital for financing Nigeria’s infrastructure, NIDF has established a
template that can bridge the infrastructure deficit facing not just Nigeria but countries across Africa.
