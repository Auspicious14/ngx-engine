LAGOS: 30TH JUNE 2026
HBM NIGERIA PLC - NOTIFICATION OF BOARD MEETING AND CLOSED PERIOD
We hereby notify the Nigerian Exchange Limited (“NGX”) and the investing public that a
meeting of the Board of Directors of HBM Nigeria Plc (the “Company”) has been
scheduled to hold on Tuesday, 28th July 2026 to consider the Company’s Unaudited
Financial Statements for the period ending 30th June 2026 (the “Unaudited Financial
Statements”).
Please note that the Closed Period for trading in the Company’s shares will commence
on Wednesday, 1st July 2026 and will continue until twenty-four (24) hours after the
Unaudited Financial Statements is filed with the NGX and released to the public in
accordance with the Rules of the NGX. Accordingly, no Insider may directly or indirectly
deal in the securities of the Company in any manner during the Closed Period.
