Corporate Actions Announcement
Guinness Nigeria Plc hereby announce as follows:
Period Ended 30 September 2026
An Interim Dividend of ₦7.00 per ordinary share, subject to appropriate
withholding tax and approval will be paid to shareholders whose names
appear in the Register of Members as at the close of business on Friday, 30
Proposed Dividend October 2026.
Proposed Bonus NIL
Closure of Register The Register of Shareholders will be closed from 02 November 2026.
Qualification Date 30 October 2026
On 13 November 2026, dividends will be paid electronically to shareholders
whose names appear on the Register of Members as at 30 October 2026,
and who have completed the e-dividend registration and mandated the
Payment Date Registrar to pay their dividends directly into their Bank accounts.
Shareholders who are yet to complete the e-dividend registration are
advised to download the Registrar’s E-Dividend Mandate Activation Form,
which is also available on our website: Microsoft PowerPoint - Mandate
E-Dividend Registration Form, complete and submit to the Registrar or their respective Banks.
Shareholders with dividend warrants and share certificates that have
Unclaimed Dividend remained unclaimed, or are yet to be presented for payment or returned for
Warrants and Share validation are advised to complete the e-dividend registration or contact the
Certificates Registrar. Please click here for the list of unclaimed dividends.
Name: Veritas Registrars Limited
Address: Plot 89A, Ajose Adeogun Street, Victoria Island, Lagos, Nigeria
Registrar Website: http://www.veritasregistrars.com/
