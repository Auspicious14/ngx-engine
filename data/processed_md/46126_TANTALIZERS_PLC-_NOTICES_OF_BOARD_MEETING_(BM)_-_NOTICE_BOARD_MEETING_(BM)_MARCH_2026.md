Lagos, Nigeria, February 26, 2026
ANNOUNCEMENT OF THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS MEETING OF TANTALIZERS PLC
This is to notify Nigerian Exchange Limited (NGX or The Exchange), shareholders
of Tantalizers PLC (the “Company”) and the investing public that the Board of
Directors of Tantalizers PLC will meet on Friday, February 27, 2026, to consider
the Company’s Audited Financial Statements for the year ended December
31, 2025 (the “2025 Financials”)
The Company had in compliance with the Closed Period Rules of The
Exchange entered a Closed Period on 1st January 2026, in respect of
transactions on its securities which continues to subsist and shall remain in
effect until twenty-four (24) hours after the release of the Company’s 2025
Audited Financial Statements for the year ended December 31, 2025, to NGX
and by extension, the general public.
Accordingly, all insiders are prohibited from dealing in the Company’s
securities during the Closed Period.
Thank you.
OLAMIDE BABAWALE-MO
COMPANY SECRETARY
