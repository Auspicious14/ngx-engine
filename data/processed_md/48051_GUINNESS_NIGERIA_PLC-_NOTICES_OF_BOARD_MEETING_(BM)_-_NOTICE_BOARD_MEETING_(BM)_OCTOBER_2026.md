Lagos, 7 October 2026
UPDATED NOTIFICATION OF THE MEETING OF THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF
GUINNESS NIGERIA PLC
This is to update our notice to the Nigerian Exchange Limited (NGX) and the Market that
the meeting of the Board of Directors of Guinness Nigeria Plc (the Company) earlier
scheduled for Wednesday, 21 October 2026 to consider, among others, the Unaudited
Financial Statements of the Company for the period ended 30th September 2026, will also
consider the following:
a. The proposal for the recommendation of interim dividend.
In view of this, the closed period for trading in the Company’s shares by insiders which
commenced on 1 October 2026 continues and will still expire 24 hours after the
Unaudited Financial Statements for the period ended 30th September 2026, is released
to the public.
Therefore, no insider of the Company and their connected persons may directly or
indirectly deal in the securities of the Company during the closed period. The Financial
Statements as approved by the Board will thereafter be uploaded through the Exchange’s
Issuers’ Portal not later than 22 October 2026.
For: Guinness Nigeria Plc.
