Lagos, 14th September, 2026
PREMIER PAINTS PLC – APPOINTMENT OF NEW DIRECTORS
Premier Paints Plc (“The Company”), will like to notify its esteemed shareholders, stakeholders,
the Nigerian Exchange Limited ("NGX") and the general public of the retirement of the following
Directors from the Board of Premier Paints Plc, effective 31st August 2026.
a) Mr. Adedoyin Adeyinka;
b) Dr. M.K.O. Balogun; and
c) Mr. Ajayi Oluyemi.
The Company would also like to announce the appointment of the following Directors as approved
by the Board on 31st August 2026, subject to ratification at the Annual General Meeting.
1) Mr. Emeka Ene – Board Chairman;
2) Mr. Adebowale Fagbemi – Non-Executive Director;
3) Dr. John Erinne – Non-Executive Director; and
4) Mrs Doseke Apkoriaye – Non-Executive Director.
The Profile of the new Directors are as follow:
MR EMEKA ENE
Emeka C. Ene is the CEO of the Oida Group, which includes the Oida Energy Limited, Xenergi Limited,
Xenoil and Enugu Industrial Park Free Zone (Enpower FTZ).
He is the immediate past Chairman (E&P Committee) of the International Gas
Union (IGU) for the 2022 - 2025 triennium, a past director on the board of the World
Free Zones Organization (WorldFZO), a member of the Institute of Directors,
Nigeria and serves on the boards of other companies in the Oil and Gas industry.
Emeka has also served in various capacities on a voluntary basis locally. He is the
immediate past Chairman of the Board of Trustees (BOT) of the Society of Petroleum
Engineers (SPE) Nigeria Council, a past Chairman of the Petroleum Technology
Association of Nigeria (PETAN), past Chairman of SPE Port Harcourt Section 103 and
was a member of the Presidential Task Force on Refineries reform in Nigeria.

MR ADEBOWALE FAGBEMI
Debo Fagbami has a twenty-eight (28) years career in the oil and gas industry that cuts across
several disciplines and specialties ranging from wireline logging and perforating, formation
evaluation, reservoir monitoring services, production testing operations, fast-track marginal field
development, scalable gas conversion plants and project management.
Debo holds a bachelor’s degree in Mechanical Engineering from the University of Ibadan, Nigeria
as well as an MBA from the Henley Business School of the University of Reading (UK).
He is a past Chairman of the Society of Petroleum Engineers Nigeria Council and served on the
Council of the Nigerian Gas Association as the Director of Communications from 2016 to 2018.
DR JOHN ERINNE
DR. JOHN ERINNE is a Chemical Engineer of thirty (30) years standing, having taken a B. Sc. From
University of Lagos in 1977, an M. Sc. from Strathclyde University, Glasgow, 1979 and a Ph. D.
from University of Technology, Loughborough, UK, 1982 and a vast experience in Operations,
Engineering & Technical Audits with work assignment to IOCs and Multinational Service
Companies.
He is a COREN registered Professional Engineer in Nigeria, as well as Fellow of Nigeria
Society of Chemical Engineers (NSChE) and the Nigerian Society of Engineers (NSE).
He is also a member of Nigerian Gas Association (NGA) and Tribology Society of Nigeria
(TSN).
Doseke Akporiaye, MBA
Doseke has more than twenty (20) years of international leadership and governance experience
spanning renewable energy, infrastructure, logistics, technology, advisory, and social impact
sectors across Africa and the United States.
She is experienced in advising Boards and Board Committees on corporate governance, financial
stewardship, risk oversight, and strategy execution, with demonstrated success in strengthening
institutional performance, mobilizing strategic partnerships, and advancing inclusive growth
across emerging and established markets. She has been recognized for bringing a balanced and
commercially grounded perspective across governance, operations, and development impact
within complex, multi-stakeholder environments.
She holds an Executive Masters in Business Administration from INSEAD, France, Singapore &
United Arab Emirates and is currently the Executive Director, Women of Renewable Industries and
Sustainable Energy (WRISE), USA as well as the Non-Executive Director, HR Indexx, Nigeria.
