Total Voting Rights and Capital
London and Lagos, 5 May 2026: In accordance with the Financial Conduct Authority's
(FCA) Disclosure Guidance and Transparency Rule 5.6.1R, the Company notifies the
market that:
1. The Company’s issued share capital as at close of business on 30 April 2026
consisted of 3,654,881,028 ordinary shares of USD 0.50 per share (“ordinary shares”),
each with one vote, including 6,136,678 ordinary shares held in treasury. Ordinary
shares held in treasury do not carry any voting rights.
2. Therefore, the total number of voting rights that may be used by shareholders as the
denominator for the calculations by which they will determine whether they are
required to notify their interest in, or a change to their interest in, the Company under
the Financial Conduct Authority's Disclosure Guidance and Transparency Rules, is
3,648,744,350.
