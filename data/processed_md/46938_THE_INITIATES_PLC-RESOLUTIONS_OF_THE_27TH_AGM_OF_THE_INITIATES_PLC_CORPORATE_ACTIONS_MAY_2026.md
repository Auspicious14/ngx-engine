RESOLUTIONS OF SHAREHOLDERS PASSED AT THE 27TH ANNUAL GENERAL MEETING OF
INITIATES PLC
At the 27th Annual General Meeting of The Initiates Plc. held at Chinenye Ijeh Hall, Golden Tulip
Garden City Port Harcourt Hotel, No.63 Ken Saro Wiwa Road, formerly Stadium Road, by
Mummy B Junction, Port Harcourt Rivers State on the 30th day of April 2026 at 11:00am, the
following Resolutions set out in the Notice of Annual General Meeting were put to the
Shareholders and were duly passed by the requisite majority;
1. That the Audited Financial Statements as at December 31st, 2025, the report of
the Directors, the Audit Committee, Auditor’s report be received;
2. That a dividend payment of N266,994,465.60 (Two hundred and sixty-six Million, nine
hundred and Ninety-four Thousand, four hundred and sixty-five Naira, sixty kobo) which
represents 30kobo per share was approved to be paid to Shareholders whose names are
registered in the Company’s Register of Members as at the close of business on Tuesday,
April 7th 2026 subject to the applicable taxes.
3a. That Prof.Edward Alikor is hereby retired as a Director of the Company;
3b. That Mr. Joe Ebinum is hereby retired as a Director of the Company;
4a. That Mr. Chijioke Mbagwu is hereby appointed as an Executive Director of the
Company;
4b. That Mr. Christian Ogidi’s appointment as an Executive Director is hereby ratified.
5. That the appointment of the Audit firm of BBC Professionals as the Company’s
External Auditors was approved
6. That the Directors are authorized to fix the remuneration of Auditors;
7. That Mr. Charles Uwaga, Mr. Adegbite Olushola and Mr. Christian Ugochukwu
Nwanma are hereby appointed as Shareholder representatives of the Audit
Committee.
