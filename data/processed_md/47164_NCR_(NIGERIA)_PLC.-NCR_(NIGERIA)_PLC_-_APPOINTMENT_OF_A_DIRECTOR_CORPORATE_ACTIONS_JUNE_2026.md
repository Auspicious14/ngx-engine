NCR (NIGERIA) PLC – NOTIFICATION OF THE APPOINTMENT OF A NEW
DIRECTOR
The Board of Directors of NCR (Nigeria) PLC (the “Company”), by way of a written resolution
dated Thursday, 11 June 2026, appointed Her Excellency, Mrs. Oluwatoyin Saraki, as a Non-
Executive Director on the Board of the Company with effect from 11 June 2026, subject to
ratification by the Shareholders at the next Annual General Meeting of the Company.
Her Excellency, Mrs. Oluwatoyin Ojora Saraki is the Founder-President of The Wellbeing
Foundation Africa (WBFA), a global advocate for women’s and children’s health and
empowerment, with over two decades of leadership and advocacy spanning reproductive,
maternal, newborn, child and adolescent health; the elimination of gender-based
discrimination and violence; and the advancement of education, socio-economic
empowerment, and community livelihoods across sub-Saharan Africa.
Mrs Saraki is the Inaugural and Emeritus Global Goodwill Ambassador for the International
Confederation of Midwives and serves as Special Advisor and member of the Independent
Advisory Group of the World Health Organization Regional Office for Africa. She is the UNFPA
Nigeria Family Planning Champion, the Save the Children Newborn Health Champion for
Nigeria, and a Global Champion for the White Ribbon Alliance for Safe Motherhood. She was
named a Universal Health Coverage Global Champion by Devex. She is a member of the
International Steering Committee of ICPD25 and was appointed the Inaugural Global Health
Ambassador of the WHO Foundation in 2022.
At the global governance level, Mrs Saraki serves on the Board of Trustees of Seed Global
Health and is a member of the Bayer Sustainability Council and the AstraZeneca Global
Breast Cancer Care Council. In 2024, she was appointed a Champion of the World Economic
Forum Global Alliance for Women’s Health and serves as a member of the World Economic
Forum Global Activators Network on Women’s Health. She is an Inaugural Leadership
Council Member of Africa REACH, a Leadership Council Member of Concordia, a Judge for
MIT Solve as Challenge Leader for Maternal and Newborn Health, and an Inaugural Member
of the African Women Leaders Network and African Women Leaders Network Nigeria. She is
the Honorary Global Patron of LifeLine International and serves on the Advisory Board of
Every Pregnancy.
Mrs Saraki brings to the Board a strong combination of legal expertise, international
governance experience, and strategic leadership across the private, public, and multilateral
sectors.
The Board and Management of the Company wish the Her Excellency, Mrs Saraki, all the best
as she takes on her new appointment.
