Lagos, 14th October, 2026
NOTICE OF BOARD MEETING AND CLOSED PERIOD
ABBEY BANK PLC ("the Bank") hereby announces as follows:
The Bank's Board Meeting will be held on Thursday, 22nd October, 2026 at
11 am to consider, inter alia, the Bank's Unaudited Financial Statements for
the period ended 30th September, 2026.
In Compliance with Rule 17.18 (a) of the Nigerian Exchange Limited's (NGX)
Closed Period Rules, as amended, the Bank Commenced a Closed Period for
transactions in the shares of the Bank by insiders from 1st October 2026 and
will continue until twenty-four (24) hours after the Unaudited Financial
Statements for the period ended 30th September, 2026 are released to the
publicv ia NGX's Issuers' Portal.
During the Closed Period, no insider, including Directors, employees and
other related parties, may directly or indirectly deal in the shares of the Bank.
