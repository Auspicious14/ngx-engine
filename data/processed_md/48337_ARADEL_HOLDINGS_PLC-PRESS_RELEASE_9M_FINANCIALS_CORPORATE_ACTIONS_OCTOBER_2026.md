PRESS RELEASE
Regulated Information
Aradel Holdings Plc reports a strong nine-month operating
performance; step-change in production and operating
margins; a return to net cash; reported earnings reduced
by Renaissance Earnout charge, and an interim dividend of
₦7.00 per share
Lagos, Nigeria – 30 October 2026: Aradel Holdings Plc ("Aradel", "Aradel Holdings", "the
Company" or "the Group"), Nigeria's leading integrated indigenous energy company, today
announces its unaudited results for the nine-month period ended 30 September 2026.
 Strong operating delivery from the enlarged Group: production of 144.7 kboepd,
revenue of ₦3,949.7 billion and EBITDA of ₦2,098.4 billion at a 53% margin; Nine-month
production running above the FY2026 guidance range of 110 – 140 kboepd. Accordingly, FY
2026 has been revised to 140 – 150 kboepd.
 Cash generation and balance sheet materially strengthened: net cash from operations
of ₦2,458.9 billion; the Group moved from a net debt of ₦475.1 billion to a net cash of ₦532.3
billion providing liquidity for funding capital expenditure, debt service, dividends and ₦543.9
billion of earnout payments.
 IFRS profit after tax of ₦43.3 billion (₦141.5 billion attributable to Aradel
shareholders; -₦98bn minority interest): after a ₦529.1 billion earnout charge under the
Renaissance acquisition; constructive engagements with the seller to address the imbalance
on the earnout arrangement are ongoing. Interim dividend of ₦7.00 per share declared.
9M 2026 Highlights
The Group delivered a strong operating performance in the first nine months of 2026, the first
reporting period in which ND Western and Renaissance operations are fully consolidated.
Production, revenue, EBITDA and operating cash flow all increased substantially, and the Group
ended the period with a net cash position of ₦532.3 billion.
IFRS profit after tax was ₦43.3 billion (9M 2025: ₦245.1 billion), of which ₦141.5 billion was
attributable to shareholders of the Company (9M 2025: ₦242.9 billion). It reflects a ₦529.1 billion
charge in respect of the earnout under Renaissance's acquisition of SPDC, which raises the
reported effective tax rate. Excluding adjusting items, profit after tax attributable to shareholders
of the Company was ₦572.4 billion, 134% above the prior year. The Board considers that the
operation of the earn-out arrangement no longer reflects the balanced participation the parties
intended following after-signing developments. Therefore, along with the other shareholders in
Renaissance, the Company is engaging the vendor to address this matter.

Basis of comparison: ND Western and Renaissance were equity-accounted associates in 9M 2025 and have
been fully consolidated since completion of the acquisition on 31 December 2025. Year-on-year percentage
movements therefore largely reflect the change in the Group's reporting perimeter rather than like-for-like
growth. 9M 2025 comparatives are as presented in the accompanying unaudited financial statements.
Operational Highlights
 Group average production increased by 502% to 144.7 kboepd (9M 2025: 24.0 kboepd), above
the FY2026 production guidance range of 110 – 140 kboepd, which has been revised to 140 –
150 kboepd.
- Average daily crude oil production of 57.9 kbopd (9M 2025: 15.3 kbopd), a 279% increase.
- Average daily gas production increased by 895% to 503.6 mmscf/d (9M 2025: 50.6
mmscf/d), driven by expanded processing capacity, sustained pipeline availability and
continued strong customer demand.
 Net refined product output of 194.2 million litres (9M 2025: 235.7 million litres), 18% lower,
reflecting constrained feedstock availability, evacuation constraints and planned maintenance
downtime in the first half; refinery utilisation stood at 45%.
- Third-quarter output of 68.0 million litres sustained the recovery achieved in the second
quarter (Q2 2026: 67.5 million litres; Q1 2026: 58.7 million litres) as measures to secure
feedstock and restore plant availability took effect.
 The Group’s Unit field operating cost stood at US$7.9/boe (9M 2025: US$11.0/boe), a 28%
reduction and is within the guidance range of US$7.0 – 9.0/boe, following increased reliability
of the Trans Niger Pipeline, lower field activity costs and inventory movements.
Financial Highlights
 Revenue up 633% to ₦3,949.7 billion (9M 2025: ₦538.8 billion), reflecting the consolidation
of ND Western and Renaissance, higher production and a firmer commodity price environment
(average realised prices of US$97.5/bbl and US$2.64/mscf vs 9M 2025: US$75.6/bbl and
US$1.64/mscf). Crude oil contributed 69% of revenue, gas 26% and refined products 5%.
 Cost of sales increased by 652% to ₦2,286.3 billion comprising a ₦455.8 billion stock
adjustment arising from an overlift position, higher royalty and statutory payments on stronger
crude prices, and increased field, host-community and well maintenance costs.
 Operating profit rose 832% to ₦1,562.1 billion (9M 2025: ₦167.5 billion), supported by other
income of ₦253.1 billion, principally crude handling fees from third-party use of the Group's
evacuation infrastructure.
 EBITDA of ₦2,098.4 billion, up 703% (9M 2025: ₦261.5 billion); EBITDA margin improved to
53% (9M 2025: 49%).
 Net finance costs of ₦797.3 billion (9M 2025: ₦6.0 billion) comprise interest on the consolidated
borrowings of ND Western and Renaissance, the unwinding discount on decommissioning
provisions and the earnout charge (unwinding of contingent consideration).
 Profits before tax increased 154% to ₦764.7 billion (9M 2025: ₦300.7 billion). Tax expense of
₦721.4 billion (9M 2025: ₦55.6 billion) reflects the higher tax rates associated with ND Western
and Renaissance.

 Profit after tax of ₦43.3 billion (9M 2025: ₦245.1 billion). Profits attributable to shareholders
of the Company stood at ₦141.5 billion (9M 2025: ₦242.9 billion), with earnings per share of
₦32.57 (9M 2025: ₦55.90).
 Net cash generated from operations of ₦2,458.9 billion (9M 2025: ₦205.4 billion), providing
liquidity to offset capital expenditure of ₦570.6 billion, interest and principal repayments of
₦580.5 billion, lease payments of ₦81.4 billion, dividends of ₦110.5 billion and earnout
payments of ₦543.9 billion.
 Cash and cash equivalents increased by 33% to ₦1,997.9 billion (31 December 2025: ₦1,504.7
billion).
 Total borrowings reduced by 25% to ₦1,507.0 billion (31 December 2025: ₦2,003.4 billion)
following the full repayment of two debt facilities in the first quarter and scheduled amortisation
across the Group.
 The Group moved to a net cash position of ₦532.3 billion at 30 September 2026 from net debt
of ₦475.1 billion at 31 December 2025.
Earnout consideration under the Renaissance acquisition
The seller of SPDC is entitled to an earnout based on their retained economic interest in a defined
perimeter within Renaissance’s assets. This is calculated by reference to the net revenues within
the set perimeter. The earnout is recognised in the Group's consolidated financial statements as
contingent consideration, with the related charge recorded within finance costs. As at 30
September 2026 the contingent consideration liability stood at ₦1,318.3 billion (31 December
2025: ₦1,420.0 billion), after cash payments of ₦543.9 billion within the period.
Impact on full year 2026. Without prejudice to the ongoing engagements to address the
imbalance, the earnout will continue to accrue for the remainder of 2026, broadly consistent with
the run-rate experienced to date.
The Group's FY2026 production guidance has been revised to 140 – 150 kboepd, and EBITDA,
operating cash generation and liquidity are expected to remain robust after the earnout payments.
Dividend
The Board has approved an interim dividend of ₦7.00 per share for the nine-month period ended
30 September 2026 (9M 2025: ₦10.00 per share), amounting to approximately ₦30.4 billion. In
setting the level, the Board has balanced the Group's strong operating cash generation and net
cash position against the capital commitments, ongoing debt service payment and other
obligations.

Mr. Adegbite Falade, Chief Executive Officer, commented:
“The first nine months of 2026 demonstrated what our enlarged portfolio can deliver. Group
production ran above our full-year guidance range, with strong growth in both crude oil and gas
output. This drove a step-change in revenue and EBITDA, alongside improved margins. Our
operations generated substantial cash, which allowed us to fund our investment programme,
reduce our debt and move the Group from net debt to net cash position.
We are well underway in constructive engagements with the seller to address the earnout
arrangements to ensure our earnings reflect the significantly improved operational performance.
As a Group we remain committed to creating long term growth and sustainability this is evidenced
in the recent major hydrocarbon discovery following successful delivery of the JK-004 exploration
well in shallow waters offshore Nigeria, in Oil Mining Lease 74 by Renaissance. We also achieved
first oil from our recently acquired Olo marginal field. This further deepens our future sustainability
by the additional hydrocarbon reserves.
Our priorities remain unchanged: optimising our enlarged asset base, improving uptime, growing
production, safe operations, deepening the share prosperity model with our host communities,
improving tax efficiency and allocating capital with discipline to deliver sustainable value for all
our shareholders."

About Aradel Holdings Plc
Aradel Holdings Plc (“Aradel Holdings” or “the Company”) is Nigeria’s leading integrated
indigenous energy company, delivering critical energy solutions in a sustainable and responsible
way. Aradel Holdings was incorporated on 25 March 1992 (as the Midas Drilling Fund), changed
its name to Niger Delta Exploration and Production Plc in November 1996, assumed its current
name in May 2023, and was listed on the main board of the NGX on 14 October 2024.
The Company operates through its subsidiaries:
 Aradel Energy Limited (100%), a wholly owned subsidiary of Aradel Holdings, as well as the
Operator of the Ogbele (PML 14), Omerelu (PPL 247), Olo and Olo West Marginal Fields, as
well as OPL 227 joint venture (subject to NUPRC approval). Established to explore and
harness opportunities in the energy industry.
 Aradel Gas Limited (100%), the only Nigerian independent Non-JV Gas Supplier to Bonny
LNG. Established to leverage investment opportunities in the gas sector. Has 100mmscf/d
gas processing facility.
 Aradel Investments Limited (100%), a wholly owned subsidiary established to hold and
manage the Group’s non-oil & gas assets. Established to hold the Company’s non-oil and gas
investments.
 Aradel Refineries Limited (95%), a 3-train 11kbbl/d independent operating midstream
refinery. Produces AGO, DPK, MDO, HFO and Naphtha.
 ND Western Limited (81.67%), an independent Nigerian oil and gas exploration and
production company comprising four leading industry players with three limited liability
companies (being Aradel Energy, First Exploration & Petroleum Development Company, and
Waltersmith Petroman Oil) as shareholders.
 Renaissance Africa Energy Company, a 53.3% total equity holding made up of
- A direct holding of 12.5% and
- Through ND Western, an indirect holding of 40.8%
