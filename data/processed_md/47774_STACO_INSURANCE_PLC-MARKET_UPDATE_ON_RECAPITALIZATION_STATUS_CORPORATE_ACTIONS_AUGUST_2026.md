MARKET UPDATE ON THE COMPANY’S RECAPITALIZATION PROCESS
Staco Insurance Plc (the "Company") wishes to inform its shareholders, the Nigerian
Exchange Limited ("NGX"), the investing public and other stakeholders that the Company
has remained actively engaged with the National Insurance Commission ("NAICOM or the
Commission") in respect of the industry's recapitalization exercise.
Throughout the recapitalization process, the Company has maintained constructive
engagement with NAICOM, including the NAICOM Recapitalization Committee, and has
kept the Commission apprised of the measures being implemented towards achieving
compliance with the applicable minimum capital requirements. As part of this process,
NAICOM recently provided the Company with the CBN account details for the statutory
deposit.
The Company has very recently obtained NAICOM's approval of its 2024 Audited Financial
Statements and has submitted the approved financial statements to the Financial Reporting
Council of Nigeria ("FRCN"). The Company is currently engaging with the FRCN to obtain its
Letter of No Objection, following which it will convene its Annual General Meeting, where
shareholders will be asked to consider and approve the Company's outstanding audited
financial statements as well as the proposed funding options to support the Company's
recapitalization and compliance with the applicable minimum capital requirements.
The Board and Management remain committed to complying with all applicable regulatory
requirements and will continue to engage with NAICOM and other relevant regulators on
matters relating to the Company's capital position and other regulatory obligations.
The Company will continue to keep the market informed of any material developments in
accordance with its continuing disclosure obligations under the NGX Issuers' Rules.
This announcement is made pursuant to Rules 17.3 and 17.5(m) of the NGX Issuers' Rules.
