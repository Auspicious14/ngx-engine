Lagos, 10th July 2026
NOTICE AND AGENDA OF THE 2026 SECOND QUARTER BOARD OF DIRECTORS’ MEETING OF
RONCHESS GLOBAL RESOURCES PLC
NOTICE IS HEREBY GIVEN that the second quarter Board of Directors Meeting of Ronchess Global
Resources Plc (“the Company”) will be held virtually on the 23rd day of July 2026, at 2:00 pm, to
consider the business as per the agenda below:
1. Consideration and Approval of Q2 2026 Unaudited Financial Statements
2. Q3 2026 Action items
3. Any other business
In compliance with the post-listing requirements of Nigerian Exchange Limited (NGX), a Closed
Period for trading in the Company’s shares commenced on Wednesday, 1st July 2026. It will continue
until twenty-four (24) hours after the Q2 2026 Unaudited Financial Statements (UFS) for the period
ended 30th June 2026 are released to the public.
Accordingly, no insider or their connected persons may, directly or indirectly, deal in the securities
of the Company during the Closed Period.
