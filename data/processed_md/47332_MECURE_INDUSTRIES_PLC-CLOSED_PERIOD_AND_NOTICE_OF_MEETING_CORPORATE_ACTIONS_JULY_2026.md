MECURE INDUSTRIES PLC
Lagos Nigeria
Wednesday 1st July 2026.
NOTIFICATION OF CLOSED PERIOD AND BOARD MEETING FOR APPROVAL OF
UNAUDITED FINANCIAL STATEMENTS FOR 2ND QUARTER ENDED 30TH JUNE 2026
This is to notify Nigerian Exchange Limited (NGX) and the investing public that the Board of
Directors of MeCure Industries Plc (the Company) will meet on Wednesday 29th July, 2026
to consider and approve the Company’s Unaudited Financial Statements for 2nd Quarter
ended 30th June, 2026, among others.
In compliance with the post-listing requirements of Nigerian Exchange Limited (NGX),
MECURE INDUSTRIES PLC will commence a closed period for trading in the Company’s
shares from 1st July, 2026 until twenty-four (24) hours after the Unaudited Financial
Statements for the 2nd Quarter ended 30th June, 2026 is filed with NGX and released to the
public.
Consequently, insiders of the Company and their connected persons are prohibited from
dealing in the shares of the Company when the trading window is closed.
Olawale Ibitoye
Company Secretary/Legal Adviser
