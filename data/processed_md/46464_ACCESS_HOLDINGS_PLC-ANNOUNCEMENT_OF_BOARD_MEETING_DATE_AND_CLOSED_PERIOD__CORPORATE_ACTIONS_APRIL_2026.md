Lagos, Nigeria
March 31, 2026
RETIREMENT OF DR. GREGORY JOBOME, EXECUTIVE DIRECTOR, RISK
MANAGEMENT AT ACCESS BANK PLC
Access Holdings Plc (“the Company”) hereby informs Nigerian Exchange Limited and
the investing public that Dr. Gregory Jobome, Executive Director, Risk Management
of the Company’s subsidiary, Access Bank Plc (‘the Bank’) will be retiring from the
Bank effective March 31, 2026 to begin a new chapter in life upon reaching the Bank’s
retirement age.
Dr Jobome joined the Bank as a General Manager and Group Head Enterprise Risk
Management in July 2010 and was appointed Executive Director Risk Management in
January 2017. His dedication and professionalism have been instrumental in
establishing the Bank’s risk management culture and fostering innovation, which has
enabled the organisation to create sustainable value to its stakeholders.
The Board extends its appreciation to Dr. Jobome for his outstanding service and
wishes him continued success in his future endeavours.
