UNITED BANK FOR AFRICA PLC
NOTICE OF RETIREMENT OF NON-EXECUTIVE DIRECTOR – ERELU ANGELA
ADEBAYO
Lagos, Monday, May 4, 2026
United Bank for Africa Plc (UBA), Africa’s Global Bank, announces the
retirement of Erelu Angela Adebayo, a Non-Executive Director, who joined
the UBA Group Board in August 2018. During her tenure, Erelu Angela
Adebayo provided distinguished leadership, serving as a member of the
Board Audit & Governance, Credit, Board Risk Management Committee and
Board Operations & Technology Committee.
The Board of UBA expresses its deep appreciation to Erelu Angela Adebayo
for her dedication and significant contributions to the Group. The Board
wishes Erelu Angela Adebayo the best in her future endeavors.
BY THE ORDER OF THE BOARD
