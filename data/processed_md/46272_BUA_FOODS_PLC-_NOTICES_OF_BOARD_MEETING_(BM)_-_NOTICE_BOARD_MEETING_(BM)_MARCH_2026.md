Lagos, 12th March 2026
ANNOUNCEMENT OF BOARD MEETING FOR THE CONSIDERATION AND
APPROVAL OF THE 2025 AUDITED FINANCIAL STATEMENTS
This notice serves to inform Nigerian Exchange Limited (NGX) and the investing public
that a meeting of the Board of Directors of BUA Foods Plc (the Company) is scheduled
for Thursday 26th March 2026 to consider amongst other agenda items, the 2025 Audited
Financial Statements for the year ended 31st December 2025 (2025 AFS) after which the
Financial Statements will be simultaneously filed with the relevant regulatory bodies and
released to the public.
As earlier announced and in compliance with the post listing requirements of the NGX
for listed Companies, the closed period in respect of 2025 AFS which commenced on 1st
of January, 2026 will continue until 24 hours after the release of the 2025 AFS on the
Issuers’ Portal of the NGX.
Accordingly, no insiders, and persons connected to them may directly or indirectly deal
in the shares of the Company, until 24 hours after the release of the 2025 AFS to NGX
and the public.
BY ORDER OF THE BOARD:
Oluseye Alayande
Company Secretary
