Stanbic IBTC Holdings PLC
Press release
Notification on Release of Audited 2026 Half Year Results
LAGOS, NIGERIA – 30 June 2026
This is to notify NGX Regulation Limited (NGX) as well as our Esteemed
Stakeholders that as with previous years, the Board of Directors has decided to
audit the Half Year Financial Results of Stanbic IBTC Holdings PLC (“Stanbic
IBTC” or “the Company”). Accordingly, the Audited Financial Statements for the
Half Year ending 30 June 2026 (HY 2026 AFS) will be presented to the Central
Bank of Nigeria (CBN) for approval, following which the HY 2026 AFS will then be
released to the Market no later than 29 August 2026.
The above is in compliance with Section 1.1.3 of the Rules for filing of Accounts
and Treatment of Default Filing, Rulebook of The Exchange (Issuers’ Rule) which
states that “An issuer that chooses, in addition, to audit its quarterly accounts shall
file such accounts not later than sixty (60) calendar days after the relevant quarter.”
Having duly notified NGX of this development, the Management of Stanbic IBTC
wishes to notify the investing public that the Company will aim to publish its HY
2026 AFS on or before 29 August 2026 in accordance with the extant Issuers Rule
as cited above.
Please note that the Company’s Closed Period which commenced on 01 June
2026, will continue to be in place until twenty-four (24) hours after the HY 2026
AFS have been published.
