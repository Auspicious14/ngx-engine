Commenting on the results, Ayodele Abioye, the Managing Director, said:
“Our nine-month 2026 results demonstrate the resilience of BUA Foods’ operating model and the
benefits of disciplined cost and margin management. Despite a 19% decline in revenue to ₦1.15 trillion,
driven by softer pricing conditions and subdued consumer demand, we maintained a substantial
improvement in profitability through effective cost optimization, improved productivity, and rigorous
execution across the business.
The sustained strength of our brands, and continued investment in route-to-market capabilities provide
a solid foundation for future growth.
Looking ahead, we remain focused on driving profitable growth, enhancing market penetration, and
maintaining operational efficiency. While operating environment remains dynamic, BUA Foods is well-
positioned to leverage its scale, strong market positions, and execution capabilities to deliver
sustainable long-term value.”

Revenue dropped by 19% year-on-year to ₦1.15 trillion in 9M 2026 (9M 2025: ₦1.42 trillion). This
was primarily due to a year-on-year decline in our Sugar, Flour, and Rice reporting segments: 17%
drop in Sugar to ₦495 billion (9M 2025: ₦599.2 billion), 30% drop in Flour to ₦415.1 billion (9M
2025: ₦591.5 billion), and 53% drop in Rice to ₦37.5 billion (9M 2025: ₦79.5 billion).
Cost of sales decreased by (-34%) to ₦592.94 billion in 9M 2026 (9M 2025: ₦900.09 billion) driven
primarily by the reduction in raw material cost, energy cost, and exchange rate stability.
Gross profit increased by 6% to ₦552.90 billion in 9M 2026 (9M 2025: ₦520.65 billion). In
alignment with the increase, gross profit margin also appreciated by 1160bps to 48.3% in 9M 2026
(9M 2025: 36.7%).
Selling and distribution expenses decreased by 15% to ₦45.04 billion in 9M 2026 (9M 2025:
₦53.22 billion) due to a decrease in logistics expenses within the period.
Administrative expenses also decreased by 28% to ₦21.95 billion in 9M 2026 (9M 2025: ₦30.43
billion) reflecting a continued focus on cost optimization and operational efficiency initiatives.
Total operating expenses decreased by 20% to ₦66.99 billion in 9M 2026 (9M 2025: ₦83.65
billion) on the back of a decrease in selling, distribution, and administrative expenses across the
supply chain network.
EBITDA increased by 12% to ₦499.91 billion in 9M 2026 (9M 2025: ₦444.88 billion), driven by
growth in gross profit. Also, EBITDA margin appreciated by 1230bps to 43.6% in 9M 2026 (9M
2025: 31.3%).
Operating profit grew by 13% to ₦492.50 billion in 9M 2026 (9M 2025: ₦437.58 billion) benefiting
from reduced cost of sales, driven by disciplined cost containment practices. In correlation,
operating profit margin appreciated by 1200bps to 43% in 9M 2026 (9M 2025: 31%).
Finance Cost (Net) increased by 146% to ₦11.98 billion in 9M 2026 (9M 2025: ₦4.86 billion) due
to a lower finance income available to offset finance expenses within the reporting period under
review.
Profit before tax increased by 11% to ₦480.52 billion in 9M 2026 (9M 2025: ₦432.58 billion), as
profit before tax margin also increased by 1150bps to 42% in 9M 2026 (9M 2025: 30.5%)
Profit after tax also increased by 11% to ₦450.47 billion in 9M 2026 (9M 2025: ₦405.27 billion).
In correlation, the Earning per Share (EPS) grew by 11% to ₦25.03 in 9M 2026 from ₦22.52 in the
corresponding period.
Total assets declined marginally by 2% to ₦1.36 trillion as of 9M 2026 (FY 2025: ₦1.39 trillion) due
to lower trade transactions.
Total liabilities increased by 4.2% to ₦702.5 billion as of 9M 2026 (FY 2025: ₦674.4 billion).
Total equity decreased by 7.5% to ₦659.91 billion as of 9M 2026 (FY 2025: ₦713.44 billion) primarily
due to a drop of 7.6% in retained earnings to ₦651.9 billion as of 9M 2026 (FY 2025: ₦705.4 billion).

Operating segment performance
Sugar division
The Sugar division contributed 43% to revenue in 9M 2026 (9M 2025: 42%). Sugar Revenue
dropped by 17% to ₦495.1 billion in 9M 2026 (9M 2025: ₦599.2 billion). The decrease in revenue
was due to the alignment with strategic and competitive market pricing initiatives, during the
period under review.
Flour division
The Flour division contributed 36% to revenue in 9M 2026 (9M 2025: 42%). Revenue declined by
30% to ₦415.1 billion in 9M 2026 (9M 2025: ₦591.5 billion). The decrease in revenue was driven
majorly by softer demand for our Flour products within the period under review.
Pasta division
The Pasta division contributed 18% to revenue in 9M 2026 (9M 2025: 10%). Revenue increased by
32% to ₦198.1 billion in 9M 2026 (9M 2025: ₦150.5 billion). The increase in sales volume recorded
was due to high demand for our Pasta products within the period under review. In addition, the
division’s contribution margin increased to 43% from 37% for the corresponding period of the
prior year.
Rice division
The Rice division contributed 3% to the revenue in 9M 2026 (9M 2025: 6%). Revenue declined to
₦37.5 billion in 9M 2026 (9M 2025: ₦79.5 billion), due to strategic and competitive market pricing
initiatives, during the period under review.

Definition of terms
Gross profit corresponds to revenue minus cost of sales.
Gross profit margin corresponds to gross profit as a percentage of revenue.
Operating expenses corresponds to Selling and distribution expenses, administrative expenses and
Other operating expenses.
EBITDA corresponds to earnings before interest, tax, depreciation and amortization.
EBITDA margin corresponds to EBITDA as a percentage of revenue.
Operating profit corresponds to gross profit minus operating expenses plus other operating income.
Operating profit Margin corresponds to EBIT as a percentage of revenue.
Profit before Tax corresponds to EBIT minus net finance (cost)/income and plus share of profit of
associates and joint venture using the equity method.
Profit before tax margin corresponds to profit before tax as a percentage of revenue.
Return on equity corresponds to net profit reported to average total equity.
Return on assets corresponds to net profit reported to average total assets.
Earnings per share corresponds to profit after tax from continuing operations reported to weighted
average number of shares.

About BUA Foods Plc
BUA Foods Plc (NGX: BUAFOODS) is a leading food business with well diversified and scalable operations
producing sugar, flour, pasta, rice and edible oils. The Company owns strategically located plants across
Nigeria, in addition to a cordial alliance with local stakeholders in host communities. Additionally, BUA
Foods is a resilient business built on a strong brand proposition and is an operator that has a well-
known reputation for delivering high-quality products.
BUA Foods continues to invest in modern technology for efficient food production, innovatively
expanding with strategic partners across the value chain. The Company is also well positioned to
leverage significant export potentials across West Africa and the larger African continent.
Headquartered and listed in Nigeria, BUA Foods is one of the most capitalized companies on the NGX
Exchange and a leading consumer goods firm by market value.
