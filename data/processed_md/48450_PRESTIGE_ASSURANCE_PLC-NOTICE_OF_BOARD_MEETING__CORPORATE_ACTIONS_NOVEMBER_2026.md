Lagos, 26 November, 2026.
NOTICE OF BOARD MEETING
Prestige Assurance Plc (the Company) wishes to inform Nigerian Exchange Limited (NGX),

Company will be held hybrid at No. 19, Ligali Ayorinde Street, Victoria Island, Lagos, on

amongst other agenda items.
