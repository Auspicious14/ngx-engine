Lagos, Nigeria 14th July 2026
NEIMETH INTERNATIONAL PHARMACEUTICALS PLC – NOTICE OF BOARD MEETING
This is to notify Nigerian Exchange Limited (NGX) and the investing public that a meeting of the Board
of Directors of Neimeth International Pharmaceuticals Plc. (the Company) is scheduled to hold on Friday,
24th July 2026, to consider and approve the Company’s Unaudited Financial Statements for the period
ended 30th June 2026 (2026 Q2, UFS).
In line with NGX’s Amended Closed Period Rules, the Company commenced a closed period from 1st July
2026 and this will run up to twenty-four (24) hours after the outcome of the meeting and the 2026 Q2,
UFS is released to the public.
Consequently, Directors and other insiders of the Company have been duly notified not to deal in the
Company’s securities during the closed period.
Thank you.
CHINENYE S. A DEKANMBI
Company Secretary
