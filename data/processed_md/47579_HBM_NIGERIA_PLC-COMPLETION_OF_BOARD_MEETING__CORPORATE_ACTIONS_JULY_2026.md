LAGOS: 28th July 2026
HBM NIGERIA PLC - OUTCOME OF BOARD MEETING
We hereby notify Nigerian Exchange Limited and the investing public that at the meeting of the Board of Directors
of HBM Nigeria Plc (the “Company”) held today, Tuesday 28th July 2026, the Board considered and approved the
following resolutions:
1. That the Company’s Unaudited Financial Statements for the quarter ended 30th June 2026 (the “Unaudited
Financial Statements”) be and is hereby approved.
2. That an interim dividend of N16 per unit of ordinary share for the quarter ended 30th June 2026 be and is
hereby declared, subject to deduction of withholding tax.
The Closed Period for trading in the Company’s shares, which commenced from 1st July 2026, will continue until
twenty-four (24) hours after the Unaudited Financial Statements is released to the public (the “Closed Period”), in
line with the Company’s Insider Trading Policy and the Rules of Nigerian Exchange Limited.
