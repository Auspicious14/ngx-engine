Lagos, Nigeria
August 14, 2026
POTENTIAL DELAY IN THE PUBLICATION OF ACCESS HOLDINGS PLC’S
INTERIM AUDITED FINANCIAL STATEMENTS FOR THE PERIOD ENDED JUNE
30, 2026
Access Holdings Plc (‘the Company’) hereby informs the investing public and the
Nigerian Exchange Limited (‘The Exchange’) of a potential delay in the release of its
Interim Audited Financial Statements for the period ended June 30, 2026 (‘the
Results’).
The potential delay is due to the ongoing finalisation of the audit process and the need
to obtain the Central Bank of Nigeria’s approval before publishing the Results.
The Company has received The Exchange’s approval for an extension of time to file
the Results on or before September 30, 2026, subject to receipt of the required
regulatory approval.
The Company’s insiders are reminded that the Closed Period previously declared for
transactions in the Company’s securities remains in effect until twenty-four (24) hours
after the Results are released.
Thank you.
SUNDAY EKWOCHI
COMPANY SECRETARY
