NOTICE OF APPOINTMENT OF NON-EXECUTIVE DIRECTOR OF VFD GROUP PLC
The Board of Directors of VFD Group Plc (the “Company”) hereby notifies the Nigerian
Exchange Limited (“NGX”) and the investing public of the appointment of Mrs. Omobola
Fowler as a Non-Executive Director of the Company, effective November 16, 2026, and
subject to approval of shareholders at a General Meeting and requisite regulators.
Mrs. Fowler is a strategy and business development executive with over seventeen (17) years
of experience in growth strategy, mergers and acquisitions, joint venture structuring and
digital transformation across the FMCG, automotive, real estate, logistics, technology and
financial services sectors in West Africa and the United Kingdom. She holds a Bachelor of
Science degree in Economics from Covenant University, Ota, and a Modular Executive MBA
from Lagos Business School. She has also completed executive education at Harvard
Business School Online and the W. P. Carey School of Business, Arizona State University,
and holds a Certificate in Corporate Governance and PRINCE2 certification.
Her career includes roles at Zenith Bank Plc and A.G. Leventis (Nigeria) Plc, where she rose
to Head, Strategy and Business Development, and as Director, Business Development and
Technology at BA Distribution (Gallo Wines) and Great Brands (British American Tobacco).
She is currently a Business Transformation and Partnerships Consultant at Zome Consulting
Ltd. She previously served on the Board of the Company as a Non-Executive Director from
2016 to 2019, where she chaired the Corporate Governance Committee and was a member
of the Audit and Risk Committee.
The Board is satisfied that Mrs. Fowler possesses the requisite competence, experience,
integrity, and leadership capacity required for the position and is confident that her
appointment will further enhance the Company’s governance framework, strategic oversight,
and long-term value creation objectNotification- Non-Executive Director- Mobolaives.
