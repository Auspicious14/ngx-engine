INTERNAL COMMUNICATION
Notification of Closed Period for International Breweries Plc
Pursuant to the provisions of the Nigerian Exchange Limited (“NGX”) Rulebook and
International Breweries Plc’s (“the Company”) Insider Trading Policy, we are
notifying NGX, our shareholders and the investing public that the Board of
Directors of the Company is scheduled to meet on Friday, 23rd October 2026 to
consider the Unaudited Financial Statements for the period ending
30th September 2026, amongst other matters.
The closed period will commence on Thursday, 1 October 2026 and will remain
in effect until twenty-four (24) hours after the Unaudited Financial Statements for
the period ending 30th September 2026 is released to NGX and the public.
During this period, all Directors, employees, insiders, or their connected
persons are advised not to trade in the shares of the Company.
