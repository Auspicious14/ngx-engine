Lagos, June 29, 2026
ANNOUNCEMENT OF BOARD MEETING DATE AND CLOSED PERIOD OF NIGERIAN AVIATION HANDLING
COMPANY PLC
This is to inform Nigerian Exchange Limited (NGX or The Exchange) and our esteemed shareholders that
a meeting of the Board of Directors of Nigerian Aviation Handling Company Plc (the Company) has been
scheduled to hold on 29th July 2026, to consider and approve the Company’s 2026 Second Quarter
Unaudited Financial Statements (2026 Q2 UFS), along with other agenda items.
Our esteemed shareholders and Insiders are also informed that in compliance with the Amended Closed
Period Rules of The Exchange, Nigerian Aviation Handling Company Plc has declared a closed period from
1st July 2026 until twenty-four (24) hours after the Company’s 2026 Q2 UFS is released to the Market at
the end of July 2026.
Accordingly, no insider of the Company and their connected persons should deal with the shares of the
Company directly or indirectly or in any manner during the closed period.
