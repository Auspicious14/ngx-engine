Corporate Actions Announcement
Lagos, 15 May 2026
C & I Leasing Plc hereby announces as follows:
Year Ended 31st December 2025
A Final Dividend of 20kobo for every share of 50K, subject to appropriate withholding tax
and approval, will be paid to shareholders whose names appear in the Register of Members

The Register of Members will be closed from 2nd day of June 2026 to 5th day of June 2026

names appear on the Register of Members as 1st day of June 2026, and who have completed

E-Dividend Registration respective Banks.
