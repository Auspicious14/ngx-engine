Lagos, Nigeria
July 31, 2026
RE: FALSE SOCIAL MEDIA PUBLICATION
Access Holdings Plc (“the Company”) wishes to notify Nigerian Exchange Limited
(“NGX”), its shareholders and the investing public of a false and malicious publication
currently circulating on social media purporting to announce that the Company’s
subsidiary, Access Bank Plc will cease banking operations.
The Company confirms that the publication is entirely false and was not issued or
authorised by Access Holdings Plc or Access Bank Plc.
Access Bank Plc remains fully operational, safe, well-capitalised and continues to
serve its customers. There has been no change to the operations or financial condition
of Access Bank Plc or Access Holdings Plc.
The matter has been referred to the relevant regulatory, security and law enforcement
authorities, and appropriate steps are being taken to identify those responsible for
originating and disseminating the false publication.
Shareholders, customers and the investing public are advised to disregard the
publication in its entirety and rely only on information released through the official
communication channels of Access Holdings Plc, Access Bank Plc and the NGX.
By Order of the Board
SUNDAY EKWOCHI
COMPANY SECRETARY
