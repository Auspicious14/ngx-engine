UNILEVER NIGERIA PLC. hereby announces as follows:
Period Ended 30th June 2026
Proposed Interim Dividend An Interim Dividend of N2.00 kobo for every ordinary share of 50k each, subject to
deduction of appropriate withholding tax, will be paid to shareholders whose names
appear in the Register of Members as at the close of business on Friday, 31 July 2025.
Proposed Bonus NIL
Closure of Register The Register of Shareholders will be closed from Monday, 3 August 2026 to Friday, 7
August 2026 (both dates inclusive).
Qualification Date Friday, 31 July 2026.
Payment Date On Friday 14 August 2026, dividends will be paid electronically to shareholders whose
names appear on the Register of Members as at Friday 31, July 2026 and who have
completed the e-dividend registration and mandated the Registrar to pay their dividends
directly into their Bank accounts.
E-Dividend Shareholders who are yet to complete the e-dividend registration are advised to
Registration download the Registrar’s E-Dividend Mandate Activation Form, which is also available
on the website of the Registrars: www.greenwichregistrars.com, complete and submit to
the Registrar or their respective Banks.
