1. General information
UAC of Nigeria PLC (the "Company") is a company incorporated and domiciled in Nigeria. The Company is a public limited
company listed on The Nigerian Exchange Limited and its registered office is at 1-5 Odunlami Street, Marina, Lagos.
UAC of Nigeria PLC and its subsidiaries (together "the Group") is a diversified business with activities in the following principal
sectors: Edibles and Feed, Paints, Packaged Food and Beverages, and Quick Service Restaurants.
