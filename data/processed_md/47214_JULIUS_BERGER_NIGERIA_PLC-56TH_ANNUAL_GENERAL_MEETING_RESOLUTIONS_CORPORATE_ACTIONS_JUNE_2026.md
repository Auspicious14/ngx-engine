JULIUS BERGER NIGERIA PLC
56TH ANNUAL GENERAL MEETING
FCT Abuja, June 19, 2026
At the 56th Annual General Meeting of Julius Berger Nigeria Plc (“the Company”) held on June 18, 2026 at
Shehu Musa Yar’Adua Center, 1 Memorial Drive, FTC Abuja, Members passed the following resolutions to:
1. Authorise a dividend of ₦4.25K per 50K share. The Dividends will be issued on June 19, 2026;
2. Approve the re-election of Mr. Chidi Anya, ESQ, Amb. Adamu Saidu Daura. MFR and Engr. Jafaru
Damulak as Non-Executive Directors of the Company;
3. Authorise the Directors to fix the remuneration of the Auditors;
4. Constitute Mr. Ernest Chukwudi Ebi MFR, FCIB, FIOD and Mr. Chidi Anya ESQ, the Board’s
representatives, and Chief Timothy Ayobami Adesiyan, HRH, Sir Sunday Nnamdi Nwosu, KSS, JP,
and Brig. Gen. Emmanuel Ebije Ikwue, GCON, the 3 (three) elected representatives of the
Shareholders into the Statutory Audit Committee for the Financial year ending December 31, 2026;
5. Approve, until further review, the Directors fees in the sums of ₦20 million (Twenty Million Naira) for
the Non-Executive Directors and ₦28 million (Twenty-Eight Million Naira) for the Chairman effective
from the January 1, 2026.
CECILIA EKANEM MADUEKE, ESQ
COMPANY SECRETARY
