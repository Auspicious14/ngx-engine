Corporate Actions Announcement
Stanbic IBTC ETF 30 hereby announce as follows:
Period Ended 30 June 2026
An interim dividend of ₦3.07 for every unit, subject to appropriate withholding tax and approval
will be paid to Unitholders whose names appear in the Register of Members as at the close
Proposed Dividend of business on 03 August 2026.
Proposed Bonus NIL
Closure of Register The Register of Unitholders will be closed from 04 August 2026 to 05 August 2026.
Qualification Date 03 August 2026
On 06 August 2026, dividends will be paid electronically to Unitholders whose names appear
on the Register of Members as at 03 August 2026 and who have completed the e-dividend
registration and mandated the Registrar to pay their dividends directly into their Bank
Payment Date accounts.
Unitholders who are yet to complete the e-dividend registration are advised to download the
Registrar’s E-Dividend Mandate Activation Form, which is also available on the website of the
Registrars: : www.firstregistrarsnigeria.com complete and submit to the Registrar or their
E-Dividend Registration respective Banks.
Unclaimed Dividend Unitholders with dividend warrants and share certificates that have remained unclaimed or
Warrants and Share are yet to be presented for payment or returned for validation are advised to complete the e-
Certificates dividend registration or contact the Registrar.
