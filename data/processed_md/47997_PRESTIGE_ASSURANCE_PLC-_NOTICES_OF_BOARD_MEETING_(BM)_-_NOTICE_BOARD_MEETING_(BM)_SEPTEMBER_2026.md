Lagos, 30 September 2026.
NOTIFICATION OF CLOSED PERIOD AND BOARD MEETING
Prestige Assurance Plc (the Company) wishes to inform Nigerian Exchange Limited (NGX),
its shareholders and the investing public that the meeting of the Board of Directors of the
Company will be held hybrid on Wednesday, 28th October 2026 at No. 19, Ligali Ayorinde
Street, Victoria Island, Lagos, to consider and approve the Q3 Unaudited Financial
Statements for the period ending 30 September 2026 (2026 Q3 UFS), amongst other
agenda items.
In line with the amended Closed Period Rules of NGX, the Closed Period for trading in the
Company’s shares by insiders will commence on Thursday, 1st October, 2026, and will
continue until twenty-four (24) hours after the 2026 Q3 UFS has been released to the
public.
Sequel to the foregoing, no insider and their connected persons may directly or indirectly
deal in the securities of the Company during the closed period.
