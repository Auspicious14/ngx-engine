October 2, 2026
Lagos, Nigeria
ANNOUNCEMENT OF BOARD MEETING DATE AND CLOSED PERIOD OF
ACCESS HOLDINGS PLC
This is to inform the Nigerian Exchange Limited (‘The Exchange’) and the investing
public that a meeting of the Board of Directors of Access Holdings Plc (‘the Company’)
is scheduled to hold on October 29, 2026. The meeting will amongst other things
consider and approve the Group’s Unaudited Financial Statements for the Quarter
Ended September 30, 2026 (‘the Results’).
In line with the Exchange’s Listing Rules, the Company’s Insiders are reminded that
the earlier declared Closed Period concerning transactions on its securities remains,
until twenty-four (24) hours after the Results are released to the public.
Accordingly, no insider and their connected persons may, directly or indirectly deal in
the securities of the Company in any manner during the Closed Period.
BY ORDER OF THE BOARD
SUNDAY EKWOCHI
COMPANY SECRETARY
