Resolutions passed at the Board of Directors Meeting
At the meeting of the Board of Directors of International Breweries Plc (“the Company”)
held via Teleconference on Friday, 23 October 2026, the following resolutions were
proposed and duly passed:
1. IT WAS RESOLVED “THAT the Unaudited Financial Statements (FS) for the period
ended 30 September 2026 be and are hereby approved”. IT WAS RESOLVED “THAT
the approved Unaudited FS be submitted to the Regulatory Authorities in their
prescribed forms, and
2. IT WAS FURTHER RESOLVED “THAT the Company Secretary be and is hereby
authorized to publish in the prescribed forms, the unaudited FS in two (2) National
dailies.
3. Please note that the Company’s Closed Period, which commenced on 1st October 2026, will
remain in effect until after the release of the unaudited financial statements.
