ZICHIS AGRO ALLIED INDUSTRIES PLC (RC. 1026336)
Ogun State, 1 April 2026
Board Meeting Resolutions
At the Board Meeting of Zichis Agro-Allied Industries PLC held virtually on the
19th February, 2026, the following resolutions were proposed and duly passed:
1. Declaration of final dividend of 20 kobo per 50 kobo ordinary share,
subject to applicable withholding tax, payable to shareholders whose name
appear in the Register Of Members as at the close of business on 16th of
March 2026; and
2. Declaration of bonus issue in the ratio of One (1) new ordinary share for
everyone (1) existing ordinary share held, to shareholders whose name
appears in the Register of Members as at close of business on 16th March,
2026, subject to the approval of shareholders at the forthcoming Annual
General Meeting .
Chief Solomon Itsede
Company Secretary
