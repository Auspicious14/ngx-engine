CORPORATE ACTIONS ANNOUNCEMENT
ETERNA PLC hereby announces as follows:
Year Ended December 31, 2025
Proposed Dividend A dividend of N0.50 kobo per ordinary share of 50 kobo each,
subject to the deduction of appropriate withholding tax and
approval at the forthcoming Annual General Meeting (“AGM”) of
the Company will be paid to shareholders whose names appear
in the Register of Members as at the close of business on
Monday, April 13, 2026.
Proposed Bonus NIL
Qualification Date April 13, 2026
Closure of Register The Register of Members will be closed from Tuesday April 14,
2026.
Payment Date On May 12, 2026, dividends will be paid electronically to
shareholders whose names appear on the Register of Members
as of April 13, 2026, and who have completed the e-dividend
registration and mandated the Registrar to pay their dividends
directly into their Bank accounts.
E-Dividend Registration Shareholders who are yet to complete the e-dividend
registration are advised to download the Registrar’s E-Dividend
Mandate Activation Form which is also available on the website
of the Registrar: https://greenwichregistrars.com/ complete
same and submit to the Registrar or their respective Banks.
Unclaimed Dividend Shareholders with dividend warrants and share certificates that
have remained unclaimed or are yet to be presented for payment
or returned for validation are advised to complete the E-Dividend
registration or contact the Registrar.
Date of Annual General The Company’s Annual General Meeting will be held on May
Meeting 12, 2026
