May 18, 2026
Lagos, Nigeria
CONVEYANCE OF THE ANNUAL GENERAL MEETING (“AGM”) OF GEREGU POWER PLC
Geregu Power PLC (“Geregu” or “the Company”) refers to its corporate action published
on January 19, 2026.
The Company hereby notifies the Nigerian Exchange Limited (NGX), its shareholders, and
the investing public of a delay in holding its Annual General Meeting (“AGM”), which was
previously expected to be convened on or before April 30th, 2026.
Following the release of the Company’s Audited Financial Statements and the
announcement of the proposed AGM date, the Company, being in its change
management and transition phase, had to prioritize alignment and coordination on key
governance and operational matters. This regrettably impacted the Company’s ability to
finalize arrangements required to convene the AGM within the initially communicated
timeline.
The Company wishes to assure its shareholders and stakeholders that it remains fully
committed to maintaining high standards of corporate governance, transparency, and
regulatory compliance. Accordingly:
• The Company is expediting all outstanding processes required to convene the AGM
within the required timeline;
• A new date for the AGM will be communicated to the market promptly upon
conclusion of these processes and receipt of all necessary approvals; and
• Dividends will be paid on the communicated date of the AGM upon the approval of
the shareholders in the general meeting.
The Board and Management appreciate the continued understanding and support of
shareholders and stakeholders.
