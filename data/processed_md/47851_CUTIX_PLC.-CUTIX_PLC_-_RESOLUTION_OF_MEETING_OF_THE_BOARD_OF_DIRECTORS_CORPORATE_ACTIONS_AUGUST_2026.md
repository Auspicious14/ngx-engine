OUTCOME OF MEETING OF THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF CUTIX PLC
Cutix Plc (“the Company”) wishes to notify Nigerian Exchange Limited (NGX) and the
investing public that the 172nd meeting of the Board of Directors of the Company was
held on Thursday, August 27, 2026, as scheduled.
The First Quarter Unaudited Financial Statements (Q1 UFS) for the period ended July 31,
2026, amongst other matters, were considered and approved by the Board subject to few
amendments.
The Q1 UFS will be filed with NGX on or before August 30, 2026. Consequently, the
closed period earlier announced by the Company will subsist until twenty-four (24) hours
after the Q1 UFS has been made public.
