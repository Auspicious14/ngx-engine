CHEMICAL AND ALLIED PRODUCTS PLC – NOTICE OF CHANGE IN MANAGEMENT
Chemical and Allied Products PLC (the “Company” or “CAP PLC”), a subsidiary of UAC of
Nigeria PLC (“UAC”), hereby notifies Nigerian Exchange Limited, its shareholders and the
investing public that its current Managing Director, Mrs. Bolarin Okunowo, will be transitioning
to a new executive role within the UAC Group, effective 25 June 2026.
The Board of Directors (the “Board”) expresses its sincere appreciation to Mrs. Okunowo for her
exceptional leadership, commitment and significant contributions to the Company
throughout her tenure. The Board wishes her continued success as she transitions into her new
role within the UAC Group.
The Board is pleased to announce the appointment of Mr. Olalekan Aluko as Managing
Director of the Company with effect from 25 June 2026. Mr. Aluko brings over 16 years of
extensive operational and supply chain expertise to the role and is currently serving as the
Company’s Chief Supply Chain Officer.
PROFILE OF MR. OLALEKAN ALUKO
Mr. Olalekan Aluko joined CAP PLC in July 2022 as Head of Operations and was subsequently
appointed Chief Supply Chain Officer in March 2025.
In his current role, he provides end-to-end leadership across the Company’s supply chain,
managing all phases of operations from planning through to logistics, ensuring seamless service
delivery to our customers, whilst remaining focused on safety, product quality and operational
efficiency.
Since joining CAP PLC, he has driven measurable improvements in operational performance,
eliminating bottlenecks, improving turnaround times and enhancing customer experiences
whilst delivering exceptional financial performance for the Company. Under his leadership, the
Company continues to maintain strong governance of ISO-aligned management systems,
reinforcing compliance, risk management, and continuous improvement.
A transformation-focused and execution-driven leader, Mr. Aluko is known for his structured
and customer-centric approach to driving operational excellence, strengthening cross-
functional alignment, and driving high performance across the organisation. His contributions
have been recognised at UAC Group level where he is a recipient of the UAC Excellence
Award.

Prior to joining CAP PLC, he served as Supply Chain and Plant Director at Suntory Beverage
and Food Nigeria. Mr. Aluko has also held roles at other notable multinationals including
GlaxoSmithKline and Guinness Nigeria PLC.
He holds a Bachelor of Science degree in Electronics and Electrical Engineering and a Master
of Business Administration, both from Obafemi Awolowo University, Ile-Ife.
