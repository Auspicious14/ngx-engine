NCR (NIGERIA) PLC – APPROVAL OF THE AUDITED FINANCIAL STATEMENTS
FOR THE YEAR ENDED 31 DECEMBER 2025
This is to notify the investing public and Nigerian Exchange Limited (NGX) that the
Board of Directors of NCR (Nigeria) PLC (the Company) held a meeting on Friday, 27
March 2026, to consider and approve, amongst other things, the Company’s Audited
Financial Statements for the full year ended 31 December 2025 (the “Audited Financial
Statements”).
The Board was unable to conclude consideration and approval of the Audited Financial
Statements at its meeting due to additional internal review arising from changes in the
Company’s shareholding structure during the 2025 financial year, which necessitated
alignment of reporting processes.
Please note that the closed period prohibiting the Company’s Insiders from transacting
in the shares of the Company, which commenced on 31 December 2025 until twenty-
four (24) hours after the 2025 Audited Financial Statements for the year ended 31
December 2025, has been released to the Market, is still in place.
