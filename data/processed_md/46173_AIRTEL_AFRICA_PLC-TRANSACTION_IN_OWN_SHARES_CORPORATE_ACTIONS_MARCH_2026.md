Airtel Africa plc
(‘Airtel Africa’, or the ‘Company’)
Transaction in Own Shares
London and Lagos, 02 March 2026: Airtel Africa announces today it has
purchased the following number of its ordinary shares of USD 0.50 each from
Barclays Capital Securities Limited pursuant to the authority granted by its
shareholders as part of its share buy-back programme, details of which were
announced on 22 September 2025.
Aggregate information:
Date of purchase 27 February 2026
Aggregate number of ordinary shares
60,000
purchased:
Lowest price paid per share (GBp): 347.80p
Highest price paid per share (GBp): 354.80p
Volume weighted average price paid (GBp): 351.87p
The purchased ordinary shares will be held in treasury by the Company.
Following the purchase of these ordinary shares, the remaining ordinary shares
of USD 0.50 each in issue will be 3,654,881,028, including 9,378,490 treasury
shares. Accordingly, the total number of voting rights in the Company is
3,645,502,538. This figure may be used by shareholders as the denominator for
the calculations by which they will determine whether they are required to notify
their interest in, or a change to their interest in, the Company under the Financial
Conduct Authority's Disclosure Guidance and Transparency Rules.
The purchases were made pursuant to the revised arrangements entered into with Barclays
Capital Securities Limited announced on 22 September 2025.
