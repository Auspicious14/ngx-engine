MECURE INDUSTRIES PLC
Lagos Nigeria
Friday 2nd October 2026.
NOTIFICATION OF CLOSED PERIOD AND BOARD MEETING FOR APPROVAL OF
UNAUDITED FINANCIAL STATEMENTS FOR 3rd QUARTER ENDED 30TH SEPTEMBER
2026
This is to notify Nigerian Exchange Limited (NGX) and the investing public that the Board of
Directors of MeCure Industries Plc (the Company) will meet on Wednesday 28th October,
2026 to consider and approve the Company’s Unaudited Financial Statements for 3rd Quarter
ended 30th September, 2026, among others.
In compliance with the post-listing requirements of Nigerian Exchange Limited (NGX),
MECURE INDUSTRIES PLC commenced a closed period for trading in the Company’s
shares from 1st October, 2026 until twenty-four (24) hours after the Unaudited Financial
Statements for the 3rd Quarter ended 30th September, 2026 is filed with NGX and released to
the public.
Consequently, insiders of the Company and their connected persons are prohibited from
dealing in the shares of the Company when the trading window is closed.
