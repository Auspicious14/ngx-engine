Corporate Actions Announcement
Stanbic IBTC Holdings PLC hereby announce as follows:
Period Ended 30 June 2026
An Interim Dividend of N4.50 (Four Naira Fifty Kobo) per ordinary share of 50 kobo
each, that is, =N=71,557,961,607, subject to deduction of appropriate withholding
tax and regulatory approval, will be paid to shareholders whose names appear in the
Proposed Dividend Register of Members as at the close of business on Thursday 15 October 2026.
Proposed Bonus NIL
The Register of Shareholders will be closed from Friday 16 October 2026 to Friday 23
Closure of Register October 2026.
Qualification Date Thursday 15 October 2026
In line with the Authority granted by Shareholders to the Board of Directors (“the
Board”) at the Annual General Meeting held on 16 May 2024, the Board has
authorised that Shareholders will have the option of electing to receive their Interim
Dividends by way of New Ordinary Shares (“Scrip Dividend”). In view of this, and in
order to allow ample opportunity for Shareholders who may wish to exercise the
option of receiving Scrip Dividend instead of Cash Dividend, the Dividend Payment
date in respect of this Interim Dividend shall be on Friday 13 November 2026
(“Payment Date”). On the above Payment Date, shareholders who have not elected
to receive their Interim Dividend by way of Scrip Dividend, will have their Cash Interim
Dividend paid electronically (provided that they have completed the e-dividend
registration and mandated the Registrar to pay their dividends directly into their Bank
accounts). This Interim Dividend (whether cash or scrip) is only applicable to
shareholders whose names appear on the Register of Members as at close of business
Payment Date on Thursday 15 October 2026.
The reference price to be used in determining any scrip dividend allotment shall be
the volume weighted average price (VWAP) of the Company’s shares on the Nigerian
Exchange Limited (NGX) for the five business days commencing on the day the
ordinary shares are first quoted ex-dividend. Accordingly, the Scrip Dividend
Allotment Price will be communicated to ALL Shareholders through the NGX on
Friday 23 October 2026. Shareholders will thereafter be given until Friday 06
November 2026 to make their election should they wish to receive Scrip rather than
Cash Dividends. All Shareholders are hereby requested to contact their Stockbrokers
Scrip Dividend Price and the Registrars for further details.
Shareholders who are yet to complete the e-dividend registration are advised to
download the Registrar’s E-Dividend Mandate Activation Form, which is also
available on our Registrars website: https://firstregistrarsnigeria.com/e-products/e-
E-Dividend Registration dividend, complete and submit to the Registrar or their respective Banks.
