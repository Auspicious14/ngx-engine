Lagos: 02 March 2026
NOTIFICATION OF CLOSED PERIOD/BOARD MEETING TO CONSIDER AND APPROVE THE
2025 UNAUDITED FINANCIAL STATEMENTS
This is to notify Nigerian Exchange Limited (NGX) and by extension, the public, that the Board of
Directors of Berger Paints Nigeria Plc. (the Company) will be meeting on Thursday, 26 March
2026, to, amongst other matters, consider and approve the Company’s 2025 Audited Financial
Statements for the year ended 31 December 2025 (2025 AFS).
Accordingly, a ‘Closed Period’ commenced from Thursday, 1 January 2026, until twenty- four (24)
hours after the 2025 AFS is filed with NGX, in line with the provisions of NGX’s Closed Period Rule
(as amended).
Consequently, all insiders of the Company are not allowed to deal directly or indirectly in the
securities of the Company within this Closed Period.
Thank you.
For: Berger Paints Nigeria Plc.
