BOARD MEETING RESOLUTION
The Okomu Oil Palm Company Plc (the Company) wishes to notify Nigerian Exchange Limited (NGX) and
the investing public that during its Board of Directors meeting held on Friday, 24th July 2026, the Board
considered and approved the Unaudited Financial Statements for the period ended 30th June 2026
(Unaudited Financial Statements).
The approved Unaudited Financial Statements will be made public on or before 28th July 2026.
Consequently, the closed period earlier communicated for trading in the shares of the Company by insiders
and their connected persons, directly or indirectly, shall continue until twenty-four (24) hours after the
Unaudited Financial Statements have been filed.
