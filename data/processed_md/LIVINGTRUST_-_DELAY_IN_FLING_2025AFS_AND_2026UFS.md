Osogbo July 06, 2026.
FURTHER EXTENSION OF TIME IN RESPECT OF FILING AND PUBLICATION OF THE 2025 AUDITED
FINANCIAL STATEMENTS AND THE Q1/Q2-2026 UNAUDITED FINANCIAL STATEMENTS-
LIVINGTRUST MORTGAGE BANK PLC
This is to notify the Nigerian Exchange Limited (The Exchange) and members of the
public, particularly the shareholders of LivingTrust Mortgage Bank Plc (the Bank) of the
further delay in the publication of the 2025 Audited Financial Statements for the year
ended 31 December 2025 (2025 AFS). In addition, the 1st and 2nd quarters Unaudited
Financial Statements (Q1/Q2-2026 UFS) have also not been filed due to the sequential
structure of the NGX Portal, requiring that the full year audited account be filed prior to
the filing of the 1st and 2nd quarter accounts.
As at the time of this announcement, the 2025 AFS is awaiting regulatory approval from
the Central Bank of Nigeria (CBN) which is required prior to publication. In view of this,
the Securities and Exchange Commission has graciously approved that the 2025 AFS of
the Bank be published within 48hours of obtaining regulatory approval.
The Bank looks forward to filing and publishing the 2025 AFS and the Q1/Q2-2026 UFS
upon approval of the CBN.
We thank our esteemed shareholders and the public for their sustained interest in our
Bank.
For: LivingTrust Mortgage Bank Plc.
Dorcas Ajayi
Ag. Company Secretary
