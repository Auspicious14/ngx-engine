12 May 2026
Lagos, Nigeria
DANGOTE CEMENT PLC – OUTCOME OF EMERGENCY BOARD MEETING
This is to inform the Nigerian Exchange Limited (NGX), the investing public and all
stakeholders that:
Dangote Cement PLC (the “Company”) is at a preliminary stage of exploring a potential
secondary listing on the London Stock Exchange (“LSE”) under the international
commercial companies secondary listing framework. The proposed listing on the LSE,
if pursued, is intended to deepen the Company’s access to international capital
markets, broaden its shareholder base, and further strengthen its position as a leading
pan-African enterprise. Any such transaction would be subject to, amongst other
things, requisite corporate approvals, finalisation of transaction terms, receipt of all
relevant regulatory approvals and market conditions.
The Company will provide further updates to the market as the transaction progresses
and as requisite regulatory requirements are complied with. There is no certainty that
the proposed listing will be completed or, if completed, as to the terms or timing
thereof.
Shareholders and investing public are therefore advised to exercise caution when
dealing in the securities of the Company pending such further announcements.
Further updates will be provided to the market as appropriate.
Thank you.
Edward Imoedemhe
Company Secretary
