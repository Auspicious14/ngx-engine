RESOLUTIONS OF MEMBERS OF UNITED BANK FOR AFRICA PLC AT THE 64TH
ANNUAL GENERAL MEETING HELD ON MONDAY, MAY 25, 2026
Lagos, Nigeria, May 25, 2026
At the 64th Annual General Meeting of United Bank for Africa Plc (the
Bank), Africa’s Global Bank, which held virtually on May 25, 2026, the
Shareholders passed the following resolutions:
ORDINARY RESOLUTIONS
1. That the Directors’ Report and the Balance Sheet as at December
31, 2025, together with the Profit & Loss Account for the year ended
on that day submitted to the meeting be received and adopted.
2. That Mrs. Caroline Anyanwu, a Non-Executive Director retiring by
rotation and offering herself for re-election, be and is hereby re-
elected as a Non-Executive Director of the Bank.
3. That Mr. Emmanuel N. Nnorom, a Non-Executive Director retiring by
rotation and offering himself for re-election, be and is hereby re-
elected as a Non-Executive Director of the Bank.
4. That Mrs. Henrietta Ugboh, an Independent Non-Executive Director
retiring by rotation and offering herself for re-election, be and is
hereby re-elected as an Independent Non-Executive Director of
the Bank.
5. That the Directors be and are hereby authorised to fix the
remuneration and expenses of the Auditors for the year ending
December 31, 2026.
6. That an Audit Committee be and is hereby constituted for the 2026
Financial Year with the following members:
i. Dr. Feyi Ogoji, FCA – Shareholder
ii. Mr. Matthew Esonanjor, SAN – Shareholder
iii. Mr. Alex Adio – Shareholder

7. That the remuneration of the Directors of the Bank for the year
ending December 31, 2026, be and is hereby fixed at NGN100
Million only for the Chairman, NGN90 million only for the Board
Committee Chairmen, and NGN75 million for other Non-Executive
Directors effective 1st of January 2026.
SPECIAL RESOLUTIONS
8. That Clause 6 of the Company’s Memorandum of Association and
Article 10 of the Company’s Articles of Association be and are
hereby amended to reflect the new share capital of the company
by substituting the words and figures N22,500,000,000.00 divided
into 45,000,000,000 ordinary shares of 50k each, and replacing
same with the following words and figures N22,098,087,652.50
divided into 44,196,175,305 ordinary shares of 50k each,
respectively.
9. That in compliance with the Rule of the Nigerian Exchange Limited
(NGX) governing transactions with Related Parties or Interested
Persons, the Company and its related entities (“The Group”) be
and are hereby granted a General Mandate in respect of all
recurrent transactions entered into with a related party or
interested person provided such transactions are of a revenue or
trading nature or are necessary for the Company’s day-to-day
operations. This Mandate shall commence on the date on which
this resolution is passed and shall continue to operate until the date
on which the next Annual General Meeting of the Company is
held.
BY THE ORDER OF THE BOARD
