Lagos November 23, 2026
AIICO Insurance Plc (the “Company”) hereby notifies Nigerian Exchange Limited (“NGX”) and the
investing public that it has been informed that LeapFrog III Nigeria Insurance Holdings Ltd (the
“Selling Shareholder”) and Insur Investments Limited (the “Purchaser”) have entered into a Share
Purchase Agreement for the proposed sale and purchase of 11,173,946,135 existing ordinary
shares, representing approximately 30.53% of the Company's issued share capital (the
“Proposed Transaction”).
The Proposed Transaction remains subject to the satisfaction or lawful waiver of applicable
conditions precedent and the receipt or completion of requisite regulatory approvals, clearances
and notifications, including NGX approval for the proposed block trade. The transaction has not
completed, and no shares have been transferred pursuant to the agreement.
The Company is not a party to the Share Purchase Agreement. The Proposed Transaction
concerns existing shares held by the Selling Shareholder and does not involve the issuance of
new shares by the Company.
The Company will continue to comply with its applicable disclosure and regulatory obligations
and will notify NGX and the investing public of further material developments, including
completion of the Proposed Transaction, as appropriate.
By Order of the Board
Donald Kanu, PhD. F.IoD
Company Secretary
AIICO Insurance Plc
