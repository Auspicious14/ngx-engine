CUTIX PLC – OUTCOME OF MEETING OF THE BOARD OF
DIRECTORS
Cutix Plc (“The Company”) wishes to notify the Nigerian Exchange Limited (“The
Exchange”), our esteemed Shareholders and the general public that the Board of
Directors of the Company met on Thursday, June 26, 2026 as scheduled but the
Draft Audited Financial Statements for the financial year ended April 30, 2026
(2026 FS) was not considered.
The Board has re-scheduled to meet on July 8, 2026 to consider the Audited
Financial Statements. Upon approval, the 2026 AFS will be submitted to the
Exchange and released to the public accordingly on or before July 30, 2026.
Please note that the Closed Period earlier announced by the Company remains in
effect and shall continue until 24 hours after the Audited Financial Statements have
been filed with the Exchange and made public.
