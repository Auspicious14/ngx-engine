RESOLUTIONS PASSED AT THE 49TH ANNUAL GENERAL MEETING OF INTERNATIONAL
BERWERIES PLC
At the 49th Annual General Meeting of International Breweries Plc (the Company) held at the
Grand Ballroom of the Federal Palace Hotel, Victoria Island, Lagos on Thursday, 30th
July 2026 at 11.00 a.m. prompt, the Chairman of the Board of the Company laid the
Audited Financial Statement for the year ended 31st December 2025 before the Shareholders.
Subsequently, the following resolutions were considered and duly passed:
1. The election of (a) Mrs. Toyin Adeniji and (b) Mr. Nicholas Kade, as Directors of the
Company.
2. The re-election of (a) His Royal Majesty, Igwe Nnaemeka Achebe (CFR), (b) Mr. Andrew
Whiting (c) Mr. David Tomlinson and (d) Mrs. Temitope Oguntokun, as Directors of the
Company.
3. The authorization of the Directors to fix the remuneration of the External Auditors.
4. The election of (a) Mr. Adetunji Ajani Babajide (b) Mr. Chisom Nwamara William (c) Mr.
Olalekan Iyiola Layi as Shareholders’ representative on the Statutory Audit Committee
and the nomination of (a) Mrs. Nkechinyere Chinyere Ugochukwu and (b) Mrs. Awuneba
Ajumogobia as Directors’ representatives on the Statutory Audit Committee.
5. In compliance with Section 257 of the Companies and Allied Matters Act 2020 (as
amended), the remuneration of the Managers of the Company was disclosed to the
members at the Annual General Meeting. The relevant disclosure is contained in Note
32 on page 208 of the Company’s Annual Report. This item was presented for disclosure
purposes only, and no resolution was required or passed in respect of the matter.
6. The shareholders approved the remuneration of the Non-Executive Directors of the
Company for the financial year ending 31 December 2026 in the aggregate amount of
₦93 million (Ninety-Three Million Naira), with sitting allowances to be paid separately
and outside the approved fixed remuneration.

were duly passed by the shareholders of the Company:
