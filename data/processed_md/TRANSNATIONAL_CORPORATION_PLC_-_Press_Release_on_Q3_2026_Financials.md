TRANSCORP GROUP DELIVERS ₦111.4 BILLION PROFIT BEFORE TAX IN 9M 2026,
EXPANDS MARGINS AND STRENGTHENS BALANCE SHEET
Lagos, Nigeria – 26 October 2026
Transnational Corporation Plc (“Transcorp Group”) (NGX: TRANSCORP),
Nigeria’s leading listed conglomerate, has announced its unaudited financial
results for the nine months ended 30 September 2026, delivering strong
profitability, an expanded profit-before-tax margin and a significantly
strengthened balance sheet.
Financial Highlights
• Revenue: ₦357.4 billion (9M 2025: ₦412.4 billion)
• Profit Before Tax: ₦111.4 billion (9M 2025: ₦124.5 billion)
• Profit After Tax: ₦75.1 billion (9M 2025: ₦91.4 billion)
• Earnings Per Share: 429 kobo (9M 2025: 534 kobo)
• Total Assets: ₦1.13 trillion (December 2025: ₦1.00 trillion)
• Total Equity: ₦387.8 billion (December 2025: ₦353.4 billion)
The Group delivered a Profit Before Tax of ₦111.4 billion and a Profit After Tax
of ₦75.1 billion, and expanded its profit-before-tax margin to 31.2% from 30.2%
in the corresponding period of 2025, reflecting disciplined cost management
and operational efficiency across its businesses. Revenue of ₦357.4 billion
reflected lower power evacuation at Transcorp Power Plc, as industry-wide
transmission and gas supply constraints limited output during the period.
Performance across the portfolio underscored the quality of the Group’s
operating businesses. Transcorp Power Plc grew shareholders’ funds by 8.9% to
₦199.8 billion and increased cash and cash equivalents more than four-fold to
₦10.3 billion. Transcorp Hotels Plc grew Profit After Tax by 5% to ₦15.5 billion,
expanded its net profit margin to 22.3% and reduced net finance cost by 36%,
while its total assets rose by 29% to ₦206.8 billion.
The Group’s hospitality portfolio, Transcorp Hotels Plc, maintained its position as
Nigeria’s foremost hospitality destination, attracting business leaders,

dignitaries and international visitors through its iconic 670-room Transcorp Hilton
Abuja and the 5,000-capacity Transcorp Centre, the country’s largest
multipurpose event venue.
Management Commentaries
President/GCEO, Transnational Corporation Plc, Owen D. Omogiafo, OON,
commented:
“Our performance reflects the strength of our diversified portfolio and our
ability to sustain strong profitability and expand margins in a challenging
operating environment.
We continue to work closely with stakeholders to address industry-wide constraints
and improve electricity delivery, while leveraging our hospitality investments to
strengthen Nigeria's position as a destination for business, tourism and major
events.
Across our businesses, we remain focused on unlocking opportunities for
growth, deepening our contribution to Nigeria's economy and delivering on
our purpose to improve lives and transform Africa.”
Group Chief Finance Officer, Transnational Corporation Plc, Festus Izevbizua,
commented:
“Our nine-month performance underscores the strength and resilience of the
Group’s underlying businesses. Year-on-year revenue was impacted by power
evacuation constraints arising from vandalisation of transmission and gas
infrastructure. Notwithstanding this, the Group expanded its profit-before-tax
margin to 31.2%, from 30.2% in the prior period, a direct result of deliberate cost
optimisation and operational efficiency across our businesses.
“Our financial position also strengthened considerably over the period. Total
assets grew by 13% to ₦1.13 trillion, driven by improved receivables,
investments in financial assets and stronger cash generation. Total equity rose

by 9.7% to ₦387.8 billion, reinforcing the Group’s robust capital base, while cash
and cash equivalents more than doubled to ₦45.9 billion from ₦21.9 billion at
December 2025, enhancing liquidity and providing greater financial flexibility
to pursue strategic priorities.”
Looking ahead, Transcorp Group remains focused on advancing strategic
opportunities across its power, hospitality and energy businesses, leveraging its
diversified portfolio and financial strength to support long-term growth and
sustainable shareholder value.

About Transnational Corporation
Transnational Corporation Plc (Transcorp Group) is one of Africa’s leading listed
conglomerates, with strategic investments in the power, hospitality, and energy
sectors, driven by its mission to improve lives and transform Africa.
Transcorp’s power businesses, Transcorp Power Plc and Transafam Power,
provide about 20% of Nigeria’s installed power capacity. Transcorp is
committed to developing Nigeria’s domestic energy value chain through its
investments in OPL281. The Group’s hospitality business, Transcorp Hotels Plc,
owns the iconic Transcorp Hilton Abuja, Nigeria’s flagship hospitality destination
and Nigeria’s largest event venue, the Transcorp Centre Abuja.
