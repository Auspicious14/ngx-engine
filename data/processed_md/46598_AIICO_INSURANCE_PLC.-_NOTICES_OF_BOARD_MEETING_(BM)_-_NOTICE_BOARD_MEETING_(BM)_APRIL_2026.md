Lagos, April 7, 2026
NOTICE OF BOARD MEETING AND CLOSED PERIOD OF AIICO INSURANCE PLC
NOTICE IS HEREBY GIVEN that the Board of AIICO Insurance Plc, (the Company) will meet on
April 30, 2026, to review and approve the draft Unaudited Financial Statements for the period
ended March 31, 2026 (Q1 UFS 2026).
Any decision reached as it affects the approval of the accounts will immediately be communicated
to Nigerian Exchange Limited (The Exchange).
In compliance with the provisions of Rule 17.18(a): Closed Period, Rulebook of The Exchange, 2015
(Issuers’ Rule) as amended, the Company has commenced a Closed Period from Wednesday, 1
April 2026 until twenty-four (24) hours after the release of the Company’s Q1 UFS 2026 and other
decisions taken at the Board Meeting.
During the closed period, no insiders of the Company and their connected persons shall deal in
the securities of the Company.
