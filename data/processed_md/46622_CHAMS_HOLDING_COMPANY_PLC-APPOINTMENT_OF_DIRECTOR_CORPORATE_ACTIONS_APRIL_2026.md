8th April 2026
Lagos, Nigeria
APPOINTMENT OF NON - EXECUTIVE DIRECTOR
Chams Holding Company Plc (the “Company”) hereby notifies the Nigerian Exchange
Limited (“NGX”) and the investing public that the Company has appointed MR. MICHAEL
UWAKWE, as a Non- Executive Director effective 1st April 2026.
PROFILE OF MR MICHAEL UWAKWE
Mr. Uwakwe is the Chairman of the Creditville Limited, a financial services company
involved in Consumer lending, Equipment leasing, Capital market operations and Real Estate.
He is a Human Resources professional by qualification, training and experience with a deep
interest in analyzing financial markets around the globe who retired from TotalEnergies after
30 years of active service. He is also well versed in all aspects of investment analysis, asset
allocation, and risk management and is a PENCOM-approved member of the Investment
Strategy Committee of the Total Closed Pension Fund. He sits on the Board of Redwood Asset
Mgt Company Limited, a SEC-approved Fund & Portfolio Manager.
Some of his Professional memberships and affiliations include:
• Fellow, National Institute of Credit Administration of Nigeria
• Member, Chartered Institute of Personnel & Development, UK
• Member, British Psychological Society, London.
• Associate Member, Nigeria Institute of Management.
The Board is confident that his dynamic leadership will foster collaboration, inspire teams,
and deliver transformative results for our stakeholders.
This notification is issued strictly for regulatory compliance and transparency purposes in the
meantime, whilst the company concludes the necessary filings at the Corporate Affairs
Commission (CAC).
