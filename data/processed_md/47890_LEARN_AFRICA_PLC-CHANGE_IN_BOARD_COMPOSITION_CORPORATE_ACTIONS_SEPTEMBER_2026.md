Learn Africa Plc Announces Changes to its Board of Directors
Lagos, Nigeria – 8th September 2026 – Learn Africa Plc (Learn Africa or the Company),
Nigeria’s largest educational publisher, announces the resignation of Mr. Iyinoluwa Aboyeji, a
Non-Executive Director from its Board of Directors effective 7th September 2026. The Board
expresses its sincere gratitude for his dedicated service and strategic guidance, which have
been instrumental to the Company's growth in the educational technology space. The Board
wishes him continued success in his future pursuits.

About Learn Africa Plc
Learn Africa Plc was incorporated in August 1961 as a book publishing company, established
as a subsidiary of the renowned Longman Group (UK) Limited, now Pearson Education Limited,
one of the world’s largest education businesses.
From inception, the company’s mission was to market and distribute imported Longman
Group titles and to develop original content in English and indigenous languages to meet the
growing demand for books in the Nigerian market.
Today, Learn Africa stands as Nigeria’s largest educational publisher, with over 5,000 titles and
a workforce of 210+ staff spread across the country. Listed on the Nigerian Stock Exchange
since 23 July 1996, the Company holds the highest equity value in the publishing sector.
