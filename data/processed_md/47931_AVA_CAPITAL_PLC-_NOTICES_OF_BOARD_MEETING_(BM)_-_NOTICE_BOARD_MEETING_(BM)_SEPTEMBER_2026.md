AVA CAPITAL PLC – NOTIFICATION OF BOARD MEETING AND CLOSED
PERIOD
NOTICE IS HEREBY GIVEN THAT a Board meeting of AVA Capital Plc (the “Company”)
will be held on Wednesday, 21 st October 2026, via the Microsoft Teams meeting platform
at 1:00 p.m. Among the items slated for consideration at the meeting is the approval of the
Unaudited Financial Statements for the period end ing 30th September 2026 (the “Financial
Statements”).
Accordingly, in compliance with the post -listing requirements of the Nigerian Exchange
Limited (“NGX”), the Company hereby declares the commencement of a closed period for
trading in its shares, commencing on 1st October 2026 and continuing until 24 (twenty -four)
hours after the Financial Statements have been uploaded to NGX Portal.
Consequently, no insider of the Company or their connected persons shall deal, directly
or indirectly, in the securities of AVA Capital Plc during this closed period.
