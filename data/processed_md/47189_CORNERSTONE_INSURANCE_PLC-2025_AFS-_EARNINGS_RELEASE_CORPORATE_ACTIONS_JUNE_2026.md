Cornerstone Insurance Plc Announces Audited FY 2025 Results and New Holding Company
Structure
LAGOS, NIGERIA – 17 June 2026 – Cornerstone Insurance Plc (NGX: CORNERST) ("Cornerstone" or the
"Group") today announced its audited financial results for the year ended 31 December 2025,
delivering strong growth in insurance revenue, further strengthening its capital position and advancing
key strategic initiatives designed to support the Group's long-term growth ambitions.
Key Highlights
• Insurance revenue increased by 33.6% to ₦51.7 billion from ₦38.7 billion in FY 2024.
• Insurance service result increased by 527.3% to ₦13.5 billion from ₦2.2 billion in FY 2024.
• Shareholders’ funds of ₦71.9 billion, an increase of 20.4% from prior year.
• Solvency margin surplus of ₦33.6 billion, significantly above regulatory requirements.
• Progress made towards the implementation of a HoldCo structure and the separation of life
and general insurance operations in line with regulatory developments.
• Proposed dividend of 28 kobo per ordinary share.
Insurance revenue increased by 33.6% to ₦51.6 billion from ₦38.7 billion in the prior year, reflecting
continued business expansion across the Group and the sustained confidence of policyholders,
brokers and business partners. Insurance service results increased by 527.3% to ₦13.5 billion from
₦2.2 billion in the prior year. Shareholders' funds increased by 20.4% to ₦71.9 billion, while the Group
maintained a solvency margin surplus of ₦33.6 billion, significantly above regulatory requirements.
The Group reported Profit After Tax of ₦11.7 billion for the financial year ended 31 December 2025.
The prior year's earnings benefited from significant foreign exchange-related fair value gains, resulting
in a higher comparative base.
Commenting on the results, the Board noted that the Group’s performance reflects the strength of
the Cornerstone franchise, the dedication of its employees and business partners, and the continued
trust of its customers and shareholders.
Building on this foundation, the Group is progressing plans to implement a holding company (HoldCo)
structure that will separate its life and general insurance operations while bringing together its general
insurance businesses, including Hilal Takaful Nigeria Limited, under a more focused operating
framework. The proposed HoldCo structure is expected to enhance strategic flexibility, strengthen
operational focus and improve capital allocation across the Group. It also aligns with the National
Insurance Commission’s (NAICOM) ongoing efforts to encourage the separation of composite insurers
into distinct life and general insurance businesses. The combination of the Group’s general insurance
operations with Hilal Takaful is expected to deepen scale, improve efficiency, broaden the Group’s
market reach and further strengthen its competitive position within the Nigerian insurance market.
The Group’s performance in 2025 was supported by the continued loyalty of its customers, brokers
and business partners.
For 35 years, Cornerstone has built its reputation on service, reliability and responsiveness, with a
longstanding commitment to meeting its obligations to policyholders and supporting customers when
they need it most. This commitment remains central to the Group's strategy and a key driver of its
enduring franchise strength, which is reflected in the Company's A- Financial Strength Rating with a
Stable Outlook from Augusto & Co.

In line with its commitment to delivering value to shareholders, the Board is proposing a dividend of
28 kobo per ordinary share for the consideration of shareholders at the Annual General Meeting, to
be held on Monday, 20 July 2025.
Looking ahead, Cornerstone remains focused on strengthening its market position, enhancing
operational efficiency and creating sustainable long-term value for all stakeholders as it enters the
next phase of its development.
