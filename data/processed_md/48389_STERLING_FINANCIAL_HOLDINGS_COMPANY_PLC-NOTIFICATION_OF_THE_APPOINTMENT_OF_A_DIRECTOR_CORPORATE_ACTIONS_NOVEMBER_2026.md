Lagos, Nigeria, November 10, 2026
STERLING FINANCIAL HOLDINGS COMPANY PLC – NOTIFICATION OF THE APPOINTMENT
OF A NEW MANAGING DIRECTOR OF ALTERNATIVE BANK LIMITED
Sterling Financial Holdings Company Plc (the Company) hereby notifies Nigerian
Exchange Limited (NGX), its shareholders, and the investing public of the appointment
of Mr. Gul Khan as Managing Director of Alternative Bank Limited, a subsidiary of the
Company, effective 5th November 2026. The appointment has received the approval of
the Central Bank of Nigeria (CBN).
Mr. Khan is a seasoned financial services executive with over 25 years of experience
across Africa, Europe, the Americas, Asia and the Middle East. He is recognized for
driving business growth, transformation and innovation across banking, fintech and
payments sectors, with expertise spanning retail banking, digital financial services,
product development and large-scale transformation initiatives.
Prior to his appointment, Mr. Khan served as Chief Executive Officer of I&M Bank Limited
in Kenya, where he led a period of accelerated growth and transformation, delivering
strong improvements in revenue, profitability and market share. Before joining I&M Bank,
he served as Head of Products at Airtel Money Africa. In his role he drove substantial
revenue growth, expanded the platform’s active customer base and supported major
equity investment initiatives.
Mr. Khan has also held senior leadership positions at leading financial institutions
including HSBC, ADIB, Gulf Bank, and Doha Bank, with responsibilities spanning retail
banking, wealth management, digital transformation and strategic planning across
multiple regions.
The appointment is a strategic step towards advancing Alternative Bank’s ambition to
become Nigeria’s leading innovative digital non-interest bank. Mr. Khan’s extensive
experience in scaling digital financial services, developing innovative products and
driving business transformation across multiple markets is expected to support
Alternative Bank’s strategic priorities.
The Company also announces the retirement of Mr. Hassan Yusuf as Managing Director
of Alternative Bank, effective 4th November 2026, following the completion of his tenure.
The Board expresses its appreciation to Mr. Yusuf for his dedication, leadership and
valuable contribution to the growth of Alternative Bank and wishes him continued
success in all his future endeavours.
