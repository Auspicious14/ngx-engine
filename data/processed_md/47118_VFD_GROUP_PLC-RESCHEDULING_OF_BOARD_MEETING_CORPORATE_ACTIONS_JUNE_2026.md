Lagos, June 3, 2026
VFD GROUP PLC – NOTICE OF RESCHEDULING OF MEETING OF THE BOARD OF
DIRECTORS
We hereby inform Nigerian Exchange Limited (“NGX” or “The Exchange”) and the investing public
that the Meeting of the Board of Directors of VFD Group Plc (“the Company”), earlier indicated to hold
on Thursday, June 4, 2026, via the Microsoft Teams Meeting Platform, has been rescheduled.
The Meeting will now hold on Tuesday, June 9, 2026, at the VFD Group Plc office located at 8
MacGregor Road, Ikoyi, Lagos. The Board will consider, as agenda items, Q2 2026 Managing
Director’s Report and Board Committee Reports, amongst other matters.
NGX will be notified of the outcome of the Meeting in accordance with the applicable post-listing
requirements.
