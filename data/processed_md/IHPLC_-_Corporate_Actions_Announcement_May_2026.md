Corporate Actions Announcement
IKEJA HOTEL PLC hereby announce as follows:
Period Ended 31st March 2026.
An Interim Dividend of 3 kobo for every share of 50 kobo, subject to
appropriate withholding tax and approval will be paid to shareholders
whose names appear in the Register of Members as at the close of business
Proposed Dividend on Friday, May 8, 2026.
Proposed Bonus NIL
The Register of Shareholders will be closed from Monday, May 11, 2026, to
Closure of Register Friday, May 15, 2026.
Qualification Date Friday, May 8, 2026.
On Friday June 5, 2026, dividends will be paid electronically to shareholders
whose names appear on the Register of Members as at Friday, May 8, 2026,
and who have completed the e-dividend registration and mandated the
Payment Date Registrar to pay their dividends directly into their Bank accounts.
Shareholders who are yet to complete the e-dividend registration are
advised to download the Registrar’s E-Dividend Mandate Activation Form,
which is also available on the website of the Registrars:
E-Dividend https://greenwichregistrars.com complete and submit to the Registrar or
Registration their respective Banks.
Shareholders with dividend warrants and share certificates that have

Warrants and Share validation are advised to complete the e-dividend registration or contact
