Lagos, 24th November, 2026
NOTICE OF CHANGES TO THE BOARD
SUNU Assurances Nigeria Plc (“the Company”) hereby notifies Nigerian Exchange Limited (“NGX”), its esteemed
shareholders and the investing public of the conclusion of the tenure of Mr. Kyari Abba Bukar and Hajia Taizir Ajala, who
served as Independent Non-Executive Directors and Chairman and Vice-Chairman respectively on the Board of Directors
(“the Board”) of the Company, effective 23 October 2026.
The conclusion of their tenure follows the completion of the maximum nine (9) years tenure permitted for Independent Non-
Executive Directors under the applicable provisions of the National Insurance Commission (NAICOM) Corporate
Governance Guidelines for Insurance and Reinsurance Companies in Nigeria, 2021.
During their respective tenures, Mr. Kyari Abba Bukar and Hajia Taizir Ajala made significant and enduring contributions to
the strategic direction, corporate governance and institutional development of the Company. Their wealth of experience,
professional insights, independent judgement and unwavering commitment to the Company's long-term sustainability were
instrumental in strengthening the Board's oversight responsibilities and promoting sound corporate governance practices.
The Company recognizes that their respective contributions extended beyond the discharge of their statutory and fiduciary
responsibilities. Their leadership, professionalism, institutional knowledge and commitment to the Company's values helped
shape the Board's culture and strengthen its capacity to provide effective oversight and strategic guidance, particularly
during significant periods in the Company's development.
The Board and Management express their profound appreciation to Mr. Kyari Abba Bukar and Hajia Taizir Ajala for their
invaluable service, exemplary leadership and dedicated contributions to the growth and development of SUNU Assurances
Nigeria Plc.
This notification is made in compliance with the applicable post-listing disclosure requirement of the NGX.
