Lagos, 30 March 2026
PZ CUSSONS NIGERIA PLC – RESIGNATION OF A DIRECTOR
This is to notify our esteemed Shareholders, the investing public and the Nigerian Exchange
Limited that the Board of Directors of PZ Cussons Nigeria PLC (“the Company”) at its meeting
held on Friday, 27 March 2026, noted the resignation of Ms. Joyce Coker as a Director of the
Company.
Ms. Coker also served as the Company’s Human Resources Director. Her resignation takes
effect from Friday, 17 April 2026, consequent upon her resignation from the employment of
the Company.
The Board expresses its appreciation to Ms. Coker for her services and tremendous
contribution to the Company and wishes her success in her future endeavours.
STATEMENT ON INVESTORS’ RELATIONS
The Company has a dedicated investors’ portal on its corporate website, which can be
accessed via this link: https://www.pzcussons.com/investors/nigeria-investors/. The
Company’s Investors’ Relations Officer can also be reached through electronic mail at
bukola.olonade-agaga@pzcussons.com or by telephone on 09169855016 for any investment-
related enquiry.
