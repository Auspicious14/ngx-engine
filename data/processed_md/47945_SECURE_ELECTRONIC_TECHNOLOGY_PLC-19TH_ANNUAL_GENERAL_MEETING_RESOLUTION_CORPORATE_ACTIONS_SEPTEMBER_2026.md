Lagos, 25 September 2026
ANNUAL GENERAL MEETING RESOLUTIONS
At the Annual General Meeting of SECURE ELECTRONIC TECHNOLOGY PLC held at
The Event Warehouse, Ilupeju, Lagos on the 23rd day of September 2026, the following
resolutions were duly proposed and unanimously adopted:
1. RESOLVED THAT the Audited Financial Statements of the Company for the year
ended 31st December 2025, together with the Reports of the Directors, the
External Auditors and the Statutory Audit Committee thereon, be and are hereby
received and adopted.
2. RESOLVED THAT Dr. Muyiwa Oladimeji, Mrs. Wobia-Agbosa, Mrs Funmi Ajose
Adeogun and Alh. Sikiru Baruwa who retire by rotation in accordance with CAMA
2020 and the Articles of Association of the Company and, being eligible, have
offered themselves for re-election, be and are hereby re-elected as Directors of
the Company.
3. RESOLVED THAT the appointment of Mrs. Leticia Otomewo as an Executive
Director of the Company with effect from 26th March 2026 be and is hereby
approved and ratified.
4. RESOLVED THAT the appointment of Mr. Kwesi Obaseki as a Non-Executive
Director of the Company with effect from 26th March 2026 be and is hereby
approved and ratified.
5. RESOLVED THAT the Directors be and are hereby authorised to fix the
remuneration of the Non-Executive Directors of the Company.
6. RESOLVED THAT Kehinde Kassim & Co being qualified and eligible, be and are
hereby re-appointed as External Auditors of the Company to hold office until the
conclusion of the next Annual General Meeting.
7. RESOLVED THAT the Directors be and are hereby authorised to fix the
remuneration of the External Auditors of the Company.
8. RESOLVED THAT Mr. Jonathan Ebora, Mr. Sosanwo Kolawole James and Mr.
Apete Wakilu be and are hereby elected as shareholders’ representatives on the
Statutory Audit Committee of the Company.
That Mr. Akin Areola and Mrs. Nwadiuto Iheakanwa were nominated to represent
the Board of Directors on the Statutory Audit Committee of the Company.

CERTIFICATION
I, Busiyi E. Osikanmi, certify that I am the duly appointed and acting Secretary of Secure
Electronic Technology Plc, and that the foregoing is a true and correct copy of the
resolution duly proposed and adopted at the 19th Annual General meeting of the
Company held on 23rd day of September, 2026, and that such meeting was duly called
and held and that a quorum was present throughout the meeting.
