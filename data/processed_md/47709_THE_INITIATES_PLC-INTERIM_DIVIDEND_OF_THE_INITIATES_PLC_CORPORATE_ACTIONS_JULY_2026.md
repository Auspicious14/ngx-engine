THE INITIATES PLC
WASTE MANAGERS & INDUSTRIAL CLEANERS RC266755
Corporate Actions Announcement
THE INITIATES PLC hereby announce as follows:
Period Ended 30th June 2026
An Interim Dividend of N0.20 for every share of 50 kobo each, subject to
appropriate withholding tax and approval will be paid to shareholders whose
names appear in the Register of Members as at the close of business on 10th
Proposed Dividend of August 2026
Proposed Bonus NIL
Closure of Register The Register of Shareholders will be closed from 11th to 14th August,2026
Qualification Date 10th August, 2026
On 31st of August 2026, dividends will be paid electronically to shareholders
whose names appear on the Register of Members as at 10th day of August,
2026 and who have completed the e-dividend registration and mandated the
Payment Date Registrar to pay their dividends directly into their Bank accounts.
Shareholders who are yet to complete the e-dividend registration are advised
to download the Registrar’s E-Dividend Mandate Activation Form, which is
also available on the website of the Registrars: www.apel.com.ng complete
E-Dividend Registration and submit to the Registrar or their respective Banks.
Shareholders with dividend warrants and share certificates that have
Unclaimed Dividend remained unclaimed, or are yet to be presented for payment or returned for
Warrants and Share validation are advised to complete the e-dividend registration or contact the
Certificates Registrar.
