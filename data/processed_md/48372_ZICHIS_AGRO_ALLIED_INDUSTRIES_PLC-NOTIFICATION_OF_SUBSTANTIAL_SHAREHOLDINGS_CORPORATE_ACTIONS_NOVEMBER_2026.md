November 6, 2026
LAGOS, NIGERIA
NOTIFICATION OF CHANGE IN SUBSTANTIAL SHAREHOLDING
Zichis Agro-Allied Industries Plc (the “Company”) hereby notifies Nigerian Exchange Limited (NGX) and the
investing public of a change in the Company's shareholding structure following a share transaction concluded
on 3 November 2026.
The transaction details and resulting shareholding are set out below:
● Selling Shareholder: Chilla Entertainment Limited
● Purchaser: Futureview Asset Management Limited
● Number of Shares Acquired: 126,967,213 ordinary shares
● Resulting Shareholding of Futureview Asset Management Limited: 126,967,213 ordinary shares
● Percentage of Issued Share Capital: 10.45%
● Nature of Transaction: Sale of Shares
● Transaction Date: 3 November 2026
Following the transaction, Futureview Asset Management Limited holds a total of 126,967,213 ordinary shares
in the Company, representing 10.45% of the Company's issued share capital, thereby becoming a substantial
shareholder of the Company.
The transaction involves the transfer of existing shares and does not result in the issuance of new shares by the
Company.
The Company remains committed to transparency, timely regulatory disclosures and compliance with the
applicable rules and regulations of Nigerian Exchange Limited and the Companies and Allied Matters Act, 2020.
By Order of the Board
Company Secretary
