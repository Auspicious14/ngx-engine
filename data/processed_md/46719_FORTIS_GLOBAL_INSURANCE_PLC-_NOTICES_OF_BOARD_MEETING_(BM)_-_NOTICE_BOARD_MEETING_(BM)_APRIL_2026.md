LAGOS, 23rd April 2026
NOTICE OF BOARD OF DIRECTORS’ MEETING
This is to notify Nigerian Exchange Limited (NGX or the Exchange) and the investing
public pursuant to NGX’s Rules that a Board Meeting of Fortis Global Insurance Plc (the
Company) has been scheduled to hold on Monday, 27th April 2026 to consider and
approve the Unaudited Financial Statements of the Company for the first quarter (Q1)
ended 31st March 2026.
Accordingly, a Closed Period to prevent insiders from trading in the shares of the
company has been in force since Thursday, 1st January 2026. The Closed Period will be
lifted twenty-four (24) hours after the Company’s 2025 Audited Financial Statements and
Q1 2026 Unaudited Financial Statements have been filed with the Exchange.
For: Fortis Global Insurance Plc
