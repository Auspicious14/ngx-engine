NOTICE OF BOARD MEETING AND CLOSED PERIOD
Abbey Mortgage Bank Pie ("the Bank") hereby notifies Nigerian Exchange Limited
(NGX), its shareholders and the investing public that the Bank's Board Meeting
would be held on Tuesday, 28th April 2026 at 11 am to consider inter alia the First
Quarter Unaudited Financial Statements for the period ended 31st March 2026.
In compliance with Rule 17.18(a) of the Nigerian Exchange Limited's Closed Period
Rules as amended, the Bank commenced a Closed Period for transactions in the
shares of the Bank by insiders from 1st April 2026 and will continue until twenty-four
(24)hours after the Unaudited Financial Statements for the period ended 31st
March 2026 is released to the public via NGX's Issuer's Portal.
During the Closed Period, no insider, including Directors, employees, and other
related parties, may directly or indirectly deal with the shares of the Bank.
