July 1, 2026
Lagos, Nigeria
ANNOUNCEMENT OF CLOSED PERIOD IN RESPECT OF THE SHARES OF
ACCESS HOLDINGS PLC
In line with the post listing rules of the Nigerian Exchange Limited (NGX), Access
Holdings Plc (the “Company”), hereby informs the NGX, its shareholders and the
investing public that the Company has declared a Closed Period with effect from 1st
of July 2026 until twenty-four (24) hours after the release of the Company’s Interim
Audited Financial Statements for the Half Year ended 30th June 2026 to the public
through NGX Issuers’ Portal (‘the Portal’).
Accordingly, no insider of the Company and their connected persons may directly or
indirectly deal in the Company’s securities until twenty-four (24) hours after the
publication of the Interim Audited Financial Statements on the Portal.
BY ORDER OF THE BOARD
SUNDAY EKWOCHI
COMPANY SECRETARY
