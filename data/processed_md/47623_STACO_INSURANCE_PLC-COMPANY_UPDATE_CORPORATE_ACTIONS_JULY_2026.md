NOTIFICATION OF THE DEMISE OF THE MANAGING DIRECTOR/CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER AND
APPOINTMENT OF PRINCIPAL OFFICERS
Staco Insurance Plc (the "Company") hereby notifies Nigerian Exchange Limited ("NGX"), its
shareholders, and the investing public of the demise of its Managing Director/Chief Executive
Officer, as well as the appointment of new Principal Officers to ensure the continued
effective management and operations of the Company.
1. Demise of Late Dr. Olawale Banmore Managing Director/Chief Executive Officer
The Board of Directors of the Company regrets to announce the passing of its Managing
Director/Chief Executive Officer, Dr. Olawale Banmore which occurred on Saturday, 27th
December 2025. The Board extends its sincere condolences to his family and loved ones.
During his tenure, Dr. Banmore provided dedicated leadership and contributed
significantly to the Company's strategic direction, operational growth, and overall
development. The Board acknowledges and appreciates his invaluable service to the
Company. Following his demise, the Board has taken the necessary steps to ensure
continuity in the Company's leadership through the appointment of new Principal
Officers, subject to the final approval of the National Insurance Commission ("NAICOM").
Details of the appointments are set out below.
2. Executive Management Appointments
The Company hereby announces the appointment of a new Managing Director and an
Executive Director to its Executive Management Team. Details of the appointments are
set out below.
A. Dr. Agboola Omoniyi Oladipupo Managing Director/CEO (Ph.D, FIN, FCIB)
Dr. Agboola previously served as the Executive Director, Technical and Operations and
acting MD/CEO of Royal Exchange Prudential Life Plc, where he drove operational
excellence, business growth and cross-functional execution across operations, sales,
marketing and customer success. Dr Agboola worked as MD/CEO of Armstrong
insurance brokers Limited and General Manager/COO of Golden Shield Insurance
Brokers Limited.
He is an accomplished insurance executive, scholar, educator, and corporate
transformation strategist with about three decades of distinguished experience in the
insurance industry. His career has been defined by an unwavering commitment to
professional excellence, leadership development, innovation, and the advancement of
insurance education. His career follows an intersection of underwriting, business
development, claims and reinsurance.

B. Ms. Elsie A. Akpabio - Executive Director, Corporate Services & General Counsel (LLM, BL,
LLB)
Ms. Elsie Akpabio, is a seasoned board-level executive, governance strategist, and legal
practitioner with over two (2) decades of experience in corporate governance,
regulatory affairs, enterprise risk management, and institutional transformation. She has
built a distinguished career advising and leading organisations across the insurance,
financial services, real estate, and professional advisory sectors, with a proven ability to
align governance, strategy, and regulatory compliance to deliver sustainable
organisational performance.
She holds a Bachelor of Laws (LL.B.) degree from the University of Lagos and a Master of
Laws (LL.M.) in International Finance and Banking Law from the University of Liverpool,
United Kingdom.
Throughout her career, Ms. Akpabio has successfully led and advised on complex
strategic transactions, including debt restructuring, project financing, transaction
advisory, public-private partnership initiatives and equity transactions. She has played a
pivotal role in high-level regulatory engagement and negotiations, delivering
commercially sound outcomes while strengthening institutional governance and
financial resilience.
As a trusted adviser to Boards of Directors and Board Committees, she provides strategic
guidance on corporate governance, legal and regulatory risk, compliance, board
effectiveness, stakeholder engagement, and enterprise-wide transformation. Her

leadership is underpinned by a strong commitment to ethical governance,
accountability, and value creation.
Ms. Akpabio provides strategic oversight for the Legal, Human Resources, Information
Technology, Administration, Procurement, and Corporate Governance functions, driving
organisational effectiveness, operational excellence, and the implementation of
governance frameworks that support long-term corporate objectives.
Ms. Akpabio is a Certified Data Protection Officer and an ISO 31000 Lead Risk Manager.
She is an advocate for strong governance systems, enterprise risk management, digital
resilience, and sustainable business practices, with a particular interest in building resilient
institutions capable of thriving in evolving regulatory and economic environments.
She is a member of the Nigerian Bar Association (NBA), the Society for Corporate
Governance Nigeria, Women in Management, Business and Public Service (WIMBIZ), the
Institute of Chartered Mediators and Conciliators (ICMC), the Association of Enterprise
Risk Management Professionals (AERMP), the Young Directors Forum (YDF), Chartered
Institute of Directors, Nigeria (CIoD).
