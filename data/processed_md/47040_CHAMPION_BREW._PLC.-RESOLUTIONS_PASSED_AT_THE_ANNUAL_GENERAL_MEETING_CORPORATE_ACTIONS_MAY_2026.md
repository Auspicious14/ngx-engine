RESOLUTIONS PASSED AT THE ANNUAL GENERAL MEETING
At the 50th Annual General Meeting (AGM) of Champion Breweries Plc. (the Company) duly
conveyed at Oriental Hotel, 3 Lekki Road, Victoria Island, Lagos on Thursday 21st May 2026, by
12.00 noon, the following Resolutions were proposed and duly passed:
A. ORDINARY BUSINESS ITEM
1. 1 DECLARATION OF THAT a dividend of ₦0.07k per share be and is
DIVIDEND hereby declared as dividend for the financial year
ended 31 December 2025 and is hereby payable
forthwith to the shareholders whose name appear
in the Register of Members as at close of business
May 8, 2026 and payable on Friday, 22nd of May
2026 subject to deduction of appropriate
withholding tax.
2. RE-ELECTION OF That Mr. Eric Idiahi be and is hereby appointed
DIRECTORS as a Director of the Company;
That Mr. David Butler be and is hereby
appointed as a Director of the Company;
That Mr. Olufunminiyi Alabi be and is hereby
appointed as a Director of the Company;
That Mr. Thompson Owoka be and is hereby
appointed as a Director of the Company;
3. APPOINTMENT OF EXTERNAL That Deloitte Touche Tohmatsu Limited as
AUDITORS External Auditors be appointed as the Company’s
Auditors for the year ending December 31, 2026
4. REMUNERATION OF AUDITORS That the Directors be and they are hereby
authorized to fix the remuneration of the
Independent Auditors for the year ending 31st
December 2026.
5. ELECTION OF MEMBERS OF That the following shareholders be and are hereby
SHAREHOLDERS' elected as shareholders’ representatives in the
REPRESENTATIVE OF THE AUDIT Audit Committee of the Company:
COMMITTEE 1. Mr. Olatunde Olayemi.
2. Mr. Adebayo Oluwafemi
3. Chief Peter Mgbeahuru
The following Directors were nominated to
Directors: I. Jacob (Chairman), I. Adoga (MD/CEO), R. Adebiyi (FD), O. Alabi, M. Akpobome (Mrs), S. Aigbedo, D.Ajayi, D. Butler (South African), E. Idiahi, T.
Owoka, S. Ottan, J. Shoboiki (Mrs.)
