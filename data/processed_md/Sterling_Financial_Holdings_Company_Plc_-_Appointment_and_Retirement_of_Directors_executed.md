Lagos, Nigeria, September 15, 2026
STERLING FINANCIAL HOLDINGS COMPANY PLC – NOTIFICATION OF APPOINTMENT AND
RETIREMENT OF DIRECTORS ON SUBSIDIARY BOARD
Sterling Financial Holdings Company Plc (the Company) hereby notifies Nigerian
Exchange Limited (NGX), its shareholders, and the investing public of the appointment
of new Directors to the Board of Sterling Bank Ltd (the Bank), a subsidiary of the
Company. The Central Bank of Nigeria has approved the appointments.
1. Mr. Laoye Jaiyeola was appointed as an Independent Non-Executive Director of
Sterling Bank Ltd, effective 17th August 2026.
Mr. Jaiyeola is a seasoned board leader, financial strategist, and policy advocate
with over thirty-five years of experience spanning financial services, corporate
governance and public policy in Nigeria.
He is the Founder and Pro-Chancellor of Hillside University of Science and
Technology (HUST). He has held key leadership positions in the financial sector that
have strengthened Nigeria's banking and capital markets.
Mr. Jaiyeola served as Managing Director and Chief Executive Officer of Kakawa
Discount House Limited (now Quest Merchant Bank), where he contributed to the
development and stability of Nigeria’s financial markets.
Prior to joining Kakawa Discount House, Mr. Jaiyeola spent nearly two decades
with Central Bank of Nigeria, where he held various positions. He has also held
several prominent leadership and board positions, including President of the
Chartered Institute of Bankers of Nigeria (CIBN) and Chief Executive Officer of the
Nigerian Economic Summit Group (NESG), where he contributed significantly to
policy dialogue and initiatives aimed at improving Nigeria’s business environment.
He currently sits on the Board of FMDQ Group Plc, the NESG Advisory Board and
the Policy Innovation Centre.
He holds a B.Sc. in Economics from Obafemi Awolowo University, a Masters degree
in Banking and Finance from the University of Lagos, and an MBA from Bangor
University. He is a Fellow of the Institute of Chartered Accountants of Nigeria, the
Chartered Institute of Bankers of Nigeria and the Chartered Institute of Directors,
as well as a Chartered Member of the Chartered Banker Institute, Scotland. He is
also a Senior Executive Fellow of Harvard Kennedy School.
2. Mr. Chinedu Ikwudinma was appointed as an Independent Non-Executive
Director of Sterling Bank Ltd, effective 20th August 2026
Mr. Ikwudinma possesses over three decades of international banking and
financial services experience, with expertise spanning Risk Management,
aPublic

Corporate and Investment Banking, Commercial Banking, Transaction Banking,
Strategic Leadership and Governance across African and Global markets.
He most recently served as Group Chief Risk Officer of Ecobank Transnational
Incorporated, with enterprise-wide responsibility for risk management and internal
controls across the Group’s banking affiliates and other non-bank financial
entities. He previously served as Group Chief Credit Officer, where he played a
key role in maintaining strong asset quality and risk management standards.
Prior to Ecobank, Mr. Ikwudinma spent over two decades with Citibank, holding
senior leadership positions across Nigeria, Zambia and Uganda. He served as
Managing Director and CEO of Citibank Uganda and as an Executive Director
and Country Risk Manager for Citibank Nigeria, overseeing a multibillion-dollar
credit portfolio. He also held senior roles in corporate and investment banking,
commercial banking and regional business management. Mr. Ikwudinma was the
pioneer Managing Director of Nova Merchant Bank Limited. He currently serves as
the Chairman of CS&C Associates Limited.
He holds a Bachelor of Engineering (Civil Engineering) Degree from the University
of Nigeria, Nsukka; an M.Sc. in Structural Engineering and an MBA in Finance from
the University of Lagos; and a Diploma in French from Alliance Française, Paris,
through its Lagos programme. Mr. Ikwudinma is a Fellow of the Nigerian Institute of
Credit Administration (FICA), and a Member of the Institute of Directors (MIoD).
The Company also wishes to notify the public of the retirement of the following Directors
of the Bank upon the expiration of their respective tenures
• Mr. Michael Ajukwu - Independent Non-Executive Director;
• Mrs. Olusola Oworu - Independent Non-Executive Director; and
• Mr. Olaitan Kajero - Non-Executive Director.
The Company further announces the retirement of Mr. Tunde Adeola, Executive Director
of the Bank, following his attainment of the prescribed retirement age for Executive
Directors.
The Board expresses its sincere appreciation to the retiring Directors for their dedication,
leadership and valuable contributions to the growth of the Bank and wishes them
continued success in all their future endeavours.
