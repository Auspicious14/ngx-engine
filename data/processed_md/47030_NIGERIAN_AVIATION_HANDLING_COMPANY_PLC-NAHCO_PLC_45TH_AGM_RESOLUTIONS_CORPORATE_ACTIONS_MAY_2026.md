LAGOS, NIGERIA – 18TH MAY 2026
RESOLUTIONS PASSED AT THE ANNUAL GENERAL MEETING OF
NIGERIAN AVIATION HANDLING COMPANY PLC
At the 45th Annual General Meeting of Nigerian Aviation Handling Company Plc (nacho aviance, or the
Company) duly convened and held electronically on 15th May 2026 at 11:00 a.m., the Company passed the
following ordinary resolutions:
1. “That a dividend of ₦6.25 kobo per share amounting to ₦12,181,640,625.00 (Twelve Billion, One
Hundred and Eighty-One Million, Six Hundred and Forty Thousand, Six Hundred and Twenty-Five Naira)
be and is hereby declared payable in respect of the year ended 31st December 2025”.
2. “That Mr. Abdulhamid Aliyu, Rev. Victor Abimbola Olaiya and Mrs. Adebisi Oluwayemisi Bakare be and
are hereby re-elected Directors of the Company.”
3. “That PricewaterhouseCoopers (PwC) be and is here appointed as the Company’s external auditors with
effect from the 2026 financial year”.
4. “That the Directors be and are hereby authorized to fix the remuneration of the External Auditors for
the year ending 31st December 2026”.
5. “That Dr. Okpan Awa Erem, Alh. Mohammed Gambo Fagge, Mr. Ridhwan Hamza (representing the
shareholders) and Mr. Akinwumi Godson Fanimokun and Mr. Tajudeen Moyosola Shobayo (representing
the Directors) be and are hereby elected members of the Audit Committee of the Company until the
next Annual General Meeting”.
6. “That the remuneration of the Non-Executive Directors for the year ending 31st December 2026, until
reviewed by the Company at the Annual General meeting, be and is hereby fixed at ₦150,000,000.00
(One Hundred and Fifty Million Naira) subject to the deduction of appropriate withholding tax where
applicable.”
7. “That pursuant to Rule 20.8 of The Nigerian Exchange Limited, Issuers’ Rule, the general mandate given
to the Company to procure goods and services and enter into such incidental transactions necessary for
its day-to-day operations from its related parties or interested persons on normal commercial terms be
and is hereby renewed.”
8. “That in accordance with Section 127 of the Companies and Allied Matters Act 2020, the share capital
of the Company be and is hereby increased from ₦974,531,250.00 (Nine Hundred and Seventy-Four

Million, Five Hundred and Thirty-One Thousand, Two Hundred and Fifty Naira) divided into
1,949,062,500 (One Billion, Nine Hundred and Forty-Nine Million, Sixty-Two Thousand Five Hundred)
ordinary shares of ₦0.50k (Fifty Kobo) each to ₦1,113,750,000.00 (One Billion, One Hundred and
Thirteen Million, Seven Hundred and Fifty Thousand Naira) divided into 2,227,500,000 (Two Billion, Two
Hundred and Twenty-Seven Million, Five Hundred Thousand) ordinary shares of ₦0.50k (Fifty Kobo) each
by the creation of additional 278,437,500 (Two Hundred and Seventy-Eight Million, Four Hundred and
Thirty-Seven Thousand Five Hundred) ordinary shares of ₦0.50k (Fifty Kobo) each, ranking pari-passu
with the existing ordinary shares of N0.50k (Fifty Kobo).”
9. “That pursuant to the resolution for the increase of the issued share capital by the allotment of new
shares, Clause 6 of the Memorandum of Association of the Company be and is hereby altered to read
“The share capital of the Company is ₦1,113,750,000.00 (One Billion, One Hundred and Thirteen Million,
Seven Hundred and Fifty Thousand Naira) divided into 2,227,500,000 (Two Billion, Two Hundred and
Twenty-Seven Million, Five Hundred Thousand) ordinary shares of ₦0.50K (Fifty Kobo) each, with power
to increase the capital and to divide the capital for the time being into several classes of shares”.
10. a) “That the Directors having recommended, the sum of ₦139,218,750.00 (One Hundred and Thirty-Nine
Million, Two Hundred and Eighteen Thousand, Seven Hundred and Fifty Naira) be transferred from the
Company’s retained earnings or other reserve accounts to the share capital account. The amount thus
transferred shall represent the value of 278,437,500 (Two Hundred and Seventy-Eight Million, Four
Hundred and Thirty-Seven Thousand, Five Hundred) ordinary shares of ₦0.50K (Fifty Kobo) each as fully
paid up and to be allotted, distributed and credited as fully paid up to and amongst the Members whose
names are registered in the Company's Register of Members at the close of business on Friday 1st May
2026 in the proportion of one (1) new share of ₦0.50K (Fifty Kobo) each for every seven (7) existing
shares of ₦0.50K (Fifty Kobo) each held by them. The shares distributed shall rank pari-passu with the
existing shares in all respects and will be treated for all purposes as capital and not as income.”; and
b) “That where the issuance of the bonus shares results in a fraction of a share being held, the Company
be authorised to round that fraction down to the nearest whole share or zero, as the Directors may
deem fit.”
11. “That the Directors be and are hereby authorized to enter into and execute agreements, deeds, notices
or any other documents and to perform all acts and to do all such other things necessary for or
supplementary, consequential or incidental to giving effect to the resolutions 8, 9 and 10 above,
including without limitation, appointing such professional parties, consultants and advisers and
complying with the directives of the regulatory authorities without being required to seek any further
consent or approval of the members.”
Seinde Oladapo Fadeni Bello A. Abdullahi
CHAIRMAN Dikko & Mahmoud Solicitors
COMPANY SECRETARY
