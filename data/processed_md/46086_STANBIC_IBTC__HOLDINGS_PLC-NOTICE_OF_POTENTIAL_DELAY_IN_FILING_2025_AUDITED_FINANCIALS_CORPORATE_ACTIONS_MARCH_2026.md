Notice of Potential Delay in the Release of 2025 Full Year Audited
Results
LAGOS, NIGERIA – 24 February 2026
This is to inform Nigerian Exchange Limited (NGX or The Exchange) as well
as our Esteemed Stakeholders that Stanbic IBTC Holdings Plc (Stanbic or
the Company) may experience delay in filing its Audited Financial
Statements for the year ended 31 December 2025 (2025 AFS), by the due
date of 31 March 2026.
The Company is currently finalising the audit of its 2025 AFS, following which
we would also be seeking the required regulatory approvals.
We are working diligently to ensure that our Company’s 2025 AFS is
submitted to NGX as soon as we have received all required regulatory
approvals, and this may occur before or shortly after the regulatory due date
of 31 March 2026.
