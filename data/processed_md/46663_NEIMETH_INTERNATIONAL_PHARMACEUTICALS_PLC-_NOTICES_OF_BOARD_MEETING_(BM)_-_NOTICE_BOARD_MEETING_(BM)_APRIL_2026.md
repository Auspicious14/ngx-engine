Lagos, Nigeria 14th April 2026
NEIMETH INTERNATIONAL PHARMACEUTICALS PLC – NOTICE OF BOARD MEETING
This is to notify Nigerian Exchange Limited (NGX) and the investing public that a meeting of the
Board of Directors of Neimeth International Pharmaceuticals Plc. (the Company) is scheduled to
hold on Tuesday, 28th April 2026 to consider and approve the Company’s Unaudited Financial
Statements for the period ended 31st March 2026 (2026 Q1, UFS).
In line with NGX’s Amended Closed Period Rules, the Company commenced a closed period from
1st April, 2026 and this will run up to twenty-four (24) hours after the outcome of the meeting
and the 2026 Q1, UFS is released to the public.
Consequently, Directors and other insiders of the Company have been duly notified not to deal in
the Company’s securities during the closed period.
Thank you.
CHINENYE S . ADEKANMBI
Company Secretary
