OVERVIEW
The Chapel Hill Denham Nigeria Real Estate Investment Trust (“NREIT” or the “Fund”) is a Listing NGX
Shariah compliant Real Estate Investment Trust (“REIT”) denominated in Naira and focused Year-end 31st December
exclusively on the Nigerian real estate market. The Fund is registered as a REIT under the
relevant rules and regulations of the Securities & Exchange Commission (“SEC”) Nigeria.

Distribution
The Fund announced a quarterly distribution for the quarter ended 31st March 2026 of N1.92
per unit for Series 1 - 4 investors, and N1.50 per unit for Series 5 investors with a qualification
date of 23rd April 2026. This distribution will be paid to eligible Unitholders on 29th June 2026
and is fully funded from the cash inflows generated by the Fund during the quarter. The
distributions will be payable in the currency in which income is earned. This will be the 21st
consecutive quarterly distribution the Fund has made since inception.
Going forward, the Fund Manager will continue paying quarterly distributions to
Unitholders, in line with the performance of the Fund.
We thank our investors for your support, and we continue to believe that NREIT’s strategy
of creating a large and diversified vehicle will deliver attractive risk-adjusted returns for
NREIT’s investors over the long-term.
