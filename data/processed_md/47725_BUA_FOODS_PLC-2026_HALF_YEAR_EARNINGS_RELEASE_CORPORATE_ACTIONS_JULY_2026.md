Commenting on the results, Ayodele Abioye, the Managing Director, said:
“The first half of 2026 was marked by significant market pressures, including softer consumer demand
and lower selling prices across key product categories. Despite these headwinds, BUA Foods delivered
a resilient performance, supported by operational excellence, disciplined cost management, and the
continued strength of our diversified product portfolio.
Beyond the financial results, we are encouraged by the strengthening of our brands, the loyalty of our
customers, and the growing confidence of our stakeholders. These remain critical foundations for our
future growth ambitions.
As we enter the second half of the year, the Company remains focused on accelerating volume recovery,
deepening market penetration, optimizing pricing, and sustaining the operating efficiencies that
supported its earnings performance during the period.
The operating environment is expected to remain price-sensitive in the near term. Accordingly, BUA
Foods will continue to balance market share growth with sustainable pricing, ensuring that the
Company remains competitive while protecting long-term shareholder value.”

BUA Foods Plc – H1 2026 Unaudited Results
NGX Ticker: BUAFOODS; Bloomberg Ticker: BUAFOODS NL; Reuters: BUAFOODS.LG
Revenue dropped by 16% year-on-year to ₦765.1 billion in H1 2026 (H1 2025: ₦912.5 billion). This
was primarily due to a year-on-year decline in our Sugar, Flour, and Rice reporting segments: 18%
drop in Sugar to ₦327 billion (H1 2025: ₦398.1 billion), 27% drop in Flour to ₦276.2 billion (H1
2025: ₦378.2 billion), and 20% drop in Rice to ₦31.3 billion (H1 2025: ₦39.3 billion).
Cost of sales decreased by (-30%) to ₦401.89 billion in H1 2026 (H1 2025: ₦573.18 billion) driven
primarily by the reduction in raw material cost and exchange rate stability.
Gross profit increased by 7% to ₦363.23 billion in H1 2026 (H1 2025: ₦339.33 billion). In alignment
with the increase, gross profit margin also appreciated by 1030bps to 47.5% in H1 2026 (H1 2025:
37.2%).
Selling and distribution expenses decreased by 15% to ₦30.44 billion in H1 2026 (H1 2025:
₦35.63 billion) due to a decrease in logistics expenses within the period.
Administrative expenses also decreased by 25% to ₦14.50 billion in H1 2026 (H1 2025: ₦19.36
billion) reflecting a continued focus on cost optimization and operational efficiency.
Total operating expenses decreased by 18% to ₦44.93 billion in H1 2026 (H1 2025: ₦54.99 billion)
on the back of a decrease in selling, distribution, and administrative expenses across the supply
chain network.
EBITDA increased by 12% to ₦325.45 billion in H1 2026 (H1 2025: ₦289.68 billion), driven by
growth in gross profit. Also, EBITDA margin appreciated by 1070bps to 42.5% in H1 2026 (H1 2025:
31.8%).
Operating profit grew by 13% to ₦320.52 billion in H1 2026 (H1 2025: ₦284.82 billion) benefiting
from reduced cost of sales, driven by cost containment practices. Consequently, Operating profit
margin appreciated 1100bps to 42% in H1 2026 (H1 2025: 31%).
Finance Cost (Net) dropped by 38% to ₦5.64 billion in H1 2026 (H1 2025: ₦9.13 billion) due to
reduced interest on loans within the reporting period under review.
Profit before tax increased by 14% to ₦314.88 billion in H1 2026 (H1 2025: ₦276.11 billion), as
profit before tax margin also increased by 1070bps to 41% in H1 2026 (H1 2025: 30.3%)
Profit after tax also increased by 12% to ₦292.27 billion in H1 2026 (H1 2025: ₦260.07 billion). In
correlation, the Earning per Share (EPS) grew by 12% to ₦16.24 in H1 2026 from ₦14.45 in the
corresponding period.
Total assets increased by 20% to ₦1.671 trillion as of H1 2026 (FY 2025: ₦1.388 trillion) driven
largely by amounts due from related companies and production capacity expansions.
Total liabilities decreased marginally by 1.3% to ₦665.5 billion as of H1 2026 (FY 2025: ₦674.4 billion).
Total equity increased by 41% to ₦1.006 trillion as of H1 2026 (FY 2025: ₦713.44 billion) largely
driven by a growth of 41% in retained earnings to ₦997.7 billion as of H1 2026 (FY 2025: ₦705.4
billion).

Operating segment performance
Sugar division
The Sugar division contributed 43% to revenue in H1 2026 (H1 2025: 44%). Sugar Revenue
dropped by 18% to ₦327 billion in H1 2026 (H1 2025: ₦398.1 billion). The decrease in revenue was
due to the alignment with strategic and competitive market pricing initiatives, during the period
under review.
Flour division
The Flour division contributed 36% to revenue in H1 2026 (H1 2025: 41%). Revenue declined by
27% to ₦276.2 billion in H1 2026 (H1 2025: ₦378.2 billion). The decrease in revenue was driven
majorly by softer demand for our Flour products within the period under review.
Pasta division
The Pasta division contributed 17% to revenue in H1 2026 (H1 2025: 11%). Revenue increased by
35% to ₦130.6 billion in H1 2026 (H1 2025: ₦96.9 billion). The increase in sales volume recorded
was due to high demand for our Pasta products within the period under review. In addition, the
division’s gross profit margin increased to 43% from 37% for the corresponding period of the
prior year.
Rice division
The Rice division contributed 4% to the revenue in H1 2026 (H1 2025: 4%). Revenue declined to
₦31.3 billion in H1 2026 (H1 2025: ₦39.3 billion), due to strategic and competitive market pricing
initiatives, during the period under review.

Definition of terms
Gross profit refers to revenue minus cost of sales.
Gross profit margin corresponds to gross profit as a % of revenue.
Operating expenses corresponds to Selling and distribution expenses, Administrative expenses and
Other operating expenses.
EBITDA refers to earnings before interest, tax, depreciation and amortization.
EBITDA margin corresponds to EBITDA as a % of revenue.
Operating profit refers to gross profit minus operating expenses plus other operating income.
Operating profit Margin corresponds to EBIT as a % of revenue.
Profit before Tax corresponds to EBIT minus net finance (cost)/income and plus share of profit of
associates and joint venture using the equity method.
Profit before tax margin corresponds to profit before tax as a % of revenue.
Return on equity corresponds to net profit reported to average total equity.
Return on assets corresponds to net profit reported to average total assets.
Earnings per share is profit after tax from continuing operations reported to weighted average number
of shares.

About BUA Foods Plc
BUA Foods Plc (NGX: BUAFOODS) is a leading food business with well diversified and scalable operations
producing sugar, flour, pasta, rice and edible oils. The Company owns strategically located plants across
Nigeria, in addition to a cordial alliance with local stakeholders in host communities. Additionally, BUA
Foods is a resilient business built on a strong brand proposition and is an operator that has a well-
known reputation for delivering high-quality products.
BUA Foods continues to invest in modern technology for efficient food production, innovatively
expanding with strategic partners across the value chain. The Company is also well positioned to leverage
significant export potentials across West Africa and the larger African continent.
Headquartered and listed in Nigeria, BUA Foods is one of the most capitalized companies on the NGX
Exchange and a leading consumer goods firm by market value.
