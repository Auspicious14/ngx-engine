Commenting on the results, Ayodele Abioye, the Managing Director, said:
“BUA Foods delivered resilient earnings in Q1 2026, underpinned by strong margin performance
despite a challenging operating environment. Profit After Tax increased by 14% year-on-year to
₦142.32 billion, notwithstanding an 11% decline in revenue and modest volume growth. EBITDA rose
by 11% to ₦157.13 billion, reflecting effective cost containment, pricing discipline, and sustained
margin expansion across core product portfolios.
Looking ahead, while geopolitical risks continue to impact energy markets and global supply chains,
the business remains well positioned. Supported by a resilient operating model and disciplined
execution, BUA Foods is focused on delivering sustainable, long-term value for investors, shareholders,
and other stakeholders.”

BUA Foods Plc – 3M 2026 Unaudited Results
NGX Ticker: BUAFOODS; Bloomberg Ticker: BUAFOODS NL; Reuters: BUAFOODS.LG
Revenue dropped by 11% q-o-q to ₦394.6 billion in 3M 2026 (3M 2025: ₦442.1 billion). This was
primarily due to a q-o-q decline in our Sugar and Flour reporting segments: 19% in Sugar to ₦171.7
billion (3M 2025: ₦211.3 billion), 22% in Flour to ₦137.4 billion (3M 2025: ₦176.2 billion).
Cost of sales decreased by (-22%) to ₦218.97 billion in 3M 2026 (3M 2025: ₦281.16 billion) driven
primarily by improved cost efficiency in raw materials and energy.
Gross profit increased by 9% to ₦175.65 billion in 3M 2026 (3M 2025: ₦160.91 billion). In
alignment with the increase, gross profit margin also appreciated by 810bps to 44.5% in 3M 2026
(3M 2025: 36.4%).
Selling and distribution expenses increased by 42% to ₦15.77 billion in 3M 2026 (3M 2025:
₦11.08 billion) due to an increase in logistics expenses within the period.
Administrative expenses albeit decreased by 49% to ₦5.82 billion in 3M 2026 (3M 2025: ₦11.32
billion).
Total operating expenses decreased by 4% to ₦21.59 billion in 3M 2026 (3M 2025: ₦22.39 billion)
on the back of a significant decrease in administrative expenses along the supply chain to
customers.
EBITDA increased by 11% to ₦157.13 billion in 3M 2026 (3M 2025: ₦141.40 billion), driven by
growth in gross profit. Also, EBITDA margin appreciated by 790bps to 39.8% in 3M 2026 (3M 2025:
31.9%).
Operating profit grew by 11% to ₦154.66 billion in 3M 2026 (3M 2025: ₦138.9 billion) benefiting
from reduced cost of sales, driven by cost containment practices. Also, Operating profit margin
appreciated by 800bps to 39% in 3M 2026 (3M 2025: 31%).
Finance Cost (Net) dropped significantly by 70.3% to ₦899 million in 3M 2026 (3M 2025: ₦3.03
billion) due to non-additional borrowing within the reporting period under review.
Profit before tax increased by 13% to ₦153.8 billion in 3M 2026 (3M 2025: ₦136.4 billion), as
profit before tax margin also increased by 810bps to 39% in 3M 2026 (3M 2025: 30.9%)
Profit after tax also increased by 14% to ₦142.32 billion in 3M 2026 (3M 2025: ₦125.28 billion).
In correlation, the Earning per Share (EPS) grew by 14% to N7.91 in 3M 2026 from N6.96 in the
corresponding period.
Total assets increased by 12% to ₦1.556 trillion as of 3M 2026 (FY 2025: ₦1.389 trillion) driven
largely by dues from related companies and production capacity expansions.
Total liabilities increased marginally by 4% to ₦699.9 billion as of 3M 2026 (FY 2025: ₦674.4 billion).
Total equity increased by 20% to ₦855.76 billion as of 3M 2026 (FY 2025: ₦713.44 billion) largely
driven by a growth of 20% in retained earnings to ₦847.7 billion as of 3M 2026 (FY 2025: ₦705.4
billion).

Operating segment performance
Sugar division
The Sugar division contributed 43% to revenue in 3M 2026 (3M 2025: 48%). Sugar Revenue
dropped by 19% to ₦171.7 billion in 3M 2026 (3M 2025: ₦211.3 billion). The decrease in revenue
is due majorly to reduced demand for our Sugar product within the period under review.
Flour division
The Flour division contributed 35% to revenue in 3M 2026 (3M 2025: 40%). Revenue declined by
22% to ₦137.4 billion in 3M 2026 (3M 2025: ₦176.2 billion). The decrease in revenue is due to the
alignment with strategic and competitive market pricing initiatives, during the period under
review.
Pasta division
The Pasta division contributed 18% to revenue in 3M 2026 (3M 2025: 9%). Revenue increased by
70% to ₦70.6 billion in 3M 2026 (3M 2025: ₦41.5 billion). The increase in sales volume recorded
was due to high demand for our Pasta products within the period under review. However,
Contribution margin dropped to 41% f rom 43% for the same period last year.
Rice division
The Rice division contributed 4% to the revenue in 3M 2026 (3M 2025: 3%). Revenue increased to
₦14.9 billion in 3M 2026 (3M 2025: ₦13.02 billion), due to steady milling operations in the division.

Definition of terms
Gross profit refers to revenue minus cost of sales.
Gross profit margin corresponds to gross profit as a % of revenue.
Operating expenses corresponds to Selling and distribution expenses, Administrative expenses and
Other operating expenses.
EBITDA refers to earnings before interest, tax, depreciation and amortization.
EBITDA margin corresponds to EBITDA as a % of revenue.
Operating profit refers to gross profit minus operating expenses plus other operating income.
Operating profit Margin corresponds to EBIT as a % of revenue.
Profit before Tax corresponds to EBIT minus net finance (cost)/income and plus share of profit of
associates and joint venture using the equity method.
Profit before tax margin corresponds to profit before tax as a % of revenue.
Return on equity corresponds to net profit reported to average total equity.
Return on assets corresponds to net profit reported to average total assets.
Earnings per share is profit after tax from continuing operations reported to weighted average number
of shares.

About BUA Foods Plc
BUA Foods Plc (NGX: BUAFOODS) is a leading food business with well diversified and scalable operations
producing sugar, flour, pasta, rice and edible oils. The Company owns strategically located plants across
Nigeria, in addition to a cordial alliance with local stakeholders in host communities. Additionally, BUA
Foods is a resilient business built on a strong brand proposition and is an operator that has a well-
known reputation for delivering high-quality products.
BUA Foods continues to invest in modern technology for efficient food production, innovatively
expanding with strategic partners across the value chain. The Company is also well positioned to leverage
significant export potentials across West Africa and the larger African continent.
Headquartered and listed in Nigeria, BUA Foods is one of the most capitalized companies on the NGX
Exchange and a leading consumer goods firm by market value.
