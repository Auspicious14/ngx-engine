23rd March, 2026
Lagos, Nigeria
CHANGE OF REGISTERED ADDRESS
The Board of Directors of Geregu Power Plc (the Company) hereby notifies
Nigerian Exchange Limited (“NGX”) and the investing public that the
Company has changed its registered address.
The Company’s former registered address was 13, Walter Carrington Crescent,
Victoria Island, Lagos. Following the relocation of its corporate head office, the
Board has approved a change of the registered address to 6, Onega Street,
Off Gana Street, Maitama, Abuja.
This change was necessitated to align the Company’s registered address with
its principal place of business, enhance administrative and operational
efficiency, and ensure that the Company’s corporate records with
stakeholders remain accurate and up to date.
Accordingly, all stakeholders are requested to take note of this change and
update their records to reflect the Company’s new registered address as
stated above.
By Order of the Board.
For: Geregu Power Plc
The Structure HQ Limited
