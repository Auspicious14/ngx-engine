RE: NOTIFICATION OF APPOINTMENT OF EXTERNAL AUDITORS
Further to our notification dated September 2, 2026, we write to notify the Nigerian
Exchange Limited (“NGX”) and the investing public of the appointment of Madu,
Onyekwena & Co as the new External Auditors of Cutix Plc (the "Company"), following the
resignation of the Company's former External Auditors, Ngozi Monica Okonkwo & Co.
After the resignation of Ngozi Monica Okonkwo & Co, the Company undertook the
necessary process for the appointment of a replacement External Auditor. Upon
consideration of the recommendation of the Audit, Risk and Compliance Committee of the
Board of Directors and completion of the applicable governance and due diligence processes,
the Board of Directors approved the appointment of Madu, Onyekwena & Co as the
Company's new External Auditors effective November 1, 2026.
The new External Auditors will undertake the statutory audit of the Company's financial
statements in accordance with the provisions of the Companies and Allied Matters Act, 2020,
Financial Reporting Council applicable regulations and other relevant regulatory
requirements.
