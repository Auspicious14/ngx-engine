APPROVAL OF THE GROUP'S AUDITED FINANCIAL STATEMENTS FOR THE HALF-
YEAR ENDED JUNE 30, 2026, AND APPOINTMENT OF A NON-EXECUTIVE
DIRECTOR
Lagos, 15 August 2026
Please refer to the announcement dated July 1, 2026, notifying Nigerian
Exchange Limited and the investing public of the meeting of the Board of
Directors of United Bank for Africa Plc scheduled to hold on Thursday, August
13, 2026.
Please be informed that the Board of Directors of United Bank for Africa Plc,
at its meeting held on Thursday, August 13, 2026, considered and approved
the Group's Audited Accounts and Financial Statements for the half year
ended June 30, 2026, subject to the approval of the Central Bank of Nigeria.
Further to the above, kindly be advised that Nigerian Exchange Limited and
the investing public will be immediately notified upon receipt of the approval
of the Central Bank of Nigeria for the 2026 Half-Year Audited Accounts and
Financial Statements.
Consequently, the Bank's closed period will continue to run until 24 hours after
the 2026 Half-Year Audited Accounts and Financial Statements are released
to the public.
The Board also approved the appointment of Mr. Ibrahim Ajimasu Puri as a
Non-Executive Director of the Bank, subject to the approval of the Central
Bank of Nigeria.
Mr. Puri is an accomplished financial services leader and corporate executive
with over 35 years’ experience across banking, fintech, telecommunications,
and FMCG. He previously served on the Board of UBA as an Executive
Director, retiring in 2022, and currently serves on several Boards as a Non-
Executive Director, including Nigerian Breweries Plc and 9mobile. He has
