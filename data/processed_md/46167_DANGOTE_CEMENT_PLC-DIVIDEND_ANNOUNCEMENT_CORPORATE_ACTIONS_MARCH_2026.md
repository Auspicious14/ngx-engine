Lagos, Nigeria
CORPORATE ACTIONS ANNOUCEMENT
Dangote Cement Plc hereby announces as follows:
Period Ended December 31, 2025
Proposed Dividend A final dividend of N45 per share, subject to the appropriate
withholding tax and approval, will be paid to shareholders whose
names appear in the Register of Members at the close of business on
Wednesday, June 17, 2026.
Proposed Bonus Nil
Closure of Register The Register of Members will be closed on Thursday, June 18, 2026.
Qualification Date Wednesday, June 17, 2026
Payment Date By Thursday, July 2, 2026, dividends will be paid electronically to
shareholders whose names appear in the Register of Members as of
Wednesday, June 17, 2026, and who have completed the e-dividend
registration and mandated the Registrar to pay their dividends
directly into their bank accounts.
E-Dividend Registration Shareholders who are yet to complete the e-dividend registration are
advised to download and complete the Registrar’s E-Mandate
Activation Form, which is available on the Registrar’s website
(www.coronationregistrars.com) and submit it to the Registrar or
their respective banks.
Unclaimed Dividend Shareholders with dividend warrants and share certificates that have
Warrants and Share remained unclaimed or are yet to be presented for payment or
Certificates returned for validation, are advised to complete the e-dividend
registration or contact the Registrar.
Date of Annual General The Company Annual General Meeting will be held on Thursday, July
Meeting (AGM) 2, 2026, at 11.00 am.
Venue To be announced in the Notice of Meeting.
