Lagos, 6th October 2026
NOTICE OF BOARD MEETING CLOSED PERIOD
Meyer Plc (the Company) wishes to notify Nigerian Exchange Limited (NGX or the Exchange),
its shareholders and the investing public that the Company’s Board of Directors will consider
and approve the Company’s Unaudited Financial Accounts for the third quarter ended 30th
September 2026 (2026 Q3 UFS) at its meeting scheduled to be held on Monday, the 26th of
October 2026.
In line with the Amended Closed Period Rules of NGX, all insiders and their connected persons
are prohibited from trading (i.e. Buying, selling, transferring or otherwise dealing) in the
Company’s shares in view of the Closed Period, which commenced on Thursday, the 1st of
October 2026, until twenty-four (24) hours after the Company’s 2026 Q3 UFS are released
to NGX and the general public.
Thank you.
