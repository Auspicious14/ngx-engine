PRESS RELEASE
Lagos, Monday, July 6, 2026
NOTICE OF RESCHEDULING OF 38TH ANNUAL GENERAL MEETING
Fidelity Bank Plc hereby notifies Nigerian Exchange Limited (NGX or The
Exchange), its esteemed shareholders and stakeholders that the Bank's 38th
Annual General Meeting earlier scheduled to hold at 10:00 a.m. on Tuesday,
July 7, 2026, has been rescheduled. A new date for the meeting will be
communicated in due course.
The rescheduling is to allow the Bank conclude all necessary arrangements for
the meeting and ensure an optimal experience for shareholders and other
stakeholders.
The Bank regrets any inconvenience this change may cause and appreciates
the understanding and continued support of its shareholders and stakeholders.
Shareholders are kindly requested to visit the Bank's website,
www.fidelitybank.ng, for further updates regarding the 38th Annual General
Meeting. Updates will also be published through the appropriate channels.
