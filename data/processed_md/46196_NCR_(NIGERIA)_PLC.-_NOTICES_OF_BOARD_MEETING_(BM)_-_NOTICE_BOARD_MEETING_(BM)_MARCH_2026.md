NOTICE OF THE MEETING OF THE BOARD OF NCR (NIGERIA) PLC AND

NOTICE IS HEREBY GIVEN THAT a meeting of the Board of Directors of NCR
(Nigeria) PLC (the “Company”) will be held on Wednesday, 25 March 2026, to
consider, amongst other things, the approval of the Audited Financial Statements
of the Company for the full year ended 31 December 2025 (2025 AFS).
Venue: St Nicholas House (10th Floor),
Catholic Mission Street,
Lagos
Platform: Zoom Meeting
Time: 3.00 pm
Accordingly, in compliance with Nigerian Exchange Limited’s post-listings
requirements, NCR (Nigeria) PLC declared the commencement of a closed period
for trading in its shares from 31 December 2025 until twenty-four (24) hours after
the 2025 AFS is released to the Market.
Consequently, no Insider shall deal directly or indirectly in the securities of NCR
(Nigeria) PLC during this closed period.
