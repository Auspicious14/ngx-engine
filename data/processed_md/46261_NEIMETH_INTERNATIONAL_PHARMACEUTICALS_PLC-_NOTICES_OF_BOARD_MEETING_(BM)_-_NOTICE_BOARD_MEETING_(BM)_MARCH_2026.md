Lagos, Nigeria, 11th March 2026
NEIMETH INTERNATIONAL PHARMACEUTICALS PLC – NOTICE OF BOARD MEETING
This is to notify Nigerian Exchange Limited (NGX) and the investing public that a meeting of the Board
of Directors of Neimeth International Pharmaceuticals Plc. (the Company) is scheduled to hold on
Wednesday, 25th March 2026 to consider and approve the Company’s Audited Financial Statements for
the year ended 31st December 2025 (2025 AFS).
In line with NGX’s Amended Closed Period Rules, the Company commenced a closed period from 1st
January, 2026 and this will run up to twenty-four (24) hours after the outcome of the meeting and the
2025 AFS is released to the public.
Consequently, Directors and other insiders of the Company have been duly notified not to deal in the
Company’s securities during the closed period.
Thank you.
CHINENYE S. ADEKANMBI
Company Secretary
