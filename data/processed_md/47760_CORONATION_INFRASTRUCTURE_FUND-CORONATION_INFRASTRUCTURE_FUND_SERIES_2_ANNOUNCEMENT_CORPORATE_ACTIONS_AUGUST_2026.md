Lagos, Nigeria – 3rd August 2026
NOW OPEN – CORONATION INFRASTRUCTURE FUND SERIES 2 OFFER OF UP TO ₦20
BILLION OF 186,915,887 UNITS ISSUED AT ₦107.00 UNDER THE ₦200 BILLION PROGRAMME
Coronation Infrastructure Fund ("CIF" or the "Fund") is pleased to announce the launch of the Series II Offer of
up to ₦20 billion under its ₦200 billion Shelf Programme, providing investors with an opportunity to participate
in financing critical infrastructure assets that underpin Nigeria's long-term economic growth.
The Coronation Infrastructure Fund (CIF) is a closed-end infrastructure debt fund that provides investors with
exposure to a diversified portfolio of infrastructure debt investments across key sectors of the Nigerian
economy, including transportation, real estate, utilities, social infrastructure, telecommunications, and energy.
Managed by Coronation Asset Management Limited, the Fund seeks to deliver attractive risk-adjusted returns
while financing essential infrastructure projects that support sustainable economic growth.
With assets under management (AuM) of over ₦9.2 billion, the successful completion of the Series II Offer is
expected to increase the Fund's AuM to over ₦30 billion, significantly strengthening its capacity to finance a
growing pipeline of eligible infrastructure projects. The additional capital will support the expansion of the
Fund’s portfolio into carefully selected, creditworthy projects that meet its disciplined investment criteria and
rigorous risk management standards.
The Series II Offer presents investors with a unique opportunity to gain exposure to an asset class that combines
the potential for stable income generation with meaningful developmental impact. As Nigeria's infrastructure
financing needs continue to expand, the Fund is well positioned to provide long-term debt capital to essential
projects across its focus sectors while seeking to deliver consistent, risk-adjusted returns to investors. Backed
by a disciplined investment process, robust governance framework, and active portfolio management, the
Coronation Infrastructure Fund offers investors the opportunity to participate in building critical infrastructure
that supports economic growth while pursuing long-term value creation.

Notes to editors:
The Coronation Infrastructure Fund ("CIF" or the "Fund") is a Naira-denominated, closed-end infrastructure debt fund established to
provide investors with access to a diversified portfolio of infrastructure investments in Nigeria. The Fund is authorised and regulated by the
Securities and Exchange Commission ("SEC") Nigeria and is managed by Coronation Asset Management Limited. The Fund is intended to
be listed on the Nigerian Exchange Limited ("NGX"), providing investors with enhanced liquidity and market visibility.
The Fund's investment strategy is focused on financing commercially viable infrastructure projects across critical sectors of the Nigerian
economy, including transportation, energy, utilities, telecommunications, social infrastructure, and real estate. Through a disciplined
investment process, robust credit underwriting, and active portfolio management, the Fund seeks to preserve capital while delivering
stable, risk-adjusted returns and supporting the development of infrastructure that contributes to Nigeria's long-term economic growth.
