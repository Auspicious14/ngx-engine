LAGOS: 2ND JULY 2026
HBM NIGERIA PLC (FORMERLY LAFARGE AFRICA PLC) - NOTIFICATION OF BOARD
MEETING AND CLOSED PERIOD
We hereby notify the Nigerian Exchange Limited (“NGX”) and the investing public that a
meeting of the Board of Directors of HBM Nigeria Plc (the “Company”) has been
scheduled to hold on Tuesday, 28th July 2026 to consider among other matters, the
Company’s Unaudited Financial Statements for the period ended 30th June 2026 (the
“Unaudited Financial Statements”) and a proposal for an interim dividend.
Please note that the Closed Period for trading in the Company’s shares commenced on
Wednesday, 1st July 2026 and will continue until twenty-four (24) hours after the
Unaudited Financial Statements is filed with the NGX and released to the public in
accordance with the Rules of the NGX. Accordingly, no Insider may directly or indirectly
deal in the securities of the Company in any manner during the Closed Period.
Adewunmi Alode
Legal Director & Company Secretary
