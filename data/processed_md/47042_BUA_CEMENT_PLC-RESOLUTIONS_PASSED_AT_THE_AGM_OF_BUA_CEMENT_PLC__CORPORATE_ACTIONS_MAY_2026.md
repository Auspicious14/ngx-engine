Abuja, 22nd May 2026.
RESOLUTIONS PASSED AT THE 10TH ANNUAL GENERAL MEETING OF BUA
CEMENT PLC.
At the 10th Annual General Meeting of BUA Cement PLC (the Company) held on Thursday,
21st May 2026, at the Congress Hall of Transcorp Hilton Hotel , Abuja, the following resolutions
were duly deliberated and passed:
1. That the Audited Financial Statements of the Company for the year ended 31st December
2025, together with the Reports of the Directors, External Auditors and the Audit
Committee thereon, be received.
2. That a dividend of ₦10.00k per each ordinary share of 50 kobo for the period ended 31st
December 2025, is hereby approved.
3. That Kabiru Rabiu, Chimaobi Madukwe, and Ganiat Adetutu Siyonbola, being Directors
of the Company retiring by rotation, be and are hereby re-elected.
4. That the Directors of the Company are hereby authorised to fix the remuneration of the
Auditors for the year ending 31st December 2026.
5. That the following individuals shall constitute the Statutory Audit Committee of the
Company for the 2026 Financial year: Mr. Ajibola Ajayi, Mr. Kabiru Tambari and Mr.
Oderinde Taiwo (as representatives of shareholders), and Mr. Kabiru Rabiu and Ms.
Ganiat Adetutu Siyonbola (as Board representatives).
6. The remuneration of the Managers of the Company was disclosed in compliance with
Section 257 of the Companies and Allied Matters Act (CAMA) 2020.
7. That the remuneration of the Non-Executive Directors of the Company for the year
ending 31st December 2026 is hereby fixed at N16,562,000 for Non–Executive Directors,
N18,928,000 for Independent Non–Executive Directors and N23,660,000 for the
Chairman.
8. That, in compliance with Rule 20.8(a) of the Nigerian Exchange Issuers Rules governing
transactions with Related Parties or Interested Persons, the general mandate granted to
the Company to procure goods and services and enter into such incidental transactions
necessary for its day-to-day operations from its related parties or interested persons on
normal commercial terms be and is hereby renewed.
By the Order of the Board
Hauwa Satomi
Company Secretary/Chief Legal Officer
