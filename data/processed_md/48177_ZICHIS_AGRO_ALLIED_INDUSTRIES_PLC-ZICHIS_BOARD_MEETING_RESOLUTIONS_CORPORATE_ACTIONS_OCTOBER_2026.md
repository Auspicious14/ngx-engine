Lagos, Nigeria
October 26, 2026
Zichis Begins Process to Acquire 221‑Hectare Palm Plantation
Zichis Agro-Allied Industries Plc (“Zichis” or “the Company”) announces that its Board of Directors has approved
management’s plan to begin the process of acquiring a 221-hectare oil palm plantation owned by Amandel Nigeria
Limited. The approval was given at a virtual Board meeting held on Friday, October 23, 2026.
The move is a strategic step to address the shortage of Fresh Fruit Bunches (FFB) in the Company’s oil palm business.
Zichis currently has 61 acres of fruiting palm, which is expected to yield about 244 tonnes of FFB this year. At an
average Oil Extraction Rate (OER) of 20%, this will produce about 49 tonnes of Crude Palm Oil (CPO).
The Company is also working to grow its own supply. At the start of the year, Zichis acquired 2,000 acres of land for
new palm cultivation, and more than 300 acres have already been cultivated. These young palms take another 4-5
years to start yielding maximally, and sourcing FFB from local farmers has remained a challenge. The Amandel
acquisition gives Zichis a faster and more dependable source of fruit.
The Amandel plantation sits on 221 hectares along the Ago-Kona Elegbeke Road, off the Owo/Ifon Express Road, in
Ose Local Government Area of Ondo State. The estate is in two phases: 74 hectares of fruiting palms and 54 hectares
of young palms that are less than two years old. The remaining 93 hectares are yet to be cultivated.
With more FFB from its own estate, Zichis will be able to raise its CPO output and expand into the extraction of Palm
Kernel Oil (PKO). The Company expects this to lift revenue in its oil palm business and improve its return on assets
and return on equity in a short distance.
At the same meeting, the Board reviewed and approved the Company’s nine months unaudited financial statements for the
period ended September 30, 2026. The statements will be released to the public on or before 30 October 2026.
Zichis has appointed Futureview Financial Services Limited as financial adviser to lead the transaction process. The
transaction remains subject to the completion of due diligence, agreement on final terms and all required approvals.
The Company will keep shareholders and the public informed as we progress.
