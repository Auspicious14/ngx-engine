Lagos, Nigeria, 26th March 2026
RESOLUTIONS PASSED AT THE BOARD MEETING OF BUA FOODS PLC
HELD ON 26TH MARCH 2026
At the meeting of the Board of Directors of BUA Foods Plc (the Company) held on 26th
March 2026, the following resolutions amongst others were considered and approved:
1. That the Audited Financial Statements for the year ended 31st December 2025
(2025 AFS) be and is hereby approved for filing with the regulators and be released
to the investing public.
2. That the Dividend proposal based on the 2025 AFS is approved subject to the
approval of the shareholders at the Annual General Meeting.
BY ORDER OF THE BOARD:
Oluseye Alayande
Company Secretary
