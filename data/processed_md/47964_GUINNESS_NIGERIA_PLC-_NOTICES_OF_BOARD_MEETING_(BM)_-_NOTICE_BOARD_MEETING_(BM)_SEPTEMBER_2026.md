LAGOS, 29 SEPTEMBER 2026
NOTIFICATION OF THE MEETING OF THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF
GUINNESS NIGERIA PLC
This is to notify the Nigerian Exchange Limited (NGX or The Exchange) and the Market that
a meeting of the Board of Directors of Guinness Nigeria Plc (the Company) will hold on
Wednesday, 21st October 2026 for the purpose of considering and approving the unaudited
financial results for the quarter ending 30th September 2026, amongst other matters.
Accordingly, in compliance with the NGX Post-Listing Rules as amended, the closed period
for trading in the Company’s shares by insiders will commence on the 1st of October 2026
and will expire 24 hours after the Unaudited Financial Statements for the period ending 30th
September 2026 is released to the public.
Therefore, no insider of the Company and their connected persons may directly or indirectly
deal in the securities of the Company during the closed period.
The results for the period as approved by the Board will thereafter be uploaded through
The Exchange’s Issuers’ Portal not later than 22nd October 2026.
