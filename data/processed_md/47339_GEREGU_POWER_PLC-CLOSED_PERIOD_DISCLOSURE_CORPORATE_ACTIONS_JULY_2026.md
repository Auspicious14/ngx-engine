Lagos, July 1st, 2026
NOTIFICATION OF CLOSED PERIOD
In accordance with the provisions of Rule 17.18(a) Closed Period Rules, Rulebook of
The Exchange, 2015 (Issuers’ Rules) as amended, the Closed Period for trading Geregu
Power Plc’s (“the Company”) shares will commence on 1st July 2026 in respect of the
Second Quarter Unaudited Financial Statements & Accounts for the period ended
30th June 2026.
Consequently, no Insider, or consultant of the Company and their connected persons
may directly or indirectly deal in the securities of the Company until 24 hours after the
release of the Second Quarter Unaudited Financial Statements & Accounts for the
period ended 30th June 2026 through the Issuers’ Portal of Nigerian Exchange Limited
and by extension, the general public.
All insiders of the Company and persons closely connected to them have been
appropriately advised.
