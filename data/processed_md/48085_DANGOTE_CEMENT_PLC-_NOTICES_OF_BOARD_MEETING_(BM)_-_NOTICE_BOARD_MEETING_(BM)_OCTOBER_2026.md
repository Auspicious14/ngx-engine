October 15, 2026
Lagos, Nigeria
UPDATED NOTICE OF BOARD MEETING CLOSED PERIOD FOR DANGOTE CEMENT PLC.
This is to inform Nigerian Exchange Limited (NGX) and all stakeholders that the meeting
of the Board of Directors of Dangote Cement Plc (“the Company”), earlier scheduled
for October 29, 2026, will now be held on October 27, 2026, to discuss various matters,
including the Unaudited Financial Statements for the quarter ended September 30,
2026 (2026 Q3 UFS).
All other information and details contained in the earlier publication of September 30,
2026, remain unchanged. The Closed Trading Period will end twenty-four (24) hours
after the release of the 2026 Q3 Unaudited Financial Statements and the relevant
resolutions passed at the Board meeting to the public.

Company Secretary
