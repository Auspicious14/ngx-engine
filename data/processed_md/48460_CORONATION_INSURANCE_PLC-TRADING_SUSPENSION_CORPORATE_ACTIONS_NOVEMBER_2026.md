Lagos, Nigeria – November 27, 2026
PUBLIC NOTICE: FULL SUSPENSION OF TRADING IN THE SHARES OF CORONATION
INSURANCE PLC
Coronation Insurance Plc (the “Company”) hereby notifies Nigerian Exchange Limited
(“NGX”), its esteemed shareholders, and the investing public that trading in the shares of
the Company has been placed on full suspension effective today, November 27, 2026.
This follows the approval of the Scheme of Arrangement (the “Scheme”) by shareholders
at the Court-Ordered Meeting held on December 22, 2023, the Formal Approval of the
Securities and Exchange Commission (“SEC”), and the sanction of the Federal High Court
obtained on November 10, 2026, for the acquisition and transfer of the shares held by the
Scheme Shareholders to the Core Shareholders of Coronation Insurance Plc. The full
suspension is to enable the Company, and its Registrars determine the final register of
shareholders entitled to receive the Scheme Consideration.
Shareholders and other investors are advised to note that, following the full suspension
effective November 27, 2026, there will be no further trading in the shares of Coronation
Insurance Plc on NGX ahead of the eventual delisting of the Company’s shares from the
Daily Official List.
BY ORDER OF THE BOARD
MARY AGHA
COMPANY SECRETARY
