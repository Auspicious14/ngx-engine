OANDO PLC – UPDATE ON THE PUBLICATION OF THE 2025 AUDITED FINANCIAL
STATEMENTS AND Q1 2026 UNAUDITED FINANCIAL STATEMENTS
29 May 2026
Oando PLC (“Oando” or the “Company”), Nigeria’s leading indigenous energy group
listed on both the Nigerian Exchange Limited (NGX) and Johannesburg Stock Exchange
(JSE Limited), wishes to inform its shareholders and the public of the following update.
Further to its prior announcement regarding the delay in the publication of its 2025
Audited Financial Statements (“2025 AFS”), the Board of Directors has now approved
the Company’s 2025 AFS and Management has subsequently submitted them to the
Financial Reporting Council of Nigeria (FRCN)for final regulatory review, in accordance
with applicable statutory requirements.
Following receipt of regulatory clearance from the FRCN, the Company will proceed with
formal publication of the 2025 AFS. This process is expected to be completed on or
before June 12, 2026, after which the Company intends to publish its Q1 2026 Unaudited
Financial Statements on or before 30 June 2026.
The Company remains committed to the highest standards of financial reporting
integrity, transparency and timely disclosure in compliance with the NGX Listing Rules.
We thank our shareholders, regulators, and stakeholders for their continued
understanding.
