LAGOS: 13TH JULY 2026
HBM NIGERIA PLC - NOTIFICATION OF CHANGE IN TRADING SYMBOL
We are pleased to inform our esteemed shareholders, Trading Licence Holders and the
investing public that further to our previous communication regarding the change of the
Company’s corporate name from Lafarge Africa Plc to HBM Nigeria Plc, the Company’s
trading symbol on the floor of The Exchange has been changed
from WAPCO to HBMNG.
Please note that all shares currently held or previously purchased through various trading
platforms will now automatically reflect under the new trading symbol, HBMNG,
without requiring any action from shareholders.
We appreciate the continued support of our shareholders and stakeholders as we embark
on this new chapter as HBM Nigeria Plc.
Adewunmi Alode
Company Secretary & Legal Director
