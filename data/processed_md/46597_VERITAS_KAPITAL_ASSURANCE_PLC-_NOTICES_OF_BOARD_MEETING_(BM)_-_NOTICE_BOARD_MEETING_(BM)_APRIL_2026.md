ABUJA: 7 April, 2026
NOTIFICATION OF BOARD MEETING OF VERITAS KAPITAL ASSURANCE
PLC
The Board and Management of Veritas Kapital Assurance Plc (the
Company) wish to notify Nigerian Exchange Limited (NGX), esteemed
Shareholders, Stakeholders, and the general public that the Board of
Directors of the Company will be approving the Unaudited Financial
Stat ements for the period ended 31 March, 2026 (2026 Q1 UFS) of the
Company by circularization.
However, notice is hereby given that a meeting of the Board of
Directors of the Company will be held on Thursday, April 30, 2026, at
10:00 a.m. to ratify the 1st Quarter, 2026 Unaudited Financial
Stat ements and review the operational strategies of the Company.
Per the post-listing requirements of Nigerian Exchange Limited, the
Closed Period for trading in the shares of the Company by Insiders
commenced with effect from April 1, 2026. Accordingly, no insider of
the Company and their connected persons may directly or indirectly
deal in the securities of the Company until twenty-four (24) hours after
the release of the 2026 Q1 UFS to NGX and by extension, the general
public.
