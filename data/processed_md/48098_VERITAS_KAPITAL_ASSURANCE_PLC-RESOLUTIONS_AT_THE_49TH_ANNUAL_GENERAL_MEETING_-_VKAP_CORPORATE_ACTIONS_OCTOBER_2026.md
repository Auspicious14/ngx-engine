ABUJA - 19th October, 2026
SUBJECT – RESOLUTIONS PASSED AT THE 49TH ANNUAL GENERAL MEETING OF VERITAS KAPITAL ASSURANCE
PLC
At the 49th Annual General Meeting (“AGM”) of Veritas Kapital Assurance Plc (“The Company”) duly convened
and held virtually via www.veritaskapital.com on Friday, 16th October, 2026 at 11.00 am the following resolutions
were dul y proposed and passed:
ORDINAR Y BUSINESS
1. That the Audited Financial Statements for the financial year ended December 31, 2025, and the Reports of
the Directors, the Auditor and Audit Committee thereon laid before the meeting be and are hereby received.
2. That the Directors be authorized to fix the remuneration of the External Auditors, Messrs Ernest & Young.
3. That the following Shareholders’ Representatives were elected as members of the Statutory Audit
Committee of the Company for the 2026 financial year:
a. Mr. Olusegun James, Akintunde
b . Mal. Usman Abaji
c. Mal. Muhammed B. Alhassan
The following Non-Executive Directors were nominated to represent the Board on the Statutory audit
Committee:
a. Mr. Emmanuel Etuh
b. Mr. Paul Oki
4. That the following Non-Executive Directors retiring by rotation be and are hereby re-elected:
a. Mr. Babatunde Irukera
b. Mr. Paul Oki
c. Mr. Emmanuel Etuh
5. That the remuneration of the Managers of the Company, as contained in the Audited Financial Statements
for the 2025 financial year, be and is hereby disclosed.
SPECIAL BUSINESS
6. That the Non-Executive Directors of the Company be and hereby authorized to fix their remuneration for
the year ending December 31, 2026.
7. That the appointment of Planet Governance Advisory Limited as Board Evaluation Consultant of the Company
be and is hereby approved.
Thank you.
FOR: VERITAS KAPITAL ASSURANCE PLC
SARATU UMAR GARBA
COMPANY SECRETARY
