LAGOS,
17th June 2026
RESOLUTIONS PASSED AT THE 35TH ANNUAL GENERAL MEETING OF C&I LEASING
PLC
At the 35th Annual General Meeting of C&I Leasing Plc (the Company) held virtually on the 15th day of
June 2026 at 1.00 pm, the following resolutions were proposed, considered and duly passed by the
shareholders of the Company:
ORDINARY BUSINESS
1. ADOPTION OF AUDITED FINANCIAL STATEMENTS
That the Audited Financial Statements for the year ended 31st December 2025, together with the Reports
of the Directors, Independent Auditors, and Audit Committee thereon, be and are hereby received and
considered.
2. DECLARATION OF DIVIEND
That a dividend of 20kobo per ordinary share be and is hereby approved and declared payable to
shareholders whose names appear in the Register of Members as at the qualification date for the
dividend.
3. RE-ELECTION OF DIRECTORS
(i) That Mr. Babatunde Edun, having retired by rotation and being eligible and offering himself
for re-election, be and is hereby re-elected as a Director of the Company.
(ii) That Omotunde Alao-Olaifa, having retired by rotation and being eligible and offering
himself for re-election, be and is hereby re-elected as a Director of the Company.
4. AUDITORS’ REMUNERATION
That the Directors be and are hereby authorized to determine and fix the remuneration of the Auditors
of the Company for the year ending 31st December 2026.
5. ELECTION OF AUDIT COMMITTEE MEMBERS
That Mr. Sulaiman B. Adenrele, Mr. Fredrick Olufemi Oduyemi, and Mrs. Christie O. Vincent-Uwalaka
be and are hereby elected as Shareholder Representatives on the Audit Committee for the financial year
ending 31st December 2026.
The Board representatives nominated to the Audit Committee shall be Mr. Omotunde Alao-Olaifa and
Mr. Oluyemi Peter Abaolu-Johnson.
6. REMUNERATION OF MANAGERS
That the remuneration of managers of the Company, as disclosed in the annual report, be and is hereby
approved.

SPECIAL BUSINESS
7. RATIFICATION OF APPOINTMENT OF NON-EXECUTIVE DIRECTORS
(i) That the appointment of Barrister Nick Onyebuchi Omeye as a Non-Executive Director of the
Company be and hereby ratified and confirmed.
(ii) That the appointment of Mr. Chima Njoku as a Non-Executive Director of the Company be and
hereby ratified and confirmed.
8. REMUNERATION OF NON-EXECUTIVE DIRECTORS
That the sum of Nine million Naira (N9,000,000) per annum for each Non-Executive Director and
Twelve million Naira (N12,000,000) per annum for the Chairman of the Board be and is hereby
approved as the remuneration of Non-Executive Directors for the year ending 31st December 2026.
9. GENERAL MANDATE FOR RECURRENT RELATED PARTY TRANSACTIONS
That the Company and its related entities be and are hereby granted a General Mandate in respect of all
recurrent transactions entered into with related parties or interested persons, provided that such
transactions are of a revenue or trading nature or are necessary for the Company’s day-to-day operations.
This mandate shall remain valid from the date of this resolution until the conclusion of the next Annual
General Meeting of the Company.
G. MBANUGO UDENZE
FOR: Mbanugo Udenze & Co.
