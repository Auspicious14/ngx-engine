Lagos, Nigeria, October 29, 2026
Nestlé Nigeria PLC Delivers Solid Revenue Growth, Improved Profitability and a Stronger Equity
Position for the Nine Months 2026
Nestlé Nigeria PLC has announced its financial results for the nine months ended 30 September 2026,
reporting double-digit revenue growth, improved profitability and a stronger equity position. Profit before
tax increased by 52% to ₦194 billion, compared with ₦128 billion in the corresponding period of 2025,
supported by sustained revenue growth, disciplined cost management, operational resilience and
improved Naira stability.
Financial Highlights: Jan – Sep 2026
• Revenue grew by 10.4% from ₦884.5 billion in 9M 2025 to ₦976.5 billion in 9M 2026
• Operating profit increased by 15% from ₦181.3 billion in 9M 2025 to ₦208.2 billion in 9M 2026
• Profit before tax rose by 52% from ₦128 billion in 9M 2025 to ₦194 billion in quoted period
• Profit after tax grew by 43% from ₦72.5 billion in 9M 2025 to ₦103.4 billion
• Total equity increased from ₦12.9 billion at the end of December 2025 to ₦116.3 billion at the
end of September 2026.

Commenting on the results, Mr. Wassim Elhusseini, CEO/Managing Director of Nestlé Nigeria PLC, said:
“Our performance in the last nine months of 2026 demonstrates continued progress in strengthening the
fundamentals of our business. Delivering our eighth consecutive quarter of profitability since returning to
profit in Q4 2024, together with revenue growth of 10.4% and Profit After Tax of ₦103.4 billion, reflects
the resilience of our brands, the discipline of our execution and the commitment of our people.”
Looking ahead, he added: “We remain optimistic about the outlook and will continue to pursue
sustainable, profitable growth with focus and prudence. Our priorities remain focused on deepening
consumer relevance through innovation and renovation, strengthening margin management, enhancing
efficiency across our value chain, and making targeted investments that support the long-term health of
our brands and business.
“At same time, we will continue to invest in our people and support the communities where we operate,
while remaining agile in responding to an evolving macroeconomic and geopolitical environment. We are
confident in our ability to navigate these dynamics responsibly, create sustainable value for our
shareholders and stakeholders, and make a positive contribution to the communities we serve.”

About Nestlé Nigeria:
Nestlé Nigeria is one of Africa’s largest food and beverage companies. For over 62 years, the company has
delivered high‑quality, nutritious food and beverages under brands including MAGGI, MILO, GOLDEN
MORN, NESTLÉ PURE LIFE, CERELAC, NESCAFÉ, and NIDO.
With three manufacturing sites, seven branch offices, and its head office in Lagos, the company continues
to help improve livelihoods, build thriving communities, and protect the planet.
