CHEMICAL AND ALLIED PRODUCTS PLC – NOTICE OF MANAGEMENT CHANGES
Chemical and Allied Products PLC (the “Company” or “CAP PLC”), a subsidiary of UAC of
Nigeria PLC (“UAC”), hereby notifies Nigerian Exchange Limited, its shareholders and the
investing public of the following changes within the Company arising from the resolution of
Board of Directors (the “Board”) of June 24, 2026:
1. APPOINTMENT OF EXECUTIVE DIRECTOR
Mr. Jethro Iruobe ("Mr. Iruobe") has been appointed as an Executive Director of the
Company with effect from June 25, 2026. Mr. Iruobe currently serves as the Company's
Chief Operating Officer, with responsibility for the Human Resources, Corporate
Communications, Fleet Management and Commercial Excellence functions. In his
capacity as Executive Director, Mr. Iruobe will participate in the formulation and
execution of the Company's strategic priorities, provide executive leadership across
critical functions, and contribute to the continued growth, sustainability and success of
the Company.
PROFILE OF MR. JETHRO IRUOBE
Mr. Iruobe joined CAP PLC in 2019 as Head of Human Resources before growing to
becoming the Chief Operating Officer with responsibility for Human Resources,
Corporate Communications, Administration, Fleet and Commercial Excellence. He has
over nineteen (19) years of experience across a range of industries, including financial
services, consulting, FMCG and manufacturing, having begun his career in Banking,
before kicking off his HR career at KPMG and went on to hold senior roles at Promasidor
Nigeria, Coca-Cola HBC and TGI Nigeria. Throughout his career, he has supported
organisations in building high-performing teams and driving sustainable business
growth. Beyond his corporate responsibilities, he is a columnist with Business Day
Newspaper, a trained relationship counsellor and co-author, and a recipient of awards
in both the human resources profession and other platforms.
Mr. Iruobe holds a Bachelor's degree in Mathematics and Economics from the University
of Benin and a Master of Business Administration (MBA) from the Manchester Business
School, United Kingdom. He is a certified Project Management Professional (PMP) and
also holds the Global Professional in Human Resources (GPHR) certification awarded
by the Human Resource Certification Institute.

2. CHANGE OF COMPANY SECRETARY
Mrs. Aramide Nwokediuko will be stepping down from her position as Company
Secretary of the Company with effect from July 13, 2026, to take up a new executive
role within the UAC of Nigeria PLC Group.
Further to the above, Mr. Olutola Bella (“Mr. Bella”) has been appointed as the
Company’s new Company Secretary effective from July 13, 2026. Mr. Bella currently
serves as the Company’s General Counsel and while functioning as both the General
Counsel and Company Secretary, he will provide guidance to the Board and
Management on corporate governance matters and ensure the Company’s
compliance with all applicable regulatory, legal and governance obligations.
PROFILE OF MR. OLUTOLA BELLA
Mr. Bella has over eighteen (18) years of extensive experience as a trusted legal advisor
to business leaders and organisations across diverse sectors. Prior to joining CAP PLC,
Mr. Bella was a Principal Adviser at DealWorks Advisory, a specialist advisory firm
focused on growth-driven businesses in Africa. Previously, he served as Deputy Chief
Legal Officer and General Counsel for Sub-Saharan Africa at Moove, a mobility fintech.
In these roles, he built and scaled the company’s Legal and Compliance teams and
supported the company’s expansion to new markets.
Prior to joining Moove, Mr. Bella was Head of Corporate & Investment Banking (CIB)
Legal at Stanbic IBTC Holdings PLC where he advised Executive Management on risk
and governance matters and led the team that provided legal advisory and
transactional support to the Bank’s CIB business. Mr. Bella’s early career at Banwo &
Ighodalo and Aluko & Oyebode provided the bedrock for his professional expertise.
Mr. Bella was admitted to the Nigerian Bar in 2007. He obtained his Bachelor of Law
(LLB) and Master of Law (LLM) degrees at Obafemi Awolowo University and the
University of Cambridge, respectively. He is a member of several professional bodies
and has attended numerous leadership programmes and earned executive
certifications throughout his career.
The Board congratulates the new appointees and is confident that they will make significant
contributions to the continued growth and success of the Company.
