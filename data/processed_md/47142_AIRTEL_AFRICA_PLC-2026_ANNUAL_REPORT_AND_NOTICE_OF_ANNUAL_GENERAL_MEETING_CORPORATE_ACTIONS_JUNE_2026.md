Airtel Africa plc
(’Airtel Africa’, or the ‘Company’)
2026 Annual Report and Notice of Annual General Meeting
London and Lagos, 9 June 2026: Airtel Africa, a leading provider of
telecommunications and mobile money services, with a presence in 14 countries
across Africa, today announces that its Annual Report and Accounts for the year ended 31
March 2026 (‘Annual Report’), Notice of Annual General Meeting (‘AGM’) and Form of Proxy
for the AGM have each been sent or made available to shareholders under the cover of a
letter from our Chair, Mr. Sunil Bharti Mittal.
The Annual Report and Notice of AGM are available to view or download from the
Company's website at www.airtel.africa/annual-reports
The Company's AGM will be held at 11.00am (UK time) on 9 July 2026 at First Floor, 15
Davies Street, London, W1K 3DE, United Kingdom and electronically via the
Computershare platform.
In accordance with Listing Rule 6.4.1, and Disclosure Guidance and Transparency Rules
(DTR) 4.1, 6.2 and 6.3.5, Airtel Africa plc will shortly be submitting its Annual Report (which
will be available in unedited full text and structured electronic format), the Notice of AGM
and other shareholder documents, to the National Storage Mechanism and they will be
available for inspection shortly at
https://data.fca.org.uk/#/nsm/nationalstoragemechanism
For further details, visit www.airtel.africa/annual-reports

About Airtel Africa
Airtel Africa is a leading provider of telecommunications and mobile money services, with
operations in 14 countries in sub-Saharan Africa. Airtel Africa provides an integrated offer
to its subscribers, including mobile voice and data services as well as mobile money
services both nationally and internationally. The company’s strategy is focused on
providing a great customer experience across the entire footprint, enabling our corporate
purpose of transforming lives across Africa.
