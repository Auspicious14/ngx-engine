CUTIX PLC – OUTCOME OF MEETING OF THE BOARD OF
DIRECTORS
Cutix Plc (“the Company”) wishes to notify the Nigerian Exchange Limited (“The
Exchange”), our esteemed Shareholders and the general public that at the
rescheduled Board of Directors meeting of the Company held on Monday, July 13,
2026, the Board approved the Directors Report and Audited Financial Statements of
the Company for the year ended April 30, 2026 subject to some amendments.
The approved Audited Financial Statements will be released to the public on or
before July 29, 2026. However, the Closed Period earlier announced by the
Company still subsists until twenty-four (24) hours after the Audited Financial
Statements is submitted to The Exchange.
