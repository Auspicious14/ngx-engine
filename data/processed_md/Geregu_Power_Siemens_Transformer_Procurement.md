Abuja, Nigeria
October 23, 2026
GEREGU POWER PLC AND SIEMENS ENERGY ANNOUNCE THE PROCUREMENT OF THREE GENERATOR
TRANSFORMERS
Geregu Power Plc (“Geregu or the Company”) and Siemens Energy have signed an agreement for
the supply of three (3) generator transformers for the Geregu Power Plant in Ajaokuta, Kogi State,
reinforcing the Company’s commitment to operational excellence and long-term asset reliability.
The transformers will serve the plant’s three (3) generating units, GT11, GT12 and GT13, and are
expected to enhance equipment reliability, reduce the risk of transformer-related outages and
strengthen the reliability of the plant’s connection to the national grid.
Under the agreement, Siemens Energy will be responsible for the design, manufacture, test and
delivery of the transformers. Geregu Power Plc will also participate in the Factory Acceptance
Testing prior to shipment to Nigeria.
The procurement forms part of Geregu’s continued investment in the maintenance, modernisation
and improvement of its generating assets. The initiative is aimed at strengthening critical plant
infrastructure, operational reliability, minimising the risk of equipment failure, and supporting the safe
and reliable operation of the plant.
The agreement also builds on the existing relationship between Geregu and Siemens Energy. The
Geregu Power Plant currently operates three Siemens V94.2 gas turbine generator units with a
combined installed capacity of 435 megawatts.
Through strategic investments in critical infrastructure, Geregu remains committed to improving its
operations and supporting the delivery of reliable electricity to Nigeria’s national grid.
About Geregu Power Plc
Incorporated in 2006, Geregu commenced operations in 2007. The Company operates the Geregu
Power Plant in Ajaokuta, Kogi State, comprising three gas turbine generator units with a combined
installed capacity of 435 megawatts.
As the first power generation company listed on the Main Board of the Nigerian Exchange, Geregu
continues to play a leading role in Nigeria’s power sector and remains committed to supporting the
country’s sustainable economic growth through the provision of reliable electricity generation.
