Lagos, 15 April 2026
NOTICE OF DELAY IN FILING Q1 2026 UNAUDITED FINANCIAL STATEMENTS
C&I Leasing Plc (‘’ the Company’’) hereby notifies Nigerian Exchange Limited and the
investing public of the likely delay in filing its Q1 2026 Unaudited Financial Statements for
the period ended 30 April 2026.
This request is due to pending regulatory approval of the December 2025 Audited Financial
Statements from our primary regulator, Central Bank of Nigeria.
Considering the above, the company has received the approval of NGX Regulation Limited to
submit the Q1 2026 Unaudited Financial Statement on or before 29 June 2026.
We appreciate the patience and understanding of all stakeholders during this period and
reaffirm our dedication to upholding the principles of good governance.
For: C&I Leasing Plc
MBANUGO UDENZE & CO
Company Secretary
