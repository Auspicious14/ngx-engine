NGX Ticker: CAP
PRESS RELEASE
Regulated Information
Unaudited Financial Results for the Period Ended 30 September 2026
Lagos, 28 October 2026 – Chemical and Allied Products Plc (“CAP” or the “Company”), Nigeria’s leading
paints and coatings company, today announced its unaudited results for the period ended 30 September
2026.
Commenting on the results, Managing Director, Olalekan Aluko, stated:
“We are pleased to report a strong performance for the first nine months of 2026, with revenue growing by
11% year-on-year and operating profit and profit before tax each increasing by 21%. These results reflect the
disciplined execution of our strategic growth initiatives, sustained investment in our people, and a continued
focus on operational efficiency. As we enter the final quarter, we remain committed to delivering profitable
growth, strengthening our organisational capabilities, and further enhancing the experience we offer our
customers.”
9M 2026 Highlights
• N33.7billion revenue, 11% higher than 9M 2025.
• Gross profit at N14.4billion, 11% higher than 9M 2025.
• Operating profit at N5.8billion, 21% higher than 9M 2025.
• Profit before tax of N6.6billion, up 21% from N5.5billion in 9M 2025.

About CAP Plc
Chemical and Allied Products Plc (CAP) is a leading paints and coatings company in Nigeria with globally
recognised brands such as Dulux, Sandtex, Caplux and Hempel. CAP manufactures and sells decorative
paints and industrial coatings. CAP is the sole technological licensee of Akzo Nobel Coatings International
B.V. and is the Nigerian representative of Hempel Paints South Africa (Pty) Limited. CAP pioneered the colour
centre concept in Nigeria in 2005, which resulted in the evolution of the Nigerian paint industry. CAP is a
public company listed on The Nigerian Exchange (“NGX”). It is a subsidiary of UAC of Nigeria PLC which holds
57.85% of the company's shares. For more information visit https://www.capplc.com/
