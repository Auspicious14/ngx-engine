LAGOS, JUNE 30, 2026,
NOTIFICATION OF CLOSED PERIOD
This is to inform Nigerian Exchange Limited (NGX) and our esteemed shareholders that in
compliance with the provisions of Rule 17.18(a): Closed Period, Rulebook of NGX, 2015 (Issuers’
Rule) as amended, Linkage Assurance Plc (the Company) will commence a Closed Period from
Wednesday 1st of July 2026 until twenty-four (24) hours after the 2026 Q2 Unaudited Financial
Statements for the period ending 30th June 2026 is released to the public via NGX’s Issuers’
Portal.
Within this period, all those who are considered as “insiders” of the Company in accordance with
the provisions of the Rules of NGX are under restriction not to trade or deal in the shares of the
Company.
