Tuesday, 20th October 2026.
Lagos, Nigeria.
OUTCOME OF MEETING OF BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF BUA CEMENT
PLC
BUA Cement Plc ("the Company") wishes to notify Nigerian Exchange Limited ("NGX")
and the investing public that the meeting of the Board of Directors of BUA Cement Plc
was held as scheduled today, Tuesday, 20th October 2026.
The Board considered and approved the Unaudited Financial Statements for the third
quarter ended 30th September 2026. The Unaudited Financial Statements will be
submitted to NGX via its Issuers' Portal on or before 30th October 2026.
Accordingly, the Closed Period remains in force until twenty-four (24) hours after the
filing of the Financial Statements.
For: BUA Cement Plc
Hauwa Satomi
Company Secretary/Chief Legal Officer
