Q3 2026 UNAUDITED RESULTS
Nascon Allied Industries Plc
29th October 2026

UNAUDITED RESULTS FOR NINE MONTHS ENDED 30 SEPTEMBER 2026
Gross profit up 4% at ₦59.4B
EBITDA up 9% at ₦40.1B
PAT up 18% at ₦28.7B
Revenue at ₦116.9B
Lagos, 29 October 2026: Nascon Allied Industries Plc announces unaudited results for the
period ended 30 September 2026.
Financial highlights
• Revenue at ₦116.9B
• Gross profit up 4% to ₦59.4B; 51% margin
• EBITDA up 9% to ₦40.1B; 34% margin
• Profit before tax up 19% to ₦43.5B; 37% margin
• Profit after tax up 18% to ₦28.7B
• Earnings per share up 18% at ₦14.61
• Cash Position up 47% at ₦62.3B
Aderemi Saka, Managing Director, said:
I am pleased to present Nascon Allied Industries Plc's unaudited financial results for the nine months
ended 30 September 2026.
Our YTD performance reflects the resilience of our business, the effectiveness of our operating strategy,
and our continued focus on delivering sustainable value despite a challenging business environment.
While revenue remained stable at ₦116.9 billion compared to prior year, improved operational efficiency
and disciplined cost management enabled us to achieve stronger profitability and margin expansion.
Gross profit increased by 4% to ₦59.4 billion, with gross margin improving to 51% from 49% in the prior
year period. This performance reflects the benefits of our cost optimization initiatives, enhanced
production efficiencies, and a continued focus on value creation across our product portfolio.
Profit before tax rose by 19% to ₦43.5 billion, while profit after tax increased by 18% to ₦28.7 billion.
Earnings per share improved by 18% to ₦14.16, demonstrating our ability to convert operational
performance into stronger returns for shareholders.
Our financial position remains robust. Cash and cash equivalents increased by 47% to ₦62.3 billion, while
net assets grew by 35% to ₦83.7 billion. This strong liquidity position enhances our flexibility to support
growth initiatives, strengthen operational resilience, and pursue opportunities that create long-term
value for stakeholders.
Looking ahead, we remain focused on sustaining operational excellence, strengthening our leadership
position in the salt and seasoning market, optimizing our route-to-market strategy, and delivering
profitable growth. Supported by our strong balance sheet, trusted brands, and dedicated workforce, we
are well positioned to close the year strongly and continue creating sustainable value for shareholders
and other stakeholders.

About Nascon Allied Industries Plc
Nascon Allied Industries Plc is Nigeria’s leading refiner and distributor of salt for household, food processing,
and industrial applications. The company has since diversified its operations to include seasoning production. It
has facilities in Lagos State (Apapa), Ogun State (Ota), and Rivers State (Port Harcourt).
The Apapa refinery, located in the Apapa Port of Lagos, was commissioned in 2001. The Port Harcourt
refinery located in the seaport in Rivers State was commissioned in 2003. The Oregun plant was
commissioned in 2004 to produce refined salt. Oregun plant was converted to a fleet workshop in 2021 with
the commissioning of the new state-of-the-art refinery at Salt Village, Apapa.
We made a strategic decision in 2011 to grow the company through new product lines. We took advantage of
our existing site in Ota and we commissioned a state-of-the-art Seasoning plant in 2014. Our plants are
primarily powered through the National Grid with generators fueled by gas or diesel, with combined capacity
to generate 6.1MW of power.
