Director/PDMR Shareholdings
London and Lagos, 1 July 2026: Airtel Africa announces the following changes to the interests of
PDMRs in the Company's ordinary shares of $0.50 each ("Ordinary Shares").
On 26 June 2026 (the "Grant Date"), the Company granted the following conditional share awards
over Ordinary Shares under the terms of the Airtel Africa Long-Term Incentive Plan ("Awards") to
the following PDMRs at nil cost. The Awards were granted using a share price of £3.439, being the
average middle market quotation of a share over the period of three dealing days ending 25 June
2026.
Awards
Name No. of Ordinary Shares No. of Ordinary Shares No. of Ordinary Shares
under Performance under Restricted Share under Deferred Bonus
Share Awards Awards Shares Awards
Sunil Taldar 418,827 110,218 79,748
Kamal Dua 128,381 32,095 25,075
The number of Ordinary Shares set out above is the maximum number which may vest under the
Awards, normally following the third anniversary of the Grant Date save for the Deferred Bonus
Shares Awards which will normally vest on the second anniversary of the Grant Date. For the
Performance Share Awards and Restricted Share Awards, the actual number of Ordinary Shares
which vest will depend on the extent to which performance conditions relating to the Awards have
been satisfied.
The performance conditions applicable to the Performance Share Awards will be based on
Compound annual growth in Net Revenue (CAGR %) over three financial years commencing 1 April
2026, Increase in Underlying EBITDA Margin and total shareholder return (TSR) ranking against
MSCI Emerging Markets Communications Service Index.
The performance underpin applicable to the Restricted Share Awards will be based on the
Company's Operating Free Cash Flow.
The Awards are subject to a holding period which will run until the fifth anniversary of the Grant Date.
This notification is made in accordance with Article 19(3) of the UK Market Abuse Regulation.
