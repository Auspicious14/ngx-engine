Lagos, Nigeria, 28th July 2026
MAY & BAKER NIGERIA PLC - NOTICE OF OUTCOME OF BOARD MEETING
May & Baker Nigeria Plc (the Company) wishes to inform the Nigerian Exchange Limited
(NGX or The Exchange) and the investing public that at the Board of Directors’ Meeting
held on Tuesday 28th July 2026, the Board approved the Unaudited Financial Statements
of the Company for the second quarter ended 30th June 2026 (2026, Q2 UFS) and the same
will be filed on or before 30th July 2026.
Therefore, the closed period earlier announced to prevent Insiders from trading in the
shares of the Company shall remain until twenty-four (24) hours after the 2026, Q2 UFS
is released to the public.
This is in line with the rules of NGX. The Securities Trading Policy of the Company can
be found and downloaded on our website: www.may-bakerng.com. If you have any
questions regarding this policy or the applicability of the policy to you or your activities,
please email us at financials@may-bakerng.com.
