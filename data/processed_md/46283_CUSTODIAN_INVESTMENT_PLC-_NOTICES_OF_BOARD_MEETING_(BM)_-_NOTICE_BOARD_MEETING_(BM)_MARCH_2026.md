Lagos, Nigeria, Monday, March 17, 2026
NOTIFICATION OF BOARD MEETING AND CLOSED PERIOD - CUSTODIAN INVESTMENT PLC
In compliance with the post-listing requirements of Nigerian Exchange Limited, Notice is hereby
given that a meeting of the Board of Directors of Custodian Investment Plc (“the Company”)
will hold on Friday, March 27, 2026, at 1:00pm, to deliberate on the Audited Consolidated
Financial Statements for the year ended December 31, 2025 (2025 AFS), amongst other things.
Consequently, the Company has entered a closed period from January 1, 2026, until twenty-
four (24) hours after the Company’s 2025 AFS is released to Nigerian Exchange Limited and the
general public.
Accordingly, no Insider of Custodian Investment Plc may directly or indirectly deal in the
products or securities of the Company in any manner during the Closed Period.
