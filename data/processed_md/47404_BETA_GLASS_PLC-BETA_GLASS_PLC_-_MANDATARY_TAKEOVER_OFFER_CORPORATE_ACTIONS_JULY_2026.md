5. 1 BACKGROUND TO THE TAKE-OVER OFFER
Emerald HoldCo B.V. (“Emerald HoldCo” or the “Acquirer”) recently completed the acquisition of 100%
of the shares of Emerald Nigeria Intermediate Holdings B.V. (formerly Frigoinvest Nigeria Holding B.V)
who owns 76.03% of Packaging Industries Nigeria Limited (formerly Frigoglass Industries (Nigeria)
