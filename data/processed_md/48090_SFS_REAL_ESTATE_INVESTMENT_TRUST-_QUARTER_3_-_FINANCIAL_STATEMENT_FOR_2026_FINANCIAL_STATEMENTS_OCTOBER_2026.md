1 Reporting entity
SFS Real Estate Investment Trust Fund (“SFS REIT” or “the Trust”) is a fund constituted under the laws of Nigeria.
The address of the Trust’s registered office is Plot 287, Ajose Adeogun Street, Victoria Island, Lagos. It is governed
by a Trust Deed approved by the Securities and Exchange Commission, and its units are listed on the Nigerian
Exchange. Its financial statements are filed with the Securities and Exchange Commission and other regulatory
authorities
SFS REIT is a close-ended Real Estate Investment Trust, primarily involved in acquiring investment properties held
for rental income and capital appreciation.
The investment activities of the Trust are managed by SFS Capital Nigeria Limited (the Fund Manager), while Apel
Capital and Trust Limited are the Trustees.
