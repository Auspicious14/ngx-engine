Outcome of Meeting of the Board of Directors
At the Meeting of the Board of Directors of Omatek Ventures Plc. (the Company)
held on the 28th day of October, 2026, it was proposed to be resolved and was
accordingly resolved:
1. That the Third Quarter Unaudited Financial Statements for the period
ended September 30, 2026 (2026 Q3 UFS) presented to the Board is hereby
approved.
2. That the exit of Mr. Yemi Ogundipe, effective November 30, 2026, as the
Group Managing Director and Director of the Company is hereby
approved. The Board appreciates his leadership role in taking the Company
through 7 ½ years of receivership and ensuring against all odds that the
Company remains resilient and wish him well in his future endeavors.
3. That the appointment of Mr. Temidayo Seriki, a Director of the Company
as the Acting Group Managing Director is approved. He currently
represents the Estate of the largest investor and founder Late Engr. Dr
(Mrs) Florence Udebu Seriki FNSE, MFR on the Board of Omatek. He will
pilot the Company into the completion of discussions with prospective
investors in the Company.
