7th July 2026
Lagos, Nigeria
CHANGE OF REGISTERED ADDRESS
The Board of Directors of Geregu Power Plc (the "Company") hereby notifies
Nigerian Exchange Limited ("NGX"), its shareholders, and the investing public
that the Company has changed its registered address.
The Board has approved the change of the Company's registered address
from 6, Onega Street, Off Gana Street, Maitama, Abuja to 4 Dala Hills Street,
Maitama, Abuja, Federal Capital Territory, to enhance administrative and
operational efficiency.
Accordingly, shareholders, regulators, business partners, and the general
public are requested to update their records to reflect the Company's new
registered address.
By Order of the Board
