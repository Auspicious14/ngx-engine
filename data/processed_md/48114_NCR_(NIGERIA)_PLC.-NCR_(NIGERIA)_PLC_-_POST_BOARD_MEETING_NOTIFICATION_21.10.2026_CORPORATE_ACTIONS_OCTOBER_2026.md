NCR (NIGERIA) PLC – APPROVAL OF THE UNAUDITED FINANCIAL
STATEMENTS FOR THE THIRD QUARTER ENDED 30 SEPTEMBER 2026
This is to notify the investing public and Nigerian Exchange Limited that the Board of
Directors of NCR (Nigeria) PLC (the Company) held a meeting on Wednesday, 21
October 2026, to consider and approve, amongst other things, the Company’s
Unaudited Financial Statements for the third quarter ended 30 September 2026 (the
“Unaudited Financial Statements”). The accounts will be filed with the Nigerian
Exchange Limited on or before 30 October 2026.
Please note that the closed period prohibiting the Company’s Insiders from transacting
in the shares of the Company, which commenced on 30 September 2026, will remain
until twenty-four (24) hours after the Company’s Unaudited Financial Statements for
the third quarter ended 30 September 2026 have been released to the public.
