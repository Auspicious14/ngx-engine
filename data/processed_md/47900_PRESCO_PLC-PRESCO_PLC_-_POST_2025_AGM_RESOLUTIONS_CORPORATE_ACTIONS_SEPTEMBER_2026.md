PRESCO PLC
2025 ANNUAL GENERAL MEETING RESOLUTIONS
Benin City, Nigeria – September 15, 2025 – Leading agro-industrial company, Presco Plc.
(Bloomberg: PRESCO:NL | Reuters: PRESCO.LG | NGX: PRESCO) held its 2025 Annual General Meeting
today 15 September 2026.
At the Annual General Meeting of PRESCO PLC (‘the Company’) held virtually on Tuesday, 15
September 2026, the following resolutions were passed:
1. “That the dividend of N14.66 per 50 kobo share amounting to the sum of N17,098,845,500.00
(Seventeen Billion, Ninety-Eight Million, Eight Hundred and Forty-Five Thousand, Five
Hundred Naira) payable from the profit of the year ended 31 December 2025, subject to the
deduction of withholding tax at the appropriate rate, be and is hereby declared.”
2. “That Mr. Olakanmi Rasheed Sarumi, Mr. Abdul Akhor Bello, and Ms. Osayi Alile, be and are
hereby re-elected as Directors of the Company.”
3. “That Mr. Ademola Adebise, Mr. Adewale Arikawe and Mr. Francois Van Hoydonck (Belgian)
be and are hereby elected Directors of the Company”.
4. “That the Directors be and are hereby authorized to fix the remuneration of the Independent
Auditors, KPMG for the year ending 31 December 2026”.
5. “The following persons were elected to represent the shareholders on the Statutory Audit
Committee until the next Annual General Meeting:
a) Engr. M.O.T Olayiwola Tobun
b) Mr. Job Ihejirika Onwughara
c) Mr. Adenrele Sulaimon Babatunde
While the following Directors were nominated to represent the Board on the Statutory Audit
Committee
d) Mr. Abdul Akhor Bello
e) Ambassador Nonye Udo

6. “That the remunerations of the Non-Executive Directors in the sum of N349,020,000 for the
year ending 31 December 2026 (in addition to sitting allowance in the sum of N56,378,000,
which will be paid per attendance at meetings) be and are hereby approved”.
7. “That in compliance with the Rule of the Nigerian Exchange Limited governing transactions
with Related Parties or Interested Persons, the Company, and its related entities be and are
hereby granted a General Mandate in respect of all recurrent transactions entered with a
related party or interested person provided such transactions are of a revenue or trading
nature or are necessary for the Company's day-to-day operations and are based on normal
commercial terms”.
By the Order of the Board
Frederick Ichekwai
Company Secretary
