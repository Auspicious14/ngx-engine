Corporate Actions Announcement
Haldane McCall PLC hereby announce as follows:
Period Ended 31st December 2025
A Final Dividend of 5kobo for every share of 50kobo per ordinary shares, subject to
appropriate withholding tax and approval will be paid to shareholders whose names
appear in the Register of Members as at the close of business on Thursday 28th day
Proposed Dividend of May, 2026.
Proposed Bonus NIL
The Register of Shareholders will be closed from Friday, May 29, 2026 -Thursday
Closure of Register June 4, 2026.
Qualification Date Thursday, May 28, 2026.
On June 18, 2026, dividends will be paid electronically to shareholders whose names
appear on the Register of Members as May 28, 2026 - Thursday June 4, 2026 and
who have completed the e-dividend registration and mandated the Registrar to pay
Payment Date their dividends directly into their Bank accounts.
Shareholders who are yet to complete the e-dividend registration are advised to
download the Registrar’s E-Dividend Mandate Activation Form, which is also
available on the website of the Registrars: www.africaprudential.com complete and
E-Dividend Registration submit to the Registrar or their respective Banks.
Unclaimed Dividend Shareholders with dividend warrants and share certificates that have remained
Warrants and Share unclaimed, or are yet to be presented for payment or returned for validation are
Certificates advised to complete the e-dividend registration or contact the Registrar.
