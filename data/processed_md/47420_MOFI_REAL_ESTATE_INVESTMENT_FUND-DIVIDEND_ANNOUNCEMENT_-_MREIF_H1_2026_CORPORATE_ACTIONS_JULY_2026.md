Corporate Actions Announcement
ARM Investment Managers Ltd -: For MOFI Real Estate Investment Fund (“MREIF”) H1 2026
Period Ended 30 June 2026
A Dividend of ₦2.8022 per unit for the Series I investor and ₦8.6136 per unit
for Series II investors, subject to appropriate withholding tax and approval
will be paid to shareholders whose names appear in the Register of
Proposed Dividend Members as at the close of business on the 10th July 2026.
Qualification Date 10 July 2026.
Closure of Register The Register of Shareholders will be closed from 13 July 2026.
On 15 July 2026, dividends will be paid electronically to shareholders whose
names appear on the Register of Members as at 10 July 2026, and who have
completed the e-dividend registration and mandated the Registrar to pay
Payment Date their dividends directly into their Bank accounts.
Shareholders who are yet to complete the e-dividend registration are
advised to download the Registrar’s E-Dividend Mandate Activation Form,
E-Dividend which is also available on our website: [https://bit.ly/E-MANDATE_FORM],
Registration complete and submit to the Registrar or their respective Banks.
