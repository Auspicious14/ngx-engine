VERITA S KAPITAL ASSURANCE PLC.

stakeh olders, and the general public of the retirement of the following Non-

1. Hajia Yabawa Lawan Wabi mni
2. Mrs. Priya Heal
3. Dr. Oluwafunsho Obasanjo
4. M r. Aminu Babangida

endeavours.

announced in due course.
