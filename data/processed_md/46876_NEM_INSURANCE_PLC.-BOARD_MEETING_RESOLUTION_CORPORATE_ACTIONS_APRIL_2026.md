Lagos, Nigeria

APPROVAL OF FIRST QUARTER UNAUDITED ACCOUNTS FOR THE PERIOD ENDED 31ST
MARCH 2026.
This is to notify Nigeria Exchange Limited (“the Exchange”), our stakeholders and the investing public
that the Board of NEM Insurance Plc (“the Company”) met on Wednesday, 29th April 2026 and
approved the First Quarter Unaudited Accounts for the period ended 31 March 2026.
Consequently, the Closed Period which commenced on 1st January 2026 will continue until twenty-
four (24) hours after the First Quarter Unaudited Accounts have been released and filed via the
Exchange’s Issuers’ Portal.
Accordingly, no insider of the Company and/or their connected persons may, directly or indirectly
deal with/in the shares of the Company during the Closed Period.
