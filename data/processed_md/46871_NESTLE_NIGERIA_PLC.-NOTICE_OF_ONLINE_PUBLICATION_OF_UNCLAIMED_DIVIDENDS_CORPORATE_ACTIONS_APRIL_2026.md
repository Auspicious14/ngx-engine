NOTICE TO SHAREHOLDERS OF NESTLĖ NIGERIA PLC
ON THE PUBLICATION
OF THE LIST OF UNCLAIMED DIVIDENDS
In line with the provisions of section 429(1) of the Companies and Allied Matters Act 2020 requiring the
publication of the list of Unclaimed Dividends in two national newspapers, the list of Unclaimed Dividends of
Nestlé Nigeria PLC will be published in the following newspapers:
Punch (online version)
BusinessDay (online version)
We request our shareholders to visit the online editions of these newspapers on 7 May 2026 to access the
list of Unclaimed Dividends. They can also download and save the list for future reference.
The list can also be accessed on our investors’ portal: www.nestle-cwa.com/en/investors/nigeria
