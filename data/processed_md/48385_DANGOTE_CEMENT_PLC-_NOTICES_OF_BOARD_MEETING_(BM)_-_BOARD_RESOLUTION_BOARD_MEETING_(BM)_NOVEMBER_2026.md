10 November 2026
Lagos, Nigeria
DANGOTE CEMENT PLC – OUTCOME OF BOARD MEETING
This is to inform the Nigerian Exchange Limited (NGX), the investing public and all
stakeholders that:
At the meeting of the Board of Directors of Dangote Cement PLC (the "Company"), held
today, 10 November 2026, the Board resolved that, in light of current market conditions
and the Company’s strategic priorities, the Company announces that it and its selling
shareholder, Dangote Industries Limited (DIL) will not proceed with the proposed
secondary listing and offering on the London Stock Exchange at this time.
The Company's ordinary shares remain listed on the NGX, and this decision does not in
any way affect the Company's operations or business plans. Dangote Cement PLC
remains focused on delivering sustainable value to its shareholders and building on its
strong market position.
Edward Imoedemhe
Company Secretary
