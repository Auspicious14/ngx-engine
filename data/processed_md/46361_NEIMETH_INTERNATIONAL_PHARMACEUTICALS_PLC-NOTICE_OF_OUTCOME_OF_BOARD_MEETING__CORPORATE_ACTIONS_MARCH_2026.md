Lagos, Nigeria, 27th March 2026
NEIMETH INTERNATIONAL PHARMACEUTICALS PLC - NOTICE OF OUTCOME OF BOARD
MEETING
Neimeth International Pharmaceuticals Plc. (the Company) wishes to inform the Nigerian Exchange
Limited (NGX) and the investing public that at the Board of Directors’ meeting held on Friday, 27th
March 2026, the Board approved the Company’s Audited Financial Statements for the year ended
31 December 2025 (2025 AFS) and same will be filed on or before 31st March 2026.
Therefore, the closed period earlier announced on trading in the Company’s shares by Insiders and
connected persons shall remain until twenty-four (24) after the 2025 AFS is released to the public.
