Lagos, Nigeria July 27th, 2026
NOTICE OF BOARD MEETING
In line with the Rulebook of the Nigerian Exchange Limited (NGX), the Board of Directors of Aradel
Holdings Plc (the “Company”) hereby notifies the NGX and the investing public that a meeting
of the Board of Directors of the Company has been scheduled to hold on Wednesday, 29th of July
2026.
Among items slated for discussion at the meeting is to consider and approve the Unaudited
Financial Statements for the period ended 30th June 2026 (“Q2 2026 UFS”).
Consequently, the closed period for trading in the Company’s shares will continue until twenty-
four (24) hours after the Q2 2026 UFS, have been released on the floor of NGX. Therefore, no
insider or their connected persons are permitted to deal in the securities of the Company during
the closed period.
By order of the Board.
