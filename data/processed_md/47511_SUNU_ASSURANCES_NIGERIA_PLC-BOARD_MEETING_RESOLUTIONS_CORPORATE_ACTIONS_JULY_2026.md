PRESS RELEASE
Lagos, Nigeria 22nd July 2026
RESOLUTIONS OF THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF SUNU ASSURANCES NIGERIA PLC
In line with the Rules of Nigerian Exchange Limited (NGX) for listed companies relating to Board Meetings and
General Meetings of Issuers, SUNU Assurances Nigeria Plc (the Company) wishes to inform its valued
shareholders and the general public that the Board of Directors at its meeting held on Wednesday, 22nd July
2026 considered and approved the Company’s Unaudited Financial Statement for the half year ended 30th June
2026 (2026 UFS Q2).
The 2026 UFS Q2 would be filed on or before 30th July 2026 in line with the rules of NGX .
The Closed Period which commenced on 1st July, 2026 will remain in effect until twenty-Four (24) hours after
the 2026 UFS Q2 have been made available to the general public.
