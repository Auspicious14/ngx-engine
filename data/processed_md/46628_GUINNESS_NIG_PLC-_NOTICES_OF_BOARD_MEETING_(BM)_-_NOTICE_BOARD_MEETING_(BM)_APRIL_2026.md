LAGOS, 9TH APRIL 2026
UPDATED NOTIFICATION OF THE MEETING OF THE BOARD OF
DIRECTORS OF GUINNESS NIGERIA PLC
This is to update our notice to the Nigerian Exchange Limited (NGX) and the Market that the
meeting of the Board of Directors of Guinness Nigeria Plc (the Company) earlier scheduled
for Wednesday, 15th April 2026, has now been moved forward to hold on Monday, 13th April
2026 due to circumstances beyond our control.
In view of the change of date, the closed period for trading in the Company’s shares by
insiders which commenced on 1st April 2026 continues and will still expire 24 hours after
the Unaudited Financial Statements for the period ending 31st March 2026, is released to the
public.
Therefore, no insider of the Company and their connected persons may directly or indirectly
deal in the securities of the Company during the closed period.
The results for the period as approved by the Board will thereafter be uploaded through
the Exchange’s Issuers’ Portal not later than 14th April 2026.
For: Guinness Nigeria Plc.
Abimbola Ajibola-Jimoh
Company Secretary
