NOTIFICATION OF CHANGE IN DATE OF BOARD MEETING OF CUTIX PLC
Further to our earlier notification of June 26, 2026 and in line with the Post Listing Regulations
and Rules on release calendar of the Nigerian Exchange Limited (NGX) for listed companies,
Cutix Plc (the Company) hereby informs its Shareholders, NGX and the investing public that the
Board meeting earlier scheduled to hold on Wednesday, July 8, 2026, will now take place on
Monday, July 13, 2026 to consider amongst others things the Company’s Audited Financial
Statements for the year ended April 30, 2026.
Given the above, the Company also announces that the existing Closed Period which commenced
on May 1, 2026, still subsists until twenty-four (24) hours after the 2026 AFS is released to the
public. All those who are considered “insiders” of the Company in accordance with the provisions
of the Rules of The Exchange are under restriction not to trade or deal in the shares of the
Company within this closed period.
