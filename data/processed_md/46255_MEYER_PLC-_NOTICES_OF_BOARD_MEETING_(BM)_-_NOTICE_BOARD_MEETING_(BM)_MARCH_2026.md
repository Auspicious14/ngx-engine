Lagos, 10th March 2026
NOTICE OF BOARD MEETING AND CLOSED PERIOD
Meyer Plc (the Company) wishes to notify Nigerian Exchange Limited (NGX or The Exchange),
its shareholders and the investing public that the Company’s Board of Directors will meet on
Tuesday, 24th March 2026, for the consideration of the Audited Financial Statements for the
year ended 31st December 2025 (2025 AFS).
In view of the above and in compliance with the provision of Rule 17.18(a): Closed Period
Rulebook of the Exchange 2015 (Issuers Rule) as amended, the Company commenced the
closed period from Thursday the 1st of January 2026 for trading in its shares by insiders until
twenty-four (24) hours after the announcement of the Company’s 2025 AFS to The Exchange
and by extension the general public.
In line with the Amended Closed Period Rules, all insiders and their connected persons are
prohibited from trading (i.e., buying, selling, transferring or otherwise dealing) in the
Company’s shares until twenty-four (24) hours after the release of the 2025 AFS.
