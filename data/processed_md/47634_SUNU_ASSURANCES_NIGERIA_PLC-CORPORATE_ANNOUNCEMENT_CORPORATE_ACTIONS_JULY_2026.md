Lagos, 29 July 2026
NOTICE TO SHAREHOLDERS OF SUNU ASSURANCES NIGERIA PLC ON THE
PUBLICATION OF THE LIST OF UNCLAIMED DIVIDENDS
SUNU Assurances Nigeria Plc (the Company) wishes to inform its esteemed shareholders that
the Company’s unclaimed dividends list (“list”) has been published and available on the
Company’s website which can be accessed via this link: https://sunuassurancesnigeria.com/wp-
content/uploads/2026/07/UNCLAIMED-DIVIDEND-LIST-AS-AT-JULY-2026.pdf
The publication of the list on the Company’s website is in lieu of publishing it in the newspapers
due to the high number of names involved.
We advise all affected shareholders to please contact our Registrars, Crescent Registrars
Limited. Address: 23, Olusoji Idowu Street, Ilupeju, Lagos State or via the email address:
https://www.crescentregistrars with a view to claiming the dividends.
We remain committed to working with all relevant stakeholders to reduce the incidence of
unclaimed dividends in Nigeria.
In that regard, shareholders are kindly requested to update their records and advise Crescent
Registrars Limited of their updated information including their bank account details for payment
of their dividends.
Taiwo Kuku
Company Secretary.
