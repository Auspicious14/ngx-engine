APPOINTMENT OF MR. AZEEZ ADETAYO, FCA, AS ACTING CHIEF FINANCIAL
OFFICER
This is to notify Nigerian Exchange Limited ("NGX"), shareholders, and the investing public
that Mr. Azeez Adetayo, ACA, has been appointed as the Acting Chief Financial Officer
of VFD Group Plc ("VFD Group" or "the Company") effective immediately.
The appointment follows the transition of the Company's substantive Chief Financial Officer
and is intended to ensure continuity in financial leadership and the effective management of
the Group's finance function.
Mr. Adetayo previously served as Financial Controller (Senior Finance Manager) bringing
over a decade of experience spanning financial management, corporate reporting,
investment management, treasury management, sustainability reporting, internal controls,
and strategic finance.
A seasoned finance professional, he has successfully led finance transformation initiatives,
strengthened financial control frameworks, and supported business growth across multiple
sectors. With a career foundation built on diverse corporate experience and further elevated
by his time at Deloitte Nigeria, he possesses extensive expertise in financial strategy, capital
management, enterprise risk management, regulatory compliance, and stakeholder
reporting.
The Company is confident that Mr. Adetayo's experience and leadership will further
strengthen the finance function and support the execution of VFD Group's strategic
objectives.
This notification is made in compliance with the disclosure requirements of Nigerian
Exchange Limited.
