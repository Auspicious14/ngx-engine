HONEYWELL FLOUR MILLS PLC
Notes to the interim financial statements
for the period ended September 30, 2026
1 REPORTING ENTITY
Honeywell Flour Mills Plc was initially registered as Gateway Honeywell Flour Mills Limited on 21 June, 1983. A change in
the Company's ownership structure led to a change of the name to Honeywell Flour Mills Limited in June, 1995. The
Company was converted to a Public Liability Company in 2008. Its shares were listed on the Nigeria Stock Exchange (NSE)
in 2009.
Honeywell Flour Mills Plc is a company domiciled in Nigeria. The Group and Company are principally engaged in the
manufacturing and marketing of wheat-based products including Flour, Semolina, Whole Wheat Meal, Noodles and
Pasta.Shagamu site was licensed by NEPZA on 1st July 2024 as a free trade zone entity.It started operations in October
2025 with its assets and liabilities carved out of Honeywell Flour Mills Plc.
