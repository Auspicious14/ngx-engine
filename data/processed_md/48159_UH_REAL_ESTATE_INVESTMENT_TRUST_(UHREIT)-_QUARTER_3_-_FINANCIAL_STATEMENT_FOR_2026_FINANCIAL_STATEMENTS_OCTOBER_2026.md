1 General information
UH Real Estate Investment Trust (“the Fund”) was constituted under the laws of Nigeria and
commenced business on 3 February 2009. The Fund is a hybrid REIT that gives unitholders access to
investments in an approved portfolio. It is governed by a Trust Deed approved by the Securities and
Exchange Commission. The Fund’s units are listed on the Nigerian Exchange, and its financial
statements are filed with the Securities and Exchange Commission and other regulatory authorities.
UH REIT is a closed-ended fund primarily engaged in acquiring investment properties held for capital
appreciation. The Fund’s activities are managed by SFS Capital Nigeria Limited, Plot 287 Ajose
Adeogun Street, Victoria Island, Lagos. United Capital Trustees Limited is the Trustee to the Fund.
