LAGOS: 23RD JUNE 2026
HBM NIGERIA PLC - NOTIFICATION OF CHANGE OF NAME
This is to formally notify the Nigerian Exchange Limited (NGX), our esteemed shareholders,
stakeholders, and the general investing public that Lafarge Africa Plc has officially changed its
corporate name to HBM Nigeria Plc.
This strategic name change is a pivotal step in our ongoing corporate rebranding exercise. It reflects
our evolved business vision, long-term growth objectives, and our renewed commitment to delivering
superior value to our stakeholders.
• Approvals: The name change received full shareholder approval at the Annual General
Meeting (AGM) held on the 30th of April 2026.
• Registration: The Corporate Affairs Commission (CAC) has officially approved and issued the
Certificate of Incorporation reflecting the name change.
• Shareholders: Existing share certificates and electronic holdings remain valid and will be
updated automatically by our Registrars.
We express our profound gratitude to our regulators, shareholders and stakeholders for their
unwavering support. HBM Nigeria Plc remains dedicated to maintaining the highest standards of
corporate governance and operational excellence.
For: HBM Nigeria PLC (formerly Lafarge Africa Plc)
Adewunmi Alode
Company Secretary & Legal Director
