1. MACROECONOMIC OVERVIEW
Gross Domestic Product
Nigeria's economy demonstrated resilience in 2025, with real GDP averaging 3.78% in Q1–Q3, up
from 3.34% in 2024FY. Growth was underpinned by the non-oil sector, which contributed 96.18% of
output. Oil sector growth of 9.39% YoY reflected Petroleum Industry Act driven upstream
improvements, though production averaged 1.65MMbpd.
Inflation
Headline inflation moderated sharply, easing steadily from 24.48% YoY in January to 14.45% in
November 2025. Food disinflation (26.08%→ 11.08%) drove the trend, supported by the CPI rebasing,
improved harvests, Naira stability, and lower energy costs. Core inflation also eased from 22.59% to
18.00% YoY.
Monetary Policy
CBN maintained the MPR at 27.50% for most of 2025 before cutting 50bps to 27.00% in September
— the first cut since 2020. The November MPC meeting surprised with a hold, though the overall
tone remained dovish.

Mortgage & Residential Market
The Nigerian mortgage and residential market remains nascent, constrained by elevated interest
rates, high construction costs, and limited long-term financing. With the monetary policy rate at
27%, mortgage rates remain prohibitive for most households. The National Housing Fund (NHF)
continues to be the primary vehicle for subsidized access, though coverage remains narrow.
Urbanization pressures — particularly in Lagos, Abuja, and Port Harcourt — sustain underlying
housing demand, especially in the mid-market and affordable segments. The transition from
stabilization to early-cycle recovery offers a constructive medium-term outlook for income-
generating real estate assets as fixed-income yields decline.
2. Portfolio Summary
Fund Overview
The MOFI Real Estate Investment Fund (MREIF) is a NGN1 trillion shelf programme structured as a
closed-end real estate investment fund under a unit trust scheme, regulated by the Securities and
Exchange Commission (SEC). The Fund is sponsored by the Ministry of Finance Incorporated (MOFI)
and managed by ARM Investment Managers Limited. MREIF commenced operations on 17 March
2025 following full subscription and SEC clearance of the NGN150 billion Series 1 issuance and
NGN100 billion Series 2 issuance. Series 2 was listed on the Nigerian Exchange (NGX) in November
2025, attracting broad positive market reception. The Fund holds a Fund Rating of Aaa (Agusto &
Co) and AA (GCR), reflecting a high level of credit quality and safety of invested funds.
The Fund's mandate is to mobilize concessionary public-sector capital alongside commercial
private-sector funding to address Nigeria's housing deficit by providing (i) long-term (up to 20
years), low-cost mortgages (9.75% per annum) through Eligible Financial Institutions (EFIs) to
eligible homebuyers; and (ii) conditional offtake guarantees to qualified developers as a credit

(2) provide reasonable assurance that transactions are recorded as necessary to permit preparation of

use, or disposition of the Fund's assets that could have a material effect on the financial statements.
