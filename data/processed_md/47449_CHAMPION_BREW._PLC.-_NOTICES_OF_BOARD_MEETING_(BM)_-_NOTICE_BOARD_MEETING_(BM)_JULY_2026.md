NOTIFICATION OF BOARD MEETING – CHAMPION BREWERIES PLC
This is to notify the shareholders of Champion Breweries Plc (the Company), Nigerian Exchange
Limited (NGX or The Exchange) and the investing public that a meeting of the Board of Directors
of the Company has been scheduled to hold on Wednesday July 22, 2026, to among other items,
consider and approve the Unaudited Financial Statements of the Company for the quarter ended
30 June 2026 (2026, Q2 UFS).
Pursuant to Rule 17:18 (a) of The Exchange’s Rulebook on Closed Period (as amended), the
Closed Period which commenced on 1 July 2026, shall remain in effect until twenty-four (24)
hours after the release of the Company’s 2026 Q2 UFS to the public.
Consequently, no Insider of the Company and their connected persons shall deal in the securities
of the Company or with sensitive information, directly or indirectly during this Closed Period.
