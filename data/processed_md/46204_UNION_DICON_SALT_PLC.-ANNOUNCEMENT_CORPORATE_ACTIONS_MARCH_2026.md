Lagos Nigeria
3rd March 2026
ANNOUNCEMENT
In line with the provisions of 17.5 Rule book of the Exchange 2015 (Issuers Rules),
Union Dicon Salt Plc (Union Dicon or the Company) hereby notifies Nigerian
Exchange Limited (NGX), the shareholders of the Company, and the General
Public of the following:
1. That Aims Limited is a majority shareholder of Union Dicon Salt Plc with a
shareholding of 64,000,000 shares amounting to 40% of the issued shares.

a period of time despite several efforts made to reach out to Aims Limited.
3. That the Company is using this medium to request that Aims Limited
should contact Union Dicon immediately via its physical address at Kirikiri
Lighter Terminal, Kirikiri Phase 2, Apapa, Lagos, or via the Company
Secretary's number 09039296466 or email
companysecretary@uniondiconsaltplc.com
