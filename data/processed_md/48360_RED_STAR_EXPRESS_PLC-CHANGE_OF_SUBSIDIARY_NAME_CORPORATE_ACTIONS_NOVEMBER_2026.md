Lagos, Nigeria, November 3, 2026
NOTICE OF BOARD MEETING
We write to inform our shareholders, the investing public and The Nigerian Exchange
Group (NGX) that the Board of Directors of Red Star Express Plc has approved the
change of name of one of its subsidiaries from RED STAR FREIGHT LIMITED to
RED STAR CARGO SERVICES LIMITED. This is to enable the company take
advantage to emerging business opportunities.
FRANCES NDIDI AKPOMUKA
COMPANY SECRETARY
