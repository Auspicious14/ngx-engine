LAGOS, 23RD APRIL 2026
RESOLUTIONS PASSED AT THE 80TH ANNUAL GENERAL MEETING
At the 80th Annual General Meeting of Nigerian Breweries Plc (“the Company”) held in the Grand
Ballroom, Oriental Hotel, Victoria Island, Lagos, on Thursday, 23rd April 2026 at 10:00 a.m., the
following Resolutions were duly proposed and passed:
1. ELECTION/RE-ELECTION Ordinary Resolutions
OF DIRECTORS (a) “THAT the approval of the appointments of the
three new Directors by a SINGLE RESOLUION be
and is hereby approved.”
(b) “THAT in line with Section 274(2) of the
Companies and Allied Matters Act, 2020, the
appointments of Mr. Thibaut F.B. Boidin, Mr.
Guillaume B.M. Duverdier, and Mrs. Maria A.
Karaseva as Directors in the Company be and are
hereby approved.”
(c) “THAT the Re-election of the two retiring Directors
offering themselves for re-election by a single
resolution, be and is hereby approved.”
(d) “THAT in line with Articles 90 and 91 of the
Company’s Articles of Association, Mrs. Adeyinka
O. Aroyewun and Mr. Roland Pirmex, be and are
hereby re-elected Directors in the Company.”
2. REMUNERATION OF Ordinary Resolution
INDEPENDENT AUDITOR “THAT the Directors be and are hereby authorised to fix
the remuneration of the Independent Auditor for the year
ending 31st December 2026.”
3. ELECTION OF MEMBERS OF Ordinary Resolution
THE STATUTORY AUDIT “THAT the following persons:
COMMITTEE (i) Chief Timothy A. Adesiyan (Shareholders’ representative);
(ii) Mazi Samuel C. Mpamaugo (Shareholders’
representative);

G.B.M Duverdier (French); Mrs. M.A. Karaseva (Russian); Mrs. S.O. Ojekwe-Onyejeli; J.A.A. Overmars (Dutch); R. Pirmez (Belgian);
