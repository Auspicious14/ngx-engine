LAGOS, NIGERIA | SEPTEMBER 29, 2026
UNITED CAPITAL PLC – NOTICE OF Q3, 2026 BOARD MEETING AND CLOSED
PERIOD
The Board of United Capital Plc (the “Company”) will meet in Lagos on Friday,
October 23, 2026, to consider, among other things, the Company’s Unaudited
Financial Statements for the quarter ending September 30, 2026 (the “Q3, 2026
Financials”).
In accordance with the post-listing requirements of the Nigerian Exchange
Limited (“NGX”), the closed period for trading in the shares of the Company
will commence on October 1, 2026, in respect of the Q3, 2026 Financials.
Hence, no Insider of the Company and their connected persons may directly
or indirectly deal in the securities of the Company until 24 hours after the
release of the Q3, 2026 Financials to NGX and, by extension, the general
public.
Thank you.
