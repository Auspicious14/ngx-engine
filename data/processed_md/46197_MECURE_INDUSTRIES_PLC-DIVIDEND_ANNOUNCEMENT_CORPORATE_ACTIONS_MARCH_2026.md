Corporate Actions Announcement
MECURE INDUSTRIES PLC hereby announce as follows:
Period Ended 31 December 2025
Proposed Dividend A Final Dividend of N0.32kobo per 50 kobo ordinary share, subject to appropriate
withholding tax and approval will be paid to shareholders, whose names appear
in the Register of Members as at the close of business on Thursday, 23rd April,
2026.
Proposed Bonus NIL
Closure of Register The Register of Shareholders will be closed on Friday 24th April, 2026 to
Thursday 30th April 2026 (both date inclusive)
Qualification Date Thursday 23rd of April, 2026.
Payment Date To be communicated once AGM date has been fixed
E-dividend Registration Shareholders who are yet to complete the e-dividend registration are advised to
download the Registrar’s E-Dividend Mandate Activation Form, which is available
on the Registrars website: www.cordros.com, complete and submit to the
Registrars.
Unclaimed Dividend Shareholders with dividend warrants and share certificates that have remained
Warrants and Share unclaimed or are yet to be presented for payment or returned for validation are
Certificates advised to complete the e-dividend registration or contact the Registrars.
Date of General Meeting To be communicated
Registrars Cordros Registrars Limited, 131, Ikorodu Road, Lagos. 07002673767
www.cordros.com; contactcentre@cordros.com
Investor Relations investor.relations@mecure.com
Dated this 3rd March, 2026
Signed:
OLAWALE IBITOYE
COMPANY SECRETARY/LEGAL ADVISER
