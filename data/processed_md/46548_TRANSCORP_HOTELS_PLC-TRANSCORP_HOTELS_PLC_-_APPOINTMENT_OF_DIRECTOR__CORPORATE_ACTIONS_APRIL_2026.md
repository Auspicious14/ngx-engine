TRANSCORP HOTELS PLC APPOINTS MUYIWA AKINYEMI TO ITS BOARD OF
DIRECTORS
ABUJA, NIGERIA – April 1, 2026
Transcorp Hotels Plc (the Company), the hospitality subsidiary of Transnational
Corporation Plc (Transcorp), has announced the appointment of Mr. Muyiwa
Akinyemi as an Independent Non-Executive Director to its Board of Directors,
with effect from April 1, 2026. This appointment will be ratified at the next
general meeting of the Shareholders of the Company.
Mr. Akinyemi brings to the Board a distinguished career spanning over three
decades across Nigeria's financial services sector, including wholesale and
retail banking, investment banking, capital markets, and strategic business
leadership. He recently served as Group Deputy Managing Director of United
Bank for Africa (UBA) Plc, with oversight for the bank’s Nigeria-wide business
operations, comprising Corporate, Business Banking, and Retail divisions.
An Associate of the Institute of Chartered Accountants of Nigeria (ICAN) and
a member of both the Chartered Institute of Bankers of Nigeria and the
Chartered Institute of Stockbrokers, Mr. Akinyemi holds a B.Sc. in Accounting
from Obafemi Awolowo University, Ile-Ife. He has also undertaken advanced
executive programmes at the University of Chicago Booth School of Business,
Cambridge University, and Harvard Business School, among others.
Speaking on the appointment, Dr. (Mrs.) Awele V. Elumelu, OFR, Chairman of
the Board of Transcorp Hotels Plc, said: "We are pleased to welcome Mr.
Akinyemi to the Board of Transcorp Hotels Plc, as we embark on our strategic
expansion phase. He is a seasoned executive whose exceptional record of
building and scaling financial and business operations across Africa speaks
for itself. We look forward to benefiting from his counsel and experience."

This appointment reinforces the Board's commitment to deepening its
strategic, financial, and governance expertise, driving sustainable value for
its shareholders as the Company continues to expand its leadership in African
luxury hospitality.
For: Transcorp Hotels Plc
