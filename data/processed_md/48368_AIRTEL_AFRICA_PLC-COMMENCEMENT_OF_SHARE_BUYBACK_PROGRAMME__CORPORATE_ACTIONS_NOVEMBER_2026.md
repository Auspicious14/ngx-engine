Airtel Africa plc
(‘Airtel Africa’, or the ‘Company’)
Commencement of share buyback programme
London and Lagos, 5 November 2026: Airtel Africa, a leading provider of telecommunications and mobile
money services with operations in 14 countries across Africa, announces that it is today commencing the next
tranche of the share buyback programme (the “Programme”) originally announced on 22 May 2026. The
Programme continues the Company’s broader approach to returning cash to shareholders as part of its
capital allocation policy.
As previously announced, the Company’s share buyback programme provides for the repurchase of up to 1% of
the Company’s issued share capital as at 22 May 2026, being the date on which the Programme was
announced. Following completion of the first tranche of the buyback on 28 September 2026 (the “Initial
Tranche”), the remaining Board authority currently provides for the repurchase of up to 12,033,748 ordinary
shares (being 0.33% of the Company’s total issued share capital as at 22 May 2026).
The Company has entered into agreements with Citigroup Global Markets Limited (“Citi”) to conduct the next
tranche of the Programme (the “Further Tranche”) and carry out on-market purchases of its ordinary shares
with the Company subsequently purchasing its ordinary shares from Citi (the “Agreements”). Citi will act as
riskless principal pursuant to the Agreements.
The Agreements entered into with Citi in respect of the Further Tranche comprise two elements which will
operate in parallel:
• a non-discretionary element pursuant to which Citi will purchase ordinary shares and will make trading
decisions independently of the Company; and
• a discretionary element pursuant to which the Company may, at its discretion and subject to the provisions
of the Market Abuse Regulation (EU) No 596/2014 (as it forms part of domestic law by virtue of the
European Union (Withdrawal) Act 2018, as amended), provide instructions to Citi for the purchase of
ordinary shares.
The Agreements have commenced today and are anticipated to terminate no later than 22 December 2026,
unless terminated earlier in accordance with its terms.
The sole purpose of the buyback programme is to reduce the capital of the Company. As such, all shares
purchased under the buyback programme will be cancelled.
Any purchases of ordinary shares under either element of the Agreements will be carried out in accordance with
certain pre-set parameters set out in the Agreements and in accordance with (and subject to the limits
prescribed by) the Company's general authority to repurchase ordinary shares granted by its shareholders from
time to time (at the annual general meeting on 9 July 2026, shareholders gave the Company authority to
purchase a maximum of 364,577,234 ordinary shares and following the completion of the Initial Tranche, the
remaining authority amounts to a maximum of 352,727,390 ordinary shares), Financial Conduct Authority's UK
Listing Rules 9.6 and the provisions of the Market Abuse Regulation (EU) No 596/2014 (as it forms part of
domestic law by virtue of the European Union (Withdrawal) Act 2018, as amended). The discretionary
arrangements may include irrevocable, non-discretionary instructions to Citi to continue to operate the
buyback programme during closed periods. Citi will therefore operate the discretionary element of buyback
programme autonomously during those periods. For the avoidance of doubt, no repurchases will be made on
the Nigerian Exchange Limited ("NGX").
