OUTCOME OF MEETING OF THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF CUTIX PLC
Cutix Plc (“the Company”) wishes to notify the Nigerian Exchange Limited (NGX) and
the investing public that the 171st meeting of the Board of Directors of the Company was
held on Friday, May 29, 2026, as scheduled.
The Fourth Quarter Unaudited Financial Statements (Q4 UFS) for the period ended
April 30, 2026, amongst other matters were considered and approved by the Board
subject to few amendments.

closed period earlier announced by the Company will subsist until twenty-four (24) hours
after the release of the Audited Financial Statement to the general public
