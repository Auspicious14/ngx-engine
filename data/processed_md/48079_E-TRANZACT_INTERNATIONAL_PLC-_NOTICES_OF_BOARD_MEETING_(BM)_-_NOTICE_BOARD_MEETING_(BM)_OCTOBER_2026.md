Lagos, 13th October, 2026
eTRANZACT INTERNATIONAL PLC – NOTICE OF BOARD MEETING AND
CLOSED PERIOD
We hereby notify Nigerian Exchange Limited (NGX), our shareholders and the investing public,
that a meeting of the Board of Directors of eTRANZACT INTERNATIONAL PLC (“the
Company”) will hold on Wednesday, 28th October, 2026 at the Company’s Registered Address, 5th
Floor, Fortune Towers, 27/29 Adeyemo Alakija Street, Victoria Island, Lagos at 10:00 am to
consider the Unaudited Financial Statements for the Third Quarter Ended 30th September, 2026
(Q3 UFS 2026).
Accordingly, in line with the provisions of Rule 17.18(a): Closed Period, Rulebook of the Exchange,
2015 (Issuers’ Rule) as amended, and which has been incorporated into Sections 5 and 6 of the
Company’s Securities Trading Policy, the Company commenced a Closed Period from Thursday,
1st October 2026.
During this Closed Period, all insiders shall not trade in the Company's securities until twenty-four
(24) hours after the Q3 UFS, 2026 have been released to the public.
