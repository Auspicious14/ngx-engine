RESOLUTIONS PASSED AT THE ANNUAL GENERAL MEETING OF CHEMICAL AND ALLIED PRODUCTS
PLC HELD ON JUNE 24, 2026.
At the 61st Annual General Meeting (AGM) of Chemical and Allied Products PLC (the
“Company”) held on June 24, 2025 at Festival Hotel, Diamond Estate, Amuwo Odofin, Lagos,
Nigeria in respect of the financial year ended December 31, 2025.
A. The Audited Financial Statements for the financial year ended December 31, 2025
together with the Directors, Auditors', and Audit Committee reports, were laid before the
Shareholders.
B. The remuneration of the Managers of the Company was disclosed in compliance with
Section 257 of the Companies and Allied Matters Act (CAMA) 2020.
The following resolutions were considered and duly passed:
(i) That a dividend of N4.00 per ordinary share of 50 kobo each be and is hereby approved
for payment to the shareholders whose names appear in the Register of Members as of
June 3, 2026, subject to appropriate withholding tax.
(ii) That Dr. Oladele Ajayi, being a Director appointed since the last AGM of the Company
be and is hereby re-elected as a Director of the Company.
(iii) That Mrs. Udo Okonjo, being one of the directors retiring by rotation and being eligible
and offering herself for re-election, be and is hereby re-elected as a Director of the
Company.
(iv) That Mr. Adebolanle Badejo, being the second director retiring by rotation and being
eligible and offering himself for re-election, be and is hereby re-elected as a Director of
the Company.
(v) That the Board of Directors be and are hereby authorised to fix the remuneration of the
Company’s External Auditors for the 2026 financial year.
(vi) That Prince Bassey Manfred, Mrs. Samiat Odunuga, Mr. Anthony Kanayo Katchy be and
are hereby elected as members of the Statutory Audit Committee to serve along with
Mrs. Udo Okonjo and Mrs. Muhibat Abbas, representatives of the Board of Directors, until
the conclusion of the next Annual General Meeting.
(vii) That the remuneration of the Non-Executive Directors is hereby fixed from year to year
until same is reviewed at a general meeting of the Company.

(viii) That a general mandate be and is hereby given, authorising the Company through the
Directors to enter into recurrent transactions with related parties or companies until
reviewed by the Company at an Annual General Meeting.
BY ORDER OF THE BOARD
