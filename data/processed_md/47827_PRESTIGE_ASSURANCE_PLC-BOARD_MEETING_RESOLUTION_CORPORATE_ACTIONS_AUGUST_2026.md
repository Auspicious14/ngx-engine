Lagos, 19 August 2026.
RESOLUTIONS PASSED AT THE BOARD MEETING OF PRESTIGE ASSURANCE PLC
This is to notify Nigerian Exchange Limited (NGX) and the Investing Public that the
meeting of the Board of Directors of Prestige Assurance Plc (‘the Company’) was held as
scheduled on Tuesday, 18th August 2026, at No. 19, Ligali Ayorinde Street, Victoria Island,
Lagos.
The Directors of the Company proposed and passed the following Resolution:
That the resignation of Mrs. Girija Subramanian as a Non-Executive Director from the
board be and is hereby accepted by the Board of Directors with effect from 31st July
2026.
The Board expressed sincere appreciation for her service, support, and contributions to
the growth of the Company during her tenure, and wished her well in her future
endeavours.
For: PRESTIGE ASSURANCE PLC.
MRS CHIDINMA IBE-LOUIS
Company Secretary
