Lagos, Nigeria, April 17, 2026
NOTICE OF BOARD MEETING
We write to inform our shareholders, the investing public and Nigerian
Exchange Limited (NGX) that a Board Meeting of the Directors of Red Star
Express Plc (the Company) will hold on Thursday, April 30th, 2026 at 11.00am,
to approve the Fourth Quarter Unaudited 2025/26 Financial Statements of the
Company, amongst other issues.
Consequently, the Company hereby declares that the closed period for trading
in its shares commenced from April 1, 2026 until twenty-four (24) hours after
the Fourth Quarter Unaudited 2025/26 Financial Statements is filed with NGX.
The Directors and other insiders of Red Star Express Plc are restricted from
dealing in the securities of the Company during this period.
FRANCES NDIDI AKPOMUKA
COMPANY SECRETARY
