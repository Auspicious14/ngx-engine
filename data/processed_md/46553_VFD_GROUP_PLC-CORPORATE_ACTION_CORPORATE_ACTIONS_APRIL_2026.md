Lagos, Nigeria, 31 March 2026
VFD Group PLC hereby announces as follows:
Period Ended 31st December, 2025
Proposed Dividend A Final Dividend of 25kobo for every 1 (One) share will be paid to
shareholders whose names appear in the Register of Members as at
the close of business on 4th day of May 2026, subject to appropriate
withholding tax and approval.
Closure of Register The Register of Members will be closed from 5th to 7th May 2026 (both
dates inclusive).
Qualification Date 4th May 2026
Payment Date On May 25, 2026, dividends will be paid electronically to shareholders
whose names appear on the Register of Members as at 4th May 2026
and who have completed the e-dividend registration and mandated
the Registrar to pay their dividends directly into their Bank accounts.
E-Dividend Shareholders who are yet to complete the e-dividend registration are
Registration advised to download the Registrar’s E-Dividend Form - Africa
Prudential, which is also available on the website of the Registrars:
www.africaprudential.com complete and submit to the Registrar or
their respective Banks.
Unclaimed Dividend Shareholders with dividend warrants and share certificates that have
Warrants and Share remained unclaimed or are yet to be presented for payment or
Certificates returned for validation are advised to complete the e-dividend
registration or contact the Registrar.
Date of Annual General The Company’s Annual General Meeting will be held on 25th May
Meeting 2026, at 11:00am.
