LAGOS, NIGERIA | MARCH 23, 2026
UNITED CAPITAL PLC – NOTICE OF Q1, 2026 BOARD MEETING AND CLOSED
PERIOD
The Board of United Capital Plc (the “Company”) will meet in Abuja on Friday,
April 24, 2026, to consider, among other things, the Company’s Unaudited
Financial Statements for the quarter ending March 31, 2026 (the “Q1, 2026
Financials”).
In accordance with the post-listing requirements of the Nigerian Exchange
Limited (“NGX”), the closed period for trading in the shares of the Company
will commence on April 1, 2026, in respect of the Q1, 2026 Financials.
Hence, no Insider of the Company and their connected persons may directly
or indirectly deal in the securities of the Company until twenty-four (24) hours
after the release of the Q1, 2026 Financials to NGX and by extension, the
general public.
Thank you.
