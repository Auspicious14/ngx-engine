Lagos, 3 March 2026
NOTICE OF DELAY IN FILING AUDITED FINANCIAL STATEMENTS
C&I Leasing Plc (‘’ the Company’’) hereby notifies Nigerian Exchange Limited and the
investing public of the likely delay in filing its Audited Financial Statement for the year ended
31 December 2025 AFS.
This request is due to unforeseen circumstances regarding the delay in obtaining regulatory
approvals from our primary regulator, Central Bank of Nigeria and ongoing finalization of audit
processes with our offshore subsidiary companies.
Considering the above, the company has received the approval of NGX Regulation Limited to
submit the 2025 AFS on or before 29 June 2026. We are currently working with our Auditors
to ensure prompt and accurate report of the financial results.
We appreciate the patience and understanding of all stakeholders during this period and
reaffirm our dedication to upholding the principles of good governance.
