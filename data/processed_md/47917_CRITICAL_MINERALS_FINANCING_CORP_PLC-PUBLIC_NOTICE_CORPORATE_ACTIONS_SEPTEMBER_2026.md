Lagos, 16 September 2026
PUBLIC NOTICE!!!
REQUEST FOR SUBMISSION OF CLAIMS BY CREDITORS, BONDHOLDERS, AND
OTHER CLAIMANTS OF CMFC PLC (FORMERLY DEAP CAPITAL MANAGEMENT &
TRUST PLC)
NOTICE IS HEREBY GIVEN that Critical Minerals Financing Corporation Plc (CMFC or the
Company) (formerly known as DEAP Capital Management & Trust Plc) is currently undertaking
a comprehensive reconciliation and regularisation of its books and records, pursuant to its ongoing
regulatory regularisation process with the Securities and Exchange Commission ("SEC"), and in
compliance with the Administrative Proceeding Committee's decision IN RE: APC/5/2013, Mr.
Ismail Idowu Adejumo &10 Ors vs. Deap Capital Management & Trust PLC & 13 Ors.
As part of this process, and on the "No objection" of the SEC, the Company is inviting all persons
and entities who have, or believe they have claims against the Company, to come forward and
submit particulars of their claims for verification and reconciliation.
Accordingly, all persons, companies, financial institutions, government ministries, departments
and agencies, bondholders, creditors and any other persons or entities who claim that Deap Capital
Management & Trust PLC is indebted to them in any amount are hereby requested to submit full
particulars of such claim together with all documentary evidence supporting the claim.
The submission should include where applicable: the name and contact details of the claimant; the
amount claimed; the nature and basis of the claim; copies of all relevant agreements, invoices,
judgments, correspondence or other supporting documents; and any other information the claimant
considers relevant.
All claims and documentary evidence supporting the claim must be delivered within twenty-one
(21) days from the date of this publication to:
Critical Minerals Financing Corp PLC
Address: Number One Lagos, 1 Akin Adesola, Victoria Island, Lagos.
Email: company.secretary@cmfc.world
Please note that this notice is issued solely for the purpose of verification and reconciliation of the
Company's records as part of its regulatory regularisation process. Any person or entity who fails
to submit its claim within the period specified above may not be included in this particular
reconciliation exercise, without prejudice to any claimant's legal rights, which remains governed
by the applicable limitation laws.
BY ORDER OF THE BOARD
CMFC PLC
(Formerly DEAP Capital Management & Trust Limited).
