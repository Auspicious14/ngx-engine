LAGOS, 23 JUNE 2026
RESOLUTIONS PASSED AT THE 16TH ANNUAL GENERAL MEETING OF
SKYWAY AVAIATION HOLDING COMPANY PLC
At the 16th Annual General Meeting of Skyway Aviation Handling Company Plc (the Company), duly
convened and held virtually on Friday, 19th June, 2026, the following Resolutions were proposed and
duly passed:
1. That the report of Directors, the report of the External Auditors, the report of the Audit
Committee and the Audited Financial Statements of the Company for the financial year ended
December 31, 2025, be and is hereby received.
2. That a dividend payment in the total of sum of ₦1,624,296,000 (One Billion, Six Hundred and
Twenty Four Million, Two Hundred and Ninety Six Thousand Naira Only) which translates
to ₦1.20 (One Naira Twenty Kobo) per ordinary share of the Company, subject to
withholding tax, be and is hereby declared and approved for payment to shareholders whose
names appeared in the Company’s Register of Members as at the close of business on June 2
2026.
3. That Mr. Kayode Filani, who having retired by rotation, being eligible and having offered
himself for re-election, be and is hereby duly re-elected as a Director of the Company.
4. That Mrs. Laila St. Mathew Daniel, who having retired by rotation, being eligible and having
offered herself for re-election, be and is hereby duly re-elected as a Director of the Company.
5. That Messrs. Deloitte Nigeria having indicated their willingness to act and being duly qualified,
be and are hereby appointed as External Auditors of the Company to fill the vacancy created
by the retirement of Messrs. Gbenga Badejo & Co., and to hold office until the conclusion of
the next Annual General Meeting of the Company, at the remuneration to be determined by
the Board of Directors.

6. That pursuant to Section 404 (3) of the Companies and Allied Matters Act 2020, the Statutory
Audit Committee of the Company be and is hereby constituted with effect from 19 June 2026,
until the conclusion of the next Annual General Meeting, comprising of the following
members:
a. Directors:
i. Mr. Anyanwu Anogwi
ii. Mrs. Laila St. Mathew-Daniel
b. Shareholders:
i. Mrs. Samiat Odunuga
ii. Mr. Ismail Adamu
iii. Mr. Rotimi Aina
7. That the remuneration payable to the Non-Executive Directors of the Company be and is
hereby fixed at ₦6,000,000 (Six Million Naira) per annum for the Chairman and ₦5,000,000
(Five Million Naira) per annum for each Non-Executive Director.
8. That the general mandate authorizing the Company to enter into recurrent transactions of a
revenue or trading nature with related parties or interested persons necessary for the day-to-
day operations of the Company be and is hereby renewed.
