NOTICE OF CLOSED PERIOD FOR NASCON ALLIED INDUSTRIES PLC.
In accordance with the Rules of the Nigerian Exchange Limited (“NGX”), Nascon
Allied Industries Plc (“Nascon” or the Company) hereby announces that its third
quarter will end on September 30, 2026.
In view of this, Nascon will commence a Closed Period from October 1, 2026, until
twenty-four (24) hours after its Financial Statements for the period ending
September 30, 2026, are released to the public via the website of NGX.
A Closed Period is applicable to all Nascon’s insiders, which includes all those who
possess information that may affect Nascon’s share price. Consequently, no insider
nor their connected persons may directly or indirectly deal in the shares of Nascon
until twenty-four (24) hours after the publication of its Financial Statements
through the Issuers’ Portal of NGX.
