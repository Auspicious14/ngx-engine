Lagos, 31 March 2026.
NOTICE OF BOARD MEETING AND CLOSED PERIOD
Prestige Assurance Plc (“the Company”) hereby wishes to inform the Nigerian Exchange
Limited (“NGX”), its shareholders, and the investing Public that a meeting of the Board
of Directors of the Company will be held at No. 19, Ligali Ayorinde Street, Victoria Island,
Lagos, on Tuesday, 28th April 2026. The Board will consider and approve the Q1
Unaudited Financial Statements (2026 Q1 UFS) for the period ending 31 March 2026,
amongst other agenda items.
Consequently, the closed period earlier announced which commenced from Thursday,
1 January 2026 is still subsisting until twenty-four (24) hours after the Audited Financial
Statements for the year ended 31st December, 2025 (2025 AFS) and (2026 Q1 UFS) have
been released to the public.
Accordingly, no insider and their connected persons as defined by NGX may directly or
indirectly deal in the securities of the Company in any manner during the Closed Period.
MRS CHIDINMA IBE-LOUIS
Company Secretary
