Lagos, 30 September 2026
NOTICE OF CLOSED PERIOD
In line with Rule 17. 17-17.18 of Nigerian Exchange Limited (NGX or The Exchange) Rulebook (as
amended), Wema Bank Plc (the Bank) has declared a Closed Period from 1st of October, 2026 till
twenty-four (24) hours after the Bank’s Unaudited Financial Statements for the third quarter
ending 30th of September, 2026 (2026 Q3 UFS) and other information have been made public
through The Exchange’s Issuer’s Portal.
Accordingly, no insiders of the Bank and their connected persons may directly or indirectly, deal
(buy or sell) in the shares of the Bank in any manner during the Closed Period until twenty-four
(24) hours after the release of the 2026 Q3 UFS through the Issuers’ Portal of NGX and by
extension, the general public.
All insiders of the Bank and persons closely connected to them have been appropriately advised.
