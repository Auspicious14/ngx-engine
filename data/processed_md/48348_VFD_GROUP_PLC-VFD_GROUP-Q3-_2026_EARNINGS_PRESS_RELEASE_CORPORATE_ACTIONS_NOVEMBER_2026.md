PRESS RELEASE
VFD Group Nearly Doubles Nine-Month Profit to ₦13.00 Billion and Deepens
Leadership Bench with the Appointment of the Group’s First-Ever Deputy
Managing Director
Profit after tax up 96%; as the Group strategically deploys ₦50.67 billion rights issue proceeds
Lagos, Nigeria — 28 October 2026 VFD Group Plc (NGX: VFDGROUP), a principal
investment company, today announced its unaudited results for the nine months ended 30
September 2026, as approved by the Board of Directors. Profit after tax rose 96% to ₦13.00
billion from ₦6.63 billion in the same period of 2025. Profit before tax rose 92% to ₦15.34
billion, as gross earnings increased 35% to ₦81.88 billion.
Profit attributable to shareholders rose 97% to ₦9.52 billion, lifting earnings per share to 75
kobo from 38 kobo.
The results come as the Group takes deliberate steps to make its performance sustainable.
During the third quarter, VFD Group continued the strategic deployment of the proceeds of
its ₦50.67 billion rights issue, raised ₦22.75 billion through an A1-rated commercial paper
issuance, and secured Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) approval for Anchoria
Capital Group as a capital market holding company. It also appointed Mrs. Ijeoma Emezie-
Ezigbo as its first-ever Deputy Managing Director, a central step in the Board’s succession
plan.

Group Performance
Growth was driven by the Group’s core investment activity. Net investment income rose 59%
to ₦72.18 billion and accounted for 93% of net revenue. Interest from placements rose 51%
to ₦21.05 billion, interest from debt instruments rose 164% to ₦10.87 billion, and fees and
commission income almost quadrupled to ₦4.47 billion.
Net revenue rose 42% to ₦77.52 billion, ahead of a 29% increase in total expenses, bringing
the cost-to-income ratio down to 24.5% from 26.9%. Impairment charges fell 44% to ₦1.29
billion, and operating profit rose 47% to ₦58.53 billion. After finance costs of ₦43.33 billion,
reflecting the cost of funding the Group’s investment book, which increased by 120%. Profit
before tax rose 92% to ₦15.34 billion.
Total assets rose 30% to ₦577.64 billion as at 30 September 2026, from ₦445.81 billion as
at 31 December 2025, as the Group deployed the proceeds of its rights issue and commercial
paper programme. Investment in financial assets more than doubled to ₦217.08 billion, and
funds under management rose 84% to ₦142.52 billion. Shareholders’ funds rose 87% to
₦130.02 billion, of which ₦105.93 billion is attributable to owners of the Company, more than
double the year-end figure of N50.19 billion.
Company Performance
Company results reflect the returns VFD Group earns on its own capital. Gross earnings rose
84% to ₦36.52 billion and net investment income rose 123% to ₦32.53 billion. Operating
profit rose 83% to ₦27.24 billion, and profit before tax rose to ₦7.23 billion from ₦0.35 billion.
Profit after tax of ₦6.18 billion lifted Company earnings per share to 49 kobo from 2 kobo.
Shareholders’ funds rose 131% to ₦94.30 billion, while borrowings fell 14% to ₦108.02 billion.
Company borrowings now stand at 1.1 times equity, down from 3.1 times at the start of the
year.

Appointment of Deputy Managing Director
The Board has appointed Mrs. Ijeoma Emezie-Ezigbo as Deputy Managing Director of VFD
Group Plc, effective 5 October 2026. She is the first person to hold the role in the Group’s
history.
Mrs. Emezie-Ezigbo brings more than two decades of experience in corporate finance,
transaction advisory, mergers and acquisitions, business valuation and strategic corporate
financial analysis across West Africa and Canada. Most recently she served as the Partner
and Head of Transaction Services, West Africa at KPMG Nigeria. She is a Fellow of the
Institute of Chartered Accountants of Nigeria (ICAN) and an Associate of the Chartered
Professional Accountants of Ontario, Canada (CPA Ontario).
With her appointment, the Group’s executive leadership comprises the Group Managing
Director, the Deputy Managing Director and two Executive Directors, within an eleven-
member Board.
Management Commentary
“Profit after tax has almost doubled, and it has been earned from our core investment activity.
But the more important work this year has been making sure results like these can be
sustained. We have recapitalised the Group, lengthened our funding and placed our capital
market businesses under a single SEC-approved holding company. With the appointment of
Mrs. Emezie-Ezigbo as Deputy Managing Director, we are building the leadership depth that
will allow this institution to keep performing well beyond any one individual or management
team.”
— Nonso Okpala, Group Managing Director/Chief Executive Officer, VFD Group Plc
“The rights issue has done what we said it would. Company borrowings fell 14% and
shareholders’ funds more than doubled. The Group’s funding is longer-dated and less
dependent on short-term financing. Into the fourth quarter, we will focus on formalising a
succession framework for key leadership positions across the Group and its portfolio
companies; continuing to lower the cost and extend the tenor of our funding; deploying capital
with discipline across our five investment verticals; and completing the integration and
expansion of our capital markets business, Anchoria Capital Group under their new corporate
structure.”
— Folajimi Adeleye, Executive Director, Finance, VFD Group Plc
