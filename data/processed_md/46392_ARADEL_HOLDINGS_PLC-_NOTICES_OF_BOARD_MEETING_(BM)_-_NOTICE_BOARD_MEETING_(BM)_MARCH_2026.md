Lagos, Nigeria March 30th, 2026
NOTICE OF BOARD MEETING
In line with the Rulebook of the Nigerian Exchange Limited (NGX), the Board of Directors of Aradel
Holdings Plc (the “Company”) hereby notifies the NGX and the investing public that a meeting
of the Board of Directors of the Company has been scheduled to hold on Wednesday, 28th of April
2026.
Among items slated for discussion at the meeting is to receive and consider general business
updates across the Company’s operations.
Consequently, the closed period for trading in the Company’s shares will continue until twenty-
four (24) hours after the Company’s Audited Financial Statements for the year ended 31st
December 2025, as well as its Unaudited Interim Financial Statements & Accounts for the period
ending 31st March 2026, have been released on the floor of NGX. Therefore, no insider or their
connected persons are permitted to deal in the securities of the Company during the closed
period.
By order of the Board.
