Lagos, 9 September 2026
PZ CUSSONS NIGERIA PLC – NOTIFICATION OF BOARD MEETING AND CLOSED
PERIOD
NOTICE IS HEREBY GIVEN THAT a Board meeting of PZ Cussons Nigeria Plc (the
“Company”) will be held on Tuesday, 29th September 2026, via the Teams Meeting
Platform from 11:00 am, to consider the Unaudited Financial Statements for the
period ended 31 August 2026 (the results).
Accordingly, in compliance with Nigerian Exchange Limited’s post-listing
requirements, the Company has declared the commencement of a closed period for
trading in its shares from 1st September 2026 until twenty-four (24) hours after the
release of the results to the Market.
Consequently, no insider of the Company or their connected persons shall deal
directly or indirectly in the securities of PZ Cussons Nigeria Plc during this closed
period.
