NOTICE OF THE MEETING OF THE BOARD OF NCR (NIGERIA) PLC AND
CLOSED PERIOD
NCR (Nigeria) PLC (the “Company”) wishes to inform the investing public and
Nigerian Exchange Limited (NGX) that, further to the Public Notice issued on 27
February 2026, the Board of Directors meeting earlier scheduled for 25 March 2026
to consider and approve the Company’s Audited Financial Statements for the Year
ended December 31, 2025 (2025 AFS) has been rescheduled.
This change is due to changes in the meeting dates of the Board Committees of
the Company. Accordingly, the meeting of the Board of Directors will now be held
on Friday, 27 March 2026 via the Zoom Cloud Platform.
Kindly be informed that the Closed Period for trading on the shares of the Company,
as previously announced remains in force until twenty-four (24) hours after the 2025
AFS is released to the Market. Consequently, no Insider shall deal directly or
indirectly in the securities of the Company during this closed period.
