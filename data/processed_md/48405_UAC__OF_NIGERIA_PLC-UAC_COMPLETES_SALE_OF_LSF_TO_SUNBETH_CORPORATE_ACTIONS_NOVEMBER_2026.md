UAC Completes Sale of Livestock Feeds PLC to Sunbeth Treenuts and Sesame Limited
Lagos, 17 November 2026 – UAC of Nigeria PLC (“UAC”) today announces the completion of the sale of its
73.29% equity interest in Livestock Feeds PLC (“LSF”) to Sunbeth Treenuts and Sesame Limited (“Sunbeth”),
following the receipt of the requisite approvals from the Federal Competition and Consumer Protection
Commission, the Securities and Exchange Commission and Nigerian Exchange Limited.
The transaction follows UAC’s announcement on 30 September 2026 of an agreement for the sale of LSF, a
pioneer in the production of animal feed in Nigeria.
Fola Aiyesimoju, Group Managing Director of UAC, commented: “We are pleased to have completed this
transaction and thank the LSF team for their contributions to the UAC Group over the years. We wish LSF and
Sunbeth every success as they embark on this new chapter.”
Peeyush Karnani, Chief Strategy Officer of Sunbeth Agro HoldCo, said: “Livestock Feeds is an established
business with a proud heritage, trusted brands and longstanding relationships across Nigeria’s livestock
industry. We recognise the responsibility that comes with becoming its majority shareholder.
“Our approach is to build on these foundations. Our immediate focus is continuity, operational stability and
supporting the people and partnerships behind the business. We intend to combine Sunbeth’s strength in
sourcing soya and other key feed ingredients with Livestock Feeds’ manufacturing expertise and established
distribution network to improve supply reliability, strengthen competitiveness and create sustainable value
for all shareholders.
“This acquisition also provides an important foundation for Sunbeth’s long-term strategy to build an
integrated protein business spanning animal nutrition, poultry and cattle production, and meat processing.
We will pursue this strategy through disciplined, phased investment, with a clear focus on strengthening
Livestock Feeds and supporting the growth of Nigeria’s livestock industry.”
