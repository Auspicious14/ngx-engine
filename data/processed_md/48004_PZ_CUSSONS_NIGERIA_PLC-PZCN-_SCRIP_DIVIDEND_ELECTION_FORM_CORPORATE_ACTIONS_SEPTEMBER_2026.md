If you wish to participate in the PZ Cussons Nigeria PLC Scrip Dividend Election Arrangement (“the Election”)*, please complete, sign and return this form to
the Registrar, First Registrars and Investor Services Limited, No 2, Abebe Village Road, Iganmu, Lagos no later than October 23, 2026. This Election Form may

election, your final dividend will be paid entirely in cash.

IMPORTANT NOTES
1. This Form of Election should be completed by shareholders electing to receive their FY2026 dividend in shares under the Scrip Dividend Arrangement
2. Please note that a separate Form of Election must be completed and submitted for each shareholder account or holding
3. In the case of joint holders, joint holders must sign this Form. If the Shareholder is a Corporate, this form must be signed by its authorised representatives
4. Shareholders should ensure that their CSCS and shareholder account details are correctly stated to facilitate the allotment and crediting of new shares
5. The completed Form of Election should be returned to the Registrar on or before 5:00 p.m. West Africa Time on October 23, 2026
6. Personal information provided in this Form of Election will be collected and processed in accordance with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023
7. Shareholders who wish to receive their final dividend entirely in cash are not required to complete or submit this Form of Election.
