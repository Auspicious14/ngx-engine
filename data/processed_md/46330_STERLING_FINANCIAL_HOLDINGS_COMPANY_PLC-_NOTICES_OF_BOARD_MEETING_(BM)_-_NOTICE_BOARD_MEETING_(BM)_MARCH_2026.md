Lagos, Nigeria, March 25, 2026
STERLING FINANCIAL HOLDINGS COMPANY PLC - NOTICE OF BOARD MEETING
TO APPROVE UNAUDITED FINANCIAL STATEMENTS FOR THE PERIOD ENDING 31ST
MARCH 2026
Sterling Financial Holdings Company Plc (the Company) wishes to notify
Nigerian Exchange Limited (NGX), its shareholders, and the investing public
that a meeting of the Board of Directors of the Company will be held in Lagos
on Friday, 24th April 2026 at 10.00 am to consider the Company’s Unaudited
Financial Statements for the period ending 31st March 2026.
The closed period for trading in the Company’s shares which commenced on
1st January 2026 will continue until twenty (24) hours after the Audited
Financial Statements for the year ended 31st December 2025 and Unaudited
Financial Statements for the period ending 31st March 2026 are released via
the Issuers’ Portal of the NGX.
Consequently, the Directors and other insiders of the Company are restricted
from dealing in the securities of the Company during the closed period.
Thank you for your usual cooperation.
For: STERLING FINANCIAL HOLDINGS COMPANY PLC
SUNNY KANABE
Company Secretary
