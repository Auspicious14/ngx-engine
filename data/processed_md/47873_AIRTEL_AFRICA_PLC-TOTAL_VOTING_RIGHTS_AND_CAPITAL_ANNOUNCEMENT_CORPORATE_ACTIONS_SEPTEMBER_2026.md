Total Voting Rights and Capital
London and Lagos, 1 September 2026: In accordance with the Financial Conduct
Authority's (FCA) Disclosure Guidance and Transparency Rule 5.6.1R, the Company
notifies the market that:
1. The Company’s issued share capital as at close of business on 31 August 2026
consisted of 3,635,772,073 ordinary shares of USD 0.50 per share (“ordinary shares”),
each with one vote, including 6,136,678 ordinary shares held in treasury. Ordinary
shares held in treasury do not carry any voting rights.
2. Therefore, the total number of voting rights that may be used by shareholders as the
denominator for the calculations by which they will determine whether they are
required to notify their interest in, or a change to their interest in, the Company under
the Financial Conduct Authority's Disclosure Guidance and Transparency Rules, is
3,629,635,395.

About Airtel Africa
Airtel Africa is a leading provider of telecommunications and mobile money services,
with operations in 14 countries in sub-Saharan Africa. Airtel Africa provides an
integrated offer to its subscribers, including mobile voice and data services as well as
mobile money services both nationally and internationally. The company’s strategy is
focused on providing a great customer experience across the entire footprint, enabling
our corporate purpose of transforming lives across Africa.
