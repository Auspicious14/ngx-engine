Abuja Nigeria, September 30, 2026
Notification of Board Meeting and Closed Period of Transcorp Hotels Plc
This is to notify Nigerian Exchange Limited (NGX), shareholders and the public
that the Board of Transcorp Hotels Plc (“the Company”) will meet on Thursday,
October 15, 2026 , to consider amongst other things, the Company’s Quarter
3, 2026 Unaudited Financial Statements for the quarter ending September 30,
2026 (Q3 2026 Financials).
In compliance with the provisions of 17.18 (a) Closed Period Rules. Rulebook of
the Exchange, 2015 (As amended) (Issuers’ Rules), the Closed Period in the
Company’s shares will commence on Thursday, October 1, 2026, until twenty-
four (24) hours after the Q3, 2026 Financials are filed with NGX and released to
the public.
Consequently, no insiders and their connected persons may directly or
indirectly deal in the securities of the Company until twenty-four (24) hours after
the release of the Q3, 2026 Financials through the Issuers’ Portal of NGX and by
extension, the general public.
Thank you.
