Corporate Actions Announcement
BETA GLASS PLC hereby announce as follows:
Period Ended 31st December 2025
A Final Dividend of ₦7.20 per ordinary share of 50 kobo, subject to
appropriate withholding tax and approval will be paid to shareholders
whose names appear in the Register of Members as at the close of
Proposed Dividend business on 3rd day of June 2026.
Proposed Bonus Nil.
Closure of Register The Register of Shareholders will be closed from 4th to 11th June 2026.
Qualification Date 3rd June 2026
On 25th June 2026, dividends will be paid electronically to
shareholders whose names appear on the Register of Members as at
3rd June 2026 and who have completed the e-dividend registration and
mandated the Registrar to pay their dividends directly into their Bank
Payment Date accounts.
Shareholders who are yet to complete the e-dividend registration are
advised to download the Registrar’s E-Dividend Mandate Activation
Form, which is also available on the website of the Registrars:
E-Dividend Greenwich Registrars and Data Solutions Limited, complete and submit
Registration to the Registrar or their respective Banks.
Shareholders with dividend warrants and share certificates that have
Unclaimed Dividend remained unclaimed or are yet to be presented for payment or
Warrants and Share returned for validation are advised to complete the e-dividend
Certificates registration or contact the Registrar.
Date of Annual The Company’s Annual General Meeting will be held at Landmark
General Meeting Event Center on Thursday, 25th June 2026 at 11:00 a.m.
