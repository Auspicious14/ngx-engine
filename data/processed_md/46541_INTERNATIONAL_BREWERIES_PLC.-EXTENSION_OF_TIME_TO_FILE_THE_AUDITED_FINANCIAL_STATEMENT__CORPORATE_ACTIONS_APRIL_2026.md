NOTICE OF DELAY IN FILING AUDITED FINANCIAL STATEMENTS
This is to inform our valued Shareholders and the investing public that International
Breweries Plc (‘’the Company’’) was unable to file its Audited Financial Statements for the
year ended 31 December 2025 (2025 AFS), by the stipulated deadline of March 31, 2026, as
required under the Rules of Nigerian Exchange Limited (“The Exchange” or “NGX”).
This is as a result of unforeseen delays in the completion of the Company’s audit process by
its External Auditors. While the audit is ongoing, the Company is working closely with its
Auditors to ensure that the 2025 AFS are completed in line with applicable regulatory
standards and best practices. The 2025 AFS and 2026 Q1 UFS will be filed no later than
April 8, 2026 and April 30, 2026, respectively.
We are currently working with our Auditors to ensure prompt and accurate report of the
financial results. We appreciate the patience and understanding of all stakeholders during
this period and reaffirm our dedication to upholding the principles of good governance.
Thank you.
