Lagos, 27th March 2026
NOTIFICATION OF CHAMS HOLDCO 80TH BOARD OF DIRECTORS’ MEETING
SCHEDULED TO HOLD ON 27TH MARCH 2026
Chams Holding Company Plc (Chams or the Company) wishes to inform Nigerian Exchange
Limited (NGX) and the investing public that the 80th Board of Directors Meeting is scheduled
to hold on Friday, 27th March 2026, to consider its Audited Financial Statements for the year
ended 31st December 2025.
In compliance with NGX’s Listing requirements, Chams Holding Company has declared the
commencement of a closed period for trading in its shares by insiders and their connected
persons from 1 January 2026 until twenty-four (24) hours after the Audited Financial
st
Statements for the year ended 31st December 2025 have been published or released to the
market.
