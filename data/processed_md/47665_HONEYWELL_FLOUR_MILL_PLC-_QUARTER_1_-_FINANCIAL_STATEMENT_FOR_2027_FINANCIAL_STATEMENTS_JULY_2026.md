1 REPORTING ENTITY

the Company's ownership structure led to a change of the name to Honeywell Flour Mills Limited in June, 1995. The
CompanywasconvertedtoaPublicLiabilityCompanyin2008.ItsshareswerelistedontheNigeriaStockExchange(NSE)
