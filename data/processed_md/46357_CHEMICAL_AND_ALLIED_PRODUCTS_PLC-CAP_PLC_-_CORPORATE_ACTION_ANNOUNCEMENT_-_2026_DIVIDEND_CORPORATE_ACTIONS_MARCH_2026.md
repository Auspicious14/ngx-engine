Chemical and Allied Products PLC hereby announces as follows:
Year Ended December 31, 2025
Proposed Dividend A dividend of N4.00 kobo per ordinary share of 50 kobo each, subject to the deduction of
appropriate withholding tax and approval at the forthcoming Annual General Meeting
(“AGM”) of the Company will be paid to shareholders whose names appear in the Register
of Members as at the close of business on Wednesday, June 3, 2026.
Proposed Bonus Not Applicable
Closure of Register The Register of Members will be closed from Thursday June 4, 2026, to Monday, June 8, 2026
(both dates inclusive).
Qualification Date June 3, 2026
Cash Dividend On Thursday, June 25 2026, dividends will be paid electronically to shareholders (“Qualifying
Payment Date Shareholders”) whose names appear on the Register of Members as of Wednesday, June
3, 2026, and who have completed the e-dividend registration and mandated the Registrar
to pay their dividends directly into their Bank accounts.
E-Dividend Registration Shareholders who are yet to claim their outstanding dividends are hereby advised to fill the
online Form available at https://docuhub3.nibss-plc.com.ng/edmms/self-service or
download the Registrar’s E-Dividend Activation Form, which is available at
https://drive.google.com/file/d/1chQJnmkkDt4WrV_tbApAqvbloO-Ij-mV/view and submit
to the Registrar at Africa Prudential PLC - 220b, Ikorodu Road, Palmgrove, Lagos State or
their respective Banks for the purpose of claiming their outstanding dividends.
Unclaimed Dividend Shareholders with dividend warrants and share certificates that have remained unclaimed
Warrants and Share or are yet to be presented for payment or returned for validation are advised to complete
Certificates the E-Dividend registration or contact the Registrar.
Date of Annual The Company’s Annual General Meeting will be held on Wednesday, June 24 2026 at
General Meeting Festival Hotel, Diamond Estate, Amuwo Odofin, Lagos State by 10:00 a.m. or on such other
date as may be communicated by the Company.
