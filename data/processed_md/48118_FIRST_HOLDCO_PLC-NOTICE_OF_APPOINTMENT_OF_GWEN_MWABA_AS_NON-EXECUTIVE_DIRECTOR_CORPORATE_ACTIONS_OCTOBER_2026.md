NOTICE OF APPOINTMENT OF GWEN MWABA AS NON-EXECUTIVE DIRECTOR
In accordance with Nigerian Exchange Limited (NGX) Rulebook, we hereby notify NGX and the
investing public that the Board of Directors (“the Board”) of First HoldCo Plc (“FirstHoldCo” or “the
Company”) is pleased to announce the appointment of Gwen Mwaba as a Non-Executive Director
(NED) with effect from October 15, 2026, following the approval of the Central Bank of Nigeria.
Profile of Gwen Mwaba, Non-Executive Director
Ms. Gwen Mwaba was appointed to the Board of Directors of First HoldCo Plc as Non-Executive
Director on 15th October 2026. She brings to the Board her almost three decades of experience
spread across global banking and development finance institutions, with particular expertise in credit
risk oversight, portfolio governance, corporate and sovereign lending, structured trade and
commodity finance, development finance and emerging markets.
Until recently, she served as Managing Director, Trade Finance & Correspondent Banking at the
African Export-Import Bank (Afreximbank) based in Cairo, Egypt. She had executive leadership
responsibility for trade finance and structured credit activities across Africa and the Caribbean and
oversaw a US$19.5 billion portfolio of sovereign, financial institution and corporate exposures. She
also contributed to the Bank's Pre Credit and Credit Committees, providing senior credit judgement
on major lending transactions and oversaw portfolio strategy, sector exposures and country risk
limits.
Prior to that role, Gwen served as Regional Head, Anglophone West Africa at Afreximbank,
overseeing regional strategy and credit origination across Nigeria, Ghana, Sierra Leone, Liberia, The
Gambia and Cape Verde. Earlier, she was Executive Vice President, Structured Trade & Commodity
Finance at Standard Bank Plc, London, where she was responsible for complex commodity finance,
export-credit-agency-backed facilities and pre-export financing transactions across Africa.
Her earlier banking career included senior roles at Stanbic Bank Zambia and Standard Bank South
Africa, including Head of Vehicle & Asset Finance and Head of Corporate Banking.
