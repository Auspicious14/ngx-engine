PUBLICATION OF FIRST HOLDCO PLC’S GROUP AUDITED CONSOLIDATED AND SEPARATE
FINANCIAL STATEMENTS FOR THE YEAR ENDED DECEMBER 31, 2025
First HoldCo Plc (“the Company”) wishes to inform Nigerian Exchange Limited (NGX) and the
investing public that the Company is in the process of securing the final approval of the Central Bank
of Nigeria (CBN) for its Group Audited Consolidated and Separate Financial Statements for the year
ended December 31, 2025 (“2025 AFS”) and it is envisaged that there could be a potential delay in
the publication of the 2025 AFS.
The Company has communicated this to the NGX and has obtained approval for an extension of
time within which the Company will publish its 2025 AFS, subject to the CBN’s approval of the
Results.
The Company’s Insiders are reminded that the Closed Period declared by the Company from January
1, 2026, will continue till twenty-four (24) hours after the 2025 AFS are filed via the Issuers’ Portal
of NGX, in line with Rule 17.18 (a) Closed Period Rules, Rulebook of the Exchange, 2015 (As
amended) (Issuers’ Rules).
