30 September 2026
NOTIFICATION OF RESIGNATION OF DIRECTOR
PZ Cussons Nigeria Plc (“PZCN” or “the Company”) hereby notifies its esteemed shareholders,
the investing public, and Nigerian Exchange Limited (“NGX”) that Mr. Kareem Moustafa, a Non-
Executive Director of the Company, has resigned from the Board of the Company with effect
from 28th September 2026.
The Board expresses its sincere appreciation to Mr. Moustafa’s contributions and service to
the Company and wishes him success in his future endeavours.
OGHENEKEVWE OGEFERE
Company Secretary
