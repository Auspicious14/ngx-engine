Red Star Express Plc. hereby announce as follows:
Updated Corporate Action
Period Ended 31 March 2026
A Final Dividend of 45 Kobo per 50 kobo ordinary share payable to shareholders whose names appear
Proposed Dividend on the Register of Members as at the close of business on Tuesday, 1st September 2026.
Closure of Register The Register of Shareholders will be closed from Wednesday, 2nd to Friday, 4th September 2026.
Qualification Date Tuesday 1st September 2026
On Friday 18th September 2026, dividends will be paid electronically to shareholders whose names
appear on the Register of Members as at Tuesday, 1st September 2026, and who have completed the
e-dividend registration and mandated the Registrar to pay their dividends directly into their Bank
Payment Date accounts.
Shareholders who are yet to complete the e-dividend registration are advised to download the
Registrar’s E-Dividend Mandate Activation Form, which is also available on their
E-Dividend website: https://registrars.apel.com.ng/?ddownload=292 complete and submit to the Registrar or
Registration their respective Banks. All other inquiries may be made via registrars.apel.com.ng/downloads
Unclaimed Dividend Shareholders with dividend warrants and share certificates that have remained unclaimed, or are yet
Warrants and Share to be presented for payment or returned for validation are advised to complete the e-dividend
Certificates registration or contact the Registrar.
Date of General
Meeting The Company’s Annual General Meeting will be held on Thursday, 17th September 2026 at 11.00am.
