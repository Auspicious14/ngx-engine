Lomé, October 02, 2026
NOTICE OF CLOSED PERIOD
We write to notify the Nigerian Exchange Limited (“NGX”), shareholders of Ecobank
Transnational Incorporated (“ETI”) and all stakeholders that the Board of Directors of ETI
shall hold a board meeting on October 22, 2026 to consider the 3rd Quarter Unaudited
Financial Statements for the period ended September 30, 2026 (Q3 2026 UFS).
In compliance with Rule 17.18(a) of the NGX Issuers Rules, 2015 as amended, a closed
period has commenced from October 1, 2026, and will continue until twenty-four (24)
hours after the outcome of the board meeting and publication of the Q3 2026 UFS on
NGX.
During the closed period, no insider of ETI or its affiliates may deal directly or indirectly in
ETI shares.
