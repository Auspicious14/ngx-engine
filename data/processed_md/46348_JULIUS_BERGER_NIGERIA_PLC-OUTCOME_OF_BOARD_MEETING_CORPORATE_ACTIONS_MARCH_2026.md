JULIUS BERGER NIGERIA PLC
OUTCOME OF BOARD MEETING
FCT Abuja, March 27, 2026
Julius Berger Nigeria Plc ("the Company") wishes to notify its shareholders,
Nigerian Exchange Limited (“NGX” or “The Exchange”) and the investing public
that the Company’s Board of Directors ("the Board") met on Thursday March 26,
2026 as scheduled. The Board considered and approved the Audited Reports and
Consolidated Separate Financial Statements (“AR & C&SFS”) for the year ended
December 31, 2025, as well as the declaration of dividend.
The AR&C&SFS will be filed with The Exchange on or before March 31, 2026.
Consequently, the closed period entered by the Company restricting its Directors
and other insiders from trading on the shares of the Company will remain in place
until 24 (twenty-four) hours after the AR & C&SFS have been released to the public.
CECILIA E. MADUEKE, ESQ
COMPANY SECRETARY
JULIUS BERGER NIGERIA PLC
