Lagos, 30th September, 2026
NOTICE OF BOARD MEETING AND CLOSED PERIOD OF SUNU ASSURANCES NIGERIA PLC
NOTICE IS HEREBY GIVEN that a meeting of the Board of Directors of SUNU Assurances Nigeria
Plc (“the Company”) is scheduled to hold on Friday 23rd October, 2026 to review and approve the
Company’s Unaudited Financial Statements for the third quarter ending 30th September, 2026.
Pursuant to the provisions of rule 17.18(a) Closed Period Rules, Rulebook of The Exchange, 2015
(as amended) (Issuers’ Rules), the Company’s closed period shall commence with effect from
Thursday, 1st October 2026, for trading in its shares. The closed period will remain until twenty-four
(24) hours after the Company’s Unaudited Financial Statements for the period ending 30th
September, 2026 have been released to the public.
Consequently, no insider of the Company and their connected persons shall deal in the securities of
the Company, directly or indirectly when the trading window is closed.
