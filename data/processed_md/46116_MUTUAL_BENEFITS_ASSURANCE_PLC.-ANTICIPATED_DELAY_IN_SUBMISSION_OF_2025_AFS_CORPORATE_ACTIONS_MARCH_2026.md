MUTUAL BENEFITS ASSURANCE PLC - SUBMISSION OF AUDITED FINANCIAL
STATEMENTS FOR THE YEAR ENDED 31ST DECEMBER 2025 & FIRST QTR 2026
UNAUDITED FINANCIAL STATEMENT EXTENSION OF TIME
Mutual Benefits Assurance Plc (the Company) hereby informs Nigerian Exchange
Limited (NGX or The Exchange), our valued shareholders, investing public, and key
stakeholders that the release of the Audited Financial Statements for the year ended
31 December 2025 (2025 AFS) and the Unaudited Financial Statements for the
quarter ending 31 March 2026 (2026 UFS Q1) of the Company will be delayed.
The board is scheduled to meet on 27th February 2026 to approve the 2025 Audited
Financial Statements. The Company has also received a notification from its primary
regulator (National Insurance Commission) that the capital verification exercise, in
line with the new Minimum Capital Requirement, will run from 23rd February to 16th
March 2026. We believe that the simultaneous review of the Audited Financial
Statements and the capital verification exercise may result in a longer-than-usual
timeline for the regulator's approval of the 2025 AFS.
We estimate that the 2025 AFS and the 2026 UFS Q1 will be released on or before
30th June 2026. An extension of time has been approved by NGX to allow for late
filing in view of the reasons adduced above.
Thank you.
