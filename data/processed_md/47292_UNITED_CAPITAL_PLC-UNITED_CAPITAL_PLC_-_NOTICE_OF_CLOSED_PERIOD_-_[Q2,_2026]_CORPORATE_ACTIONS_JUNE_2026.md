LAGOS, NIGERIA | JUNE 29, 2026
UNITED CAPITAL PLC – NOTICE OF Q2, 2026 BOARD MEETING AND CLOSED
PERIOD
The Board of United Capital Plc (the “Company”) will meet in Lagos on Friday,
July 24, 2026, to consider, among other things, the Company’s Unaudited
Financial Statements for the quarter ending June 30, 2026 (the “Q2, 2026
Financials”).
In accordance with the post-listing requirements of the Nigerian Exchange
Limited (“NGX”), the closed period for trading in the shares of the Company
will commence on July 1, 2026, in respect of the Q2, 2026 Financials.
Hence, no Insider of the Company and their connected persons may directly
or indirectly deal in the securities of the Company until 24 hours after the
release of the Q2, 2026 Financials to NGX and by extension, the general public.
Thank you.
