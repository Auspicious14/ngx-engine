Notification of Board Meeting and Commencement of Closed Period for
International Breweries Plc
Pursuant to the provisions of the Nigerian Exchange Limited (“NGX”) Rulebook and
the Insider Trading Policy of International Breweries Plc (“the Company”), we hereby
notify NGX, our shareholders and the investing public that the Board of Directors of
the Company is scheduled to meet on Friday, 27 March 2026 to consider the
Audited Financial Statements for the year ended 31st December 2025, amongst
other matters.
The closed period which commenced on Thursday 1st January 2026 will remain in
effect until twenty-four (24) hours after the Audited Financial Statements for the
year ended 31st December 2025 is released to NGX and the public.
During this period, all insiders and their connected persons are advised not to trade
in the shares of the Company.
