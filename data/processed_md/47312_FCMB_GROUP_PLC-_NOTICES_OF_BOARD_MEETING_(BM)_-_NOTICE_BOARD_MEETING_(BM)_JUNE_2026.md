Lagos, Nigeria- June 30, 2026
NOTICE OF FCMB GROUP PLC BOARD OF DIRECTORS’ MEETING AND CLOSED PERIOD
Notice is hereby given that the Board of Directors’ meeting of FCMB Group Plc (the
Company) is scheduled to hold on Friday, July 24, 2026, at 10:00 am via Microsoft Teams.
The meeting is the regular quarterly meeting to consider the 2026 Q2 performance report of
the Group.
FCMB Group Plc hereby declares the commencement of the closed period for trading in the
Company's shares by insiders and related parties with effect from July 1, 2026, in respect of
the Unaudited Financial Statements for the quarter ending June 30, 2026. The closed period
shall remain in effect until 24 hours after the publication of the financial statements.
Olufunmilayo Adedibu (Mrs.)
Company Secretary
