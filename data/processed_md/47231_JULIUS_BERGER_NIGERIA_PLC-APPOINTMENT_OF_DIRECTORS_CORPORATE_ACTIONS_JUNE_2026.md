JULIUS BERGER NIGERIA PLC (“JULIUS BERGER” OR “THE COMPANY”)
NOTICE OF CHANGES TO THE DIRECTORATE OF JULIUS BERGER
FCT Abuja, June 22, 2026
This is to notify Nigerian Exchange Limited (NGX) and the public that the Board of Directors of Julius
Berger Nigeria Plc (“Julius Berger” or “the Company”) at its Meeting held on Wednesday, June 17, 2026:
appointed:
1. Dr. Muiz Adeyemi Banire, Ph. D, SAN, OON, as a Non- Executive Director on the Board, with effect from
July 1, 2026;
2. Mrs. Chinazo Okoye as Alternate Director to Dr. Ernest N. Azudialu- Obiejesi, OFR.
Please find stated hereunder a brief profile of the new Directors which shows the wealth of experience they
will bring to bear on their functions as Directors as well as the future directions of the Group:
Dr. Muiz Adeyemi Banire, Ph. D, SAN, OON is a Legal Practitioner. He is a graduate of the University of
Lagos, Lagos State and holds a Master of Law Degree as well as a Doctor of Philosophy Degree from the
same University. He was called to the Nigerian Bar in 1989. He also holds a Master Certificate in Arbitration
Master Course from the Lagos Court of Arbitration Training Institute. He was admitted to the Inner Bar and
conferred with the rank of Senior Advocate of Nigeria. He is a Member of the International Bar Association,
Institute of Chartered Secretaries and Administrators, Associate Members of the Chartered Institute of
Arbitrators, Fellow of the Institute of Transport Administration of Nigeria, and Nigerian Institute of
Management. He is registered with the Law Society England.
He had served in various capacities in the Legal profession, academic community and publicly. He was
conferred with the National Honour of the Officer of the Order of the Niger (OON).
He is the Founder and Principal Partner, M. A. Banire & Associates, Member of the Board of Trustees,
Fountain University, Osogbo, Osun State, and Pro-Chancellor of the University of Uyo, Akwa Ibom State.
The Board of Julius Berger Nigeria Plc appointed him as a Non-Executive Director with effect from July 1,
2026.
