Lagos, 9th July 2026.
NOTIFICATION OF BOARD MEETING – HALDANE MCCALL PLC
This is to notify the shareholders of Haldane McCall Plc (the Company), Nigerian Exchange Limited (NGX) and the
Investing Public that a meeting of the Board of Directors of the Company has been scheduled to hold on Friday July
24, 2026.
The purpose of the meeting is to consider and approve the Unaudited Financial Statements for the period ended June
30, 2026 (Q2, 2026 UFS).
Pursuant to Rule 17:18(a) of the Exchange’s Rulebook on Closed Period (As amended), the Closed Period which
commenced on 1st June 2026, shall remain in effect until twenty-four (24) hours after the release of the Company’s
Q2 2026 UFS to the public.
Consequently, no Insider of the Company and their connected persons shall deal in the products of the Company or
with sensitive information, directly or indirectly during this Closed Period.
FOR: HALDANE MCCALL PLC
