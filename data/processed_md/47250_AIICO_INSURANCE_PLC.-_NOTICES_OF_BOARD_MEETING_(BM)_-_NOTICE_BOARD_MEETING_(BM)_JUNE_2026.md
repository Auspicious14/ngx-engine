Lagos, June 24, 2026
NOTICE OF Q2, 2026 BOARD MEETING OF AIICO INSURANCE PLC AND CLOSED PERIOD
NOTICE IS HEREBY GIVEN that the Board of AIICO Insurance Plc (the Company) will meet on July
30, 2026, to review and approve the draft Unaudited Financial Statements for the period ending
June 30, 2026 (Q2, 2026).
Any decision reached that affects the approval of the accounts will immediately be communicated
to Nigerian Exchange Limited (The Exchange).
In compliance with the provisions of Rule 17.18(a): Closed Period, Rulebook of The Exchange, 2015
(Issuers’ Rule) as amended, the Company shall commence a Closed Period from Wednesday, 1
July 2026 until twenty-four (24) hours after the release of the Company’s Unaudited Financial
Statements for the period ending June 30, 2026, and other decisions taken at the Board Meeting.
During the closed period, no insider of the Company shall deal in the securities of the Company.
