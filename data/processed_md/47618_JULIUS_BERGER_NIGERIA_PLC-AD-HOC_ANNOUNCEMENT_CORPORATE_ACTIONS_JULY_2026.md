JULIUS BERGER NIGERIA PLC (“JULIUS BERGER” OR “THE COMPANY”)
AD-HOC ANNOUNCEMENT
ESTABLISHMENT OF JULIUS BERGER GULF LLC
FCT Abuja, July 29, 2026
Julius Berger Nigeria Plc (“Julius Berger” or “the Company”) wishes to notify its Shareholders,
Nigerian Exchange Limited (“The Exchange”) and the investing public that its wholly owned
subsidiary, Julius Berger International GmbH (“JBI”), has resolved to establish, incorporate, and
register a subsidiary company, limited by shares, under the provisions of the laws of the Kingdom
of Saudi Arabia, (“KSA”) to carry on the business of advisory services, consulting, design and
engineering services and project management in the KSA. The establishment of this subsidiary
would enable JBI and Julius Berger be responsive to the emerging business opportunities, in the
KSA and environs.
