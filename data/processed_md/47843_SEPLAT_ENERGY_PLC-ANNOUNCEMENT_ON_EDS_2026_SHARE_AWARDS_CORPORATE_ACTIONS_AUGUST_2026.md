NOTIFICATION OF TRANSACTIONS OF PERSONS DISCHARGING MANAGERIAL
RESPONSIBILITIES
Seplat Energy PLC (“Seplat” or “Company”)
Share Awards granted under the Seplat Energy PLC 2024 Long Term Incentive Plan ("LTIP")
The following awards (“LTIP Awards”) over ordinary shares of Seplat were granted under the LTIP to
Executive Directors of the Company on 18 August 2026 (the “Award Date”) as set out below using the
average share price over the 5-day period preceding to the date of grant. For the avoidance of doubt, the
LTIP awards are granted in respect of the 2026 financial year.
Vesting of the LTIP Awards is dependent on the achievement of business and individual performance
underpins and continued employment over a three-year performance period which began on 01 January
2026. The LTIP Awards will be subject to a two-year post vesting holding period. Full details of the vesting
conditions of these LTIP Awards will be set out in the Company’s 2026 Annual Report.
Name Position Total Number of Shares over
which the award is granted*
Effiong Okon Chief Executive Officer 194,512
Samson Ezugworie Chief Operations Officer 123,582
Eleanor Adaralegbe Chief Financial Officer 117,550
*In line with the 2025 Directors’ Remuneration Policy, the number of shares awarded was calculated by
dividing the monetary value of the salary multiple relevant for each Executive Director by the grant price,
which equals the five-day average closing share price prior to the date on which the LTIP Awards were
granted (i.e. £6.106).
The Remuneration Committee retains discretion to make adjustments to the LTIP Awards vesting level and
amounts payable resulting from the application of the performance measures if it considers that the
outcomes are not a fair and accurate reflection of corporate performance.

On 18 August 2026 (the Award date), the Company also granted the following awards ("Deferred Share
Awards") in respect of the 2025 Annual Performance Bonus over Shares under the LTIP to Executive
Directors of the Company as set out below. Under the Directors' Remuneration Policy approved by
shareholders at Seplat's 13th Annual General Meeting, 25% of the executive directors' annual performance
bonus relating to the 2025 financial year is deferred into an award over shares and the shares will normally
vest and be released on 31 December 2027 (two years following the end of the performance year in respect
of which the Deferred Share Award is granted) subject to continued employment in line with the LTIP rules.
