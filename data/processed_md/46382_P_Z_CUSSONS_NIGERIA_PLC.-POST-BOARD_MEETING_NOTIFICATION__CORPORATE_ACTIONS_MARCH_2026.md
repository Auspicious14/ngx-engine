Lagos, 30th March 2026
PZ CUSSONS NIGERIA PLC – APPROVAL OF THE UNAUDITED FINANCIAL
STATEMENTS FOR THE THIRD QUARTER OF 2025/2026 FINANCIAL YEAR
This is to notify our esteemed Shareholders, the investing public and the Nigerian
Exchange Limited that the Board of Directors of PZ Cussons Nigeria PLC (“the
Company”) held a meeting on Friday, 27th March 2026, to consider and approve,
amongst other things, the Company’s Unaudited Financial Statements for the third
quarter ended 28th February 2026.
Please note that the Company’s closed period, which commenced on 1st March 2026,
will remain in effect until twenty-four (24) hours after the release of the Unaudited
Financial Statements to the market.
