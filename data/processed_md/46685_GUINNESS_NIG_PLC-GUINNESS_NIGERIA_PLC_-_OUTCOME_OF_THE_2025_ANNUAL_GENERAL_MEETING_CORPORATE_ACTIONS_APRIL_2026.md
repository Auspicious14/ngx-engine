GUINNESS NIGERIA PLC
Lagos, 16 April 2026
RESOLUTIONS PASSED AT THE 75TH ANNUAL GENERAL MEETING OF
GUINNESS NIGERIA PLC
Guinness Nigeria Plc (the “Company”) hereby notifies Nigerian Exchange Limited (“NGX”)
and the investing public that the 75th Annual General Meeting of the Company was held on
the 15th day of April 2026, at the Grand Banquet Hall, Civic Centre, Ozumba Mbadiwe Road,
Victoria Island, Lagos, where the following resolutions were considered by the Members, and
duly passed:
1) That the Audited Financial Statements for the year ended 31st December 2025, the
Report of the Directors, the Independent Auditors Report, and the Statutory Audit
Committee’s Report thereon, were laid before the meeting and were duly received.
2) That the appointment of the following persons as Directors of the Company was
approved:
a. Mr. Mayank Kabra.
b. Mrs. Olusola Oworu.
c. Mrs. Bola Adesola.
3) That the re-election of the following Directors of the Company, was approved:
a. Mrs. Vivien Shobo.
b. Mr. John Musunga.
c. Mr. Deepak Singhal.
4) That the disclosure of the remuneration of the Managers of the Company, pursuant to
the Companies and Allied Matters Act (CAMA) 2020, was noted by the Members.
5) That the appointment of Ernst & Young as the Independent External Auditors for the
Company with effect from 1 January 2026 (i.e. Financial Year 2026) was approved, and
that the Directors were authorized to fix the remuneration of the Independent External
Auditors for the Financial Year 2026.
6) That the following persons were re-elected as members of the Statutory Audit
Committee:
a. Mr. Moses Igbrude.
b. Mr. George Ibhade.
c. Mrs. Joy Onome Adewuyi.
