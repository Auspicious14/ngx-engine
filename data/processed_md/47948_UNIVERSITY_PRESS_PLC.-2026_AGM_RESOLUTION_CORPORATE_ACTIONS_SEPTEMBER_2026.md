Ibadan, 25th September 2026
RESOLUTIONS REACHED AT THE 48TH ANNUAL GENERAL MEETING (AGM) BY
THE MEMBERS OF UNIVERSITY PRESS PLC HELD AT KAKANFO INN &
CONFERENCE CENTRE, 1, NIHINLOLA STREET, JOYCE B ROAD, OFF RING ROAD,
IBADAN, OYO STATE ON THURSDAY, 24TH SEPTEMBER, 2026
At the 48th Annual General Meeting of University Press Plc (the Company), duly convened and
held on Thursday 24th September 2026 at Kakanfo Inn & Conference Centre, 1, Nihinlola Street,
Joyce B Road, off Ring Road, Ibadan, the following resolutions presented before members of the
Company were duly passed and approved:
ORDINARY RESOLUTIONS
1. To lay the Audited Financial Statements of the Company for the year ended 31st March
2026, and Reports of the Directors, the Auditor and Audit Committee’s Report thereon.
2. Declaration of a dividend of 18kobo per share for the financial year ended 31st March
2026.
Re-election of ARC. AYODEJI OLORUNDA , MR. YOMI ADEWUSI & HRM
3.
JOSEPHINE DIETE-SPIFF as Non-Executive Directors.
-
Ratification of the appointment of Maj-Gen. Daniel Danjuma Kitchener (Rtd) as
4.
Independent Non-Executive Director with effect from 1st August 2026.
5. Authorisation of the Directors to fix the remuneration of the Auditors in line with the
provisions of Section 408 of the Companies & Allied Matters Act 2020.
6. Election of shareholders’ representative on the Statutory Audit Committee: MR. AYUBA
QUADRI YEMI, MRS. UTERE INIBONG OBOT and MR. FOLOWOSELE MICHEAL
OLUDARE were elected as shareholder members of the Statutory Audit Committee of
the Company. HRM DR. JOSEPHINE A. DIETE-SPIFF AND PROF. TRACIE CHIMA
UTOH-EZEAJUGH will represent the Board on the Committee.
SPECIAL BUSINESS
7. Approval of Non-Executive Directors’ remuneration.
