NOTICE OF BOARD MEETING AND CLOSED PERIOD FOR CUTIX PLC
Cutix Plc (the Company) hereby notifies Nigerian Exchange Limited (NGX) and the investing
public of the meeting of the Board of Directors of the Company scheduled to hold on Friday,
November 27, 2026, to consider, amongst other things, approve the Second Quarter Unaudited
Financial Statements (Q2 UFS) for the period ending October 31, 2026.
Consequently, in compliance with the Rulebook of the Nigerian Exchange Limited, the
Company hereby announces the commencement of a Closed Period for trading on its shares
from November 1, 2026, until twenty-four (24) hours after the release of the Q2 UFS to the
general public.
All Cutix Plc’s insiders and related persons are prohibited from dealing on the Company’s
shares until 24 hours after the Q2 UFS has been made public.
