Lagos, Nigeria – April 29, 2026
Notice of Delay in Publishing the Q1 2026 Unaudited Financial Statements of
FCMB Group Plc.
FCMB Group Plc (“FCMB Group” or “the Company”) hereby notifies Nigerian
Exchange Limited (NGX or The Exchange), our esteemed stakeholders, and the
investing community at large of a possible delay in the release of our Unaudited
Financial Statements for the quarter ended 31 March 2026 (Q1 2026 Unaudited
Financial Statements).
The delay is due to the pending approval of our Audited Financial Statements for the
year ended 31 December 2025 (2025 AFS), from our primary regulator, the Central
Bank of Nigeria (CBN). The approval and filing of the 2025 AFS is a prerequisite to the
publication of the Company’s Q1 2026 Unaudited Financial Statements.
We are working earnestly to ensure that both the Audited Financial Statements for
the financial year ended 31 December 2025, and the Q1 2026 Unaudited Financial
Statements are submitted to NGX as soon as we receive approval of the 2025 AFS
from the CBN. This is likely to occur on or before 31 May 2026. We will promptly
notify The Exchange and the investing public as soon as we obtain the necessary
approval.
Olufunmilayo Adedibu (Mrs.)
Company Secretary
