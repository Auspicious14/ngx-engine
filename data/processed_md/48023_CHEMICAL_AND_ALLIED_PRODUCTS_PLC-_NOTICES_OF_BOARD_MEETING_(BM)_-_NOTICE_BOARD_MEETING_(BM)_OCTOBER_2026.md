NOTICE OF Q3 2026 BOARD MEETING AND CLOSED PERIOD
The Board of Directors of Chemical and Allied Products PLC (the “Company”) will meet in
Lagos on Tuesday, October 27, 2026, to consider, among other things, the Company’s

Financials”).
In accordance with the relevant provisions of the Rule Book of the Nigerian Exchange Limited
(“NGX”), the Closed Period for trading in the shares of the Company commenced on October
1, 2026, in respect of the Q3 2026 Financials.

information, advisers and consultants of the Company, other insiders, and their connected
persons may directly or indirectly deal in the securities of the Company until twenty (24) hours
after the release of the Q3 2026 Financials via NGX’s Issuers’ Portal and by extension, to the
general public.
