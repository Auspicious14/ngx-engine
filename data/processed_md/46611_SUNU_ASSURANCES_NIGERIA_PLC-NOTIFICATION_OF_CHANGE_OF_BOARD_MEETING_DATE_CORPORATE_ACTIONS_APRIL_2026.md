Lagos, 8th April, 2026
NOTIFICATION OF CHANGE OF BOARD MEETING DATE
SUNU Assurances Nigeria Plc (the “Company”) wishes to inform its esteemed shareholders and the investing
public that the Board meeting of the Company, earlier scheduled to hold on Wednesday, 22nd April 2026 to review
and approve the Quarterly Financial Statements for the first quarter ended 31st March 2026 (2026 Q1 UFS), has
now been rescheduled to hold on Monday 27th April, 2026.
The Closed Period shall remain in effect until twenty-four (24) hours after the release of the Company’s 2026 Q1
UFS to Nigerian Exchange Limited (NGX).
