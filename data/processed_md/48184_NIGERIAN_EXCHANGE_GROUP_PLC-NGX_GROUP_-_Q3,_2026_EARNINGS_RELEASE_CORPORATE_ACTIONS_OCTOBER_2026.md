Digital Access Drives NGX Group to ₦20.6 Billion Nine-Month Profit as Transaction
Revenue More Than Double
Profit before tax up 83.3% to ₦20.6 billion | Transaction fees more than double to ₦20.7 billion | Cost-to-
income ratio improves to 49.7%
Lagos, Nigeria – 27 October 2026: Nigerian Exchange Group Plc (“NGX Group” or “the Group”) has
reported profit before tax of ₦20.6 billion for the nine months ended 30 September 2026, up 83.3% from
₦11.2 billion in the same period of 2025. Profit after tax rose 86.4% to ₦14.7 billion, already 40% above
the Group’s full-year 2025 profit after tax of ₦10.5 billion, with a quarter of the year still to run.
The performance reflects a structural shift in Nigeria’s capital market: more investors, increasingly retail
and increasingly onboarding through digital self-service channels, trading more often across a deeper
market. NGX Group is converting that activity into earnings while improving efficiency and investing in the
people and infrastructure the next phase of growth requires.

Significant Wealth Creation for Investors
The Group’s results mirror the value being created for investors across the market. As at 30 September
2026, the NGX All-Share Index had gained 61.43% year-to-date, while equity market capitalisation rose
by 64.12% from ₦99.38 trillion at December 2025 to ₦163.10 trillion, representing an increase of ₦63.72
trillion. This growth reflects the significant expansion in the value of listed equities held by millions of
Nigerians, pension funds and institutional investors.
Disciplined Investment for Scale
Total income grew faster than operating expenses (64.8% versus 56.8%), lowering the cost-to-income
ratio to 49.7% from 52.3% and lifting operating profit 73.5% to ₦14.9 billion.
Cost growth reflects deliberate investment in capacity ahead of the next stage of market development,
chiefly:
• People: strengthening talent in technology, market surveillance, product and investor engagement
to support a larger, faster-moving and more digital market;
• Market infrastructure and facilities: upgrading the physical and operational infrastructure that
underpins market resilience and continuity;
• Market development and partnerships: investor education, issuer engagement and the African
partnerships that broaden the Exchange’s reach.
These investments lay the foundation for the Group’s technology roadmap, including platform
modernisation, data services and digital access, which management expects to drive further operating
leverage as volumes scale.
Strategic Investments and Balance Sheet
Share of profit from equity-accounted investees rose 69.6% to ₦5.7 billion, driven primarily by Central
Securities Clearing System Plc (CSCS), underlining the value of the Group’s position across the market
value chain.
Total assets stood at ₦79.1 billion and shareholders’ equity at ₦61.1 billion as at 30 September 2026.
Shareholders received ₦8.2 billion in dividends during the period, up 86.7%, in addition to the 1-for-3
bonus issue.
