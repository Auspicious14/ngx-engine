NOTIFICATION OF SCHEME OF ARRANGEMENT AND EVOLUTION TO HOLDING COMPANY
STRUCTURE
LAGOS, NIGERIA – 18th November 2026 – Cornerstone Insurance Plc (NGX: CORNERST) ("Cornerstone"
or the "Group") hereby notifies The Nigerian Exchange Limited (“NGX”), its shareholders and the
investing public that it has received Federal Competition and Consumer Protection Commission
Approval, Sanction by the Federal High Court and Approval-in-Principle from the National Insurance
Commission (“NAICOM”) for its corporate evolution, establishing a new holding company with three
distinct insurance businesses: Cornerstone Life Assurance Limited, Cornerstone General Insurance
Limited, and Hilal Takaful Insurance Limited.
The realignment creates a more focused operating structure, with each business having dedicated
management, capital, and resources to serve its respective market. It also aligns the Group with the
Nigerian Insurance Industry Reform Act 2025, which mandates the separation of life and non-life
insurance operations.
Details of the Evolution
Under the new structure, Cornerstone Insurance Plc becomes Cornerstone Plc operating as a holding
company with oversight for growth initiatives, capital management and insurance businesses through:
• Cornerstone Life Assurance Limited, undertakes the Group's life insurance operations.
• Cornerstone General Insurance Limited (formerly FIN Insurance Company Limited),
undertakes the Group's general insurance operations.
• Hilal Takaful Insurance Limited, which will continue to undertake the Group's Takaful
insurance operations.
The Company's life insurance assets, liabilities and undertakings transfer to Cornerstone Life Assurance
Limited, while its general insurance assets, liabilities and undertakings transfer to Cornerstone General
Insurance Limited.
Implications for Shareholders, NGX Listing and Policyholders
Existing shareholders of Cornerstone Insurance Plc retain their shares and current holdings in
Cornerstone Plc. Cornerstone Plc will remain listed on NGX, maintaining its ticker symbol – CORNERST.
The new structure does not change insurance protection for any existing policyholders. All policies
remain valid under their existing terms and conditions, with premiums, benefits, policy periods, and
claims rights unaffected. Customers are not required to take any action, and existing service channels
and relationships remain available.
Customers of the former FIN Insurance Company Limited will now be served by Cornerstone General
Insurance Limited. This change does not affect their policy coverage, contractual benefits, or
outstanding claims, which continue without interruption.
Customers seeking further information can contact their existing Cornerstone or FIN Insurance
representatives or visit www.cornerstoneplc.com for additional details.
Next Steps
The evolution which is subject to NAICOM’s final approval represents an important step in positioning
Cornerstone for its next phase of growth, with a simplified corporate structure, stronger operating
businesses and greater strategic flexibility.
