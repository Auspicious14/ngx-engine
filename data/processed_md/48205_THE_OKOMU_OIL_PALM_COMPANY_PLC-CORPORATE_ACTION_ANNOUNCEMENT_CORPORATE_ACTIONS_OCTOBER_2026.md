Corporate Actions Announcement
THE OKOMU OIL PALM COMPANY PLC hereby announce as follows:
Period Ended 30th September 2026
An interim Dividend of N11.50 kobo per 50kobo ordinary share, subject to
appropriate withholding tax, will be paid to shareholders whose names appear
in the Register of Members as at the close of business on November 11th,
Proposed Dividend 2026.
Proposed Bonus NIL
The Register of Shareholders will be closed from November 12, 2026, to
Closure of Register November 13, 2026.
Qualification Date November 11, 2026
On November 17, 2026, dividends will be paid electronically to shareholders
whose names appear on the Register of Members as at November 11, 2026,
and who have completed the e-dividend registration and mandated the
Payment Date Registrar to pay their dividends directly into their Bank accounts.
Shareholders who are yet to complete the e-dividend registration are advised
to download the Registrar’s E-Dividend Mandate Activation Form, which is
also available on the website of the Registrars:
www.cardinalstoneregistrars.com, complete and submit to the Registrar or
E-Dividend Registration their respective Banks.
Shareholders with dividend warrants and share certificates that have
Unclaimed Dividend remained unclaimed, or are yet to be presented for payment or returned for
Warrants and Share validation, are advised to complete the e-dividend registration or contact the
Certificates Registrar.
