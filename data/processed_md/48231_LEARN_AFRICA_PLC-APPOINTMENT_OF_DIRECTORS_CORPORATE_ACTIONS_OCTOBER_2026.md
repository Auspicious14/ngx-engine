Learn Africa Plc Announces Changes to its Board of Directors
Lagos, Nigeria – 28th October 2026 – Learn Africa Plc (Learn Africa or the Company), Nigeria’s
largest educational publisher, announces the appointments of Dr. Bisi Aina, Mr. Babalola
Oluwole Awe and Mrs. Olanrewaju Oniyitan as Independent Non-Executive Directors effective
28th October 2026. In the same vein, Dr. Mufida Bello Hussain has also been appointed as a
Non-Executive Director effective 28th October 2026.
Dr. Bisi Aina is a seasoned business and technology executive with over 25 years of experience
in information technology, enterprise solutions, project management and strategic leadership.
He has extensive expertise in implementing large-scale Oracle ERP, Human Capital
Management and financial management systems across the public and private sectors,
including multinational operations. His experience spans business development, revenue
assurance, IT solutions architecture, organisational transformation and operational efficiency.
A trained veterinary doctor who transitioned into technology and business leadership, he
brings a multidisciplinary perspective to innovation, business sustainability and strategic
growth. He is also a certified Project Management Professional and Oracle Certified
Professional.
Mr. Babalola Oluwole Awe is a seasoned capital markets professional with over 30 years of
experience spanning stockbroking, investment banking, asset management and financial
services in Nigeria. He has held senior executive positions, including Managing Director/Chief
Executive Officer, and brings extensive experience in capital market operations, investment
management and corporate leadership. He is a Fellow of the Chartered Institute of
Stockbrokers of Nigeria and the Chartered Institute of Auctioneers of Nigeria, with additional
experience in entrepreneurship, business development and board governance. He holds a
Postgraduate Diploma in Financial Management and a Bachelor’s degree in Philosophy.
Mrs. Olanrewaju Oniyitan is a seasoned development and business professional with over
two decades of experience driving economic empowerment, education reform,
entrepreneurship, and sustainable development initiatives. She is widely recognized for her
strategic leadership, policy expertise, and commitment to human capital development, having
collaborated with diverse stakeholders across the public, private, and development sectors to
deliver impactful programs and institutional transformation. A respected thought leader,
author, and advocate for inclusive growth, she is passionate about empowering individuals
and organizations through innovation, capacity building, and practical solutions that foster
long-term social and economic progress.
Dr. Mufida Bello Hussain is an accomplished science educator, researcher, and academic
professional with over a decade of experience in STEM education, environmental biology,
teacher development, and educational leadership. She possesses strong expertise in
curriculum development, higher-order thinking skills, inclusive teaching practices,
environmental management, and student mentorship, having guided hundreds of students

and supervised numerous undergraduate research projects. A dedicated contributor to
educational innovation and capacity-building initiatives, she has participated in international
collaborations, professional development programs, and impactful research focused on STEM
learning, environmental sustainability, and teacher effectiveness. Her work reflects a strong
commitment to advancing quality education, fostering academic excellence, and promoting
sustainable development through research, teaching, and community engagement.
The Board expressed its full confidence in the appointees’ expertise, leadership capabilities,
and commitment to the Company’s vision and values. The Board believes that their diverse
experience and strategic insight will further strengthen governance, enhance organizational
performance, and support the Company’s long-term growth objectives. These appointments
represent a significant milestone in positioning the Company for sustained success, greater
innovation, and enhanced value creation for shareholders and stakeholders alike as it
continues to pursue its strategic ambitions and future opportunities.

About Learn Africa Plc
Learn Africa Plc was incorporated in August 1961 as a book publishing company, established
as a subsidiary of the renowned Longman Group (UK) Limited, now Pearson Education Limited,
one of the world’s largest education businesses.
From inception, the company’s mission was to market and distribute imported Longman
Group titles and to develop original content in English and indigenous languages to meet the
growing demand for books in the Nigerian market.
Today, Learn Africa stands as Nigeria’s largest educational publisher, with over 5,000 titles and
a workforce of 185 staff spread across the country. Listed on the Nigerian Stock Exchange since
23 July 1996, the Company holds the highest equity value in the publishing sector.
