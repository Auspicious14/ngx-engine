Lagos, 2nd October 2026
NOTICE OF CLOSED PERIOD
This is to inform Nigerian Exchange Limited (NGX or The Exchange) and our esteemed shareholders that
in compliance with the provisions of Rule 17.18(a) Rulebook of NGX, 2015 (as amended), FORTIS
GLOBAL INSURANCE Plc (the Company) has commenced a Closed Period from Thursday, 1st of October,
2026 until twenty-four (24) hours after the Company’s Unaudited Financial Statements for the third quarter
ended 30th of September, 2026 (2026 Q3 UFS) and other information have been made public through The
Exchange’s Issuer’s Portal.
Accordingly, no insiders of the Company and their connected persons may directly or indirectly, deal (buy
or sell) in the shares of the Company in any manner during the Closed Period until twenty-four (24) hours
after the release of the 2026 Q3 UFS through the Issuers’ Portal of NGX and by extension, the general
public.
All insiders of the Company and persons closely connected to them have been appropriately advised.
