Lagos, July 21 2026
RESOLUTIONS PASSED AT THE ANNUAL GENERAL MEETING OF CORNERSTONE
INSURANCE PLC
At the 34th Annual General Meeting (AGM) of Cornerstone Insurance Plc (the ‘Company’) held at Shell Hall,
MUSON Centre, Onikan, Lagos on Monday, 20th July, 2026, at 12:00 pm, the following resolutions were
duly passed:
1. That the Audited Financial Statements for the year ended 31st December 2025, together with the reports
of the Directors, Auditors and Audit Committee, be received.
2. That pursuant to Section 238 of the Companies and Allied Matters Act (CAMA) 2020, a total dividend
of ₦5,086,590,040.00(Five Billion, Eighty-Six Million, Five Hundred and Ninety Thousand and Forty
Naira Only), that is twenty-eight (28) kobo per ordinary share of fifty (50) kobo each, declared, be
distributed to members of the Company as dividend
3. That pursuant to Section 238 of CAMA, 2020, the election of the following Directors: Mr. Afolabi
Balogun, Mr. Ibrahim Ibisomi, Mr. Philip Southwell and Mr. Olukayode Pitan, are hereby approved.
4. That pursuant to Section 285 (1) of CAMA 2020, the re-election of the retiring Directors- Mrs.
Christabel Onyejekwe, Prof. Ogechi Adeola, and Mr. Peter Ekwueme, are hereby approved.
5. That pursuant to Section 408 of CAMA 2020, the Directors be and are hereby authorized to fix the
remuneration of the Company’s External Auditor, Messrs Deloitte & Touche, for the financial year
ending 31st December 2026.
6. That pursuant to Section 404 (3) of CAMA 2020, the persons below were appointed as representatives
of the shareholders on the Statutory Audit Committee:
a. Mr. Leo Ibe;
b. Mr. Chibuzor Eke;
c. Mr. Emelius Oyetunde Olaitan;
The Board representatives on the Statutory Audit Committee are
d. Mrs. Christabel Onyejekwe; and
e. Mr. Ibrahim Ibisomi
7. That the Directors be and are hereby authorized to approve the remuneration of the Non- Executive
Directors.
8. That pursuant to the rules of the Nigerian Exchange Limited governing transactions with related parties
or interested persons, the Company is authorized to procure goods and services necessary for its
operations from related third parties.
