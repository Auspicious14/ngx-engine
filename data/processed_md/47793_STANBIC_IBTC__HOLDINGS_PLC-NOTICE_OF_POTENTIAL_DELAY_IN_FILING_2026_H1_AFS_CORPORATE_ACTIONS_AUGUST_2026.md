Notice of Potential Delay in the Release of 2026 Half Year Audited
Results
LAGOS, NIGERIA – 10 August 2026
This is to inform Nigerian Exchange Limited (NGX or The Exchange) as well
as our Esteemed Stakeholders that Stanbic IBTC Holdings Plc (Stanbic or
the Company) may experience delay in filing its Audited Financial
Statements for the Half Year ended 30 June 2026, by the due date of 28
August 2026.
The Company is currently finalising the audit of its 2026 Half Year Results,
following which we would also be seeking the required regulatory approvals.
We are working diligently to ensure that our Company’s 2026 Audited Half
Year Financial Statements is submitted to NGX as soon as we have received
all required regulatory approvals, and this may occur before or shortly after
the regulatory due date of 28 August 2026.
