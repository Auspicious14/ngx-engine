Lomé, March 31, 2026
NOTICE OF DELAY IN FILING OF AUDITED FINANCIAL STATEMENTS FOR
THE YEAR ENDED 31 DECEMBER 2025 AND CLOSED PERIOD
NOTIFICATION FOR PERIOD ENDNG 31 MARCH 2026
Ecobank Transnational Incorporated (“ETI”) hereby notifies Nigerian Exchange Limited
(“NGX”), its shareholders, and the investing public that it will be unable to file its Audited
Financial Statements for the year ended 31 December 2025 by 31 March 2026.
This delay is due to the additional time required to conclude outstanding audit and reporting
processes. ETI is working diligently with all relevant parties to finalise these matters and expects
to submit the 2025 Audited Financial Statements on or before 30 April 2026.
In compliance with Rule 17.18(a) of the NGX Listing Rules 2015 (as amended), the closed period
previously communicated remains in force and shall continue until twenty-four (24) hours after
the release on NGX of ETI’s Audited Financial Statements for the year ended 31 December 2025
and First Quarter 2026 Unaudited Financial Statements for the period ending 31 March 2026.
During this period, no insider of ETI or any of its affiliates may, directly or indirectly, deal in ETI
shares.
