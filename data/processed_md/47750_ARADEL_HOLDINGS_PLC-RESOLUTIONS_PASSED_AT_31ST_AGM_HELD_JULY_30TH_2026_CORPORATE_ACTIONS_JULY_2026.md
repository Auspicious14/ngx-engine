Lagos, July 31st, 2026
RESOLUTIONS PASSED AT THE 31st ANNUAL GENERAL MEETING HELD ON
July 30th, 2026
At the 31st Annual General Meeting of Aradel Holdings Plc (the “Company”) which held
virtually via zoom at 10:00am on Thursday, July 30th, 2026, the Company’s Audited
Financial Statements for the year ended 31st December 2025, together with the report
of the Directors, Auditors and the Audit Committee, thereon, was formally laid before the
members. Thereafter, the following resolutions were proposed and duly passed:
1. That a final dividend of N23.00 for every ordinary share be paid to shareholders
whose names appear in the Register of Members as at the close of business on
Friday July 10th, 2026.
2. That the following Directors retiring by rotation be and are hereby re-elected as
Directors of the Company:
2.1. Mr. Augustine Olorunsola - Chairman
2.2. Mr. Adegbite Falade - Chief Executive Officer
2.3. Mr. Adegbola Adesina - Chief Financial Officer
2.4. Ms. Kerin Gunter - Non-Executive Director
3. That the appointment of the following Directors be and are hereby ratified:
3.1. Mr. Olusola Adeeyo - Non-Executive Director
3.2. Mr. George Osahon - Non-Executive Director
3.3. Mr. Mahmud Tukur - Independent Non-Executive Director
3.4. Engr. Nnoli Akpedeye - Independent Non-Executive Director
4. That KPMG Professional Services be and is appointed as auditors for the financial
year ending December 31st, 2026, to replace the retiring audit firm of Deloitte &
Touch.
5. That the remuneration and expenses of KPMG Professional Services (as auditors)
be determined by the Directors for the financial year ending December 31st, 2026.

6. That the following people having been validly elected/appointed, shall constitute
the Statutory Audit Committee of the Company for the year 2026:
6.1. Mr. Femi Akinsanya – Shareholder
6.2. Mr. Eddie Efekoha – Shareholder
6.3. Chief Gbola Akinola, SAN – Shareholder
6.4. Mr. Afolabi Oladele – Non-Executive Director
6.5. Ms. Patricia Simon-Hart – Non-Executive Director
7. That the Severance benefit payable to the Non-Executive Directors of the Company
(inclusive of Non-Executive Directors who retired during the 2025 calendar year)
be and are hereby fixed as follows:
7.1. Chairman
7.1.1. Annual Fee: US$68,750/annum, multiplied by number of years served on
the Board; and
7.1.2. Annual medical insurance cover for a maximum period of five (5) years
after retirement subject to an annual limit in the sum of $16,700
7.2. Other Directors
7.2.1. Annual Fee: US$40,500/annum multiplied by number of years served on
the Board; and
7.2.2. Annual medical insurance cover for a maximum period of five (5) years
after retirement subject to an annual limit in the sum of $14,500
