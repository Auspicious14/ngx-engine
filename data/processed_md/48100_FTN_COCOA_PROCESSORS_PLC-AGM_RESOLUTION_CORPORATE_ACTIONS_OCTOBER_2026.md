RESOLUTIONS PASSED AT THE 17TH ANNUAL GENERAL MEETING OF THE
COMPANY HELD VIRTUALLY ON THURSDAY, 16TH OCTOBER, 2026
At the Annual General meeting of FTN Cocoa Processors Plc held virtually on 15 October 2026, the
following resolutions were duly proposed and unanimously approved:
That the Report of the Directors, the Financial Statements for the year ended 31st December 2025
and the Reports of the Auditors and the Audit Committee thereon were recieved.
RE-ELECTION OF DIRECTORS:
That Mrs. Titilayo Aderonmu, who retired in accordance with Section 273(1) of the Companies and
Allied Matters Act 2020, being eligible for re-election, be and is hereby re-elected as a Non-Executive
Director of the Company.
APPOINTMENT OF MESSRS. BAKERTILLY NIGERIA (CHARTERED ACCOUNTANTS) AS
EXTERNAL AUDITORS
THAT the appointment of Messrs. Bakertilly Nigeria (Chartered Accountants) as External Auditors
of the Company, pursuant to the provision of Section 401 of the Companies and Allied Matters Act,
2020, be approved and the Directors are hereby authorized to fix the remuneration of the said
Auditors in compliance with Section 408 (1) (b) of the Act.
ELECTION OF MEMBERS OF THE STATUTORY AUDIT COMMITTEE FOR THE COMPANY:
THAT Mr. Emmanuel Olaniyi Oladosu, Mr. Chinwendu Achara and Mr. Fawole Taiwo Ganiyu be
and are hereby elected as members of the Statutory Audit Committee of the Company for the
2026/2027 financial year, and Otunba Wale Jubril and Mr. Nathaniel Durant were nominated to
represent the Board on the Statutory Audit Committee.
DISCLOSURE OF MANAGERS’REMUNERATION:
Pursuant to Section 257 of the Companies and Allied Matters Act, 2020, the Managers’ remuneration
as contained in the Financial Statements just laid before the meeting was disclosed.
