THE INITIATES PLC
WASTE MANAGERS & INDUSTRIAL CLEANERS RC266755
Port Harcourt, 29 May 2026
The Initiates Plc expands Continental footprint with additional 20% Stake in The Initiates
Uganda (TIU)
The Initiates Plc (TIP) wishes to notify its shareholders of a major milestone in TIP’s Growth
strategy:
TIP has acquired an additional 20% stake in The Initiates Uganda bringing her total stake to
55%. This makes TIU a full subsidiary of TIP.
On May 5th, 2026, TIP completed the acquisition by an additional 20% equity stake in TIU,
a company valued at $15million, for $2.02M.
Why this matters:
- Regional Expansion: This gives TIP direct access to Uganda’s growing oil and gas
industry and a gateway into East Africa’s oil & gas market.
- Diversification: TIU Earnings in US Dollars will help to boost the overall earnings and
performance of TIP.
- Synergy: TIU will leverage on TIP’s vast industry experience and technical knowhow to
improve margins on both sides.
The deal is funded through internal cash flow.
What Changes for You (Shareholders):
1. Exposure: Your investment now has indirect exposure to Uganda’s oil and gas sector
without additional FX risk at the holding company level.
2. Governance: TIP will have 3 board seats and veto rights on CAPEX above $500,000.
3. Reporting: Starting Q4 2026, TIU results will be equity-accounted in our financials.
Thank you for your continued trust in TIP. We’re building a pan-African waste management
company, and this is just the beginning.
Reuben Mustapha Ossai
Managing Director/CEO
