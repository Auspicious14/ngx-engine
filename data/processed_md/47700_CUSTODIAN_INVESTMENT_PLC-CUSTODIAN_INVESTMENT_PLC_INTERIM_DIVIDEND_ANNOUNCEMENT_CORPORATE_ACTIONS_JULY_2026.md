Corporate Action Announcement
Custodian Investment Plc hereby announces as follows:
Period Ended June 30, 2026
Proposed Dividend An Interim Dividend of 25kobo for every share of 50k each, subject to
appropriate withholding tax, will be paid to Shareholders whose names
appear in the Register of Members as at the close of business on August 17,
2026.
Closure of Register The Register of Members will be closed from August 18, 2026, to August 21, 2026
(both dates inclusive).
Qualification Date August 17, 2026
Payment Date On September 8, 2026, dividends will be paid electronically to shareholders
whose names appear on the Register of Members as at August 17, 2026, and
who have completed the e-dividend registration and mandated the Registrar
to pay their dividends directly into their Bank accounts.
E-Dividend Registration Shareholders who are yet to complete the e-dividend registration are advised
to visit the link below to upload their e-mandate request in order to process
their outstanding and subsequent dividend payments seamlessly:
e-dividend Mandate Management System (e-DMMS) portal:
https://docuhub3.nibss-plc.com.ng/edmms/self-service
Unclaimed Dividend Shareholders with dividend warrants and share certificates that have remained
Warrants and Share unclaimed or are yet to be presented for payment or returned for validation
Certificates are advised to complete the e-dividend registration form or contact the
Registrar.
e-Dividend Mandate Management System (e-DMMS) portal:
https://docuhub3.nibss-plc.com.ng/edmms/self-service
