NOTIFICATION OF APPOINTMENT OF COMPANY SECRETARY
LAGOS, NIGERIA – 17th November 2026 – Cornerstone Insurance Plc (NGX: CORNERST) hereby notifies
Nigerian Exchange Limited (“NGX”), its shareholders and the investing public that the Board of
Directors has approved the appointment of Ms Aramide Oyeneyin as Company Secretary.
Ms Oyeneyin succeeds the Company’s erstwhile Company Secretary - PAC Solicitors. The Board thanks
PAC Solicitors for their support and professional conduct during their tenure of service to the Company.
Prior to her appointment, Ms Oyeneyin led the legal function at Chapel Hill Denham, where she
provided legal and transaction support on investment banking and financial advisory, investment
management and securities trading transactions. She also advised the firm on various aspects of
corporate and commercial law and was involved in several landmark transactions.
Before joining Chapel Hill Denham, Ms Oyeneyin was at Jackson, Etti & Edu, where she acted as
company secretary to a number of companies listed on NGX and advised on a wide range of
transactions, including banking and finance, private equity, capital markets, and mergers and
acquisitions. She holds a Bachelor’s Degree in Law and a Master’s Degree in International Finance from
University College London, and was called to the Nigerian Bar in 2013.
