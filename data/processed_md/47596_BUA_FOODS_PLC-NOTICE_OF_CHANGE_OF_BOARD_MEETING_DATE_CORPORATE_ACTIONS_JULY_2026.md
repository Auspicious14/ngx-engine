Lagos, 28th July 2026
NOTICE OF CHANGE OF BOARD MEETING DATE FOR THE
CONSIDERATION AND APPROVAL OF 2ND QUARTER 2026 UNAUDITED
FINANCIAL STATEMENTS
We refer to our earlier notice dated 2nd July 2026 informing Nigerian Exchange Limited
(NGX) and the investing public that a meeting of the Board of Directors of BUA Foods
Plc (the Company) was scheduled to hold on Wednesday, 29th July 2026 to consider
amongst other matters, the 2nd Quarter 2026 Unaudited Financial Statements for the
period ended 30th June 2026 (2026 Q2 UFS.
This is to notify NGX and the investing public that the Board meeting has been
rescheduled and will now hold on Thursday, 30th July 2026.
The Closed Period declared by the Company remains effective and will continue until 24
hours after the release of the 2026 Q2 UFS.
BY ORDER OF THE BOARD
Oluseye Alayande
Company Secretary
