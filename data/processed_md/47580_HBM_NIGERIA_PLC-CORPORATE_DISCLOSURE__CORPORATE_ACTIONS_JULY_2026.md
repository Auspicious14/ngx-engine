Corporate Actions Announcement
HBM Nigeria Plc hereby announce as follows:
Period Ended 30th June 2026
Proposed Dividend An interim dividend of N16 per unit of ordinary share, subject to deduction of
withholding tax, will be paid to shareholders whose names appear in the
Register of Members as at the close of business on Wednesday, 12th August
2026.
Closure of Register The Register of Shareholders will be closed from Thursday, 13th August 2026
to Friday, 14th August 2026 (both dates inclusive).
Qualification Date Wednesday, 12th August 2026
Payment Date On the Monday, 17th August 2026, dividends will be paid electronically to
shareholders whose names appear in the Register of Members as at
Wednesday, 12th August 2026, and who have completed the e-dividend
registration and mandated the Registrar to pay their dividends directly into
their Bank accounts.
E-Dividend Registration Shareholders who are yet to complete the e-dividend registration are advised
to download the Registrars e-Dividend Mandate Activation Form, which is
available on the website of our Registrar www.cardinalstoneregistrars.com,
complete and submit to the Registrar.
Unclaimed Dividend Warrants Shareholders with dividend warrants and share certificates that have remained
and Share Certificates unclaimed or are yet to be presented for payment or returned for validation
are advised to complete the e-dividend registration or contact the Registrar.
Registrar The details of our Registrar are:
Address: CardinalStone Registrars Limited – 335/337, Herbert Macaulay Road,
Yaba, Lagos State.
Website: www.cardinalstoneregistrars.com;
Email Address: registrars@cardinalstone.com
Investor Relations Investorrelations.ng@ng.huaxin.com
Dated this 28th day of July 2026.
Mrs. Adewunmi Alode

DIRECTORS: Mr. Gbenga Oyebode, MFR - Chairman, Mr. Lolu Alade-Akinyemi, Mrs. Oyinkansade Adewale, FCA, Mrs.

(Chinese), Mr. Zhang Lin (Chinese), Mrs. Nella Andem-Ewa, SAN and Dr. Fatima Akilu.
