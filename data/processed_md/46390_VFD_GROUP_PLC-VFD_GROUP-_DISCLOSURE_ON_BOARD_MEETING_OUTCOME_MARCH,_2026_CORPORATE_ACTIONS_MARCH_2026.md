VFD Group Plc: Outcome of Meeting of the Board of Directors Meeting
The Board of Directors of VFD Group Plc (the Company) wishes to inform the general Public
that the Board, at its meeting which took place on Friday, 27th March 2026, considered and
approved as follows:
1. The Audited Financial Statements and Accounts of the Company for the year ended 31
December 2025.
2. Recommendation of the payment of N0.25k per ordinary share as dividend, subject to
shareholders’ approval at the Annual General Meeting.
The Closed Period earlier communicated for trading in the shares of the Company by Insiders
and their connected persons will be lifted twenty (24) hours after the release of the Audited
Financial Statements and Accounts for the year ended 31 December 2025.
