Caverton Offshore Support Group Plc – Notice of Board Meeting and Closed
Period
This is to inform Nigerian Exchange Limited (NGX) and the investing public that
the meeting of the Board of Directors of Caverton Offshore Support Group Plc
(COSG or the Company), which was previously scheduled to hold on Tuesday, the
31st of March, 2026 has been postponed. A new date will be communicated.

period for trading in its shares by insiders of the Company and their connected
persons from Thursday, 1 January 2026 until twenty-four (24) hours after the
announcement of the Audited Financial Statements for the year ended 31
December 2025 and the Unaudited Financial Statements for the period ending 31
March 2026 to the Market.
