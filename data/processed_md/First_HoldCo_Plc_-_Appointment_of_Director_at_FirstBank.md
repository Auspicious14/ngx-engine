Dr. Julius B. Omodayo-Owotuga Appointed as an Executive Director at First Bank of Nigeria Limited
This is to notify Nigerian Exchange Limited (“NGX”) and the investing public that First Bank of Nigeria
Limited, a subsidiary of First HoldCo Plc (the Company), has appointed Dr. Julius B. Omodayo-
Owotuga (Dr. JB) as an Executive Director, effective 13 May 2026, further strengthening the Bank’s
leadership capacity across strategic finance, governance, risk management, and institutional
transformation.
Dr. Julius B. Omodayo-Owotuga brings twenty-four (24) years of experience spanning banking and
financial services, infrastructure finance, power, oil & gas, and audit and consulting. His appointment
reflects the Bank’s continued commitment to strong governance, disciplined execution, financial
resilience, and sustainable long-term growth.
Prior to this appointment, he served on the Board of First HoldCo Plc as a Non-Executive Director,
between 2021 and 2026.
He most recently served as Deputy Chief Executive of Geregu Power Plc, Nigeria’s first listed power
generation company, where he played a pivotal role in institutional transformation, governance
strengthening, capital market positioning, operational optimisation, and major financing initiatives,
including the company’s landmark listing on NGX.
Dr. Julius B. Omodayo-Owotuga previously served as Group Executive Director, Finance & Risk
Management at Forte Oil Plc (Now Ardova Plc), where he was instrumental in the company’s financial
and operational transformation, leading strategic restructuring, capital raising, treasury optimisation,
enterprise risk management, and governance improvement initiatives that strengthened long-term
shareholder value.
His professional career also includes roles at Africa Finance Corporation, Standard Chartered Bank,
KPMG Professional Services and MBC International Bank (Now First Bank Nigeria Limited), providing
him with deep experience in institutional finance, treasury management, financial controls,
regulatory engagement, and corporate advisory.
Dr. Julius B. Omodayo-Owotuga is a CFA Charter Holder, KPMG-trained Accountant, and a Fellow of
the Institute of Chartered Accountants of Nigeria (ICAN), the Chartered Institute of Taxation of
Nigeria (CITN), and the Institute of Credit Administration. He is also a member of the Institute of
Directors (IoD) Nigeria and a Certified Management Accountant.
He holds a Doctorate in Business Administration, a Master’s in Business Administration and
Bachelor’s degree in Accounting. He is an alumnus of Saïd Business School, University of Oxford, IE
Business School, Geneva Business School, and The University of Lagos.

Dr. Julius B. Omodayo-Owotuga (Dr. JB) is an accomplished finance, governance, and transformation executive with
extensive leadership experience spanning banking and financial services, infrastructure finance, power, oil and gas,
and the audit and consulting industry.
He was appointed as an Executive Director at First Bank of Nigeria Limited on May 13, 2026, one of Africa’s leading
financial institutions. Prior to this, he was on the Board of First HoldCo Plc as a Non-Executive Director, bringing deep
expertise in finance, risk management, governance, treasury management, capital optimisation, and institutional
strengthening.
Dr. Julius B Omodayo-Owotuga is widely respected for his track record in institutional transformation, governance
enhancement, capital raising, and operational restructuring across regulated and capital-intensive sectors.
Prior to this appointment, he served as Deputy Chief Executive of Geregu Power Plc, Nigeria’s first listed power
generation company and a subsidiary of Amperion Power. During his tenure, he played a pivotal role in strengthening
corporate governance frameworks, driving enterprise transformation, improving operational efficiency, supporting
major capital raising initiatives, and contributing significantly to the company’s landmark listing on the Nigerian
Exchange.
He previously served as Group Executive Director, Finance & Risk Management at Forte Oil Plc (now Ardova Plc),
where he was instrumental in balance sheet restructuring, capital optimisation, enterprise risk management, and the
transformation of the company into a consistently profitable and governance-driven enterprise. He also led and
supported several strategic financing, acquisition, divestment, and institutional restructuring initiatives during a critical
phase of the company’s growth.
His experience includes extensive involvement in large-scale bond programmes, institutional funding transactions,
investment-grade credit positioning, treasury management, and long-term capital structuring within highly regulated
operating environments.
Earlier in his career, Dr. JB served at Africa Finance Corporation (AFC) as Asset & Liability Management Specialist and
Deputy to the Treasurer, having previously served as Finance Manager of the institution. As part of AFC’s pioneer
team, he played a key role in establishing core financial operations, control systems, treasury processes, and
institutional finance structures. His accomplishments included supporting major funding transactions, managing
strategic financial institution relationships, and contributing to the generation of significant treasury and investment
income.
Prior to AFC, he worked with Standard Chartered Bank and KPMG, where he gained extensive experience in financial
reporting, audit, regulatory engagement, project finance oversight, and advisory services within the financial services
sector.
Dr. JB is a CFA Charter Holder, a KPMG-trained Accountant, and a Fellow of the Institute of Chartered Accountants
of Nigeria (ICAN), the Chartered Institute of Taxation of Nigeria (CITN), and the Institute of Credit Administration. He is
also a member of the Institute of Directors (IoD) Nigeria.
He is an alumnus of Saïd Business School, University of Oxford, IE Business School, Geneva Business School, and
University of Lagos. He holds a B.Sc. in Accounting, an MBA with Distinction, and a Doctorate in Business
Administration.
Dr. JB is widely recognised for his strategic leadership, governance expertise, capital markets experience, and ability
to drive sustainable institutional growth and transformation within complex regulatory environments.
He is married with children and enjoys playing tennis, mentoring professionals, and watching football in his leisure
time.
