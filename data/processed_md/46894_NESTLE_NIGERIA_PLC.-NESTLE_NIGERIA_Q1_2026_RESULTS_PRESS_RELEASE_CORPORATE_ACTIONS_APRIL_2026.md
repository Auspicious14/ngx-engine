Nestlé Nigeria PLC Reports Double-Digit Growth, Improved Total Equity and Profitability in
Q1 2026
Nestlé Nigeria PLC has reported a strong operating and financial performance in the first quarter
ended 31 March 2026, delivering double-digit revenue growth and sustained improvement in
profitability. Profit before tax rose to ₦73.8 billion, representing a 44% increase compared to
₦51.2 billion in the corresponding period of 2025.
Highlights: Jan - Mar 2026
• Revenue increased by 11% to ₦326.1 billion in Q1 2026 from ₦294.9 billion in Q1 2025
• Profit before tax rose to ₦73.8 billion, compared to ₦51.2 billion in Q1 2025
• Profit after tax rose by 29% to ₦38.9 billion from ₦30.2 billion in Q1 2025
• Total equity increased from ₦12.9 billion at the end of December 2025 to ₦51.6 billion
at the end of March 2026.

Commenting on the results, Mr. Wassim Elhusseini, CEO/Managing Director of Nestlé Nigeria
PLC, said: “Our Q1 2026 performance marks the sixth consecutive quarter of profitability since
our return to profit in Q4 2024. The double-digit revenue growth of 11% and profit after tax of
₦38.9 billion reflect the discipline and focus behind our execution and the strength of our
operating performance supported by the stability of Naira against the Dollar.”
Looking ahead, he added: “We will continue to accelerate innovation and renovation to stay
ahead of evolving consumer needs, strengthen margin management, cost and operational
efficiencies across our value chain to fund growth. We will also continue to invest in community
programmes that create shared and sustainable value for all stakeholders.”
“We will leverage targeted marketing investments to deepen brand relevance, win market share,
and strengthen our competitive position while remaining alert to macroeconomic and
geopolitical dynamics.”
