ELLAH LAKES PLC - ANNUAL GENERAL MEETING RESOLUTIONS
At the Annual General Meeting of Ellah Lakes Plc (the “Company”) held at Conference Centre, Naval
Dockyard, Ahmadu Bello Way, Victoria Island, Lagos on Tuesday, 5 May 2026, the following resolutions
were proposed and passed:
Ordinary business:
1. That the Audited Financial Statements of the Company for the year ended 31 December 2025,
together with the reports of the Directors, Auditors and Audit Committee, be and are hereby received
and accepted.
2. That the re-election of Mr. Enotie Ogbebor as Director, retiring by rotation and having offered himself
up for re-election, be and is hereby rejected.
3. That the re-election of Dr. Evans Jakpa Johns as Director, retiring by rotation and having offered
himself up for re-election be and is hereby approved.
4. That the re-election of Mr. Charles Anajemba as Director, retiring by rotation and having offered
himself up for re-election be and is hereby rejected.
5. That the re-election of Mr. Emmanuel Jakpa as Director, retiring by rotation and having offered
himself up for re-election be and is hereby approved.
6. That the re-appointment of Messrs Olabode Akande & Co as Auditors of the Company for the year
ending 31 December 2026 be and is hereby approved.
7. That the Board of Directors be and are hereby authorised to fix the remuneration of the Auditors for
the year ending 31 December 2026.
