MUTUAL BENEFITS ASSURANCE PLC – APPROVAL OF AUDITED CONSOLIDATED
AND SEPARATE FINANCIAL STATEMENTS FOR THE YEAR ENDED 31 DECEMBER
2025
This is to notify Nigerian Exchange Limited (NGX or The Exchange), shareholders
and the investing public that the Board of Mutual Benefits Assurance Plc (the
Company) met on 27th February 2026 and approved the Group Audited Separate
and Consolidated Financial Statements for the year ended 31 December 2025 and
payment of a dividend, subject to the approval of the National Insurance
Commission.
The Exchange and the investing public would be notified upon approval of the Group
Audited Separate and Consolidated Financial Statements of the year ended 31
December 2025 by the National Insurance Commission.
The Closed Period declared from 1st January 2026 will remain in place until twenty-
four (24) hours after the release of the Group Audited Consolidated and Separate
Financial Statements to The Exchange and, by extension, the investing public.
Accordingly, no insider of the Company or their connected persons may, directly or
indirectly, deal in the Company's shares during the Closed Period.
