Seplat Energy Plc (“SEPLAT” or the “Company”)
Announcement on Notification of Dealing in Shares by a PDMR
Lagos and London – September 30, 2026: The Company was notified of the purchase of
6,000,000 ordinary shares by Heirs Energies Ltd, a company controlled by Mr. Tony Elumelu, a
Non - Executive Director of the Company.
Following the above transaction, Heirs Energies Limited now holds 27,943,867 shares in the
Company. As a result, the entities controlled by Mr. Tony Elumelu (Heirs Energies Limited and
Heirs Holdings Limited) now hold a combined 126,400,000 shares, which equate to a voting
interest of 21.07% based on the issued shares of 599,944,561.
Further details of the transaction are set out in the attached Notification and Public Disclosure of
Transactions by Persons Discharging Managerial Responsibilities and Persons Closely Associated
with them.
This notification is made in accordance with Rule 17.15(c) Disclosure of Dealings in Issuers' Shares,
Rulebook of The Exchange, 2015 (Issuers' Rule) and Article 19(3) of the UK Market Abuse
Regulations.
Mrs. Edith Onwuchekwa
Director, Legal/Company Secretary
