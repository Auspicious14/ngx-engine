Corporate Action Announcement
Custodian Investment Plc hereby announces as follows:
Year Ended December 31, 2025
Proposed Dividend A Final Dividend of N2:50 for every share of 50k each, (bringing the total
dividend for the financial year ended December 31, 2025, to N2:75kobo),
subject to appropriate withholding tax and approval, will be paid to
Shareholders whose names appear in the Register of Members as at the close
of business on April 13, 2026.
Proposed Bonus
NIL
Closure of Register The Register of Members will be closed from Tuesday, April 14, 2026 to Friday,
April 17, 2026 [both dates inclusive].
Qualification Date
April 13, 2026
Payment Date
On May 8, 2026, dividend will be paid electronically to shareholders whose
names appear on the Register of Members as at April 13, 2026 and who have
completed the e-dividend registration and mandated the Registrar to pay
their dividends directly into their Bank accounts.
E-Dividend Registration
Shareholders who are yet to complete the e-dividend registration are advised
to download the Registrar’s E-Dividend Mandate Activation Form, which is
available on our Registrars website: MeristemRegistrars_Bank-Mandate-
Form.pdf (meristemng.com) complete and submit to the Registrar.
Unclaimed Dividend
Shareholders with dividend warrants and share certificates that have remained
Warrants and Share
unclaimed or are yet to be presented for payment or returned for validation
Certificates
are advised to complete the e-dividend registration form or contact the
Registrar.
Date of General Meeting The Company’s Annual General Meeting will hold virtually via
https://www.custodianplc.com.ng on Friday, May 8, 2026, at 10.00a.m
