RC. 2321
Corporate Actions Announcement
NIGERIAN EXCHANGE GROUP PLC hereby announce as follows:
Period Ended 30 June 2026
An Interim Dividend of ₦1:30k (One Naira Thirty Kobo) per ordinary share of
Proposed Dividend 50kobo each, subject to appropriate withholding tax.
Closure of Register 30 July 2026
Qualification Date 29 July 2026
Payment Date Dividend will be paid on 5 August 2026 to shareholders whose names appear
in the Register of Members as at the close of business on 29 July 2026.
Shareholders who are yet to complete the e-dividend registration are advised
to download the Registrar’s E-Dividend Mandate Activation Form, which is
also available on the website of the Registrars:
E-Dividend https://www.cognitoforms.com/DataMaxGroup1/emandateform and submit to
Registration the Registrar or their respective Banks.
Unclaimed Shareholders with dividend warrants and share certificates that have
Dividend Warrants remained unclaimed or are yet to be presented for payment or returned for
and Share validation are advised to complete the e-dividend registration or contact the
Certificates Registrar.
