Corporate Actions Announcement
Stanbic IBTC Holdings PLC hereby announce as follows:
Period Ended 31 December 2025
A Final Dividend of N4.00 (Four Naira) per ordinary share of 50 kobo each, that is,
N63,607,076,984.00, subject to deduction of appropriate withholding tax and
approval, will be paid to shareholders whose names appear in the Register of
Members as at the close of business on Monday 04 May 2026. This is in addition
to the Interim Dividend of N2.50 paid in September 2025, thus bringing the total
Proposed Dividend 2025 dividend to N6.50.
Proposed Bonus NIL
The Register of Shareholders will be closed from Tuesday 05 May 2026 to Tuesday
Closure of Register 12 May 2026.
Qualification Date Monday 04 May 2026
On Tuesday 26 May 2026, dividends will be paid electronically to shareholders
whose names appear on the Register of Members as at close of business on
Monday 04 May 2026 and who have completed the e-dividend registration and
Payment Date mandated the Registrar to pay their dividends directly into their Bank accounts.
Shareholders who are yet to complete the e-dividend registration are advised to
download the Registrar’s E-Dividend Mandate Activation Form, which is also
available on our Registrars website: https://firstregistrarsnigeria.com/download-
E-Dividend Registration forms/, complete and submit to the Registrar or their respective Banks.
Unclaimed Dividend Shareholders with dividend warrants and share certificates that have remained
Warrants and Share unclaimed or are yet to be presented for payment or returned for validation are
Certificates advised to complete the e-dividend registration or contact the Registrars.
The Company’s 14th Annual General Meeting will be held at The Jasmine Hall, Eko
Hotel & Suites, Adetokunbo Ademola Street, Victoria Island Lagos, on Monday 25
Date of General Meeting May 2026 at 10.00 a.m.
