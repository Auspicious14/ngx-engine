NOTICE OF BOARD MEETING AND CLOSED PERIOD – CADBURY NIGERIA PLC
We hereby notify the Nigeria Exchange Limited (NGX), the investing public, shareholders and insiders of
Cadbury Nigeria Plc (the Company), that a meeting of the Board of Directors of the Company is scheduled
to hold on 30th July 2026, to consider among other things the Unaudited Financial Statements (UFS) of the
Company for the quarter ended 30th June 2026 (2026 Q2 UFS).
In compliance with the Closed Period Rules of The Exchange (as amended), the Closed Period for trading
in the shares of the Company by Insiders commenced from 1st July 2026 and will continue until twenty-
four (24) hours after the 2026 Q2 UFS has been released to the public.
During this period, insiders and other interested parties of Cadbury Nigeria Plc, should observe this
earnings-related closed trading period and not buy or sell Cadbury Nigeria Plc shares.
This is in compliance with the rules of the Nigerian Exchange Limited. The rules are also spelled out in our
C-14-Insider Trading policy of the Company.
