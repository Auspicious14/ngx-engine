PRESS RELEASE
STANBIC IBTC HOLDINGS PLC ANNOUNCES REFERENCE PRICE FOR 2026 INTERIM
SCRIP DIVIDEND
Lagos, Nigeria | 23 October 2026
Stanbic IBTC Holdings PLC (the “Company”) hereby notifies its shareholders and the investing public that the
reference price for the allotment of new ordinary shares under the Company’s 2026 Interim Scrip Dividend
Programme has been determined at ₦159.86 per ordinary share.
As previously announced on 30 September 2026, shareholders entitled to the interim dividend of ₦4.50 per
ordinary share, as approved by the Board of Directors, may elect to receive the dividend either in cash or in the
form of new ordinary shares in the Company.
The scrip dividend option is available to shareholders whose names appeared in the Company’s Register of
Members as at the qualification date of Thursday, 15 October 2026. The allotment of new ordinary shares
pursuant to the scrip dividend election remains subject to the receipt of all required regulatory approvals.
In accordance with the terms of the Scrip Dividend Programme, the reference price was calculated using the
volume-weighted average price of the Company’s ordinary shares on the Nigerian Exchange Limited over the
five business days commencing from the date on which the shares were first quoted ex-dividend. Accordingly,
the reference price applicable to the 2026 Interim Scrip Dividend is ₦159.86 (one hundred and fifty nine naira,
eighty-six kobo) per ordinary share.
Shareholders who wish to receive their interim dividend in the form of new ordinary shares should complete and
submit the Scrip Dividend Form of Election, which may be downloaded using the link below:
H1 2026 Scrip Dividend Form of Election
Copies of the Form may also be obtained from the Group Company Secretary, the Head of Investor Relations,
or the Company’s Registrars using the contact details set out below.
Completed Forms must be received by First Registrars and Investor Services Limited no later than the close of
business on Friday, 6 November 2026.
Shareholders who elect to receive their dividend in cash are not required to take any further action. Where a
valid e-dividend mandate is in place, the relevant bank account will be credited on the dividend payment date of
Friday, 13 November 2026.
