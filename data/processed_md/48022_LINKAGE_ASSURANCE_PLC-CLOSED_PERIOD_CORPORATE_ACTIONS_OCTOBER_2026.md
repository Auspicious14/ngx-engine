LAGOS, October 02, 2026
NOTIFICATION OF CLOSED PERIOD
This is to inform Nigerian Exchange Limited (NGX) and our esteemed shareholders that
in compliance with the provisions of Rule 17.18(a): Closed Period, Rulebook of NGX,
2015 (Issuers’ Rule) as amended, Linkage Assurance Plc (the Company) has
commenced a Closed Period from Thursday 1st of October 2026 until twenty-four (24)
hours after the 2026 Q3 Unaudited Financial Statements for the period ended 30th
September 2026 is released to the public via NGX’s Issuers’ Portal.
Within this period, all those who are considered “insiders” of the Company in accordance
with the provisions of the Rules of NGX are restricted from trading or dealing in the shares
of the Company.
