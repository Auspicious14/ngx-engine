July 31, 2026
Lagos, Nigeria
ANNOUNCEMENT OF BOARD MEETING DATE OF ACCESS HOLDINGS PLC
This is to inform Nigerian Exchange Limited (‘The Exchange’) and the investing public
that a meeting of the Board of Directors of Access Holdings Plc (‘the Company’) is
scheduled to hold on August 27, 2026, to consider and approve, among other matters,
the Group’s Audited Interim Financial Statements for the half year ended June 30,
2026.
The Group’s Audited Interim Financial Statements, once approved by the Board, will
be submitted to the Central Bank of Nigeria for approval before release to The
Exchange and the investing public.
In line with The Exchange’s Listing Rules, the Closed Period earlier announced by the
Company in respect of transactions in its securities will remain in effect until 24 hours
after the release of the Group’s Audited Interim Financial Statements.
Accordingly, no insider may directly or indirectly deal in the securities of the Company
in any manner during the Closed Period.
BY ORDER OF THE BOARD
SUNDAY EKWOCHI
