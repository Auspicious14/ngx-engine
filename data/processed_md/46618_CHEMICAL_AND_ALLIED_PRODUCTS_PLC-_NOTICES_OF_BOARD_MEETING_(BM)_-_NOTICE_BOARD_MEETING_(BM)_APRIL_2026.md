NOTICE OF Q1 2026 BOARD OF DIRECTORS’ MEETING AND CLOSED PERIOD
Notice is hereby given that the meeting of the Board of Directors (the “Board”) of Chemical and Allied
Products PLC (the “Company”) will be held on Monday, the 27th day of April 2026 at 10:00 am at 2,
Adeniyi Jones Avenue, Ikeja Lagos and hosted virtually via Teams conferencing platform.
The Board will consider, among other things, the Company’s Unaudited Financial Statements for the
period ended March 31, 2026 (the “Unaudited Q1 2026 Accounts”).
In accordance with the relevant provisions of the Rule Book of the Nigerian Exchange Limited (“NGX”),
the Closed Period for trading in the shares of the Company commenced on April 1, 2026, in respect of
the Unaudited Q1 2026 Accounts.
Hence, no insider of the Company and their connected persons may directly or indirectly deal in the
securities of the Company traded on NGX until twenty-four (24) hours after the release of the Unaudited
Q1 2026 Accounts via NGX’s Issuers’ Portal and by extension, the general public.
Thank you.
