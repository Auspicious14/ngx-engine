NOTICE OF DELAY IN FILING 2025 AUDITED FINANCIAL STATEMENTS.
ABBEY MORTGAGE BANK PLC ("the Bank"), hereby notifies the Nigerian
Exchange Limited (NGX), shareholders and the Investing public of the
following:
1. That its Audited Financial Statements for the year ended 31st December, 2025
("2025 AFS") may not be ready for filing on the due date of 30th March 2026.
2. That this is anchored on the probability that the Bank may not be able to
secure the approval of its primary regulator -the Central Bank of Nigeria
before the due date of 30 March, 2026.
3. That the anticipated date for the filing the 2025 AFS is on or before 30th
April, 2026
4. That the Bank has sought and obtained the approval of NGX Regulation
Limited an extension to file the 2025 AFS outside the regulatory due date.
