PRESS RELEASE
NOTICE OF DELAY IN THE RELEASE/PUBLICATION OF 2026 Q2
UNAUDITED FINANCIAL STATEMENTS
ABUJA, NIGERIA - 10th March 2026
This is to inform Nigerian Exchange Limited (“NGX” or The “Exchange”) and our esteemed
shareholders and stakeholders that Legend Internet Plc (the “Company”) was unable to file its
Unaudited Financial Statements for the period ended 31 January 2026 (“2026 Q2 UFS”) by the
regulatory filing deadline of 2 March 2026.
The delay was occasioned by the need to complete final internal reviews and reconciliation of
certain financial information to ensure the accuracy and completeness of the Company’s financial
disclosures before submission to The Exchange.
The Company is working diligently to finalise the outstanding processes and anticipates filing its
2026 Q2 UFS on or before 22 March 2026.
The Company sincerely regrets any inconvenience that the delay may cause to its shareholders and
the investing public and remains committed to maintaining the highest standards of transparency
and regulatory compliance.
