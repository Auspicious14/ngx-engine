NOTICE OF CLOSED PERIOD FOR NASCON ALLIED INDUSTRIES PLC.
In accordance with the Rules of the Nigerian Exchange Limited (“NGX”), Nascon
Allied Industries Plc. (“Nascon”) hereby announces that its first quarter will end
on March 31, 2026.
In view of this, Nascon will commence a Closed Period from April 1, 2026, until
twenty-four (24) hours after its Financial Statements for the quarter ending
March 31, 2026, are published on the website of NGX.
A Closed Period is applicable to all Nascon’s insiders, which includes all those
who possess information that may affect Nascon’s share price. Consequently, no
insider nor their connected persons may directly or indirectly deal in the shares
of Nascon until twenty-four (24) hours after the publication of its Financial
Statements through the Issuers’ Portal of NGX.
