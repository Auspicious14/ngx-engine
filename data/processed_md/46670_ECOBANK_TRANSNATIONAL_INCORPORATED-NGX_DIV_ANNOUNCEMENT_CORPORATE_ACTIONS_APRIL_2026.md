Lagos, 15 April 2026: Ecobank Transnational Incorporated (ETI), the Lomé-based parent company of the Ecobank
Group, with shares listed on the Nigerian Exchange Limited (“NGX”), the Ghana Stock Exchange (GSE), and the
Bourse Régionale des Valeurs Mobilières (BRVM) today announced a full-year dividend of 0.16 US cents
(US$0.0016) per ordinary share for the year ended 31 December 2025 as declared by the board of directors of ETI,
subject to shareholders’ approval at the 2026 Annual General Meeting. The dividend is payable on 30 June 2026
to shareholders whose names appear on the Register of Members at the close of business on 12 June 2026.
Corporate Actions Announcement
Ecobank Transnational Incorporated hereby announce as follows:
Period Ended 31st December 2025
A final dividend of 0.16 US cents for every ordinary share, subject to appropriate withholding
tax and approval, will be paid to shareholders whose names appear in the Register of Members
Proposed Dividend at the close of business on 12 June 2026
Proposed Bonus NIL
Closure of Register The Register of Shareholders will be closed from 15 – 19 June 2026 all inclusive
Qualification Date Close of business 12 June 2026
The dividend is payable electronically on 30 June 2026 to shareholders whose names appear
on the Register of Members as of 12 June 2026 and who have completed the e-dividend
Payment Date registration and mandated the Registrar to pay their dividends into their bank accounts.
Shareholders who are yet to complete the e-dividend registration are advised to download the
Registrar’s E-Dividend Mandate Activation Form, which is also available on the website of the
E-Dividend Registrars:www.greenwichregistrars.com, complete and submit to the Registrar or their
Registration respective Banks.
Unclaimed Dividend We advise shareholders with unclaimed, yet to be presented for payment or returned for
Warrants and Share validation, dividend warrants and share certificates to complete the e-dividend registration
Certificates form or contact the Registrar.
Date of Annual
General Meeting The Company’s Annual General Meeting will be held on 3 June 2026 at 10.30am GMT
