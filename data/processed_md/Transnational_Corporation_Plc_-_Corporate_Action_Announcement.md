Corporate Actions Announcement
Transnational Corporation Plc hereby announces as follows:
Period Ended 30 June, 2026
Proposed Dividend An Interim Dividend of Forty Kobo (40 kobo) per ordinary share, subject
to appropriate withholding tax deduction and approval will be paid to
shareholders whose names appear in the Register of Members as at
the close of business on Thursday, July 23, 2026.
Closure of Register The Register of Shareholders will be closed from Friday July 24, 2026 to
Monday, July 27, 2026 (both days inclusive).
Qualification Date Thursday, July 23, 2026
Payment Date On Tuesday, July 28, 2026 dividends will be paid electronically to
shareholders whose names appear on the Register of Members as of
Thursday, July 23, 2026, and who have completed the e-dividend
registration and mandated the Registrar to pay their dividends directly
into their Bank accounts.
E-Dividend Registration Shareholders who are yet to complete the e-dividend registration are
advised to fill the e-Dividend Mandate Activation Form on the Self-
service | NIBSS Document Management Hub Portal, or download the
form from the website of the Registrars at www.africaprudential.com,
complete and submit same to the Registrar or your respective banks.
Unclaimed Dividend Warrants Shareholders with dividend warrants and share certificates that have
and remained unclaimed or are yet to be presented for payment or
Share Certificates returned for validation are advised to complete the e-dividend
registration or contact the Registrar.
