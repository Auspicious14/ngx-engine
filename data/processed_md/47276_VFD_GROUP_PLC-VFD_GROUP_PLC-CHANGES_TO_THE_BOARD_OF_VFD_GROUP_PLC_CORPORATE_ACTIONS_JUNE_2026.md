VFD GROUP PLC – CHANGES TO THE BOARD OF VFD GROUP PLC
In addition to the disclosure made during the recently held Annual General Meeting, we hereby
inform the Nigerian Exchange Limited (“NGX” or “The Exchange”) and the investing public that the
following Directors have retired from the Board of VFD Group Plc (the Company) in accordance with
the provisions of Section 285(1) of the Companies and Allied Matters Act (CAMA) 2020, having not
offered themselves for re-election:
1. Mr. Adegboyega Fatoki
2. Mr. Azubike Emodi
3. Mr. Hubert Asamoah
Also, Mr. Mobolaji Adewumi stepped down from the Board effective immediately. This is to enable
him focus on his primary responsibilities
The Board appreciates their contributions to the Company during their tenure and wishes them
success in their future endeavours.
We will continue to keep The Exchange and the investing public informed of any further material
developments as required.
