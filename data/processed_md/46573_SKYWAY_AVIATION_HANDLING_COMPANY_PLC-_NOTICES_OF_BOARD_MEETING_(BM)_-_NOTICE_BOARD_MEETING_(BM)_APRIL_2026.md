Lagos, 2nd April 2026
NOTICE OF MEETING OF THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF SAHCO PLC
Skyway Aviation Handling Company Plc. (SAHCO or the Company) wishes to notify Nigerian
Exchange Limited (NGX), the Company’s shareholders and the investing public that the Board of
Directors of SAHCO will meet on Friday, 24th April 2026, by 11.00 am prompt, to consider, among
others, the following proposed agenda items:
1. Consideration and approval of the Unaudited Financial Statements for the first quarter
ended 31st March 2026 (2026, Q1 UFS); and
2. Any Other Business the Board may deem fit.
In accordance with the Rulebook of The Exchange, the Company commenced its Closed
Period on 1 April 2026. Consequently, the Directors of the Company and all other insiders
have been advised that they are prohibited from trading in the Company’s shares during the
Closed Period until twenty-four (24) hours after the release of the Company’s 2026, Q1 UFS
to NGX and by extension, the general public.
Jesuyemisi Odeyemi
Company Secretary
