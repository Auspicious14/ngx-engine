LAGOS, 16th October, 2026
NOTICE OF BOARD OF DIRECTORS MEETING
This is to notify Nigerian Exchange Limited (NGX or The Exchange) and the investing
public pursuant to NGX’s Rules that a Board Meeting of Fortis Global Insurance Plc (the
Company) has been scheduled to hold on Thursday, 22nd October 2026 to consider and
approve the Unaudited Financial Statements of the Company for the third quarter (Q3)
ended 30th September 2026.
Consequently, the closed period for trading in the Company’s shares, which commenced
from 1st October 2026 in compliance with NGX’s amended closed period rule, will run until
twenty-four (24) hours after the Q3 Unaudited Financial Statements for the period ended
30th September 2026, is released to NGX and by extension the general public.
Accordingly, no Insider of the Company, and their connected persons may directly or
indirectly deal in the securities of the Company in any manner during the Closed Period.
