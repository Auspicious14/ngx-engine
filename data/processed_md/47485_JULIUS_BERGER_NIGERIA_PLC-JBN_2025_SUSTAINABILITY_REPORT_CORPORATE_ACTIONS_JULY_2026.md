Message From the Managing Director | 2025 Sustainability Report 2025 Sustainability Report | Message From the Managing Director
Message From the
Managing Director
Delivering complex infrastructure projects requires disciplined execution, deep technical expertise, and strong collaboration
across our workforce and partners. Throughout 2025, Julius Berger maintained steady operational performance while
continuing to strengthen efficiency across its project portfolio.
The Group recorded strong financial results during the year, reflecting sustained project activity and improved cost management.
Revenue increased by 34.09% to ₦759.9 billion, while Profit Before Tax rose to ₦41.0 billion, supported by effective coordination
across multiple project locations and continued focus on operational discipline.
Operational efficiency improved across several areas of the business. Total energy consumption declined by 8%, reducing
from 1.15 million GJ to 1.06 million GJ in 2025, driven by improved fuel management practices and increased utilisation of grid
electricity. In parallel, core construction material consumption decreased by 23.9%, reflecting more effective material planning
and enhanced resource utilisation across project sites.
Enhancing environmental monitoring remained a key focus throughout the year. Julius Berger expanded its tracking of Scope
1 and Scope 2 greenhouse gas emissions, improving visibility into operational emissions and providing a stronger foundation
for future climate-related performance management. Our alternative energy consumption scheme rose by 707.7% due to solar
energy consumption.
Safety continues to be fundamental to our operations. In 2025, the Group maintained a Lost Time Injury Frequency Rate below
1 for the fifteenth consecutive year, demonstrating the effectiveness of strengthened safety procedures, comprehensive training
programmes, and robust operational controls implemented across our project locations.
Our workforce remains central to delivering these outcomes. During the year, the Group delivered 46,454 learning hours
across 106 training programmes, strengthening technical capabilities and supporting leadership development throughout the
organisation.
Beyond project execution, Julius Berger remains committed to supporting the communities in which we operate. In 2025,
the Group invested over ₦830 million in corporate social responsibility initiatives, supporting programmes across education,
community development, social welfare, and emergency response.
The Group also continued to strengthen its sustainability reporting systems during the year in preparation for the transition
to the International Sustainability Standards Board (ISSB) framework, including IFRS S1 and IFRS S2. These efforts focus on
improving sustainability data governance, enhancing climate-related risk assessment, and further integrating sustainability
considerations into operational planning and decision-making.
Looking ahead, Julius Berger will remain focused on disciplined project execution, responsible resource management,
and continued investment in our people as we support Nigeria’s ongoing infrastructure development. I extend my sincere
appreciation to our employees, clients, partners, and host communities for their continued commitment and collaboration.
Engr. Dr. Peer Lubasch
Managing Director
