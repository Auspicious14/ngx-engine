Lagos, Nigeria – 24 July 2026
OUTCOME OF FCMB GROUP PLC BOARD OF DIRECTORS’ MEETING
At the Board of Directors’ meeting of FCMB Group Plc (“the Company”) held on Friday, July 24,
2026, the following was approved:
1. The Unaudited Financial Statements of the Company for the quarter ended June 30,
2026 (Q2 2026 UFS).
2. The Company’s Long-term Incentive Plan, subject to regulatory approval.
Following the Board’s approval, the Unaudited Financial Statements will be published on or
before July 30, 2026, in compliance with applicable regulatory requirements.
The Closed period earlier declared from 1 July 2026, restricting the Company’s Insiders from
transacting in the shares of the Company, will continue until 24 hours after the Q2 2026 UFS
has been released to the public.
Olufunmilayo Adedibu (Mrs.)
Company Secretary
