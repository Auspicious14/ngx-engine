28th August 2026
PZ CUSSONS NIGERIA PLC – RESOLUTION FROM THE SPECIAL BOARD
MEETING HELD ON 27TH AUGUST 2026
This is to notify our esteemed Shareholders, the investing public and the Nigerian
Exchange Limited that the Board of Directors of PZ Cussons Nigeria PLC (“the
Company”), at the special meeting held on Thursday, 27th August 2026, considered
and approved the following:
1. The Audited Financial Statements for the year ended 31st May 2026
2. A final dividend of N9,926,192,613 (Nine Billion, Nine Hundred and Ninety-
Two Million, One Hundred and Ninety-Two Thousand, Six Hundred and
Thirteen Naira) that is ₦2.50 (Two Naira, Fifty Kobo) per ordinary share of 50
Kobo (Fifty Kobo), for the financial year ended 31st May 2026 be proposed to
the members of the Company for approval at the Company’s next Annual
General Meeting.
3. The recommendation to the shareholders of the Company, for approval at the
Annual General Meeting, Scrip Dividend to qualifying shareholders who may
elect to receive new ordinary shares in the Company in lieu of all or part of
their cash dividend entitlement.
Please note that the Company’s closed period, which commenced on 1st June 2026,
will remain in effect until twenty-four (24) hours after the release of the Audited
Financial Statements for the year ended 31st May 2026 to the market.
