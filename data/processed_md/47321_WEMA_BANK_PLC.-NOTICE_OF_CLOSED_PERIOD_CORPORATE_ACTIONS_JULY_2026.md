Lagos, 30 June 2026
NOTICE OF CLOSED PERIOD
In line with Rule 17. 17-17.18 of Nigerian Exchange Limited (NGX or The Exchange) Rulebook (as
amended), Wema Bank Plc (the Bank) declared a Closed Period from 1st of July, 2026 till twenty-four
(24) hours after the Bank’s Unaudited Financial Statements for the second quarter ending 30th of
June, 2026 (2026 Q2 UFS) and other information have been made public through The Exchange’s
Issuer’s Portal.
Accordingly, no insiders of the Bank and their connected persons may directly or indirectly, deal (buy
or sell) in the shares of the Bank in any manner during the Closed Period until twenty-four (24) hours
after the release of the 2026 Q2 UFS through the Issuers’ Portal of NGX and by extension, the
general public.
All insiders of the Bank and persons closely connected to them have been appropriately advised.
