NOTIFICATION OF RESIGNATION OF EXTERNAL AUDITORS – NGOZI MONICA
OKONKWO & CO
We write to notify Nigerian Exchange Limited (“NGX”) that Ngozi Monica Okonkwo & Co, the
External Auditors of Cutix Plc (“the Company”) have resigned their appointment as the
Company’s External Auditors.
The Company received the Auditors’ formal notification of resignation dated July 24, 2026, with
the resignation taking effect from the date of the Company’s 43rd Annual General Meeting (AGM)
held on August 28, 2026. The reason for the resignation as stated by the Auditors followed a
strategic review of their audit portfolio which gave rise to their decision to discontinue as the
External Auditors of the Company beyond the completion of the audit for the financial year ended
April 30, 2026.
The Board of Directors has taken note of the resignation and has commenced the process of
appointing a replacement External Auditor, subject to the necessary regulatory and shareholder
approvals.
The Company will make further disclosure to NGX upon the appointment of the new External
Auditors and completion of the applicable approval processes.
We hereby notify NGX of the development in compliance with the applicable Listing Rules and
disclosure requirements.
