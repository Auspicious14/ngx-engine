EXTRACT OF THE BOARD OR DIRECTORS MEETING OF
INFINITY TRUST MORTGAGE BANK
HELD ON FEBRUARY 10TH, 2026
Abuja: 11 FEBRUARY 2026
Infinity Trust Mortgage Bank Plc (the Bank) wishes to notify its shareholders, the investing
public and Nigerian Exchange Limited (NGX) that the Board of Directors of the Bank met on
the 10th day of February 2026 at its registered office, No. 11, Kaura Namoda Street, Off
Faskari Crescent Area 3, Garki, Abuja, as scheduled.
At the meeting, the Board resolved and approved as follows:
1) “That the Audited Financial Statements for the year ended December 31, 2025,
together with the Management letter, are hereby approved by the Board to be
forwarded to CBN for approval.
2) “That a proposed Dividend of 35 Kobo per Ordinary Share with respect to the 2025
Financial Year is hereby approved, subject to ratification at the Annual General Meeting
(AGM).
3) “That a Bonus share Issue of one to one (1:1) was approved by the Board subject to
AGM ratification and regulatory approval.
4) “That 21st May, 2026 was proposed and approved for the Bank’s AGM
5) “That the appointment of Dr. Obasegun Adetokunbo Oni as Non-Executive
Director is hereby approved by the Board, subject to CBN approval.
Mr. Obasegun Adetokunbo Oni is a certified Information Security Professional
(CISSP, CSSP) with over 25 years’ experience securing enterprise systems and
driving compliance at executive corporate level. He is adept at integrating security
into major application development, leading and supporting cross-functional
projects, mitigating risks and modernising policies to align with industry
frameworks. He has a proven track record in balancing strategic initiatives with
security operations. Mr. Oni obtained a Post Graduate Studies degree in Cyber
security from Northumbria University in 2017. He also holds a BSc. (Hons) in
Pharmaceutical Sciences in 1993 from University of Greenwich, London. He is
certified in CISSP - Certified Information Systems Security Professional, CCSP -
Certified Cloud Security Professional, CISA - Certified Information Systems
Auditor, AWS CCP - AWS Certified Cloud Practitioner, CCIE (Written), CCNP - Cisco

Certifications achieved and CCSA - Check Point Security Administrator. His core
technical proficiencies includes Cyber security consultancy, digital business
entrepreneur/owner, Cloud Security experience with AWS, Azure, and Oracle cloud
security, strategy policy development, risk assessment and management,
compliance audit, supplier assurance, Information Security Frameworks - NIST
CSF, ISO27001, PCI DSS, ISF, security operational tools such as Covert-Swarm,
Threat Modelling (Threat Dragon), Penetration Testing, Vulnerability Assessment,
SaaS Security Posture Management, Secure SSDL, CD-CI, software testing. He is
also skilled in IP Network and Security Technologies - Virtualised/Extended
Infrastructure, Network Architecture/Topology (Design and Implementation) and
Data/voice networking appliances – Device/Vendor Management. He started his
career as a Senior Information Security Consultant and later worked as a Senior
Consultant at ICBC Standard Bank, Gresham Street London. Mr. Oni worked as a
Senior Engineer (Consultant) at Intercity Technology - Regent House, Allum Gate,
Ordinance Survey Southampton. He is an experienced IT Specialist Engineer.
