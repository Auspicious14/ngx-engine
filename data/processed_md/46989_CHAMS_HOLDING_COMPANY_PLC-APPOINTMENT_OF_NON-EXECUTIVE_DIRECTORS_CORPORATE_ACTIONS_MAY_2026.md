5th May 2026
Lagos, Nigeria
APPOINTMENT OF NON-EXECUTIVE DIRECTORS
Chams Holding Company Plc (the “Company”) hereby notifies the Nigerian Exchange
Limited (“NGX”) and the investing public that the Company has appointed MR. FEMI
OKANLAWON and MR. OLABISI MAKANJUOLA, respectively, as Non-Executive
Directors effective 1st May 2026.
PROFILE OF MR FEMI OKANLAWON
Mr. Okanlawon is a Stakeholder / Businessman in the Real Estate Industry. He founded
Two (2) CBN Licensed Institutions (Snow Microfinance Bank Limited (Financial Services) &
Snow Financial Company Limited (Financial Services).
He is a Computer Science graduate of Yaba College of Technology and holds an M.Sc. Computer
Science, from Lagos State University. He also holds an MBA in International Business from Lagos
State University and a certification in the Advanced Management Programme (AMP), from Said
Business School at the University of Oxford, among others.
Part of his proven track record is pioneering the first Card technology at the Chams Group as the
First General Manager of Super Card Limited. His core area of expertise is in Information
Technology.
PROFILE OF MR. OLABISI MAKANJUOLA
Mr. Olabisi Makanjuola is a Partner and the head of the Dispute Resolution Practice Group at
the law firm of Olaniwun Ajayi LP. He is an alumnus of the Lagos Business School and the
immediate past Chairman of the Nigerian Bar Association, Lagos Branch. He also currently
sits as a member of the Governing Council of the Lagos Multi-Door Courthouse. He has a
proven track record in Dispute resolution.
His comprehensive understanding of complex disputes, including those related to corporate
shareholding, capital market regulations, commercial contracts, fraud, white-collar crimes,
and infrastructure projects, earned him recognition as a "Rising Star" by Legal 500 in April
2022, “Next Generation Partner,” and a “Recommended Lawyer.
The Board is confident that the dynamic leadership and wealth of experience of both Mr. Femi
Okanlawon and Mr. Olabisi Makanjuola will foster collaboration with our stakeholders,
contribute to the fulfilment of the Company’s strategic goals, and deliver transformative
results.
