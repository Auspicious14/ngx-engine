March 31, 2026
Lagos, Nigeria
ANNOUNCEMENT OF BOARD MEETING DATE AND CLOSED PERIOD
This is to inform Nigerian Exchange Limited (‘The Exchange’) and the investing public
that the quarterly meeting of the Board of Directors of Access Holdings Plc (‘the
Company’) has been scheduled for Thursday, April 30, 2026, to among other things
consider and approve the Group’s Unaudited Financial Statements for the quarter
ending March 31, 2026 (‘the Results’).
In line with The Exchange’s Listing Rules, the Company’s insiders are reminded that
the earlier declared Closed Period on transactions on the Company’s securities
remains in force until twenty-four (24) hours after the release of the Results.
Accordingly, no insider may directly or indirectly deal in the Company’s securities in
any manner during the Closed Period.
