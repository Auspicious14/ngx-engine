October 16, 2026
LAGOS, NIGERIA
ANNOUNCEMENT OF BOARD OF DIRECTORS MEETING OF CORONATION
INSURANCE PLC
This is to inform the Nigerian Exchange Limited (“The Exchange”) and the Investing
Public that a meeting of the Board of Directors of Coronation Insurance Plc (“the
Company”) is scheduled to hold on Wednesday, the 28th day of October 2026 to
amongst other things review the Company’s performance for the third quarter of the
2026 Financial Year as well as to consider and approve the Company’s Q3, Unaudited
Financial Statements for the period ended 30th September 2026 (Q3, 2026 UFS).
The Company had in compliance with the Closed Period Rules of The Exchange entered
a Closed Period on 1st October 2026, in respect of transactions on its securities which
will continue to be in force until twenty-four (24) hours after the release of the
Company’s 2026 Q3 UFS to the Exchange and by extension, to the general public.
Accordingly, all insiders are prohibited from dealing in the Company’s securities during
the Closed Period.
FOR: CORONATION INSURANCE PLC
MARY AGHA
COMPANY SECRETARY
