Lagos, 30th March, 2026
NOTICE OF BOARD MEETING AND CLOSED PERIOD OF SUNU ASSURANCES NIGERIA PLC
NOTICE IS HEREBY GIVEN that the meeting of the Board of Directors of SUNU Assurances Nigeria Plc (the
Company) is scheduled to hold on Wednesday, 22nd April 2026 to review and approve the Quarterly Financial
Statements for the first quarter ending 31st March 2026 (2026 Q1 UFS).
Pursuant to Rule 17:18 (a) of the Exchange’s Rulebook on Closed Period (as amended), the Closed Period
which commenced on 1 January 2026 shall remain in effect until twenty-four (24) hours after the release of the
Company’s Audited Financial Statements for the year ended 31st December 2025 (2025 AFS) and the 2026 Q1
UFS to the public.
Consequently, no insider of the Company and their connected persons shall deal in the securities of the
Company directly or indirectly during this Closed Period.
