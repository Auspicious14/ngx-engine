Lagos, 30th October 2026
ARADEL HOLDINGS PLC: BOARD MEETING RESOLUTION
The Board of Directors of Aradel Holdings Plc (the “Company”) wishes to inform the general
public and Nigerian Exchange Limited (NGX) that the Board, at its meeting which held on
Tuesday, 27th October 2026, reviewed and approved the Third Quarter Unaudited Interim
Financial Statements and Accounts for the period ended 30th September 2026 (2026, Q3
UFS).
Further, vide a written resolution dated Friday, 30th October 2026, the Board approved an
interim dividend payment of ₦7.00 (Seven Naira) per share for the period ended 30th
September 2026, subject to applicable withholding tax deductions.
The 2026 Q3 UFS will be released to the public through the Issuers’ Portal today 30th
October, 2026.
Consequently, the Closed Period restricting all insiders of the Company and their connected
persons from dealing in the shares of the Company will continue until twenty-four hours
after the 2026 Q3 UFS is released to the general public.
For: Aradel Holdings Plc
