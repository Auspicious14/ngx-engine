22nd May 2026
LAGOS, NIGERIA
RESOLUTIONS PASSED AT THE 3RD ANNUAL GENERAL MEETING OF CONSOLIDATED
HALLMARK HOLDINGS PLC
At the Annual General Meeting (AGM) of Consolidated Hallmark Holdings Plc (the Company)
held at 10 a.m. on Thursday 21st May 2026 at the External Ball Room, Federal Palace Hotel,
6-8 Ahmadu Bello Way, Victoria Island, Lagos, the following resolutions were considered and
passed by the Shareholders:
ORDINARY BUSINESS
1. That the Audited Financial Statements of the Company for the year ended 31st
December 2025 together with the reports of the Directors, Auditors and Audit
Committee, were received and approved.
2. That a final dividend of 15 Kobo per share was declared in addition to the interim
dividend of 10 Kobo per share which was paid on the 27th of August 2025 bringing the
total dividend to 25 Kobo for the financial year ended 31st December 2025.
3. That (a) Dr. Tony Anonyai, (b) Mr. Adegbola Adesina, and (c) Chief Sunny Obidegwu
being Directors retiring by rotation and offering themselves for re-election, be and
were hereby re-elected as Directors of the Company.
4. That the re-appointment of PKF Professional Services as the External Auditors of
Consolidated Hallmark Holdings Plc with effect from January 1, 2026, be and was
hereby approved.
5. That the Directors be and were thereby authorised to fix the remuneration of the
External Auditors for the year ended 31st December 2026.
6. That the remuneration of Managers of the Company was disclosed.
7. That the election of (a) Chief James Emadoye, (b) Chief Simon Okiotorhoro and; (c)
Mr. John Azubuike Ogbeka as shareholders’ representatives of the Statutory Audit
Committee be and were hereby approved and, (a) Mr. Adegbola Adesina and (b) HRH
Eze Ben Onuora were appointed to represent the Board on the Audit Committee.
SPECIAL BUSINESS
1. That the remuneration of the Non-Executive Directors for the year ending 31st
December 2026 be and was hereby approved.
Thank you.
CONSOLIDATED HALLMARK HOLDINGS PLC
