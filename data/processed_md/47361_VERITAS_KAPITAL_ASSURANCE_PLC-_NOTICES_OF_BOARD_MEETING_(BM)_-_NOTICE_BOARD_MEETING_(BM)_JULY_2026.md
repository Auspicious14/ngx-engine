ABUJA: 1 July, 2026
NOTIFICATION OF BOARD MEETING OF VERITAS KAPITAL ASSURANCE
PLC
The Board and Management of Veritas Kapital Assurance Plc (the
Company) wish to notify Nigerian Exchange Limited (NGX), esteemed
Shareholders, Stakeholders, and the general public that the Board of
Directors of the Company will be approving the Unaudited Financial
Stat ements for the period ended 30 June 2026 (2026 Q2 UFS) of the
Company by circularization.
However, notice is hereby given that a meeting of the Board of
Directors of the Company will be held on Thursday, July 30, 2026, at
10:00 a.m. to ratify the 2026 Q2 UFS and review the operational
stra tegies of the Company.
Per the post-listing requirements of NGX, the Closed Period for trading
in the shares of the Company by Insiders commenced with effect from
July 1, 2026. Accordingly, no insider of the Company and their
conn ected persons may directly or indirectly deal in the securities of
the Company until twenty-four (24) hours after the release of the 2026
Q2 UFS to NGX and by extension, the general public.
