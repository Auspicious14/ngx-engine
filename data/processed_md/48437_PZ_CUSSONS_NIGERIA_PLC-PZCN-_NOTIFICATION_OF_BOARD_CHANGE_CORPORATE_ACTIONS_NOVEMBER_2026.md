23 November 2026
NOTIFICATION OF THE RESIGNATION OF A DIRECTOR
PZ Cussons Nigeria Plc (“PZCN” or “the Company”) hereby notifies its esteemed shareholders,
the investing public, and Nigerian Exchange Limited (“NGX”) that Mallam Ballama Manu, an
Independent Non-Executive Director of the Company, has resigned from the Board of the
Company with effect from 21st November 2026.
The Board expresses its sincere appreciation to Mallam Manu for his contributions and
service to the Company and wishes him success in his future endeavours.
OGHENEKEVWE OGEFERE
Company Secretary
