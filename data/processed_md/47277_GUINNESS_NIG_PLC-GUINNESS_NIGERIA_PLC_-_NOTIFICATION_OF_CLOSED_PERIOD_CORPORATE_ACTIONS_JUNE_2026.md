GUINNESS NIGERIA PLC - NOTIFICATION OF CLOSED PERIOD
In line with Nigerian Exchange Limited (NGX) post listing requirements, Guinness Nigeria Plc
(the “Company”), hereby notifies NGX, its shareholders and the investing public that it is
declaring a Closed Period with effect from 1st of July 2026 until twenty-four (24) hours after
the release of the Company’s Unaudited Financial Statements for the quarter ending 30th June
2026 to the public through NGX Issuers’ Portal.
Therefore, no insider of the Company and their connected persons may directly or indirectly
deal in the securities of the Company during the closed period.
