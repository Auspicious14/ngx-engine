GUINNESS NIGERIA PLC
Lagos, 1 April 2026
NOTIFICATION OF BOARD MEETING OF GUINNESS NIGERIA PLC
Guinness Nigeria Plc (the Company) wishes to notify the Nigerian Exchange Limited (NGX) and
the investing public that a meeting of the Board of Directors of the Company will hold on
Wednesday, 15 April 2026 for the purpose of considering the following, amongst other
matters:
a. Unaudited Financial Statements for the quarter ended 31 March 2026.
b. The proposal for the recommendation of interim dividend.
Consequently, the Company has declared the commencement of a closed period for trading
in the shares of the Company by its insiders from 1 April 2026, until twenty-four (24) hours
after the Unaudited Financial Statements for the quarter ended 31 March 2026 has been
released to the public.
During this period, insiders and other interested parties are expected to observe the closed
trading period and refrain from dealing in Guinness Nigeria Plc shares.
The results for the period as approved by the Board will thereafter be uploaded through the
Nigerian Exchange Issuers’ Portal not later than 16 April 2026.
