UNITED BANK FOR AFRICA PLC
NOTIFICATION OF BOARD APPROVAL OF THE GROUP’S UNAUDITED
FINANCIAL STATEMENTS FOR THE NINE MONTHS ENDED 30 SEPTEMBER
2026
Lagos, Friday, October 30, 2026
Further to its earlier notification, United Bank for Africa Plc (the
“Bank”) wishes to inform the Nigerian Exchange Limited and the
investing public that its Board of Directors met on Thursday, 29
October 2026, and considered and approved the Group’s
unaudited financial statements for the nine (9) months ended 30
September 2026.
The financial statements will be published alongside the Group’s
audited half-year financial statements, subject to the requisite
approval of the Central Bank of Nigeria.
Accordingly, the Bank’s closed period shall remain in effect until 24
hours after the publication of the financial statements to the
investing public.
BY THE ORDER OF THE BOARD
BILI A. ODUM
GROUP COMPANY SECRETARY/LEGAL COUNSEL
