Corporate Actions Announcement
Zichis Agro Allied Industries Plc hereby announce as follows:
Period Ended
31st December, 2025
Proposed Dividend A Final Dividend of 20 kobo per 50 kobo ordinary share, subject to withholding
tax,and on approval will be paid to shareholders whose
name appear in the share register of members as at the close of business on
the 16th March, 2026
Proposed Bonus Bonus shares in the ratio of One (1) new share for every One (1) existing
share held by shareholders whose names appear in the Register of
Members at the close of business on 16th March, 2026, subject to the
approval of the shareholders at the annual general meeting.
Closure of Register
The Register of Shareholders will be closed from 17th of March 2026
Qualification Date
16th March, 2026
Payment Date On 7th May 2026, dividends will be paid electronically to shareholders whose
names appear on the Register of Members as at 16h of March, 2026 and who
have completed e-dividend registration and mandated the
Registrar to pay their dividends directly into their Bank accounts.
E-Dividend Registration
Shareholders who are yet to complete the e-dividend registration are
advised to download the Registrar’s E-Dividend Mandate Activation Form
from Datamax Registrars Limited website at
http://www.datamaxregistrars.com complete and submit to the Registrar or
their respective Banks.
