Lagos: Wednesday, 1sth July 2026
NOTICE OF BOARD MEETING AND CLOSED PERIOD OF BUA
CEMENT PLC
In compliance with the post-listing requirements of Nigerian Exchange Limited
(NGX), BUA Cement Plc (the Company) hereby notifies its shareholders, NGX,
and the investing public that a meeting of the Board of Directors of the Company
has been scheduled to hold on 30th July 2026 to consider, amongst other matters,
the Company's Unaudited Financial Statements for the period ended 30th June
2026.In compliance with the post-listing requirements of Nigerian Exchange
Limited for listed Companies, BUA Cement Plc (the Company) closed period for
trading in the Company’s shares commenced on Wednesday 1st July 2026 and will
continue until twenty-four (24) hours after the Unaudited Financial Statements for
the period ended 30th June 2026 have been released to the relevant regulatory
bodies and the public.
Accordingly, no insider of the Company and their connected persons may directly
or indirectly deal in the securities of the Company during the closed period.
BY ORDER OF THE BOARD
Satomi Hauwa
Company Secretary/CLO
