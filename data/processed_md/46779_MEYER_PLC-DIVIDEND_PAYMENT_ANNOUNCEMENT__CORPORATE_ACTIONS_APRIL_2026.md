Corporate Actions Announcement
MEYER PLC hereby announce as follows:
Period Ended 31st December 2025
A Final Dividend of 45 kobo for every share of 50 kobo, subject to appropriate
withholding tax and approval, will be paid to shareholders whose names appear
in the Register of Members as at the close of business on Thursday, the 30th day
Proposed Dividend of April, 2026.
Proposed Bonus NIL
The Register of Shareholders will be closed from Friday, the 1st of May to the 4th
Closure of Register of May 2026.
Qualification Date 30th of April, 2026
On 25th of May 2026, dividends will be paid electronically to shareholders
whose names appear on the Register of Members as at 30th of April 2026 and
who have completed the e-dividend registration and mandated the Registrar to
Payment Date pay their dividends directly into their Bank accounts.
Shareholders who are yet to complete the e-dividend registration are advised
to download the Registrar’s E-Dividend Mandate Activation Form.
https://e-forms.greenwichregistrars.com, which is also available on the website
of the Registrars: Greenwich Registrars & Data Solutions, complete and submit
E-Dividend Registration to the Registrar or their respective Banks.
Unclaimed Dividend Shareholders with dividend warrants and share certificates that have remained
Warrants and Share unclaimed, or are yet to be presented for payment or returned for validation,
Certificates are advised to complete the e-dividend registration or contact the Registrar.
The Company’s Annual General Meeting will be held at Radisson Blu Hotel,
Date of Annual General Ikeja, 38/40 Isaac John Street, GRA Ikeja, Lagos on Friday, 22nd May 2026 at 11
Meeting am.
